# Flynt Boardwalk Social Constitution V1

Status: active additive draft; Boardwalk occupant organization adopted 2026-10-07
Backbone: `HOLLOW_GROVE_COMPROMISE_V1_DRAFT.md`
Parent authorities: `FLYNT_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`,
`HOLLOW_GROVE_CONSTITUTIONAL_GEOGRAPHY_V2.md`, and the common Bond
Constitution

## Constitutional Boundary

The Boardwalk remains the Glaüshouse / Flynt route whose dominant verb is
**Return**:

```text
Recovery Ward
→ Boardwalk
→ Flynt
→ Recognition
```

A Being leaves recovery by choice and returns to ordinary civic presence. The
Boardwalk may stage public recognition, affiliation, glamour, loyalty, debt,
and conflict. It may not:

- replace Glaüshouse discharge, consent, recovery, or Clearance;
- grant Stonebend Name or Title;
- replace Sandmanor proof;
- create a second Flynt sovereign;
- bypass Chimera or Tross;
- turn a cultural title into constitutional office;
- treat a person as property;
- make bodily condition proof of virtue, vice, competence, or authority.

`flynt-constitution` remains the sole executable Flynt authority. The Pimp,
Gimp, Trooners, Gooners, Hoes, Goons, and Goon Bond are Boardwalk cultural and
social identities above that locked authority. The two retinue names and their
rival organization are adopted here; detailed membership mechanics remain open.

## Root Polarity

### Canonical Naming Lock

The following titles are now fixed terminology for Hollow Grove and must not
be interchanged:

| Title | Layer | Pole | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gimp of the Underway** | Underway | Current | hidden routes, endurance, sanctuary, protection, and continuity |
| **Pimp of the Boardwalk** | Boardwalk | Aura | public visibility, patronage, access, reputation, and return |

The titles are cultural identities, not constitutional offices, species,
medical diagnoses, ownership claims, or automatic Sympiote outcomes. The
selection, challenge, and ending procedures remain open for the playable trial;
the names and their jurisdictions do not.

```text
The Pimp and his Trooners project Aura.
The Gimp and his Gooners carry Current.
The Boardwalk makes their rivalry visible.
```

The **Pimp of the Boardwalk** is the public Aura pole: attraction, glamour,
patronage, access, leverage, rumor, and recognition.

The **Gimp of the Underway** is the hidden Current pole: endurance, embodied
burden, loyalty, protection, labor, force, route knowledge, and continuity
through visible deterioration. The Gimp may operate from the Boardwalk, but
the Underway is the jurisdictional layer that gives the title its depth.

Neither pole is automatically lawful or unlawful. Each may shelter people,
create belonging, and distribute opportunity. Each may also become coercive:
the Pimp through control of visibility and desire; the Gimp through demanded
fealty, debt, and force.

### Boardwalk Occupants and Rival Retinues

The Boardwalk's two principal resident camps are **the Pimp and his Trooners**
and **the Gimp and his Gooners**. The Gimp keeps his Underway title and route
depth while maintaining a visible Boardwalk retinue.

| Camp | Boardwalk gathering place | Shared expression | What they compete to provide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pimp and Trooners | Pimp Court | Aura, public presence, patronage, performance, invitation | the welcome, opportunity, and reputation that make a return visible |
| Gimp and Gooners | Gimp Retinue | Current, loyalty, escort, sanctuary, route knowledge | the protection, dependable work, and continuity that make a return endure |

They are rivals for influence, public credit, willing followers, and the trust
of people returning to the Boardwalk. A disputed welcome, escort, job, or
performance can bring that rivalry into a scene. Neither camp's claim settles
what an individual actually needs or chooses.

The Neutral Promenade is common ground: unaffiliated occupants, returning
residents, and witnesses can hear both camps without choosing a side. The
Recognition Stage makes their contributions and competing accounts public.
The camps can cooperate on a bounded task, challenge each other's account,
or lose a person's support to lawful independence. Their rivalry is a recurring
social relationship shaped by competition, occasional cooperation, and changing
support.

## The Pimp

**The Pimp of the Boardwalk** is the singular cultural center of the Boardwalk's glamour pole
for the first playable slice. The title is public, theatrical, and locally
recognized. It is not the Tross office, a Flynt government office, a
constitutional sovereign, or a source of House authority.

The Pimp's influence is real only while other people continue to project,
repeat, and recognize it.

The Pimp may:

- host public performance and social presentation;
- sponsor adult affiliates;
- broker introductions and opportunities;
- shape fashion, rumor, reputation, and invitation;
- offer mutually accepted patronage;
- recognize a public association within the Boardwalk;
- use wealth and spectacle to make a return visible.

The Pimp may not:

- own a Hoe or any other Being;
- treat patronage as consent;
- convert debt into bodily or sexual entitlement;
- conceal terms, retaliation, or exit conditions;
- use Aura pressure to manufacture agreement;
- substitute popularity for Flynt constitutional recognition;
- grant Name, Title, proof, Clearance, office, or Synthesis authority.

The title survives only through continuing social recognition. It does not
become constitutional authority through fame.

## Trooners

**Trooners** are the Pimp's voluntarily aligned Boardwalk retinue. A **Trooner**
participates in the camp's public presence through welcome, presentation,
performance, introductions, patronage, reputation, or other disclosed work.
Their collective activity projects the Pimp's Aura, while each retains an
individual reputation, purpose, and ability to question him.

Trooner names a cultural affiliation. It does not assign a Nature, gender,
office, private service, or compulsory profession. A Trooner can refuse a
particular offer, leave the camp, or work with the Gooners on an agreed task.
The Pimp's effective influence depends on their continuing participation.

Hoes remain a distinct, self-chosen role. A Hoe may take part in the Trooners,
but neither term automatically establishes the other. Patronage alone also
does not establish Trooner affiliation.

## Hoes

**Hoes** are adult, self-directing Boardwalk affiliates whose performance,
desire, style, information, rumor, hospitality, negotiation, and recognition
project the Pimp's public Aura.

Hoe is a cultural role, not a species, gender, Frame, profession lock, property
class, or automatic sexual obligation. Individual Hoes may be performers,
hosts, artists, negotiators, escorts, fixers, courtiers, information brokers,
or other adult public figures. The individual determines the exact work and
boundaries.

Every lawful affiliation requires:

- an adult Being with capacity;
- explicit and revocable participation;
- disclosed terms and obligations;
- the right to refuse a particular act;
- an exit path;
- preserved identity independent of the Pimp;
- no retaliation for protected refusal or truthful evidence;
- no inference of sexual consent from role, clothing, debt, prior conduct, or
  public affiliation.

Hoes are not passive Aura batteries. They possess their own reputations,
relationships, ambitions, rivalries, evidence, and power. Withdrawal of their
participation may reduce or end the Pimp's effective social reach.

## The Gimp

**The Gimp of the Underway** is the singular kingly cultural center of the
Underway's endurance pole. The figure is inspired
by the afflicted-sovereign archetype: bodily deterioration is visible, but it
does not erase identity, continuity, dignity, or an otherwise lawful Title.

Gimp is a reclaimed in-world Boardwalk title. It is not a general descriptor
for disabled or ill people and must not be applied to an unwilling Being.

The Gimp's exact condition remains a future Hollow Grove medical content
decision. The draft does not equate it automatically with real-world Hansen's
disease, make affliction contagious, or use disease as proof of moral
corruption.

The Gimp may:

- maintain a voluntarily bonded retinue;
- accept service, protection, escort, and witness;
- preserve continuity through visible bodily change;
- offer shelter, mutual defense, work, and belonging;
- challenge the Pimp's control of public value;
- remain present when others would hide or discard an altered body.

The Gimp may not:

- become Tross or bypass Chimera;
- derive authority solely from suffering;
- claim that illness makes every command legitimate;
- own a Goon;
- demand violence outside a lawful Bond;
- convert protection into permanent unreviewable debt;
- use bodily condition to bypass evidence, competence, or House law.

The Gimp embodies a Stonebend-compatible truth without receiving Stonebend
authority from the role:

> An altered body does not erase a Name.

## Goons

**Goon** is the broad Boardwalk term for ordinary people, crews, workers,
escorts, lookouts, protectors, carriers, fighters, fixers, and unaffiliated
operators who live between the Pimp and Gimp poles.

A Goon is not necessarily criminal, violent, unintelligent, subordinate, or
Bonded. It is a social register broad enough to describe the Boardwalk's common
people without creating a species or constitutional class.

A Goon may:

- remain unaffiliated;
- work temporarily for either pole;
- form a lawful Goon Bond;
- challenge or default on a Bond;
- leave after lawful resolution;
- become a public witness;
- refuse coercive Current or Aura;
- retain identity when affiliation changes.

## Gooners

**Gooners** are the Gimp's voluntarily aligned Boardwalk retinue. A **Gooner**
participates in the camp's protection, escort, work, sanctuary, route knowledge,
and witnessed loyalty. Together they carry the Gimp's Current and make his
Underway depth present on the Boardwalk.

Goon remains the broad social term; an unaffiliated Goon or one working with
the Pimp is not automatically a Gooner. This specific retinue meaning
supersedes the earlier loose use of Gooner for any deeply engaged Boardwalk
participant.

Gooner is a cultural affiliation, not a Frame, Skill, office, credential,
criminal classification, or automatic Bond phase. A Gooner may challenge the
Gimp, refuse an obligation outside its lawful scope, cooperate with Trooners,
or leave through the applicable terms. A Goon Bond records only its disclosed
participants and obligations; the name does not create additional membership
or service beyond that record.

## Goon Bond

A **Goon Bond** is a regional social projection of the common constitutional
Bond lifecycle. It records a relationship through which one or more Goons
carry Current, obligation, protection, labor, evidence, and loyalty associated
with the Gimp's retinue or the broader Pimp / Gimp polarity.

It is not ownership, slavery, automatic obedience, a hidden affiliation flag,
or a second Bond engine.

### Constitutional lifecycle mapping

| Common Bond law | Boardwalk meaning |
|---|---|
| Formed | Stonebend-backed identity establishes the participants, terms, obligations, and starting Current |
| Validated | Sandmanor proves that the stated arrangement and obligations are coherent and evidenced |
| Active | the Goon Bond begins circulating protection, labor, resources, favors, risk, and public consequence |
| Current moved / accumulated | service, supplies, escort, protection, debt payment, and material burden become recorded action |
| Aura observed | reputation, humiliation, glamour, fear, trust, rumor, and public response become evidence |
| Evaluated | the runtime derives the Bond's Current / Aura polarity without collapsing them |
| Challenge / default | a participant contests terms, evidence, performance, coercion, or an unmet obligation |
| Matured | the active term ends lawfully after pending challenges and defaults are resolved |
| Eligibility decided | Glaüshouse determines whether the accumulated relationship may lawfully condense |
| Tombstone and recognition | the completed Bond's history is preserved and Flynt may recognize what it became |
| Resolved | the Bond completes, renews, merges, branches, splits, transfers, or dissolves |

Boardwalk-friendly language such as approached, recognized, bonded, tested,
kept, broken, or released may be used in dialogue and presentation only when it
projects an actual authoritative state. Those words do not create additional
reducer phases.

### Goon Bond protections

Every lawful Goon Bond requires:

- stable participant identity;
- disclosed scope and term;
- explicit obligations;
- evidence;
- challenge and default paths;
- no hidden transfer of bodily autonomy;
- no ownership claim;
- no automatic inheritance of debt by unrelated Beings;
- no use of a Hoe's role as evidence of consent;
- no use of a Gimp's condition as evidence of incapacity;
- a lawful resolution and preserved history.

## Pimp / Gimp Conflict

The Pimp and his Trooners and the Gimp and his Gooners are the Boardwalk's
recurring rival camps. Their rivalry organizes who gathers where, whose offer
is being heard, whose account earns trust, and which group receives public
credit. It gives a scene social stakes without deciding its outcome in advance.

The Boardwalk's central question is:

> Does power come from controlling how people see, or from enduring what
> people see?

The strongest stories do not answer with a simple alignment.

The Pimp may give someone visibility the Gimp cannot provide, while making that
visibility conditional on performance. The Gimp may give someone protection
the Pimp will not provide, while making that protection heavy with loyalty and
burden.

Trooners may question whether a public welcome has become a performance for
the Pimp's credit. Gooners may question whether protection has become a demand
for loyalty to the Gimp. Either camp can witness a genuine contribution from
its rival or criticize a failed promise from its own leader.

Hoes may challenge the Pimp, recognize the Gimp, assist unaffiliated Goons, or
form Bonds outside either retinue. Goons may work with either camp, refuse
both, or preserve loyalty while challenging a specific obligation.

Crossing the polarity does not erase prior evidence or identity.

## House Participation

Boardwalk cases preserve separate House acts:

- Stonebend identifies participants, Names obligations, establishes valid
  Title where one exists, and preserves continuity;
- Sandmanor proves terms, evidence, claims, and challenged accounts;
- Glaüshouse governs capacity, consent, recovery, coercive injury, and lawful
  resolution;
- Flynt recognizes performed competence and public function.

No Boardwalk personality replaces these acts.

## First Playable Case

The first case follows a Goon returning from Glaüshouse recovery:

```text
Discharge
→ public return to the Boardwalk
→ offer from the Pimp
→ offer from the Gimp
→ testimony from Hoes and Goons
→ faculty-assisted evaluation
→ player-supported choice
→ persistent outcome and recognition consequence
```

The returning Goon must be able to choose affiliation with the Pimp, a Goon
Bond with the Gimp, limited cooperation, or lawful independence. The player may
inform and influence the choice but may not choose on behalf of a capable
Being.

The implementation design lives in
`BOARDWALK_GOON_BOND_VERTICAL_SLICE_V1_DRAFT.md`.

### Implementation status

The case now records all four outcomes through live House authority. Pimp
Patronage, Goon Bond, and Limited Cooperation are distinct finite common
Bonds; Independent Return is a protected refusal record with no Bond. Formal
Pimp patronage does not establish Hoe identity, so the separate question of
formal Hoe affiliation remains open.

The implemented terms are binding for
`case.boardwalk.returning-goon.v1`. They do not automatically become universal
Boardwalk custom, title-selection law, or the default terms for unrelated
future cases.

## Validation Lock

A conforming implementation must prove:

- Pimp and Gimp remain cultural titles, not Flynt sovereign offices;
- Trooners align with the Pimp and Gooners align with the Gimp; their rivalry
  is visible without making every Boardwalk occupant a member;
- Hoe, Goon, retinue affiliation, patronage, and Bond remain distinct;
- Tross and the unique Chimera remain untouched;
- all Hoes are adult, autonomous Beings with explicit boundaries and exit;
- no Being is property;
- Goon remains a broad social term rather than a species or mandatory
  affiliation;
- Goon Bond reuses the common constitutional Bond lifecycle;
- Current and Aura remain distinct;
- affliction does not automatically erase or grant Name, Title, competence, or
  authority;
- Glaüshouse discharge and consent precede voluntary Boardwalk return;
- Flynt recognition does not become Title;
- dialogue labels never commit unrecorded state;
- replay reconstructs every authoritative consequence exactly.

## Open Ratification Questions

The following remain deliberately open:

1. the exact Name and material behavior of the Gimp's condition;
2. how the locked Pimp of the Boardwalk and Gimp of the Underway titles are
   selected, inherited, challenged, or ended;
3. whether a formal Hoe affiliation is itself a Bond or another lawful
   affiliation record;
4. whether the first-case Goon Bond obligations should ever become a
   Boardwalk default rather than remain case-specific;
5. the exact joining, departure, and reputation records for Trooners and
   Gooners beyond their adopted cultural affiliation;
6. how Boardwalk districts visually distinguish invitation, neutrality,
   coercion risk, sanctuary, and public recognition.
