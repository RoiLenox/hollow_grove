## Constitutional Boundary

The Boardwalk remains the Glaüshouse / Flynt route whose dominant verb is
**Return**:

```text
Recovery Ward
→ Boardwalk
→ Flynt
→ Recognition
```

A Being leaves recovery by choice and returns to ordinary civic presence. The
Boardwalk may stage public recognition, affiliation, glamour, loyalty, debt,
and conflict. It may not:

- replace Glaüshouse discharge, consent, recovery, or Clearance;
- grant Stonebend Name or Title;
- replace Sandmanor proof;
- create a second Flynt sovereign;
- bypass Chimera or Tross;
- turn a cultural title into constitutional office;
- treat a person as property;
- make bodily condition proof of virtue, vice, competence, or authority.

`flynt-constitution` remains the sole executable Flynt authority. The Pimp,
Gimp, Troons (historically Trooners), Gooners, Sympiotes, Hoes, Goons, and Goon Bond are Boardwalk cultural and
social identities above that locked authority. The two retinue names and their
rival organization are adopted here, as are the two bosses' differentiated
commercial offerings; detailed membership mechanics and product catalogs
remain open.

## Goon Bond

A **Goon Bond** is a regional social projection of the common constitutional
Bond lifecycle. It records a relationship through which one or more Goons
carry Current, obligation, protection, labor, evidence, and loyalty associated
with the Gimp's retinue or the broader Pimp / Gimp polarity.

It is not ownership, slavery, automatic obedience, a hidden affiliation flag,
or a second Bond engine.

### Constitutional lifecycle mapping

| Common Bond law | Boardwalk meaning |
|---|---|
| Formed | Stonebend-backed identity establishes the participants, terms, obligations, and starting Current |
| Validated | Sandmanor proves that the stated arrangement and obligations are coherent and evidenced |
| Active | the Goon Bond begins circulating protection, labor, resources, favors, risk, and public consequence |
| Current moved / accumulated | service, supplies, escort, protection, debt payment, and material burden become recorded action |
| Aura observed | reputation, humiliation, glamour, fear, trust, rumor, and public response become evidence |
| Evaluated | the runtime derives the Bond's Current / Aura polarity without collapsing them |
| Challenge / default | a participant contests terms, evidence, performance, coercion, or an unmet obligation |
| Matured | the active term ends lawfully after pending challenges and defaults are resolved |
| Eligibility decided | Glaüshouse determines whether the accumulated relationship may lawfully condense |
| Tombstone and recognition | the completed Bond's history is preserved and Flynt may recognize what it became |
| Resolved | the Bond completes, renews, merges, branches, splits, transfers, or dissolves |

Boardwalk-friendly language such as approached, recognized, bonded, tested,
kept, broken, or released may be used in dialogue and presentation only when it
projects an actual authoritative state. Those words do not create additional
reducer phases.

### Recurring rivalry

The Pimp and his Troons and the Gimp and his Gooners are the Boardwalk's
recurring rival camps. Their rivalry organizes who gathers where, whose offer
is being heard, whose account earns trust, and which group receives public
credit. It gives a scene social stakes without deciding its outcome in advance.

The Boardwalk's central question is:

> Does power come from controlling how people see, or from enduring what
> people see?

The strongest stories do not answer with a simple alignment.

The Pimp may give someone visibility the Gimp cannot provide, while making that
visibility conditional on performance. The Gimp may give someone protection
the Pimp will not provide, while making that protection heavy with loyalty and
burden.

Troons may question whether a public welcome has become a performance for
the Pimp's credit. Gooners may question whether protection has become a demand
for loyalty to the Gimp. Either camp can witness a genuine contribution from
its rival or criticize a failed promise from its own leader.

Hoes may challenge the Pimp, recognize the Gimp, assist unaffiliated Goons, or
form Bonds outside either retinue. Goons may work with either camp, refuse
both, or preserve loyalty while challenging a specific obligation.

Crossing the polarity does not erase prior evidence or identity.
