## Established procedure

`MINOAN_COUNTY_COURT_SYSTEM_AND_RESTITUTION_CYCLE_V1.md:315–358` requires:
proposal → public notice → affected-party testimony → conciliation where
appropriate → first procedural hearing → appeal where challenged →
constitutional review → proper ratification → Stonebend seal/public record →
implementation and Restitution review → constitutional equilibrium.

House-local amendments use that House's lawful process; cross-House amendments
require separate assent from every affected House; foundational amendments
require all four. The Court reviews scope and process; it cannot ratify.
House ratification, Court certification and Stonebend sealing are distinct
records. The four-House evidence required for an individual Tross succession
is **not** an amendment-ratification vote or evidence of who may cast one.

The proposed text must identify exact changes, superseded text, purpose,
affected Houses/offices/Titles/communities, altered authority, expected Yield
and ratification scope (`:333–340`). The proper process must review that scope;
this packet does not declare the combined package House-local, cross-House or
foundational. Retaining existing House gates does not by itself settle scope.
