# Ancestral Cultural Memory Model

Status: Working guidance; no dialogue, holiday, oath, street name, or curriculum mandate is canonized here

The model prevents every NPC from behaving like a historian. Ancestry may be encountered through school lessons, family stories, monuments, professional training, libraries, museum exhibits, streets, architecture, and archived media, but knowledge depth depends on role and education.

## Stonebend

### Common Knowledge

Residents recognize that public works, names, boundaries, measures, and maintenance records carry history; they know collapse and unlawful administration can destroy durable form. Sources: [I:1 — Birth of Cities](ages/age_01_ancient_civilizations/point_01_birth_of_cities.md), [I:5 — Great Bronze Age Powers](ages/age_01_ancient_civilizations/point_05_great_bronze_age_powers.md), [II:10 — Pax Romana](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_10_pax_romana.md), [V:6 — World War II](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_06_world_war_ii.md).

### Educated Knowledge

Students compare civic archives, roads, legal administration, scientific measurement, and industrial standards while learning that ancestry never grants present authority. Sources: [I:3 — Restoration Kingdoms](ages/age_01_ancient_civilizations/point_03_restoration_kingdoms.md), [II:5 — Achaemenid World](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_05_achaemenid_world.md), [IV:3 — Scientific Revolution](ages/age_04_early_modern_civilization/point_03_scientific_revolution.md), [V:2 — Age of Science](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_02_age_of_science.md).

### Specialist Knowledge

Archivists, engineers, historians, and officials study exact chronologies, institutional failure, provenance, and competing interpretations, with uncertain assignments visibly marked. Sources: [II:6 — Alexander and the Diadochi](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_06_alexander_and_the_diadochi.md), [II:9 — Fall of the Republic](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_09_fall_of_the_republic.md), [V:6 — World War II](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_06_world_war_ii.md), [VI:5 — AI Age](ages/age_06_information_civilization/point_05_ai_age.md).

Use [Stonebend cultural synthesis](houses/STONEBEND_ANCESTRAL_SYNTHESIS.md) for institutions, everyday culture, public memory, and Hueman hooks.

## Sandmanor

### Common Knowledge

Residents encounter ancestry through stories, theater, gardens, harbor memory, ritual, clothing, and the expectation that important designs can be questioned and revised. Sources: [I:3 — Restoration Kingdoms](ages/age_01_ancient_civilizations/point_03_restoration_kingdoms.md), [II:1 — Early Iron Age](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_01_early_iron_age.md), [II:5 — Achaemenid World](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_05_achaemenid_world.md), [III:7 — Late Medieval World](ages/age_03_medieval_civilization/point_07_late_medieval_world.md).

### Educated Knowledge

Students learn comparative myth, diplomacy, translation, religious and literary traditions, psychology, and the separation of performance from Proof. Sources: [II:5 — Achaemenid World](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_05_achaemenid_world.md), [III:2 — Rise of Islam](ages/age_03_medieval_civilization/point_02_rise_of_islam.md), [IV:2 — Reformation](ages/age_04_early_modern_civilization/point_02_reformation.md), [V:5 — Interwar World](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_05_interwar_world.md).

### Specialist Knowledge

Historians, theologians, translators, diplomats, designers, and Court professionals study sources, power, appropriation, failed synthesis, and the provenance of each interpretation. Sources: [I:5 — Great Bronze Age Powers](ages/age_01_ancient_civilizations/point_05_great_bronze_age_powers.md), [II:12 — Christian Empire](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_12_christian_empire.md), [III:3 — Carolingian World](ages/age_03_medieval_civilization/point_03_carolingian_world.md), [VI:4 — Network Age](ages/age_06_information_civilization/point_04_network_age.md).

Use [Sandmanor cultural synthesis](houses/SANDMANOR_ANCESTRAL_SYNTHESIS.md) for institutions, everyday culture, public memory, and Hueman hooks.

## Glaüshouse

### Common Knowledge

Residents meet ancestry through water and sanitation systems, care customs, libraries, recovery spaces, translated information, and respect for maintenance and return. Sources: [I:1 — Birth of Cities](ages/age_01_ancient_civilizations/point_01_birth_of_cities.md), [I:2 — First Empires](ages/age_01_ancient_civilizations/point_02_first_empires.md), [II:10 — Pax Romana](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_10_pax_romana.md), [III:1 — Successor Kingdoms](ages/age_03_medieval_civilization/point_01_successor_kingdoms.md).

### Educated Knowledge

Students learn the histories of preservation, medicine, ecology, biology, cognition, public health, and the difference between evidence, consent, Clearance, and recovery. Sources: [II:10 — Pax Romana](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_10_pax_romana.md), [III:2 — Rise of Islam](ages/age_03_medieval_civilization/point_02_rise_of_islam.md), [V:2 — Age of Science](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_02_age_of_science.md), [VI:2 — Space Age](ages/age_06_information_civilization/point_02_space_age.md).

### Specialist Knowledge

Clinicians, librarians, translators, ecologists, and historians study source limits, adverse outcomes, lost knowledge, research ethics, longitudinal records, and living-system uncertainty. Sources: [II:11 — Crisis and Conversion](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_11_crisis_and_conversion.md), [III:6A — Mongol Conquests and Late Song](ages/age_03_medieval_civilization/point_06_mongol_world.md), [V:6 — World War II](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_06_world_war_ii.md), [VI:5 — AI Age](ages/age_06_information_civilization/point_05_ai_age.md).

Use [Glaüshouse cultural synthesis](houses/GLAUSHOUSE_ANCESTRAL_SYNTHESIS.md) for institutions, everyday culture, public memory, and Hueman hooks.

## Flynt

### Common Knowledge

Residents meet ancestry through tools, route names, ports, machine halls, repair customs, after-action stories, and respect for clear handoffs under pressure. Sources: [I:4 — Old Babylonian World](ages/age_01_ancient_civilizations/point_04_old_babylonian_world.md), [II:8 — Roman Republic](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_08_roman_republic.md), [III:6A — Mongol Conquests and Late Song](ages/age_03_medieval_civilization/point_06_mongol_world.md), [V:1 — Industrial Acceleration](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_01_industrial_revolution.md).

### Educated Knowledge

Students learn logistics, fortification, navigation, industrial systems, computing, rescue, and the human costs and limits of command. Sources: [II:1 — Early Iron Age](ages/age_02_classical_civilization/point_01_early_iron_age.md), [III:3 — Carolingian World](ages/age_03_medieval_civilization/point_03_carolingian_world.md), [V:4 — World War I](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_04_world_war_i.md), [VI:1 — Early Computing](ages/age_06_information_civilization/point_01_early_computing.md).

### Specialist Knowledge

Engineers, officers, historians, maintainers, and network operators study failure modes, custody, institutional crime, trauma, refusal, continuity, and exact system provenance. Sources: [V:3 — Second Industrial Revolution](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_03_second_industrial_revolution.md), [V:6 — World War II](ages/age_05_industrial_civilization/point_06_world_war_ii.md), [VI:3 — Digital Revolution](ages/age_06_information_civilization/point_03_digital_revolution.md), [VI:5 — AI Age](ages/age_06_information_civilization/point_05_ai_age.md).

Use [Flynt cultural synthesis](houses/FLYNT_ANCESTRAL_SYNTHESIS.md) for institutions, everyday culture, public memory, and Hueman hooks.

## Memory record guidance

A future NPC, lesson, monument, oath, exhibit, saying, or street name should record: House; knowledge level; Point references; derived Gift IDs; manifestation ID if any; source certainty; and the existing canon that constrains the expression.
