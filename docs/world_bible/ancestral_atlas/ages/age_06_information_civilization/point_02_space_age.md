## Historical summary

Spaceflight, rocketry, electronics, systems engineering, and multinational science expanded technical reach while the Cold War structured investment and risk. This is a nested technological coordinate overlapping the end of Early Computing.

## Reason this Point exists

This Point carries the approved four-House coordinate for **Space Age** and adds a reviewable inheritance layer without making any source culture a literal predecessor state of Hollow Grove. Any departure from the supplied baseline is recorded in [Chronology Revision 1](../../CHRONOLOGY_REVISION_01_REPORT.md).

This Point is deliberately nested across the closing years of Age VI, Point I; Sputnik opens the technological branch in 1957.

## Transition into next Point
