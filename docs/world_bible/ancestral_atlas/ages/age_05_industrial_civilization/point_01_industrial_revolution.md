## Historical summary

Post-Napoleonic mechanized production, transport, urbanization, finance, and administrative capacity accelerate at different rates across the assigned Atlantic powers. The title no longer implies that industrialization began in 1815.

## Reason this Point exists

This Point carries the approved four-House coordinate for **Industrial Acceleration** and adds a reviewable inheritance layer without making any source culture a literal predecessor state of Hollow Grove. Any departure from the supplied baseline is recorded in [Chronology Revision 1](../../CHRONOLOGY_REVISION_01_REPORT.md).
