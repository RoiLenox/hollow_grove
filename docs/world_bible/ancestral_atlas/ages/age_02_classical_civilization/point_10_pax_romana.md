**House:** [Stonebend](../../houses/STONEBEND_ANCESTRY.md)<br>

**Religion and worldview:** Imperial cult, local religions, philosophical schools, household practice, and varied provincial traditions coexisted.<br>
**Science and knowledge:** Law, surveying, engineering, medicine, astronomy, geography, rhetoric, and administration circulated through imperial and local institutions.<br>
**Technology and infrastructure:** Roads, bridges, aqueducts, ports, concrete, cities, forts, archives, ships, and water systems connected public works and frontier supply.<br>
**Art and cultural expression:** Imperial portraiture, civic monuments, mosaics, inscriptions, temples, funerary art, and local styles negotiated power and belonging.<br>

**Resources and trade:** Grain, wine, oil, metals, textiles, slaves, coin, and information moved through imperial routes and local markets.<br>
**Major conflicts and relationships:** Rome engaged Parthia, Judea, Egypt, Germanic communities, Britain, North Africa, and eastern cities through law, diplomacy, trade, and war.<br>
