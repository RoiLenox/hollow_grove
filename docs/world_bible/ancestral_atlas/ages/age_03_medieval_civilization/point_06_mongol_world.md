## Historical summary

The Fourth Crusade's aftermath, the rise and division of the Mongol Empire, late Song resilience, and the emergence of Mamluk rule form a deliberately staggered thirteenth-century coordinate.

## Reason this Point exists

This Point carries the approved four-House coordinate for **Mongol Conquests and Late Song** and adds a reviewable inheritance layer without making any source culture a literal predecessor state of Hollow Grove. Any departure from the supplied baseline is recorded in [Chronology Revision 1](../../CHRONOLOGY_REVISION_01_REPORT.md).
