## Chronology decision

The supplied title and c. 2100–1900 BCE range are preserved, but the Point is not uniformly concurrent. Ur III is Partial / Terminal at c. 2112–2004; Middle Kingdom Egypt is Emergent / Partial especially c. 2030–1900; Minoan Crete is Boundary / Transforming in late Prepalatial EM III–MM IA; Šimaški is Partial / Transition, strongest after c. 2004. “Restoration Kingdoms” is therefore a comparative transition label, not a structural classification shared by all four assignments.

## Historical uncertainties

- Ur III: absolute chronology varies; administrative practice was not uniform; the bala mechanism remains debated; the fall of Ur had several interacting causes; attribution and date of the Laws of Ur-Nammu require caution.
- Middle Kingdom Egypt: scholarly start dates vary around 2055/2030; Itjtawy remains unlocated; composition dates, surviving manuscript dates, and fictional speakers in literary texts must remain separate.
- Minoan Crete: `Minoan` is a modern label; late Prepalatial evidence is uneven; the first palaces sit at the Point boundary; scripts, government, rulers, and named beliefs are not recoverable for the reviewed slice.
- Šimaški: its location, extent, offices, and succession are poorly resolved; evidence is mostly external, later, or archaeologically difficult; late Šimaški and early Sukkalmah/Epartid rule overlap.
