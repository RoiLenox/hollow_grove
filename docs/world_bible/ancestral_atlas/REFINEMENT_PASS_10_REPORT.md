The dossier layer prevents the transition from reading as an instantaneous replacement.

## Historical uncertainties

- Achaemenid: regional administrative hierarchy, `satrapy` terminology, local legal continuity, audience for multilingual royal communication, labor status, and late-fourth-century implementation vary across uneven archives.
- Greek: the umbrella label conceals hundreds of communities; Athenian evidence is overrepresented, civic participation was exclusionary, and non-Athenian institutions and audience responses are less securely documented.
- Late Egypt: local continuity through 525, 404, and 343, the representativeness of Elephantine and Udjahorresnet, and the second Persian period are unevenly evidenced.
- Macedonia: early Argead administration, exact army-reform sequence, manpower totals, resource-to-force causation, and later literary reconstruction require phase and source-distance controls.
