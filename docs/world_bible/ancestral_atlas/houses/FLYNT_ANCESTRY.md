## Status rule

The framework is the canonical descriptive ancestry layer. Every assignment in Working Version 1 remains **Working** unless an explicit ratification later changes that status. The supplied legacy master-line labels are preserved in [Master Timeline](../MASTER_TIMELINE.md); this navigable lineage follows the Point assignments where labels disagree.

### Constitutional law boundary

Flynt's constitutional House Law is the **Law of Capacity**. Recognition remains available as a Festival, confirmation, honor, reputation, and judgment theme, but the Atlas never treats Recognition as Flynt's House Law.
