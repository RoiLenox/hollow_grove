Status: Working cultural institution; foundational collection preserved<br>

## Public purpose

Preserve and test durable knowledge about law, permanence, government, architecture, mathematics, physics, standards, public works, and the philosophy of structure.

The library is an Atlas-informed House institution, but this document creates no new access authority. Constitutional privacy, custody, clinical, military, intellectual-lineage, and public-record rules continue to govern its materials.

## Major collections

- law and civic form
- architecture and public works
- mathematics and measurement
- physics and astronomy
- standards and scientific change
