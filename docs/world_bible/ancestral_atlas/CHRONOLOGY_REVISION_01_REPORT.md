# Ancestral Atlas — Chronology Revision 1

Date: 2026-08-01<br>
Status: approved operational refinement; all individual assignments remain Working

## Purpose

This revision improves temporal precision without replacing the six-Age structure, House authority, cumulative lineage rule, or supplied assignment skeleton. Point pages and read-only metadata are the operational chronology after this revision. Changed supplied forms remain visible below so the correction is explicit rather than silent.

## Coordinate semantics added

- Points are representative historical windows, not continuous or equally sized bins.
- Point types are Sequential, Transition, Nested, and Open.
- Every assignment exposes a temporal-fit classification, culture-specific relevance range, and relevance note.
- Gaps between supplied Points are transition corridors, not claims that no history occurred.
- Age names are Atlas navigation coordinates derived mainly from Mediterranean, European, and technological periodization, not universal stages shared by every region.
- Open Points require an `As of` date and scheduled review.
