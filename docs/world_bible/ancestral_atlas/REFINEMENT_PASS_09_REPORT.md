## Chronology decision

The supplied 612–539 BCE range remains a Sequential Point. `Judah` is Kingdom partial / terminal; communities continue: its monarchy ends in 587/586, but homeland, Babylonian, Egyptian, and other communities and records continue. `Saite Egypt` is Full / Continues and remains independent until 525. `Neo-Babylonian Empire` is Full / Terminal; institutions continue because the dynasty ends in 539 while city, temple, family, legal, and scholarly systems persist. `Medes` is Partial / Terminal; legacy continues because Cyrus defeats Astyages around 550, while Medes and Media remain within the expanding Persian polity.

The supplied terminal event, Cyrus captures Babylon, remains unchanged. The dossier layer treats the Cyrus Cylinder and other royal records as interested legitimation, not proof of a bloodless universal liberation or a modern rights charter.
