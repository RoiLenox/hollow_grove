## Chronology decision

The supplied 281–146 BCE range remains a long sequential Point, but not a synchronized four-state container. `Antigonid` is Precursor 281–277/276 / Kingdom 277/276–168 / Post-dynastic transition 168–146. Durable Antigonid rule begins after the Point opening; Pydna and the 168 settlement abolish the monarchy; local and regional institutions persist through the Andriscus episode and provincial reorganization.

`Hellenistic Greek` is a Full civic-federal horizon / Politically plural / Continues assignment. It denotes a bounded comparison among poleis, koina, sanctuaries, schools, associations, and mobile networks, not one state or uniform public. `Ptolemaic` is Full / Multiple internal ruptures / Continues to 30 BCE: the Great Revolt, dynastic division, Seleucid invasion, and Roman intervention require regional continuity and recovery records. `Seleucid` is Full / Major contraction after 188 / Continues to 63 BCE: route, mint, treaty, local-institution, and succession evidence replace territorial reach as the measure of capacity.

The destruction of Corinth in 146 remains the supplied terminal event and marks Roman supremacy in mainland Greece and the wider Mediterranean. It does not end Ptolemaic or Seleucid rule, Greek civic life, or all Macedonian institutions. Nested Point VIII begins in 264 within this Point and is not treated as its chronological successor.
