# Historical Dossier — Holy Roman Empire, Ottonian Layers, and Law

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

Ottonian rulers built imperial authority through kingship, church offices, aristocratic agreements, duchies, cities, monasteries, and military campaigns. The Holy Roman Empire was a layered polity, not a centralized nation-state or simple continuation of Carolingian Rome.

## Chronology

919 Henry I; 936 Otto I; 962 imperial coronation; Otto II and Otto III; 1002 Henry II; 1024 Conrad II; 1039 Henry III and the Point's 1050 boundary.

## Permanent Gifts

- municipal and legal plurality
