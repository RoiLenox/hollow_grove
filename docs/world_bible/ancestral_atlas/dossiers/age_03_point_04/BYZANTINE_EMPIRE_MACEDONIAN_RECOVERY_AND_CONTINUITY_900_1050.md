## Historical overview

The Macedonian dynasty period saw military, fiscal, literary, artistic, and urban recovery amid frontier wars and court politics. Byzantine continuity included law, church, cities, and diplomacy, but regional inequality and coercion remained visible.

## Chronology

867 Basil I; 886 Leo VI; 913–959 Constantine VII and court scholarship; 963–1025 Nikephoros II, John Tzimiskes, and Basil II; 1018 Bulgarian conquest; 1054 church rupture lies just beyond the Point.
