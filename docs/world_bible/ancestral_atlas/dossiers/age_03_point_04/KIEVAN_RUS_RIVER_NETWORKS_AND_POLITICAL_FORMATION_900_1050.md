# Historical Dossier — Kievan Rus’, River Networks, and Political Formation

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

Kievan Rus’ formed through overlapping river routes, settlements, princely households, tribute, trade, diplomacy, and religious change. It was a network of communities and dynastic centers, not a modern nation-state.

## Chronology

882 Oleg's capture of Kyiv in the conventional narrative; 907 and 911 treaty traditions; 945 Igor's death; 957/988 Olga and Volodymyr's Christianization; 1019–1054 Yaroslav; eleventh-century legal and dynastic consolidation.

## Permanent Gifts

- river-route and cargo logistics
- distributed political and settlement networks
- cross-cultural diplomatic and material exchange
