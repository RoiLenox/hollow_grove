# Mongol Empire — Conquests, Logistics, and Successor Polities (1206–1279)

Reviewed dossier for Age III, Point 6A. Mongol expansion joined mobile command, relay routes, tribute, diplomacy, and successor formation with conquest, massacre, enslavement, and displacement. Hollow Grove receives only bounded logistics and communication practices, never conquest authority.
