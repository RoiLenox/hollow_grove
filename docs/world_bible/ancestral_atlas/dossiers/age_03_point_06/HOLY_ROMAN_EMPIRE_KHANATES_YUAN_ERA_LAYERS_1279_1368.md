# Holy Roman Empire — Khanate and Yuan Era Layers (1279–1368)

Reviewed dossier for Age III, Point 6B. The Empire remained a negotiated field of emperor, princes, bishops, cities, guilds, and local courts while wider Eurasian successor states changed. Hollow Grove receives layered jurisdiction and maintenance provenance, not imperial authority.
