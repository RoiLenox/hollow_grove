# Mamluk Sultanate — Baybars to 1368: Routes and Endowments

Reviewed dossier for Age III, Point 6B. Mamluk rule consolidated through military households, fortified routes, urban institutions, and waqf endowments while resisting Mongol pressure and managing commercial and religious networks. This is a bounded operations lens, not sultanate command.

Sources: [Britannica — Mamluk](https://www.britannica.com/place/Mamluk); [Metropolitan Museum — Mamluk art](https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/hd/maml/hd_maml.htm).
