# Song China — Printing, Hydraulics, and Maritime Exchange (1204–1279)

Reviewed dossier for Age III, Point 6A. Late Song institutions sustained printing, examinations, hydraulic works, cities, and maritime exchange while Mongol conquest culminated in the loss of Southern Song rule in 1279. The dossier preserves local service, labor, and conquest costs.

Sources: [Britannica — Song dynasty](https://www.britannica.com/place/China/The-Song-dynasty-960-1279); [Metropolitan Museum — Yuan dynasty](https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/hd/yuan/hd_yuan.htm).
