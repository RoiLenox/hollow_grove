# Mongol Khanates — Successor Diplomacy and Logistics (1279–1368)

Reviewed dossier for Age III, Point 6B. The Golden Horde, Chagatai, Ilkhanate, and Yuan courts were distinct successor polities linked by routes, kinship, diplomacy, and contested legacies. Their connectivity remained inseparable from war, tribute, enslavement, and unequal rule.

Sources: [Britannica — Mongol empire](https://www.britannica.com/place/Mongol-empire); [Metropolitan Museum — Yuan dynasty](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/yuan-dynasty-1271-1368).
