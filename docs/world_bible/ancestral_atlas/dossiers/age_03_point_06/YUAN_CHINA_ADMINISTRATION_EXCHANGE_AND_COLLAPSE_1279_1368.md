# Yuan China — Administration, Exchange, and Collapse (1279–1368)

Reviewed dossier for Age III, Point 6B. Yuan rule integrated China into a Mongol imperial formation while adapting Chinese institutions. Printing, urban craft, hydraulic systems, and exchange continued amid status hierarchy, disaster, faction, rebellion, and the 1368 transition.

Sources: [Metropolitan Museum — Yuan dynasty](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/yuan-dynasty-1271-1368); [Metropolitan Museum — China, 1000–1400](https://82nd-and-fifth.metmuseum.org/toah/ht/07/eac.html).
