# Mamluk Sultanate — Households, Routes, and Endowments (c. 1250–1279)

Reviewed dossier for Age III, Point 6A. Early Mamluk rule formed through military households, urban institutions, fortified routes, and waqf endowments amid Crusader and Mongol pressure. The inheritance remains a bounded operations and public-service lens, not sultanate command.

Sources: [Britannica — Mamluk](https://www.britannica.com/place/Mamluk); [Metropolitan Museum — Mamluk art](https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/hd/maml/hd_maml.htm).
