## Review boundary

- The supplied title, 336–281 range, assignments, order, and transition note remain intact.
- Achaemenid kingship ends in 330; later use of `Achaemenid` means attributed
  institutional inheritance, not continuous sovereignty.
- `Macedonia` changes referent across Alexander's kingdom and empire, nominal
  joint kingship, regencies, and rival successor monarchies.
- Ptolemaic formation begins in Egypt after 323 and becomes royal in 305/304.
- Seleucus's 321 appointment is interrupted; durable Seleucid formation begins
  with the 312/311 return to Babylonia, followed by royal title in 305/304.
- The 281 boundary closes a first-generation formation arc but does not end
  successor war or instantly stabilize Antigonid Macedonia.
- Conquest, siege, execution, enslavement, conscription, coerced settlement,
  appropriation, and dynastic victory remain evidence or cautions—not Gifts.
