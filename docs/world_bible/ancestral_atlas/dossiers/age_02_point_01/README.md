## Review boundary

- The Point title, date range, House assignments, and order are preserved.
- `Kingdom of Israel`, `Phoenicia`, and `Aram` remain supplied labels rather
  than claims of uniform statehood or identity throughout the Point.
- Neo-Assyria contributes only c. 911–900 BCE; later capitals, relief programs,
  siege systems, and imperial administration are not backdated.
- Later biblical, Assyrian, Greek, and classical accounts retain their dates,
  genres, political positions, and evidentiary limits.
- Conquest, coerced movement, tribute, colonial romance, ethnic diagnosis, and
  royal ideology remain cautions rather than Gifts.
- Every reviewed manifestation is descriptive and read-only; no institution,
  office, ritual, territory, identity judgment, or gameplay behavior is created.
