## Review boundary

- The supplied title, 281–146 range, assignments, order, and terminal event remain intact.
- Durable Antigonid possession of Macedonia begins in 277/276, not automatically in 281.
- Roman victory in 168 abolishes the Antigonid monarchy; `Antigonid` from 168
  to 146 means attributable institutional legacy and Macedonian transition, not
  continuous dynastic government.
- `Hellenistic Greek` remains a bounded umbrella for diverse poleis, koina,
  sanctuaries, festivals, schools, associations, and mobile networks—not one polity.
- Ptolemaic and Seleucid histories are internally phased; neither polity ends in 146.
- Corinth's destruction and the Roman settlement of 146 are a mainland Greek
  terminal event and a Mediterranean power transition, not a simultaneous end
  for all four assignments.
- Conquest, garrisoning, enslavement, coerced settlement, extraction, dynastic
  prestige, exclusion, and punitive destruction remain evidence or cautions—not Gifts.
