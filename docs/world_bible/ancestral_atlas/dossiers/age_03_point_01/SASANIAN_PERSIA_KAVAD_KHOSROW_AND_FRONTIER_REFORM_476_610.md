# Historical Dossier — Sasanian Persia, Kawād, Khusrow, and Frontier Reform

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Evidence boundary

Royal inscriptions, coins, seals, Syriac and Armenian records, Arabic and Persian histories, and archaeology preserve different agendas. Administrative reform is reconstructed unevenly, and royal claims do not prove uniform control.

## Historical overview

Kawād I's reign (488–531, with interruption) and Khusrow I's reign (531–579) mark major changes in Sasanian fiscal and military organization. The empire competed with Byzantium, negotiated with frontier peoples, and sustained plural religious and scholarly communities. The 602–610 crisis begins a later phase.

## Chronology

488–531 Kawād I and the Mazdakite controversy; 531–579 Khusrow I and reform; 532 treaty with Byzantium; 540s renewed Roman war; 570 Sasanian intervention in Yemen; 579–590 Hormozd IV; 590–628 Khusrow II, whose early consolidation begins within the Point.

## Permanent Gifts

- phased fiscal and military administration
- frontier diplomacy and route resilience
- multilingual scholarly and religious custody
