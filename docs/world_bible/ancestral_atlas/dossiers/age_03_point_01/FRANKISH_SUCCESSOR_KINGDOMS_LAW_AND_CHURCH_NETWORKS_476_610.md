# Historical Dossier — Frankish Successor Kingdoms, Law, and Church Networks

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Evidence boundary

Gregory of Tours, royal histories, law codes, letters, saints' lives, coins, burials, churches, and settlement archaeology preserve elite and ecclesiastical views unevenly. “Frankish” covers changing Merovingian kingdoms and mixed populations.

## Historical overview

Clovis's conversion and conquests expanded a Merovingian sphere in Gaul, but rule remained negotiated among kings, aristocrats, bishops, cities, local communities, and military households. Succession partitions, customary and written law, church patronage, and regional identity continued through the sixth century.

## Chronology

481–511 Clovis; 496 is a traditional but debated conversion date; 511 partition; sixth-century conflicts among Austrasia, Neustria, Burgundy, and Aquitaine; 561 and 584 partitions; 613 Chlothar II reunification lies just beyond the supplied terminal emphasis.

## Permanent Gifts

- negotiated successor kingship and succession memory
- plural customary and written law
- church, city, and royal patronage networks

## Modern Hollow Grove manifestation

Succession and Custom Forum: a Sandmanor public-memory setting comparing law, kinship, office, church patronage, local voice, and contested royal memory without creating a present court or kingship.
