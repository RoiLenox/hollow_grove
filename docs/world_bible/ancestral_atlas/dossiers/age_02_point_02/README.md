## Review boundary

- The Point title, date range, assignments, order, and terminal label remain
  supplied canon data.
- `Israel` means the northern kingdom centered at Samaria in this Point; it
  does not silently include Judah, a debated united monarchy, or every later
  use of Israel as a people-name.
- Phoenician cities remain independent political actors; their connected
  ports and settlements do not constitute one territorial state.
- `Aram` remains a regional umbrella. Damascus, Hamath, Arpad, Sam'al,
  Guzana, and other polities retain separate records and annexation dates.
- `Assyria` is operationally clarified as Neo-Assyria and continues beyond
  the Point. Conquest, siege, tribute, deportation, forced labor, and royal
  ideology remain evidence or cautions rather than Gifts.
- The fall of Samaria is preserved at 722 BCE while the Shalmaneser V,
  Sargon II, biblical, chronicle, and archaeological evidence remains
  separately visible; the dossier does not manufacture one certain sequence.
- Every reviewed manifestation is descriptive and read-only; no institution,
  office, ritual, territory, identity judgment, or gameplay behavior is created.
