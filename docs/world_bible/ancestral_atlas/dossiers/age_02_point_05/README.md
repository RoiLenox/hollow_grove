## Review boundary

- The title, date range, assignments, order, and Philip–Alexander transition
  remain supplied Atlas data.
- Achaemenid kingship continues under Darius III from the 336 boundary to 330.
- `Greek` is a regional and cultural umbrella, not a single polity and not a
  synonym for Athens.
- `Late Egypt` crosses Saite, first Persian, independent Dynasties 28–30, and
  second Persian phases; the broader Late Period continues to 332.
- Macedonia exists throughout the coordinate, but Philip II's integrated
  expansion belongs specifically to 359–336 and cannot be backdated.
- Imperial extraction, civic exclusion, slavery, conquest, coerced service,
  dynastic victory, and hegemonic compliance are evidence or cautions—not Gifts.
