## Review boundary

- The Point title, date range, assignments, order, and fall-of-Nineveh
  terminal label remain supplied Atlas data.
- `Assyrian Apex` is interpreted as an apex-to-collapse window, not a stable
  century or a moral endorsement of imperial reach.
- Judah continues past 612 to 586 BCE; its survival in 701 does not erase
  the destruction of Lachish and other settlements or prove divine favor.
- The Egyptian assignment is composite. Kushite Dynasty 25 and Saite
  Dynasty 26 overlap and represent different regional coalitions.
- `Babylon` names a city, institutional world, and contested kingship across
  several regimes before Nabopolassar's dynasty.
- Nineveh falls in 612, but Ashur-uballit II maintains Assyrian resistance
  from Harran until 609. Capital loss and polity end are not simultaneous.
- Conquest, siege, deportation, forced labor, imperial extraction, divine
  kingship, and royal restoration ideology remain evidence or cautions—not
  Gifts or modern authority.
