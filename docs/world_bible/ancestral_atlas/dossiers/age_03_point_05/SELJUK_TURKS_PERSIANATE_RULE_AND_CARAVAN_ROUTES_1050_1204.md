# Historical Dossier — Seljuk Turks, Persianate Rule, and Caravan Routes

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

Seljuk power expanded through military households, sultans, Persianate administrators, cities, madrasas, caravan routes, and negotiated relationships with local rulers and communities. Great Seljuk, Rum Seljuk, and successor formations must not be collapsed into one state.

## Chronology

1037 Seljuk power in Khorasan; 1055 Baghdad; 1071 Manzikert; 1077 Sultanate of Rum; 1092 Nizam al-Mulk and Malik Shah's deaths; twelfth-century Anatolian and Iranian fragmentation; 1204 regional transition.

## Permanent Gifts

- mounted and route-based military logistics
- Persianate administrative practice
- caravan and urban network resilience
