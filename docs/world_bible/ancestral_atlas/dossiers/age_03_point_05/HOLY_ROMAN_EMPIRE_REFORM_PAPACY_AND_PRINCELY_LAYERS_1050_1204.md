# Historical Dossier — Holy Roman Empire, Reform, Papacy, and Princely Layers

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

The Investiture Controversy, papal reform, princely authority, cities, monasteries, and imperial campaigns reshaped the Holy Roman Empire without producing a centralized state. Law and office were negotiated across courts, bishoprics, communes, and aristocratic territories.

## Chronology

1056 Henry IV; 1075–1122 Investiture Controversy and Concordat of Worms; 1152 Frederick I; 1180 princely and territorial shifts; 1198–1204 conflict before the Fourth Crusade.

- municipal and legal plurality
- guild and craft production networks
