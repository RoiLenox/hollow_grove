# Historical Dossier — Byzantine Empire, Comnenian Continuity, and 1204

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

The Comnenian emperors restored military and fiscal capacity through court, army, diplomacy, cities, and regional relationships, while negotiating with Seljuks, western powers, and Crusaders. The Fourth Crusade's 1204 sack ruptured Constantinople but did not erase Byzantine successor states.

## Chronology

1054 church rupture context; 1081 Alexios I; 1096–1099 First Crusade; 1143 Manuel I; 1176 Myriokephalon; 1185 Angeloi; 1204 sack and Latin occupation.
