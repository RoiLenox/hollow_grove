# Historical Dossier — Crusader States, Frontiers, Pilgrimage, and Contact

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Emergent / Partial and Working.

## Historical overview

The Crusader States emerged after the First Crusade, with Latin, Byzantine, Islamic, Jewish, Armenian, and local Christian communities negotiating warfare, pilgrimage, taxation, trade, law, and coexistence. They were not a single state or uncomplicated bridge between cultures.

## Chronology

1098–1099 conquest and state formation; 1120s–1140s institutional consolidation; 1187 Hattin and Jerusalem's loss; 1192 treaty settlement; 1204 Fourth Crusade and sack of Constantinople.

## Permanent Gifts

- frontier fortification and infrastructure
- cross-cultural contact records with asymmetry visible
- pilgrimage and route logistics
