# Historical Dossier — Abbasid Caliphate, Baghdad, Scholarship, and Commerce

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

The Abbasid revolution of 750 shifted the caliphal center eastward. Baghdad, founded in 762, became a major court, commercial, and scholarly center, while provincial dynasties, sectarian communities, merchants, and local institutions retained substantial agency.

## Chronology

750 Abbasid revolution; 762 Baghdad foundation; al-Mansur and al-Mahdi; Harun al-Rashid (786–809); al-Ma'mun (813–833) and scholarly patronage; ninth-century provincial autonomy and political fragmentation.

## Permanent Gifts

- translation and scholarly patronage
- urban archive and library networks
- long-distance commercial and correspondence systems

## Modern Hollow Grove manifestation

House of Translation and Exchange: a Sandmanor forum comparing source language, patron, scholar, urban institution, commercial route, and local reception without importing caliphal authority.
