# Historical Dossier — Carolingian Empire, Reform, Manuscripts, and Rule

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

Pippinid and Carolingian rulers joined conquest, church patronage, capitularies, local aristocracies, monasteries, and manuscript production. Charlemagne's 800 coronation and the division after Louis the Pious did not erase regional law or local institutions.

## Chronology

751 Pippin; 768 Charlemagne; 800 imperial coronation; 814 Louis the Pious; 843 Treaty of Verdun; 877/888 succession and regional fragmentation near the terminal boundary.

## Permanent Gifts

- administrative and capitulary reform
- manuscript and script standardization
- school, monastery, and archive networks

## Modern Hollow Grove manifestation

Reform-and-Record Academy: a Stonebend program tracking office, capitulary, manuscript witness, school, monastery, local law, and revision without importing empire or clerical authority.
