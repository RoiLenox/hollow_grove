# Historical Dossier — Viking Mobility, Trade, and Raid Networks

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

From the late eighth century, Scandinavian maritime mobility connected North Atlantic, European river, Baltic, and eastern routes. Raiding was one practice among trade, settlement, diplomacy, mercenary service, craft, migration, and cultural exchange; “Vikings” is not one polity.

## Chronology

793 Lindisfarne raid as a conventional marker; ninth-century river and maritime networks; 859–862 Mediterranean ventures; 865 Great Heathen Army; 870s settlement and treaty in England; 882–900 Rus and North Atlantic consolidation.

## Permanent Gifts

- adaptive maritime and river-route logistics
- distributed trade, settlement, and communication networks
- cross-cultural material and diplomatic exchange
