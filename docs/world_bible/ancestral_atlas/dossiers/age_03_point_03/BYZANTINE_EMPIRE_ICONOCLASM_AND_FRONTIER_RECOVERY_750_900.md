# Historical Dossier — Byzantine Empire, Iconoclasm, and Frontier Recovery

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Historical overview

After the seventh-century contraction, Byzantine rulers rebuilt military, fiscal, and urban capacity while negotiating with Abbasid, Slavic, Bulgar, and western powers. Iconoclast policies and their reversals affected court, church, monastery, image, and regional memory in different phases.

## Chronology

717–718 Constantinople's siege; 726/730 icon policies; 787 Second Council of Nicaea; 815 second iconoclast phase; 843 restoration of icons; ninth-century frontier and administrative recovery.

## Permanent Gifts

- institutional recovery and provincial defense
- diplomatic and fiscal continuity
- contested image, archive, and memory stewardship
