# Historical Dossier — Rashidun and Umayyad Caliphal Administration and Mobility

Status: Working; Atlas assignment remains Working.

## Evidence boundary

The Qur'an, hadith, biographies, chronicles, papyri, coins, inscriptions, archaeology, and non-Muslim accounts differ in date, genre, and retrospective framing. “Caliphate” names changing political arrangements, not one uniform state or society.

## Historical overview

Muhammad's movement in western Arabia, the Rashidun succession, civil wars, and Umayyad rule created new political and religious institutions across an already connected Afro-Eurasian world. Conquest and taxation interacted with local elites, languages, churches, Jewish communities, Zoroastrian institutions, cities, and rural economies.

## Chronology

610 first revelations in traditional chronology; 622 Hijra; 632 Muhammad's death; 632–661 Rashidun period and first civil wars; 661 Umayyad accession; 691 Dome of the Rock; 705–715 al-Walid's building and expansion; 717–750 reforms, revolts, and Abbasid revolution.

## Permanent Gifts

- Arabic public culture and multilingual translation
