# STATUS

This document establishes the **chronological and cultural assignment skeleton** of the Ancestral Atlas.

Points are representative coordinates, not necessarily continuous chronological bins. Culture-specific relevance ranges, transition Points, nested Points, and open Points preserve uneven historical timing without forcing false synchrony.

It does not yet permanently lock every post-medieval assignment. Ages I and II contain the most developed historical work. Ages III through VI remain working assignments that can be refined while preserving the Age → Point → House structure.
