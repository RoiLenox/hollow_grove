## Current physical layout

There are **exactly two physical corridors** connecting Stonebend and Flynt:

| Physical element | Current V2 design |
|---|---|
| One outer curved mountain-frontier highway | **One physical road**, carved through the mountain corridor and through rock toward Stonebend. Mountains lie on this outer curved frontier. |
| Downhill traversal of that same outer road | **Stonebend: H.T.T. — Highway to Hell:** Stonebend → Flynt, descending. |
| Uphill traversal of that same outer road | **Flynt: S.T.H. — Stairway to Heaven:** Flynt → Stonebend, ascending. This is the reverse traversal of the same physical highway, not a parallel curved road. |
| Second physical corridor | **Basin Motor Speedway (B.M.S.):** a separate, straight, direct, bidirectional chord between the same endpoints. |
| Interior land and venue | Comparatively **flat land between the outer curved arc and the straight B.M.S. chord** contains **The Hippo Dome**. The venue does not sit between two curved roadways and does not form a third corridor. |

new traversal ID is created. Physical corridor identity and legacy

## The Hippo Dome and Hippodrones

**The Hippo Dome** is the venue's current name: a monumental Colosseum-like
exterior enclosing elongated Circus Maximus/hippodrome-style interior
multi-sport and spectacle grounds. “Dome” does not require a roof; roof design
remains **OPEN**. No particular sport or event program is selected.

**Hippodrones** names the venue's concession workers: a **job/crew brand** for
fixed kiosks and optional licensed roving snack/refreshment carts. It is not a
robot species, Form, Agency, official political office, House, currency,
sovereign power, clinical privilege or automatic trade license. Naming the crew
does not issue a permit or authorize a cart to operate. Licensing authority,
permit rules and actual permissions remain **OPEN**.

**Hippodrone** remains the former working venue name in historical material.
An individual worker may optionally be called a **Hippodrone** where the job
context is clear; the active venue is always **The Hippo Dome**. The historical
venue name and the new worker singular must not be collapsed into one entity
or used to reinterpret an old saved identifier. Older football-field concepts
remain history, not a newly selected sport or arena footprint.
