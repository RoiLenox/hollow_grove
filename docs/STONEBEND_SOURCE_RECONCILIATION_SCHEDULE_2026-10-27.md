## SB-04 — professional selection and seal recognition

### Local appointment sentence

**Exact current target:** the second sentence of
`STONEBEND_CONSTITUTION_V2.md:198`:

> The Freemason institution nominates, the Proliteriate reviews, and the Hypergiant confirms.

The first sentence's mastery, discipline and disqualification requirements are
not proposed for deletion. The conflicting current selection/transfer rule is
at `STONEBEND_CONSTITUTION_V2.md:172–179,455–461` and
`STONEBEND_REV100_GOVERNANCE_AMENDMENT.md:47–65`.

**Proposed replacement of the second sentence only:**

> The registered Craftsmanship branch electorate selects the independently
> qualified Freemason by the professional election process. Certification of
> that result and independent seal transfer establish the incoming tenure.
> Institutional nomination, qualification review, Yield testimony and Title
> recognition do not appoint an officeholder, substitute a preferred candidate
> for the certified result, or permit incoming or outgoing self-certification.
> Any surviving qualification or recognition act requires its own lawful
> scope and independently competent reviewer.

**Effect:** distinguish qualification and recognition from choosing the
officeholder. Preserve the six-year term, three-year stagger, branch recall,
single office ID and no active seal during vacancy. High Freemason remains the
same elected Freemason's seal function, not another office
(`STONEBEND_REV100_GOVERNANCE_AMENDMENT.md:39–65`).

**Open:** exact branch-roll/candidate evidence, neutral certifier appointment,
challenge procedure and first cohort remain law/evidence requirements; the
adapter does not supply production defaults (`:102–118`). This wording does
not appoint the institution, Proliteriate or Hypergiant as election certifier.
Review whether retained nomination is an optional candidate proposal or a
required qualification act; do not infer an exclusive nomination gate.

### Shared recognition clause

**Exact current target:** `HOLLOW_GROVE_COMPROMISE_V1_DRAFT.md:890–894`:

> High Freemason replacement requires an independently examined Claim,
> Proliteriate Yield accountability, and Hypergiant-boundary recognition when a
> Hypergiant is active. No candidate may self-certify and the outgoing bearer
> cannot appoint a replacement. Proliteriate witnesses remain temporary while
> the distributed network persists.

This clause does not expressly permit unilateral appointment. Its unresolved
relationship to certified elections and vacancy must be made explicit; its
independent Claim/Yield protections should not be discarded.

**Proposed replacement:**

> Freemason replacement requires a certified professional election,
> independently examined Claim, Proliteriate Yield accountability, and an
> independently certified transfer of a fresh unique seal to the incoming
> tenure. Hypergiant-boundary recognition, where lawfully required while a
> Hypergiant is active, records a bounded Title determination; it does not
> choose or replace the elected officeholder. Neither the candidate nor the
> outgoing bearer may certify the transfer. The outgoing seal closes with its
> tenure and remains in history; vacancy has no active bearer seal. Temporary
> Proliteriate witnesses and the distributed network remain distinct from the
> elected Proliteriate office.

**Open:** whether recognition is a condition of installation, its review
grounds, conflict remedy and vacancy treatment require express resolution.
This draft does not resolve a refusal by treating it as automatic consent or
install a replacement certifier. The separate final Permanence Seal remains
subject to active Hypergiant authorization
(`STONEBEND_CONSTITUTION_V2.md:542–553`); it is not the Freemason tenure seal.

## SB-06 — House determinations and independent judicial review

**Exact current target:** the second sentence of
`STONEBEND_CONSTITUTION_V2.md:331`:

> High appeals involving constitutional identity, exclusive jurisdiction, high office, irreversible Hollowing, multi-House boundaries, or new evidence go to the Hypergiant with recorded Proliteriate and Freemason input.

Preserve the first sentence's Proliteriate hearing functions pending scope

(`STONEBEND_REV100_GOVERNANCE_AMENDMENT.md:76–92`).

**Proposed replacement of the second sentence only:**

> A Hypergiant determination within Stonebend's lawful identity or Title
> domain records the relevant Proliteriate and Freemason input and remains
> subject to available independent judicial review. Judicial appeal and
> constitutional review follow the Minoan County Court System within its
> lawfully established jurisdiction; an executive determination does not
> replace either process. No sovereign or participating officer adjudicates
> their own Trial of Duty or reviews that Trial as an executive appeal.
> Independent Court findings do not themselves remove the sovereign, select
> a successor, ratify an amendment or issue a Stonebend seal.

**Effect:** separate a House-domain determination from the shared judiciary's
review. This is proposed reconciliation, not a finding that all administrative
review has already been abolished or that every matter has a newly created
appeal right. There remains one shared Court.

**Open:** whether any nonjudicial Hypergiant reconsideration survives, which
