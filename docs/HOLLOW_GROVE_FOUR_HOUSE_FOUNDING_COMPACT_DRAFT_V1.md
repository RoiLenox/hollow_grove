## 3. Separate founding ballots and exact approval arithmetic

For each House H, use its independently authenticated eligible civic roll and
one exact compact text/version. Define:

- `E_H`: number of eligible people on the certified roll; it must be positive.
- `Y_H`: authenticated valid affirmative ballots from eligible unique voters.
- `N_H`: authenticated valid negative ballots from eligible unique voters.
- `V_H = Y_H + N_H`, with `0 <= V_H <= E_H`.

**Each House passes if and only if both conditions hold:**

```text
2 * V_H > E_H       participation strictly greater than one half
3 * Y_H >= 2 * V_H  affirmative support at least two thirds
```

Equivalently, `V_H >= floor(E_H / 2) + 1` and
`Y_H >= ceil(2 * V_H / 3)`. Use exact integer comparisons, not rounded displayed
percentages. An empty roll, uncertified roll or unvalidated tally cannot pass.
Blank, spoiled, invalid and abstention records are outside V_H and do not
reduce E_H. Procedures for validity, correction and challenges must still be
published and authorized before use.

| E_H | V_H | Y_H | Founding numerical result |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1,000 | 500 | 500 | Fails: exactly half participation is insufficient |
| 1,000 | 501 | 333 | Fails: below two-thirds affirmative |
| 1,000 | 501 | 334 | Passes both numerical conditions |
| 1,000 | 502 | 334 | Fails: two-thirds requires 335 affirmative |
| 1,000 | 502 | 335 | Passes both numerical conditions |
| 9 | 4 | 4 | Fails: below strict-majority participation |
| 9 | 6 | 4 | Passes: exactly two-thirds affirmative is sufficient |
| 0 | 0 | 0 | Invalid: no eligible electorate |

These are arithmetic examples, not election results. Numerical success alone
does not establish valid notice, eligibility, independent review or effectivity.

The compact requires **Stonebend AND Sandmanor AND Glaüshouse AND Flynt** to
pass separately. Do not pool ballots across Houses, count three of four as
approval, weight one House by population, or activate only the passing Houses.
An office or representative signature cannot replace its civic community's
founding ballot. Exactly four House assent records must refer to the same
text/version and founding process. A failed, missing or disputed required
result leaves the compact ineffective; any repeat process needs an express
procedure, with no automatic reuse of assent for changed text.

The proposed later Flynt local referendum retains its distinct **40%** turnout
and two-thirds approval design
(`docs/FLYNT_ELECTORAL_COLLEGE_WARDEN_PARLEY_PROPOSED_CONSTITUTION_V1.md:73–86`).
This compact's **strictly greater than 50%** founding participation rule neither
comes from that future referendum nor silently changes it. Neither rule is
automatically an office election, recall, command election or Q threshold.

> Once this compact is effective, each House's existing lawful civic community
