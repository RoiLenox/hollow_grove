## Recommended schedule

1. Build each founding roll from **all evidenced lawful civic members**, not
   an office, profession, Agency or volunteer-only subset. Resolve omissions
   and disputed records before certification.
2. Count one person once within each House, using authenticated continuity and
   membership evidence. **Recommended overlap rule:** preserve separately
   proven lawful membership in more than one House and permit one ballot in
   each such House. This creates no dual citizenship from travel, employment
   or learning a Form, and is a new proposal requiring express selection.
3. Publish a limited **pre-adoption administrative warrant** under the
   prospective origin declaration, naming temporary coordinators, independent
   verifiers and alternates. Establish their initial authority in that source;
   do not make them depend on an office the compact has yet to enable.
4. Separate preparation, verification, judicial review and sealing. Give staff
   procedural duties only, with no discretionary power to approve the compact,
   change eligibility, choose rulers or enlarge their own mandates.
5. Freeze the certified electorate before voting. Preserve its denominator and
   audit history; correct material defects through an authorized recorded
   remedy, never by quietly dropping people or changing the roll after counting.

The overlap rule and the initial-warrant mechanism are the main new creator
choices. This document makes both concrete rather than asking again who the
founding communities are. Actual civic evidence, named staff and lawful dates
remain unpopulated; no founding event is represented as having happened.

## FC-01 — proposed civic roll clauses

### Inclusion and House boundaries

> Each House's founding roll comprises the distinct people whose existing
> lawful civic membership and voting eligibility at the published qualifying
> cutoff are established by authenticated source records and applicable law.
> Include all such members identified in the certified source population,
> whether or not they have already asked for a ballot. Provide an accessible
> correction process for omitted members. Existing lawful restrictions require
> an exact source and individual disposition; administrators may not invent
> new restrictions or treat missing evidence as an adjudicated disqualification.

No new age, wealth, residence, rank, Form, professional degree, property,
Agency or service prerequisite is proposed. A location, color, learned Form,
nickname, employment record or institutional access grant proves no civic
membership by itself. Temporary absence does not erase established standing.
Records affected by death, withdrawal or loss of standing must identify the
applicable rule, authenticated event and its relation to the cutoff; a closed
office tenure alone does not end citizenship.

| House roll | Required evidence and boundary |
|---|---|
| Stonebend | Evidence of Gerald civic standing and applicable eligibility. Preserve witness-node history; multiple nodes yield one person, not multiple votes. The Proliteriate/Freemason professional rolls are narrower and cannot substitute. |
| Sandmanor | Evidence for civic members of both Minorian and Minoan communities. Provide notice, correction access and affected-tradition review to both. Use one deduplicated Sandmanor tally; invent neither separate regional ratification thresholds nor a fifth House. |
| Glaüshouse | Evidence of civic admission or an expressly reconciled equivalent. An old Nightingale clinical-role label, patient status, Muse affiliation or office alone is insufficient. No chart, diagnosis or treatment history is needed for the roll. |
| Flynt | Evidence of lawful Warden civic membership and applicable eligibility. College enrollment, Privateer/Corsair rank, command employment or a future Registrar record cannot manufacture founding standing. |

**Proposed overlap clause:**

> A person with separately established lawful civic membership and eligibility
> in more than one House may participate once in each applicable House's
> founding ballot. Review each claim independently. Multiple memberships
> within one House confer only one ballot there. This schedule neither creates
> new House membership nor revokes an existing one, and one active Agency does
> not determine the person's civic franchise.

This recommendation preserves proven membership rights in separate constituent
communities. An alternative single founding-House designation would be a new
restriction needing explicit rules for choice, default, review and preservation
of other memberships. Neither alternative is treated as already enacted.

### Identity, disputed evidence and the denominator

Maintain one proposed roll entry per authenticated person and House, with
links to the original membership records, source-law versions and any explicit
identity reconciliation. Preserve historical identifiers and source records.
Multiple incarnations or aliases do not create extra voters; a missing
cross-domain link requires review, not a guessed identity merge.

Use three draft dispositions: **included**, **excluded with sourced reason**,
and **pending evidence/review**. Pending cases remain visible in the restricted
audit and receive notice/correction access; do not silently count them as
ineligible or shrink the proposed denominator. A roll is not certified as
complete while material eligibility questions affecting its population remain
undetermined. Public objections require standing and a specific reviewable
claim; an unsupported accusation is not automatic disenfranchisement or a stay.

The coordinator prepares the roll; an independent verifier checks completeness,
sources, deduplication, dispositions and the count. Certification binds a roll
version, qualifying cutoff, exact `E_H`, applicable legal rules and review
record. A digest identifies the bytes but cannot prove lawful membership.

The date schedule must establish, in order, adequate notice of eligibility and
access, the qualifying cutoff, correction/review, certification/freeze, ballot
opening and close. Use lawfully specified dates and intervals under FC-03 and
GEN-08; no invented 100-day year or borrowed election deadline. Clerical
drafts may be prepared during notice; authoritative freeze follows completed
required review.

After freeze, preserve the original record and count. A necessary correction
creates a new version with its reason, authority and notice. Before opening,
recertify and republish the changed roll/count. After opening, a competent
review process must determine the lawful remedy and whether a fresh ballot
is required; no retroactive denominator rewrite or silent ballot reuse.
Existing appeal does not automatically stay judgment
(`MINOAN_COUNTY_COURT_SYSTEM_AND_RESTITUTION_CYCLE_V1.md:193–199`).

The approved founding calculation remains `V_H = Y_H + N_H`, `2*V_H > E_H`
and `3*Y_H >= 2*V_H`, with a positive certified E_H and four separate passing
Houses. Blank/spoiled/invalid votes do not count toward V_H and do not reduce
E_H. This schedule does not import the later Flynt 40% participation rule.

### Records and privacy — proposed documentary schema

| Record | Minimum content; no runtime schema change is made |
|---|---|
| House roll header | Founding process/compact version, House, eligibility-source versions, qualifying cutoff, freeze version, source-population references, E_H, preparer/verifier mandates and dispositions. |
| Restricted person entry | Stable person/domain references, House membership evidence and effective dates, eligibility basis, explicit alias reconciliation, inclusion/exclusion/pending decision, reason and review link. |
| Public roll notice | House, qualifying rules, notice/correction access, version, certified count and independently auditable commitment; no public list of clinical or private identity details. |
| Ballot audit | Separate eligibility/issuance audit and secret-choice tally, unique-use protection, invalidity/replacement dispositions and source versions. No public person-to-choice link or coercion-enabling vote receipt. |
| Correction record | Old/new version, affected scope, exact reason, competent authority, notice and preservation of the superseded record. |

Authenticate the person without displaying their choice. The future ballot
protocol must prevent duplicate voting while keeping identity and choice
separate; hashing a public person-choice pair is not ballot secrecy. No
cryptographic protocol or new database is implemented by this table.

## FC-02 — proposed original administrative warrant

**Recommended initial appointment method:** the finalized prospective
founding-origin declaration expressly names the initial temporary procedural
staff and their alternates in a public warrant annex. Their authority is an
original, bounded delegation of the four civic communities authored into that
origin settlement, not something inferred from future compact offices or
issued by the appointees themselves. This is a proposed new origin rule.

The creator authors the starting source and named mandates as worldbuilding;
the creator is not added as an in-world fifth ratifier. The declaration's
limited administrative mandate must have its own authentic prospective public
effective event **before official roll certification or ballot issuance**.
The compact's substantive amendment authority remains conditional on the
later four House votes. This avoids inventing a second undefined election
merely to select the people who administer the first.

This schedule does not declare that origin mandate effective. Its exact text,
named people, conflicts, source relationship and originating public event
still require creator resolution and authentic records. No clerk, sovereign,
Court or software policy string acquires authority by writing its own name.

### Temporary functions and separation

| Function | Proposed assignment and duties | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| House coordinator | One named accountable coordinator per House, with named limited clerical delegates as needed; compile records, issue notice, provide access, conduct the ballot and prepare the tally. | Cannot certify their own work, change the franchise or decide challenged legal questions. |
| Independent verifier | A distinct named person per House verifies the roll and independently checks the ballot/count audit. The same verifier may cover both stages if independent throughout; the mandate identifies any additional technical auditors. | Verification reports facts and defects; it does not cast the House's assent, veto a valid result for policy reasons or issue the Stonebend seal. |
| Alternate | Named before the process; used only by the warrant's recorded substitution procedure when a conflict, incapacity or absence is established. | No self-appointment, deputy sovereign or automatic future Registrar. An unresolved vacancy blocks the affected certification. |
| Shared Court reviewer | A lawfully appointed, independent reviewer within the existing Court; mandate and jurisdiction referenced separately. | The warrant does not appoint a judge, replace the Court's appointment law or grant an unprovided election remedy. |
| Stonebend sealer/record custodian | The authenticated lawful bearer and custodian under existing, reconciled seal/record authority. | A warrant cannot cure a vacant or disputed seal, ratify for a House or substitute publication for missing assent. |

These are temporary service assignments, not four new constitutional offices
or a permanent electoral commission. They do not replace the proposed future
Flynt Council's fixed Registrar-certification rule
(`docs/FLYNT_ELECTORAL_COLLEGE_WARDEN_PARLEY_PROPOSED_CONSTITUTION_V1.md:149–166`).
Cross-House assistance may be explicitly commissioned for competence, but
House identity alone proves neither independence nor appointment authority.

### Conflicts, substitution and closure

**Proposed conflict rule:** coordinators and verifiers cannot be current
sovereigns, holders of offices whose authority the same instrument directly
changes, campaign directors or paid campaign staff for the founding question.
They disclose direct financial interests, controlling relationships and prior
participation in contested decisions. No person prepares and independently
certifies the same work or resolves a challenge to their own mandate,
eligibility, ballot or conduct. An alternate handles the conflicted matter
under an independently recorded assignment.

Ordinary civic membership, an ordinary political opinion or casting a secret
ballot does not by itself forfeit staff civic rights or prove procedural bias.
This is a proposed founding-specific rule; any stricter applicable Court
conflict law remains controlling. A Court reviewer cannot adjudicate a case
in which they administered, certified or are a party to the contested act.
The source's judge-selection gaps remain unresolved rather than filled by
an invented judicial panel.

Every substitution records trigger, authority, old/new actor, scope, time and
preserved work. No missing verifier may be replaced by reducing required
independence. A coordinator may not remove a verifier to obtain a favorable
certificate. Objections and disagreements retain their reasons for independent
review; silence, recusal and an unfilled position never count as certification.

The warrant closes its election-administration authority when the process
ends through final disposition and authenticated record handover. Necessary
custody/preservation duties survive only within their stated scope and end
conditions. A new ballot, a renewed mandate or a later enabling vote requires
express authority; staff do not automatically become permanent officers.
