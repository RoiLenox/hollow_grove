### Core rule

**Light = emergence; dark = mastery.** A base Hueman begins with individual natural flesh tones. Synthesis ascent enlarges and refines anatomy and assigns progressively darker House colors. Color expresses form identity; it does **not** calculate height, health, damage, maturity, privileges, or unlocks. All 34 hex swatches/names remain identical to v2.2; only Form-to-swatch mappings change.

### Hueman Chromatic Growth System — companion rules

- Natural flesh-toned base Hueman is not one of the 32 Synthesis Forms, nor assigned a fixed House skin color.
- Approved illustrative Growth Indices: **Gremlin = 0**, **Goblin = 1**, **Gargoyle = 2**. Gargoyle is a **direct Gremlin branch**, not a Goblin descendant.
- Other 29 proposed Growth Indices, target height ratios (100%–250%), silhouette notes, pixel bounds, animations, collision shapes, and art approvals remain **WORKING**, NOT creator-locked. See `hueman_chromatic_growth_system_v0_3_v2_3_bindings_working.json`.
- Form color from the inverted v2.3 mapping is **creator approved**. Form size is **separate data**, and physical growth must be newly drawn, readable anatomy; do not merely increase sprite scale or derive size directly from RGB hex brightness.
- Keep all Hueman-derived Forms upright, bipedal, humanoid, and recognizable, including Centaur, Pegasus, Chimera, and all six Flynt siblings. 16×16 overworld tiles are different from a sprite atlas/frame size; inspect the current 24×32 native art baseline before changing bounds.
- Current Synthesis effects should grow out of the body, including reversible hand/arm weapon or tool reconfiguration; do not convert this visual direction into new gameplay abilities or weapons without separate review.

### Mathematical invariants

- Stonebend, Sandmanor, Glaüshouse swatch levels 3, 5, 7 are per-channel half-up RGB midpoints of adjacent preexisting swatches. v2.3 does not change these channel values.
- Flynt swatches at *identical palette positions* equal arithmetic-mean grayscale derived from Stonebend RGB. This stays true even though Flynt **Form assignment order** is inverted: do **not** recompute gray from the lineage root Form or recompute shades from darkness.
- Sandmanor darkest Wine `#5A1F2A` belongs to Hecaton. Earlier user typo `5A152AA` was seven hex characters, and v2.3 approval followed explicit clarification using existing Wine `#5A1F2A`.
- Oxford Blue `#0A1024` legacy outlines and other historic tokens are outside the 34-color swatch inventory; outline strategy still requires review. Do not silently remove or recolor working assets.
