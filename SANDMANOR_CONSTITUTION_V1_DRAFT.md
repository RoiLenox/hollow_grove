# Sandmanor Constitution V1 Draft

Status: Draft pending ratification

Authority: Normative where this document restates existing canonical law; proposed where it orders that law into a constitutional instrument

Date: 2026-07-18

## 1. Constitutional Status

This document is the proposed House constitution for Sandmanor.

It consolidates existing Sandmanor law concerning proof, measurement, configuration, reciprocal teaching, witnessed improvement, the Sandmen, the Minorian and Minoan bodies, The Sandman, and the two equal transformation lineages.

It does not invent a crowd algorithm, contest scheduler, vote rule, tie breaker, office term, or automatic succession engine.

Upon ratification, this document SHALL govern Sandmanor-specific authority. The Hollow Grove V2 Constitutional Specification SHALL continue to govern the common Bond lifecycle, signed Current, signed Aura, Tombstone, Toke, persistence, replay, and final Synthesis Resolution.

Where this draft labels a matter Reserved, implementation MUST reject reliance upon an assumed answer.

## 2. Constitutional Identity

Sandmanor is the second immutable House position in the Four-House grammar.

Sandmanor carries:

- Prove It;
- Configuration;
- measurement;
- comparison;
- modeling;
- diagnosis;
- classification;
- allocation;
- public proof;
- witnessed improvement.

Its material statement is:

> Crystal measures.

Its inherited constitutional profile is:

- action: Prove It;
- logic: negative;
- expression: Matural;
- operating-system domain: Configuration;
- color: red;
- House rock: Crystal;
- Aura leaning: Reflective.

Negative logic is a House grammar term. It is not Negative Current or Negative Aura.

Its constitutional question is:

> What is happening, how can it be measured, does the proof survive inspection, and how should the arrangement improve?

Sandmanor is House 2. Its grammar recurs at Rule-of-Twelve positions 2, 6, and 10.

Position 6 remains Sandmanor measurement and diagnosis at the threshold into Glaüshouse. It is not Glaüshouse treatment or Clearance.

Geographic position, screen direction, color, mineral, expression, and operating-system analogy are projections. They do not independently confer proof authority.

## 3. Purpose

Sandmanor exists because a named claim is not constitutional proof.

The engine needs a House that can inspect assumptions, preserve measurement, compare alternatives, expose failure, classify consequence, allocate attention, and show whether improvement actually occurred.

Sandmanor makes proof reproducible and challengeable.

Sandmanor cannot make a false claim true, declare a system cleared, perform final Synthesis, or create recognition merely by producing an attractive model.

## 4. Responsibilities

Sandmanor SHALL:

- define what claim is being tested;
- define measurable scope;
- identify methods, units, conditions, assumptions, and evidence;
- preserve observations and contrary evidence;
- compare like with like under explicit rules;
- classify results;
- model arrangements;
- diagnose conditions;
- expose which assumption failed;
- expose what requires redesign;
- preserve reproducibility;
- distinguish internal order from external expression;
- preserve Minorian and Minoan equality;
- require reciprocal teaching for the Sandman contest described by existing law;
- require witnessed improvement rather than inherited bloodline for the Sandman office;
- preserve the two equal Sandmanor transformation lineages;
- reject cross-lineage substitution and illegal stage skipping;
- support deterministic replay;
- participate in Bonds under the common Bond constitution.

## 5. Non-responsibilities

Sandmanor SHALL NOT:

- create the claim it evaluates;
- treat measurement as ownership;
- treat classification as punishment;
- hide failed assumptions;
- choose a result by insertion order, randomness, visual appeal, or species preference;
- confuse public attention with valid proof;
- declare Glaüshouse Clearance;
- perform final Synthesis;
- grant Flynt recognition;
- collapse Minorians and Minoans into one body;
- rank the two lineages as superior and inferior;
- reverse Gnome and Elf associations;
- treat cultural interior and exterior orientation as geographic imprisonment;
- infer signed Current or Aura from Sandmanor's negative logic;
- infer Positive Aura from Reflective Aura;
- activate an automatic Sandman contest or succession cycle without ratified procedure;
- bypass the Bond lifecycle.

## 6. Constitutional Questions

Every Sandmanor act MUST answer the applicable questions:

- What claim is being tested?
- Who requested or authorized proof?
- What is the measurement scope?
- Which units, methods, assumptions, and thresholds apply?
- What was observed?
- Which evidence supports the observation?
- Which contrary evidence exists?
- Can another competent observer reproduce the result?
- What category does the result occupy?
- What failed?
- Which assumption broke?
- What arrangement should change?
- Who witnessed improvement?
- Was improvement produced through reciprocal teaching?
- Were Minorian count and Minoan arrangement both represented?
- Does the result remain proof, challenge, or unresolved ambiguity?
- Can replay reach the same result?

## 7. Domain Model

### 7.1 House identity

Sandmanor House identity is immutable.

The canonical House value is the existing `House::Sandmanor` identity.

All institutions, offices, roles, groups, sites, contests, proofs, and lineage records MUST use stable typed identities. Display name and crowd prominence are not identities.

### 7.2 Governing institution

The current canonical institution is:

- `institution.sandmanor.sandmen` — Sandmen.

Its existing domains are:

- witnessed improvement;
- measurement;
- design;
- allocation;
- public expression.

The Sandmen are the people and witness body. The institution does not become The Sandman office.

### 7.3 Public office

The current canonical singular House office is:

- `office.sandmanor.sandman` — The Sandman.

Its existing authority vocabulary is:

- WitnessedImprovement;

The Sandman is an office, not a species, lineage, permanent family title, or automatic mastery stage.

The office holder and the office are distinct identities.

### 7.4 Minorians

Minorians measure. They do not acquire ownership merely by counting or allocating.

### 7.5 Minoans

Minoans are the exterior Sandmanor body and are canonically associated with Elves.

Minoans reveal. Presentation does not make an unsupported claim true.

### 7.6 Sandmanor duality

The canonical contrast is:

> Elves externalize Aura into expression.

Interior and exterior describe culture and specialization. They do not establish permanent geographic class restrictions.

Neither side is complete constitutional proof without inspectable relation to the other side's discipline where the governing process requires both.

### 7.7 Sites

Both are public proof spaces under current scene policy. Public access does not grant office, contest standing, or authority to alter evidence.

### 7.8 Material domain

Sandmanor's House rock is Crystal.

Crystal contributes Structure, refraction, classification, precision, memory, pattern, multiple perspectives, internal order, and visible information.

The existing material expression is:

- Regular Current plus Reflective Aura produces Prism Sand under an applicable lawful Recipe;
- Prismiron is the accepted hard proof-ready branch.

Regular Current is not Positive Current. Reflective Aura is not Positive Aura.

### 7.9 Biological and civic domain

Sandmanor's primary biological logic includes nerves, senses, measurement, pattern recognition, classification, memory, timing, dosage, signaling, records, diagnosis, interpretation, resource allocation, and exterior expression.

Sandmanor may determine where attention should go. It does not physically perform every action it recommends.

### 7.10 Ownership, lifetime, and persistence

House identity is perpetual and immutable.

Proof records, measurements, observations, assumptions, witness records, challenges, contest results, and office accession records become immutable history when committed.

Derived models MAY be recomputed. The evidence and rules from which they derive MUST remain available.

Office tenure and contest status are historical relationships and MUST NOT be embedded as mutable fields on a person or lineage identity.

## 8. Proof Semantics

A Sandmanor proof MUST include:

- proof identity;
- subject and claim;
- requester and authority;
- method;
- units and scale;
- assumptions;
- input evidence;
- observation boundary;
- measurement results;
- comparison set, if any;
- classification rule;
- findings;
- contrary or excluded evidence;
- witnesses;
- reproducibility information;
- rule version;
- causal position.

A result is not proof when its method, evidence, scope, authority, or replay basis is missing.

Failure is a valid proof result when it deterministically shows which assumption failed and what remains unresolved.

Proof MUST NOT be rewritten to make a favored arrangement pass.

## 9. Proof Lifecycle and State Machine

Legal proof states are:

- Proposed;
- Measuring;
- Measured;
- UnderInspection;
- Proven;
- Failed;
- Ambiguous;
- Challenged;
- Superseded;
- Historical.

Legal transitions are:

- Proposed to Measuring after scope, method, authority, and evidence prerequisites validate;
- Measuring to Measured when the complete measurement boundary commits;
- Measured to UnderInspection when reproduction and contradiction checks begin;
- UnderInspection to Proven when the claim survives every applicable check;
- UnderInspection to Failed when the claim deterministically fails;
- UnderInspection to Ambiguous when the available evidence cannot lawfully decide;
- Proven, Failed, or Ambiguous to Challenged through a competent challenge;
- Challenged to the applicable Proven, Failed, or Ambiguous decision through review without historical rewrite;
- any decided proof to Superseded when a later proof addresses a later causal boundary or governing rule;
- decided or Superseded proof to Historical when its active reliance ends.

Illegal transitions include:

- Proposed directly to Proven;
- Measuring directly to Proven without a complete measurement record;
- Failed to Proven by editing inputs;
- Ambiguous to Proven by guessing;
- Challenged to Historical to avoid review;
- any result changed by collection order or display prominence.

## 10. Reciprocal Teaching Contract

Existing Sandmanor law requires:

- a Minorian teaches a Minoan to account;
- a Minoan teaches a Minorian to design like a song;
- each contender improves at the opposing discipline;
- the improvement is witnessed;
- rule is earned through the greatest witnessed reciprocal improvement rather than inherited bloodline.

The teaching record MUST identify:

- teacher;
- learner;
- native discipline;
- opposing discipline;
- starting condition;
- taught practice;
- evidence of practice;
- resulting condition;
- witnesses;
- comparison rule.

Lineage, species, beauty, wealth, title, or crowd size alone MUST NOT constitute improvement.

The exact curriculum, contest duration, admissible metrics, witness quorum, and equivalence rule are Reserved.

## 11. Sandman Office and Contest

The Sandman is the singular office of Sandmanor rule.

Either Minorian or Minoan body may produce the office holder.

The office MUST be awarded only after witnessed reciprocal improvement under a lawful contest.

The office MUST NOT be inherited solely by bloodline.

The office holder governs configuration through the authority lawfully attached to the office. The holder does not own the Sandmen, Minorians, Minoans, proof records, or House.

The current descriptive law says the office may not remain vacant and the contest turns again when a holder falls. The repository does not yet implement the procedure that guarantees continuity.

The Sandman office therefore has a constitutional continuity requirement. A vacancy is a failure condition requiring another lawful witnessed contest; it is not permission to fabricate a winner.

The following remain Reserved:

- contender eligibility;
- contest initiation;
- contest term;
- admissible improvement dimensions;
- crowd composition and standing;
- witness quorum;
- cross-discipline comparison;
- tie and inconclusive result;
- accession commit;
- removal;
- appeal;
- vacancy continuity.

Until those rules are ratified, no implementation may select The Sandman automatically.

## 12. Equal Transformation Lineages

Sandmanor contains two equal transformation lineages.

The interior Minorian lineage is:

- Gnome;
- Minotaur;
- Hecaton.

The exterior Minoan lineage is:

- Elf;
- Centaur;
- Pegasus.

Their matched stages are:

| Stage | Minorian | Minoan |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Gnome | Elf |
| Synthesis-stage form | Minotaur | Centaur |
| Mastery | Hecaton | Pegasus |

The stage label Synthesis does not itself perform or authorize Glaüshouse Synthesis. Any actual cross-boundary transformation remains subject to Recipe legality and Glaüshouse authority.

Legal adjacent transitions are:

- Gnome to Minotaur;
- Minotaur to Hecaton;
- Elf to Centaur;
- Centaur to Pegasus.

Cross-lineage transitions and stage skipping are illegal under the current lineage contract.

Hecaton expands capacity through four-armed material mastery. Hecaton has four arms; the one-hundred-hands association is symbolic.

Pegasus expands reach through flight and horizon mastery.

Hecaton and Pegasus are equal mastery outcomes. Equality does not mean identical traits.

The law that a Gnome becomes whatever it keeps governs horizontal cultural and professional specialization. It does not erase the separate typed Frame transformation lineage.

## 13. Configuration and Allocation

Configuration is an explicit arrangement derived from proof.

Every authoritative configuration MUST identify:

- the proven or measured condition;
- the arrangement being changed;
- affected participants or objects;
- resources and constraints;
- allocation rule;
- expected consequence;
- reversibility where applicable;
- authority;
- evidence;
- causal position.

Allocation MUST NOT create resources, erase obligations, change ownership without authority, or treat diagnosis as consent.

A later configuration MAY supersede an earlier one. It MUST preserve the earlier record and the proof basis for both.

## 14. Relationship to Current and Aura

Sandmanor's negative logic is not Negative Current and is not Negative Aura.

Regular Current is a Current state, not Positive Current.

Reflective Aura is an Aura state, not Positive Aura.

Crystal, measurement, proof, visibility, crowd approval, and exterior expression do not determine signed Aura by themselves.

A Sandmanor Bond may lawfully occupy any of the four constitutional polarity states.

Positive Current may be measured under Negative Aura when productive capacity is concealed or distorted.

Negative Current may be measured under Positive Aura when burden is truthfully revealed and recognized.

Sandmanor MUST preserve gross signed histories and MUST NOT reduce proof to one moral score.

## 15. Relationship to Bond

A Sandmanor-governed Bond uses Sandmanor law to define proof scope, measurements, assumptions, comparison, diagnosis, configuration, evidence, and witnessed improvement.

It MUST still complete every applicable common Bond stage.

Sandmanor proof MAY support Bond validation, Current/Aura evaluation, maturity readiness, condensation eligibility, or Tombstone validation when the validator has applicable authority.

Sandmanor proof is not automatically a Tombstone.

A proof result does not create Current, alter Aura history, complete a term, or grant recognition.

Glaüshouse retains exclusive final Synthesis authority.

## 16. Challenges and Appeals

A challenge MUST identify:

- challenged proof, configuration, contest, or office decision;
- challenger identity and standing;
- challenged scope;
- alleged methodological, evidentiary, comparative, witness, authority, or causality failure;
- evidence;
- requested review.

Challenge preserves the original record.

An appeal MUST apply a competent review rule to the original inputs and any lawfully admitted later evidence. It MUST NOT silently change the method after seeing the result.

Contradictory proofs remain separately addressable. A later authoritative decision may supersede reliance; it does not delete the contradiction.

Contest appeal authority and timing remain Reserved.

## 19. Failure Modes and Recovery

### 19.1 Measurement failure

The proof remains Proposed, Measuring, Failed, or Ambiguous according to the committed boundary. Missing values MUST NOT be replaced with favorable defaults.

### 19.2 Reproduction failure

The claim does not become Proven. Divergence is recorded with the method, environment, and causal inputs necessary to investigate it.

### 19.3 Contradictory evidence

Contradiction produces inspection, challenge, or Ambiguous status. It is not resolved by deletion or majority display.

### 19.4 Invalid comparison

The comparison fails when units, stages, scopes, or methods are incompatible. The engine MUST NOT rank incomparable contenders.

### 19.5 Contest tie or insufficient witness

No accession commits. The office-continuity consequence remains Reserved. The engine MUST NOT use randomness or first-write order.

### 19.6 Lineage violation

The transition is rejected before transformation authority commits. No partial mastery or cross-lineage state becomes authoritative.

### 19.7 Office vacancy

The institution records vacancy or dispute and blocks acts requiring The Sandman. It MUST NOT invent an interim holder without ratified law.

### 19.8 Corruption

Recover proof and configuration from authoritative evidence and deterministic replay. If exact recovery is impossible, dependent decisions remain blocked.

## 24. Regional Synthesis Implementation Addendum

The common V2 constitution now implements a narrow regional proof scope in
`src/constitutional/regional.rs`.

Sandmanor does not perform final integration. It proves the regional
configuration and lineage prerequisites through an accepted
`HouseFunction::Prove` decision from `institution.sandmanor.sandmen` carrying
`WitnessedImprovement`.

For Gnome→Minotaur, Sandmanor proof MUST be accompanied by established standing
at `site.sandmanor.aura-fields` and predecessor-bound standing, lineage,
readiness, rule, and supporting evidence.

For Elf→Centaur, Sandmanor proof MUST be accompanied by established standing at
`site.sandmanor.aura-beach` and the same categories of predecessor-bound
evidence.

This implementation reuses `SandmanorForm`, `SandmanorLineage`, and
`validate_sandmanor_transition`. It does not create a second lineage model.

The following remain illegal:

- Gnome→Centaur;
- Elf→Minotaur;
- transformation inferred solely from a Sandmanor location;
- proof issued by another House or another institution;
- proof for one Being transferred to another Being;
- Minotaur coastal authority;
- Centaur field authority.

Minotaur→Hecaton and Centaur→Pegasus remain adjacent descriptive lineage forms
but have no ratified regional execution rule in V2. Their existence in the
lineage enum does not authorize Synthesis.
