# Hollow Grove Playable Expansion Roadmap V1

Status: active implementation order
Backbone: `HOLLOW_GROVE_COMPROMISE_V1_DRAFT.md`

## One Standard Everywhere

All Hollow Grove regions use the same completion standard. A playable slice is
complete only when it has:

- the Compromise's twelve functional-lore fields;
- one persistent case Being or subject;
- exact House jurisdiction and live authority;
- addressable evidence and explicit uncertainty;
- player-visible but lawfully bounded choice;
- refusal, challenge, incapacity, or failure behavior;
- typed canonical events and lawful state changes;
- checksummed persistence and deterministic replay;
- map, collision, dialogue, and consequence projection;
- tests proving House non-substitution and client non-authority.

The universal recursion kernel remains untouched. Shared gameplay mechanisms
may be extracted only after at least two real slices prove their common shape.

## Route Skeleton — Implemented

All ten constitutional routes are now canonical, traversable gameplay maps
over one validated network:

- five Straight routes;
- four Round routes, presented as curved geography;
- Current Sea as the distinct Sea Ordeal.

Transfers require a shared House endpoint and replay through gameplay archive
schema V2. Each route projects its stable identity, endpoints, geometry,
dominant verb, purpose, and process. The network is specified in
`HOLLOW_GROVE_ROUTE_NETWORK_V1.md`.

## Interior Surface Fill

With the connective skeleton complete, the interior map layer is:

1. **Aura Field — implemented** — one stable geographic surface and gameplay
   map containing multiple Aura farms, cultivation plots, orchard, paddock,
   irrigation, barn, granary, seed house, farmstead, tool works, produce
   market, windbreak, apiary, compost yard, Sandmanor proving plots, and the
   voluntary finite-joint-work Engagement Farm. Older
   plural machine identifiers are save-compatible symbols, not geography.
2. **Aura Beach — implemented** — singular Sandmanor Minoan exterior surface
   with coastal proof, public access, navigation, tide and weather observation,
   rescue, Elf design, Centaur mobility, landing, pier, recovery, dunes, market,
   and salvage.
3. **Aura Basin — implemented** — singular Flynt-first collision and return
   surface with hunting, dens, conflict, rescue, racing, trials, lawful
   Hollowing, salvage, material works, shelter, triage, Frame recovery,
   competition, and a non-sovereign We Fairy Men waycamp.

These are fill regions bounded by routes, not additional routes and not kernel
layers.

## Build Order

### Wave 1 — Stonebend

1. **Current Sea identity continuity** — implemented. Mercy Deep may receive an
   affirmed existing Name, a linked provisional transformed-form Name, or a
   protected high-review referral.
2. **Aura Ridge public Name** — provisional civic renaming, conflict search,
   public notice, challenge window, and preserved history.
3. **Mt. Aura Illegal Hollowing** — distinct subject and extracted-Hollow
   identity, provenance, emergency custody, nondestructive refusal, and
   investigation.

Stonebend establishes the durable identity vocabulary on which later proof,
Clearance, recovery, recognition, Bonds, property, and regional consequences
can safely rely.

### Wave 2 — Sandmanor

1. **Aura Way public-design trial** — equal Minorian interior method and Minoan
   exterior demonstration, measurements, criticism, revision, and scoped proof.
2. **Glausbahn recovery-design failure** — negative results remain evidence;
   clinical stop and later redesign cannot be edited out.
3. **Aura Beach / Current Sea role proof** — regional competence without
   automatic Synthesis, Title, office, or identity change.

### Wave 3 — Glaüshouse

1. **Riptide emergency intake** — narrow stabilization, capacity, consent,
   refusal, Nightingale stop, recovery, and discharge.
2. **Current Seanad high-risk Clearance** — proven design remains separate from
   capable consent and clinical scope.
3. **Aura Ridge recovery discharge** — protected transfer, continuing recovery,
   and refusal of premature duty.

### Wave 4 — Flynt

1. **Boardwalk return recognition** — implemented, including the Pimp/Hoes and
   Gimp/Goons social polarity, three finite Bonds, and independent return.
2. **Stairway functional recognition** — performed function without accidental
   office or Stonebend Title.
3. **Basin Motor Speedway dual expression** — equal urban and rural Flynt
   functions without duplicate sovereignty.

## Cross-Region Consequence Pass

The first cross-region consequence substrate is implemented. Living Field,
Beach, Basin, mine, and rig events now connect outcomes without rewriting prior
events:

- reinforced Mt. Aura stone strengthens Field irrigation and Beach dunes;
- Highway deep iron supports Basin Frame recovery;
- unresolved Riptide contamination harms Beach fish and the Basin;
- Riptide shut-in sends quarantined fluid to Current Sea;
- Current Sea certification supports Beach and Basin rescue logistics;
- Beach rescue can transfer a care burden into the Basin/Glaüshouse loop.

The remaining authored House cases will join that substrate rather than create
a separate consequence store:

- Current Sea Name status changes later Boardwalk and Aura Ridge reference;
- Riptide recovery and discharge constrain Boardwalk return;
- Sandmanor proof changes which procedures or works may be considered, never
  who consents;
- Flynt recognition changes public function and access, never Stonebend Title;
- Illegal Hollowing evidence changes custody, restoration, and appeal paths;
- refusals remain visible and cannot be converted into incapacity or failure.

Party progression, combat, economy, and procedural generation may
consume the shared consequence model only through its committed events and
custody records. The detailed contract is
`HOLLOW_GROVE_LIVING_SURFACES_AND_EXTRACTION_V1.md`.

## First Character-Driven Cross-Region Campaign — Implemented

Deep Pressure now consumes that consequence substrate as one authored
campaign. It adds classified speech, an evidence journal, physically scheduled
people, persistent relationship memory, distinct endings, four separate House
acts, optional finite recovery Bonds, and visible aftermath.

This proves that Hollow Grove can carry one decision through people and places
without a generic quest reducer. Combat and procedural encounters remain later
consumers; they may not bypass the campaign's evidence, memory, custody,
authority, or refusal records. See
`HOLLOW_GROVE_DEEP_PRESSURE_CAMPAIGN_V1.md`.

## First Party and Recruitment Vertical Slice — Implemented

Hueman may now travel in a party of at most six total members. Corin, Brindle,
Harrow, Oren, Maela, and Tess are rename-safe authored candidates; a completed
Deep Pressure outcome, their relationship memory, condition, role boundary,
trust, physically faced presence, and the player's disclosed way of asking all
shape their own persistent decision.

Refusal creates no debt and cannot be badgered. Exhausted accepted companions
rest before leading. Five of the six candidates may join alongside Hueman, each
with one exact map-bound field action. Party decisions, recovery, lead, and
field evidence archive and replay exactly. Godot exposes the party and
recruitment panels without deciding acceptance. See
`HOLLOW_GROVE_PARTY_AND_RECRUITMENT_V1.md`.
