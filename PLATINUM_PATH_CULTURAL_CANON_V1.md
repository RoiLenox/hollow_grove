# Platinum Path Cultural Canon V1

Status: additive lore canon beneath the Foundation Freeze  
Stable identity: `path.platinum.v1`  
Common name: **the Platinum Path**  
Ordinary shorthand: **the Plath**

## 1. Shared meaning

The Platinum Path is Hollow Grove's next version of the Golden Rule.

The Golden Rule asks a person to treat another as they would want to be
treated. The Platinum Path asks a person to preserve the other's ability to
remain themselves while helping them reach a lawful, witnessed, and reversible
next state.

> **Carry the consequence without carrying away the person.**

The Path has five shared tests:

1. **Continuity** — What must remain true about this Being?
2. **Consent** — What may this Being choose, refuse, or delay?
3. **Witness** — What evidence can others inspect and replay?
4. **Custody** — Which House or institution has the lawful decision?
5. **Return** — What safe route remains if the answer is no, not yet, or review?

No culture may use its local definition to erase one of these tests.

## 2. Cultural definitions

### Roi and Roselina, the Twofold Witness

**Roi:** “Keep the when, and do not counterfeit the consequence.”  
**Roselina:** “Keep the where and what, and leave the Being room to arrive.”

Together, Roi and Roselina are the Twofold Witness: continuity and embodiment
must agree before a consequence crosses a layer. They are not a formal Hollow
Grove office, guild, governing class, or sovereign. They observe, attest,
clarify, accompany, and warn; they do not replace the responsible House.

If they disagree, the case pauses. The disagreement becomes a recorded
boundary conflict for the responsible House or Court, no Federation transfer
occurs, and the affected Being retains refusal and safe-return rights.

### The Federation

**“Carry what was accepted, exactly as accepted, to the next lawful place.”**

The Federation defines the Path as provenance-preserving continuity. It carries
accepted consequences, refusals, and failed attempts without reopening the
underlying House decision.

### Central Junction

**“Make the shared record useful without making it sovereign.”**

Central Junction turns four House outputs into public evidence, settlement,
publication, and shared Function. It coordinates and publishes; it does not
become a fifth House.

### Stonebend

**“Do not name a Being past the evidence that can stand.”**

Stonebend hears the Path as continuity of form, provenance, Name, Claim, Title,
seal, and lawful boundary. A transformed form may be recognized provisionally,
but uncertainty must remain visible.

### Sandmanor

**“Let relation be chosen, reciprocal, and capable of release.”**

Sandmanor expresses the Path through design, belonging, reciprocity, teaching,
and protection of the vulnerable. No relationship, patronage, or public image
is valid if exit has been removed.

### Glaüshouse

**“Repair the Function without hiding the wound.”**

Glaüshouse makes the Path clinical and practical: consent, compatibility,
care, restoration, and maintained Function. Repair may preserve a life or
system, but it may not falsify its history or conceal its limits.

### Flynt

**“Build the next working state so it can be inspected, refused, and repaired.”**

Flynt treats the Path as deployable persistence. Engineering must leave an
exit, a service record, a responsible operator, and a repair route. A working
system that cannot be questioned is not a Platinum result.

### The Gimp of the Underway

**“Keep the hidden route open until the traveler can choose in daylight.”**

The Gimp's version is endurance, secrecy without captivity, and protection of
return. The Underway may conceal a person from danger, but it may not claim
ownership of the person it protects.

### The Pimp of the Boardwalk

**“Make public life possible without turning visibility into debt.”**

The Pimp's version is patronage, presentation, access, and public recognition.
The Boardwalk may offer a stage, but the performer retains identity, consent,
and the right to leave without retaliation.

### Cleopatra

**“A route is a promise to carry, not proof that the traveler consented.”**

Cleopatra reads the Path through Underway continuity, protected return, and
clear handoff to the affected Being and Stonebend review.

### Hal

**“A public welcome is valid only when the person can decline the spotlight.”**

Hal reads the Path through Boardwalk visibility, patronage, independent
identity, and Flynt public-function review.

### Clouseau and Current Synthesis

**“A clue may preserve a relation without becoming the authority that decides it.”**

The PLEB-PLEB witness preserves grammar, clue-continuity, and Bond evidence for
review. It may not create a Bond, choose a partner, or authorize movement.

### Hueman

**“Ask what happened, look at what remains, listen to every refusal, then choose
what you can carry.”**

Hueman experiences the Path as a practice of judgment. Player support is
meaningful testimony and solidarity, but the responsible Being, House, or
institution still decides.

## 3. The Plath as a public saying

People shorten the Path differently:

- Stonebend: **“Name what stands.”**
- Sandmanor: **“Leave room.”**
- Glaüshouse: **“Repair in the open.”**
- Flynt: **“Ship the exit.”**
- Central Junction: **“Publish the handoff.”**
- Underway: **“Keep the way back.”**
- Boardwalk: **“No spotlight without a door.”**
- Twofold Witness: **“When and where agree.”**
- Hueman: **“Carry, don’t claim.”**

These sayings are not competing laws. They are local faces of one shared Path.

## 4. Failure of the Platinum Path

A person or institution fails the Path when it:

- preserves an outcome by erasing the person;
- treats advice as decision;
- turns visibility into ownership;
- hides uncertainty that affects consent or custody;
- removes the safe return route;
- carries a consequence beyond the authority that accepted it.

Failure does not automatically mean punishment. It means the record pauses,
refuses, returns, or enters review according to the responsible authority.

## 5. Canonical relationship to the kernel

The Platinum Path is cultural interpretation, not a new sovereign layer. The
kernel, Houses, Court, paired witnesses, Federation, and gameplay reducers
retain their existing authority. The Path supplies a shared vocabulary for
why their boundaries matter and how different cultures recognize the same
lawful care.
