# Flynt Marquise Canon V1

Status: **CANONICAL**

This document owns the compact lore contract for The Marquise. The executable
record is `src/world/marquise.rs`; the Flynt constitutional hierarchy and
neutral institutional projection must agree with it.

## Identity and authority

```text
FLYNT
└── THE TROSS
    └── MYSTERY MEN
        └── THE MARQUISE
```

The Marquise are the **maritime extension of the Mystery Men of Flynt**. They
are not a sibling or rival of Mystery Men, an independent military, a generic
navy, or a separate House institution. The Tross is Flynt's sovereign and the
ultimate authority over Mystery Men and The Marquise. The exact command chain
is:

```text
THE TROSS → MYSTERY MEN → THE MARQUISE
```

`The Marquise` has two canonical meanings determined by context:

1. the collective maritime extension; and
2. the leadership office and exact title of the individual who leads it.

No substitute leadership title is inferred.

## Civic identity and progression

The House civic identities established here are:

```text
Wardens = Flynt
Geralds = Stonebend
Sandmen = Sandmanor
```

Warden is Flynt's primary civic identity, not a naval pay grade. A Warden who
enters the maritime Mystery Men tradition becomes a Privateer. Privateer is the
primary working identity within The Marquise and carries **licensed
independence** under sovereign authority. Corsair is the highest ordinarily
attained status.

```text
WARDEN       civic identity
  ↓
PRIVATEER    practitioner / operational identity
  ↓
CORSAIR      apex attained rank
  ↓
THE MARQUISE leadership office
```

The final step is visually and semantically distinct: The Marquise is an
occupied leadership office, not another earned rank. No intermediate maritime
ranks are canonical.

## Corsair

Corsair joins maritime mastery—navigation, vessels, water routes, currents,
command at sea, long-distance operations, and expeditionary judgment—with
computational mastery—computers, networks, remote devices, communications,
autonomous and distributed systems, and system control.

**A Corsair pilots systems.** A vessel is one system; a computer or network may
be another. Corsair is neither merely a senior sailor nor merely a hacker.

## Synthesis and The Push

```text
Merman ↔ Mermaid
```

Merman and Mermaid are complementary members of one maritime synthesis
ecology, not inherently adversarial categories. The Marquise may contain both.

The Marquise practice The Push as a broad Flynt principle expressed through
propulsion, direction, momentum, force, pressure, current, steering,
displacement, navigation, and remote control. It is not reduced to water magic.

**At sea, The Push becomes navigation.**

This is descriptive lore; detailed mechanics remain open.

## Operations

The Marquise operate on lakes, rivers, waterways, harbors, ports, coastlines,
open water, vessels, and maritime infrastructure. Their work includes
navigation, search and rescue, patrol, vessel operation, exploration, salvage,
recovery, transport, escort, maritime security, port operations,
communications, reconnaissance, disaster response, technical operations,
remote systems, and maritime logistics. Their authority supports both formal
public service and irregular independent operations.

## Short canon

**The Marquise are the maritime extension of the Mystery Men of Flynt. They
answer ultimately to The Tross. Flynt Wardens may become Privateers and advance
to Corsair, the highest attained rank. The organization and its leader are both
called The Marquise. Their tradition centers on Merman and Mermaid synthesis
and applies The Push to navigation, maritime operations, technology,
communications, and complex systems.**
