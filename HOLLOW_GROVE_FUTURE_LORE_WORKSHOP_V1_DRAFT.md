# Hollow Grove Future Lore Workshop V1

Date: 2026-10-08

Purpose: develop the remaining setting through people, objects, conflicts,

## What we are completing

The four Houses, ten routes, seasonal Functions, universal Practitioner
grammar, and the amended Flynt synthesis order already have substantial canon.
Deep Pressure already supplies a character-driven cross-region campaign, and
party recruitment already follows its aftermath. This workshop builds on those
stories rather than replacing their people, choices, or recorded endings.

For this milestone, **lore complete** means the existing setting has a coherent
account of daily life, House specialties, character motives, progression,
institutions, costs, and consequences. Every adopted major principle should
have an ordinary use, a difficult choice, a failure, and a way forward. Every
remaining uncertainty should be an explicitly bounded mystery or a named
decision awaiting discussion. This is a completion target, not a claim that
the current draft or the whole game is finished.

Authoring, user adoption, gameplay implementation, and verified inspection are
different states. The twelve functional-lore cases are already authored.
Boardwalk and Current Sea have playable slices; the foundation and survey frame
provide two additional bounded gameplay bindings. Architecture V3 also covers
the ten historical routes through headless backend services, with zero unbound
canonical backend routes. Their ten game presentation integrations remain deferred.
See [Backend House Handoffs V1](docs/BACKEND_HOUSE_HANDOFFS_V1.md) for scoped
consequences, persistence and private execution witnesses.

## A proposed thread for the next stories

**What makes a return last?**

A Being can be discharged, welcomed, Named, employed, or celebrated and still
find that ordinary life does not fit. A shelter needs an entrance, an offer
needs honest terms, a body needs maintenance, and a public promise needs people
who will keep it after the ceremony. The Grove's ascending spiral becomes
visible when a familiar problem returns and people answer it differently.

This thread can follow any supported Deep Pressure aftermath. It introduces
no universal catastrophe, mandatory ending, new route, or replacement campaign.
One proposed starting case is a returning resident whose damaged doorway must
be measured before temporary work is offered. The Boardwalk camps want credit
for helping; the workshop needs material; the resident wants a home they can
use without owing their life to a benefactor.

The same case can connect PrimePunk structure, MachPunk movement, PetPunk
deployment, and CordPunk compatibility. Those relationships are opportunities,
not a requirement that every small repair visit all four Houses.

### Proposed motives and recurring pressure

The Pimp wants a return to be seen: a person who stays invisible may never get
the next opportunity. His generous act is an introduction that actually opens
a door. His temptation is to make the beneficiary's public gratitude part of
the price. His fear is becoming irrelevant when people succeed without him.

The Gimp wants a return to endure: a welcome is hollow if the person has no
safe way home tomorrow. His generous act is keeping a route open after the
crowd leaves. His temptation is to turn sustained protection into sustained
loyalty. His fear is being abandoned when people no longer need sanctuary.

A Trooner should sometimes criticize the Pimp's spectacle; a Gooner should
sometimes criticize the Gimp's demanded loyalty. Give each camp members who
want work, company, craft, recognition, or quiet rather than one shared voice.
Neither leader needs to become the setting's permanent villain or savior.

### Proposed customs for discussion

- **Joining:** a person declares a voluntary association and hears what the
  camp offers and expects. It does not form a constitutional Bond by itself.
- **Work:** every particular job or patronage agreement has disclosed terms.
  A job with the rival camp need not erase prior affiliation.
- **Departure:** a member may leave or suspend association. Already accepted
  finite duties need an honest closeout or handoff; they cannot imprison the
  person in the camp or create undisclosed debt.
- **Reputation:** people remember witnessed help, broken promises, repairs,
  and refusals separately. Departure alone is not a failure of character.
- **Independence:** the Neutral Promenade has ordinary work and social life;
  remaining unaffiliated must be a livable path.
- **Hoe affiliation:** provisionally treat identity and affiliation as a
  person's self-description; use the common Bond only for a separately agreed
  obligation. Whether a formal affiliation record is needed remains a user
  decision.

These customs need adoption before they become universal Boardwalk lore.
The user has now selected a tourist-facing red-light waterfront atmosphere,
citing Amsterdam and *Boardwalk Empire*: fun visitor commerce alongside serious
rival business interests. The [Boardwalk social constitution](FLYNT_BOARDWALK_SOCIAL_CONSTITUTION_V1_DRAFT.md)
owns that adopted direction. These ordinary customs remain proposals; exact
venues, product lines, prices and the first commercial dispute remain open.
The constitutional Return corridor remains a
stable, voluntary public passage under every tonal choice.

## 5. Glaüshouse: composition that makes its relationships visible

Existing Chromacord is a clinical record institution; `chroma_cord` is its
charting application. Its ordered account of presence, performance, perception,
and position already has a protected evidentiary role.

Proposed broader CC definition: **a cord is a maintained, attributable
relationship among components whose interaction produces a function**.
The components may be material, living, or expressive; the applicable care,
consent, and jurisdiction depend on which are involved. Chroma makes some
qualities of the relationship legible through proportion, color, resonance,
optics, acoustics, or interface. It does not make every colored object a cord.

Harmony would mean the relationship supports its declared use within observed
limits. Dissonance would mean mismatched timing, load, need, interpretation,
or compatibility. Either needs observations and context; a hue alone cannot
judge a person or authorize an intervention. Composition can be revised or
released, and disagreement must not be treated as an illness.

Proposed scene: a recovery aid is beautifully fitted and mechanically proven,
but its wearer tires sooner or cannot use the release comfortably. The chart
holds their testimony beside the measurements. A revised interface may work;
refusal may remain the right outcome. The wearer receives care either way.
This supplies an ordinary CordPunk use and a failure without turning the
software into consent, Clearance, or Synthesis authority.

## 9. How discussion becomes completed lore

Work through one family at a time. For each, settle the premise, ordinary
scene, opposed wants, boundary, failure, and lasting outcome. Record user
decisions here, then amend the owning source document with an explicit adoption
record. Keep historical and machine compatibility names attributable.

Review each adopted section against the Compromise, Synthesis Canon, House
constitutions, geographical corrections, and prior campaigns. Keep one owning
definition for each term. Link summaries to it rather than introducing rival
versions. Remove redundant explanations when they add no different use.

Only then translate the agreed lore into gameplay work. That pass binds actual
actors, evidence, authority, choices, event records, presentation, and replay.
No new Recipe IDs, actor identities, offices, archive fields, or kernel meaning
are created by this document.

The verified `lore-sync-2026-10-08-v3` archive remains a preserved earlier
milestone. This workshop is later source work and is not included in that kit.
After the adopted lore milestone is committed, prepare a new matching source,
release, state, and handoff checkpoint before transferring the new work.
