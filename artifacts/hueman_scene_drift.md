# Hueman Scene Drift

## Structural Rule

Scene intent can drift into a different scene type when bias pressure persists over time without a full system resolving it.

## Drift Vectors

### Seam Market

- drifts toward Pressure Shelter when exchange slows and stored continuity takes over
- drifts toward Threshold Weather when structures fail and exposure outruns arrangement

### Threshold Weather

- drifts toward Split Trace when warning persists without settlement
- drifts toward Seam Market when repeated crossings stabilize the edge into exchange

### Pressure Shelter

- drifts toward Seam Market when guarded stores reopen into circulation
- drifts toward Split Trace when shelter empties and only residue remains

### Split Trace

- drifts toward Threshold Weather when ambiguity spills outward into exposure
- drifts toward Pressure Shelter when traces are hoarded, muffled, or enclosed

## Drift Drivers

- sustained `current` accumulation pulls scenes toward storage, continuity, and reopened exchange
- sustained `aura` accumulation pulls scenes toward exposure, drift, shimmer, and unstable edges
- mixed unresolved pressure preserves Split Trace longer instead of forcing a clean resolution
- repeated crossings can stabilize a scene back into exchange even after warning or ambiguity
- a preserved HAL/Cleo relay packet slows drift by holding one shared confirmation point across surface and underground layers
