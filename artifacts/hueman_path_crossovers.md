# Hueman Path Crossovers

## Structural Rule

Different kingdom-facing approaches may enter AuraTriad differently while still crossing through shared regions and shared world pressure.

## Shared Entry Crossovers

- Glaushouse and Sandmanor cross immediately at Aura Beach.
- Flynt and Stonebend do not share first entry, but they both begin inland before reaching the coast.

## Interior Crossovers

- Flynt and Sandmanor cross at Aura Basin.
- Stonebend and Glaushouse cross at Aura Field.
- Stonebend and Sandmanor cross at Aura Basin after different openings.

## Aura Ridge Trade Legs

- Aura Ridge / Witness is the elevated public route between restored Glaushouse life and the Stonebend-facing Central Junction district.
- Central Junction contains the South Ridge Exchange, Junction Board, Clearing House, and Junction Wire; it is not merely a stock exchange.
- the Craft Corridor leads toward Stonebend, the Repair Corridor toward Glaushouse, the Design Corridor toward Sandmanor, and Flynt powers the district through its Engineering Ring and infrastructure network.
- Sandmanor designs Form, Flynt engineers Function, Stonebend makes Form endure, and Glaushouse keeps Function alive.
- one unnamed standard currency measures ordinary exchange; the four public indexes are noncurrency measurements, Toke/Tokens remain earned evidence, and Gremlincoin remains the Gremlin Way.
- House Sector Halls verify professional facts without setting prices; the Junction Board governs shared market standards, the Clearing House settles recognized contracts, and the Junction Wire publishes results.
- Current Haze remains unresolved possibility, Equal Gaze remains reconciled perspective, and Aura Beam remains revelation or transmission of a visible shared future rather than a market ticker.
- Equal Gaze, ceremony, tourism, trade, and public reintegration remain visible along the ridge.
- Aura Ridge East remains a local screen-map segment toward Sandmanor rather than another major constitutional route.
- Current Sea / Certify is the separate northern ordeal and may not be collapsed into Aura Ridge.
- the ridge remains part of the same large circular map body rather than a universal holding route.

## Confirmed Route Law

- routes are constitutional verbs rather than mere connections.
- straight routes remain process geometry and curved routes remain transformation geometry.
- Flynt <-> Glaushouse uses Boardwalk / Return and Riptide / Retrieve.
- Glaushouse <-> Stonebend keeps Current Sea / Certify separate from Aura Ridge / Witness.
- Glaushouse <-> Sandmanor uses Glausbahn / Refine and Current Seanad / Deliberate.
- Stonebend <-> Sandmanor uses Aura Way / Design and Mt. Aura / Aspire.
- Stonebend <-> Flynt uses Basin Motor Speedway / Produce and Stairway to Heaven / Ascend.

## Meaning

- crossover means the world can feel shared without erasing start identity
- shared regions carry different descriptive pressure depending on entry order
- the coast is the earliest common threshold for the western and southern starts
- inland turns remain the main crossover pressure for the northern and eastern starts
- the visible map stays circular, so straight ridge legs and rounded turns feed back into the same overall loop
- the relay packet gives one explicit proof point that a crossover may hold more than one function at once without losing route identity
