# Hueman Path Crossovers

## Structural Rule

Different starts may enter AuraTriad differently while still crossing through shared regions and shared world pressure.

## Shared Entry Crossovers

- Glaushouse and Sandmanor cross immediately at Aura Beach.
- Flynt and Stonebend do not share first entry, but they both begin inland before reaching the coast.

## Interior Crossovers

- Flynt and Sandmanor cross at Aura Basin.
- Stonebend and Glaushouse cross at Aura Fields.
- Stonebend and Sandmanor cross at Aura Basin after different openings.

## Full-Triad Convergence

- all four starts eventually touch Aura Basin
- all four starts eventually touch Aura Fields
- all four starts eventually touch Aura Beach
- the difference is order, not exclusion

## Meaning

- crossover means the world can feel shared without erasing start identity
- shared regions carry different descriptive pressure depending on entry order
- the coast is the earliest common threshold for the western and southern starts
- inland turns remain the main crossover pressure for the northern and eastern starts

## Status

- crossovers are descriptive-only for now
- no meeting mechanics or shared events are active
- start-path order remains unchanged
- archetype lens remains interpretive above the crossover map
- no feedback into Current Synthesis
- no feedback into Hollow Grove

## Boundary Reminder

Path crossovers declare where starts can meaningfully overlap in AuraTriad. They do not create procedural encounters or alter lower-layer routing.
