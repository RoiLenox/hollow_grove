## Adopted common origin

Glaüshouse is the source House of all Synthesis, from an item to a whole Form.

The user defined:

> Well I would like synthesizing all things to come from Galushouse synthesis in a way from small to big like an item to your whole form

Synthesis is one connected practice across scales. It may establish a result in
an external Object or in a living Being. The Being/Object distinction remains:
an item's Synthesis is recorded on that item's identity and history; a bodily
Synthesis is recorded on its actual host and Form. Making an item changes no
practitioner Form, Sympiote grade, office or affiliation by implication.

Glaüshouse owns the Synthesis origin and integration framework. Stonebend
retains material identity, provenance, Name and applicable boundary law;
Sandmanor retains design, method and Prove; Flynt retains Capacity, its practices
and Recognize. Their contributions can enter the same Synthesis method without
turning any House into the sole owner of the result or overriding another act.

## WORKING scale descriptions

| Scope | Target/result to describe | Separate requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Item or construct | a synthesized item, component, tool or equipment | object identity, inputs, method, actual changed qualities and upkeep |
| Interface or graft | an attachment or integrated living component | distinguish the object, host, connection, compatibility and actual result |
| Partial bodily change | adjustment, reconstruction or changed capacity | exact affected system, continuing person, applicable consent and care |
| Whole Form | a supported Synthesis embodiment | host continuity, technical/lived viability, establishment and Continuance |

These are descriptions of scope, not four mandatory stages or fixed ranks.
A small target can have high risk; size does not establish lawful authority,
complexity, price, grade or success. Exact small-item methods, material rules,
upkeep, supported interfaces and whole-Form methods remain to be decided.

The shared method should identify the target, inputs, Recipe/method, intended
change, applicable responsibilities, compatibility, stopping conditions,
observed result and continuing upkeep. Living procedures retain the existing
clinical consent, Clearance, recovery and Continuance requirements. Object work
retains its own material, identity, custody and proof requirements. Whether
item Synthesis can use nonliving materials or always needs living Sympiote
material remains open. Local execution, teaching and delegation policy is also
open; source House does not by itself settle the physical work site.

## Grades and branches within the wider practice

Sympiote Grade I → Grade II → Grade III describes the adopted living foundation
before distinct supported branches. It does not mean item → graft → whole body
or grade every synthesized object. Exact grade meanings remain open. Gargoyle,
Werewolf and Mariner/Merman remain separate examples; Gargoyle and Werewolf
never combine. The older Manticorp body model still needs revision.

## Existing execution and future work

Ordinary repair and crafting retain their actual classifications and histories.
The existing survey-frame repair case is not retroactively rewritten as item
Synthesis. The existing Sympiote episode remains an ungraded clinical case.
Earlier powers, Recipes and constitution records retain compatibility meaning;
this common origin does not make every use of a power a new Synthesis act.

Synthesis engine. The broad source relationship is adopted; an executable
common record/dispatch model, item methods and migrations remain future work.
Keep subject kind, actual scope, adopted specification and verified execution
separate. No new item, graft, grade, Form or successful case is created here.
