## Adopted Synbalm, Synblocks and Sympiote Form

Adopted Synthesis material and scales, 2026-10-08: Synbalm is the squishy magical healing substance; Synblocks are the small living building units; Sympiote Form is the larger bodily expression.

Then adopted the current unit name:

> Oh and can you call them synblocks and that should do it

**Synbalm** is Glaüshouse's squishy magical substance and named material
product. It has healing uses and supplies the material worked into Synblocks.
**Synblocks** are small living building units organized from Synbalm.
**Sympiote Form** is the larger bodily expression established with a host.

| Term | What it names | Use at this scale |
|---|---|---|
| Synbalm | the squishy magical healing substance/product | healing preparation and material for making Synblocks |
| Synblock / Synblocks | a small living building unit / multiple units | small things, components and supported integrations |
| Sympiote Form | the larger bodily expression | host-specific integration and foundation development |

Synbalm names the substance; a Synblock names an organized unit of it. The
earlier Synbiote block/Synbiote blocks wording is a historical naming alias.
Use Synblock/Synblocks in active definitions. Sympiote remains the established
bodily and host-specific graft name.

The material, unit and bodily Form have separate identities and records.

Healing use may support or restore an existing body. Establishing a new
Synblock or Sympiote Form concerns its actual Synthesis target. Disclose
treatment and transformation separately when a procedure does both. Applying
or buying Synbalm does not itself establish a bodily Form or Sympiote grade.
Exact healing effects, preparation, application, potency, storage, renewal and
block-making Recipes remain open. Synbalm has no implemented healing effect
or inventory product, adopted vendor, package, price or commerce contract yet.

The Stonebend comparison is a House-material parallel. Synbalm does not rename
Stonebend's Hollow, material Current or Aether; their existing meanings and
provenance remain intact. No universal Current-to-Synbalm conversion is fixed.

This gives the practice a material scale beneath the body stage: small living
units, things made with them, and bodily integration. An item made with
Synblocks remains its actual Object; producing or using it does not
establish its maker's Sympiote Form. Grades I, II and III belong to development
of the Sympiote Form, rather than to block size, quantity or an item's quality.

Block means a building unit. Exact shape, size, tissue composition, cultivation,
joining, compatibility and upkeep remain open. No universal block count or
automatic block-to-body transition is specified. The existing clinical
Sympiote graft record keeps its name and host-specific meaning; it is not
retroactively split into a newly invented inventory of blocks.

The Stonebend comparison concerns the smallest material scale. The earlier
Lightening/inverse-Hollowing interpretation is superseded as the explanation
of this request. No material conversion direction or Lightening process name
is adopted. Stonebend's material Current records remain separate from
unqualified practitioner Current. Whether every Synthesis needs a living
block, and whether nonliving materials can be synthesized directly, remain
open questions beyond the adopted small-unit and body distinction.

## WORKING scale descriptions

| Scope | Target/result to describe | Separate requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Item or construct | a small thing or component made with Synblocks, or another supported synthesized object | object identity, inputs, method, actual changed qualities and upkeep |
| Interface or graft | an attachment or integrated living component | distinguish the object, host, connection, compatibility and actual result |
| Partial bodily change | adjustment, reconstruction or changed capacity | exact affected system, continuing person, applicable consent and care |
| Whole Form | a supported Synthesis embodiment | host continuity, technical/lived viability, establishment and Continuance |

These are descriptions of scope, not four mandatory stages or fixed ranks.
A small target can have high risk; size does not establish lawful authority,
complexity, price, grade or success. Exact small-item methods, material rules,
upkeep, supported interfaces and whole-Form methods remain to be decided.

The shared method should identify the target, inputs, Recipe/method, intended
change, applicable responsibilities, compatibility, stopping conditions,
observed result and continuing upkeep. Living procedures retain the existing
clinical consent, Clearance, recovery and Continuance requirements. Object work
retains its own material, identity, custody and proof requirements. Whether
item Synthesis can use nonliving materials directly or always needs Synbalm
and living Synblocks remains open. Local execution, teaching and delegation policy is also
open; source House does not by itself settle the physical work site.
