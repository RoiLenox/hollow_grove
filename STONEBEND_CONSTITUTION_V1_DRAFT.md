# Stonebend Constitution V1 Draft

Status: Draft pending ratification

Authority: Normative where this document restates existing canonical law; proposed where it orders that law into a constitutional instrument

Date: 2026-07-18

## 1. Constitutional Status

This document is the proposed House constitution for Stonebend.

It consolidates existing Stonebend law. It does not redesign Stonebend, create a new succession system, or turn cultural description into an executable mechanic without an existing constitutional basis.

Upon ratification, this document SHALL govern Stonebend-specific authority. The Hollow Grove V2 Constitutional Specification SHALL continue to govern the common Bond lifecycle, signed Current, signed Aura, Tombstone, Toke, persistence, replay, and Synthesis Resolution.

Where this draft labels a matter Reserved, the repository does not yet contain enough constitutional law to choose one deterministic rule. Reserved matters MUST NOT be implemented by assumption.

The canonical spelling is Stonebend.

## 2. Constitutional Identity

Stonebend is the first immutable House position in the Four-House grammar.

Stonebend carries:

- Name It;
- Craft;
- Claim;
- establishment;
- holding;
- embodiment;
- stabilization;
- pressure reallocation;
- durable form.

Its material statement is:

> Diamond claims.

Its inherited constitutional profile is:

- action: Name It;
- logic: positive;
- expression: Natural;
- operating-system domain: Craft;
- color: blue;
- House rock: Diamond;
- Aura leaning: Reflective.

Positive logic is a House grammar term. It is not Positive Current or Positive Aura.

Its constitutional question is:

> What is this, whose responsibility is it, what must it bear, and in what form may that claim stand?

Stonebend is House 1. Its grammar recurs at Rule-of-Twelve positions 1, 5, and 9. Position 1 is its primary pole.

Geographic position, screen direction, color, mineral, expression, and operating-system analogy are projections of Stonebend identity. None independently grants House authority.

## 3. Purpose

Stonebend exists because no constitutional process can act upon an unnamed claim with unspecified title, purpose, burden, ownership, or responsibility.

Stonebend establishes the form in which a thing can be held accountable.

Stonebend makes the claim visible enough to be tested by Sandmanor, cleared by Glaüshouse, and recognized by Flynt.

Stonebend cannot guarantee that a claim is true, safe, complete, or recognized. It guarantees that the claim is explicit, attributable, embodied, and burden-bearing.

## 4. Responsibilities

Stonebend SHALL:

- name the subject of a constitutional claim;
- establish explicit title;
- establish constitutional identity without confusing identity with display name;
- state purpose;
- state responsibility;
- state ownership and custody where applicable;
- identify the burden or load attached to a claim;
- identify who bears that burden;
- identify the structure expected to carry it;
- determine where pressure, load, burden, or authority must be allocated;
- preserve the difference between Title, Labor, and Craft;
- expose title to labor witness;
- expose named structure to craft inspection;
- preserve public challenge to illegitimate Title;
- govern deliberate Hollowing under lawful ownership, custody, evidence, and conservation;
- preserve both Hollow and Hollowed outputs;
- support durable construction, repair scaffolding, vessels, tools, roads, channels, and pressure systems;
- support deterministic replay of title, burden, craft, and reallocation decisions;
- participate in Bonds under the common Bond constitution.

## 5. Non-responsibilities

Stonebend SHALL NOT:

- prove its own claim merely by naming it;
- grant Sandmanor proof;
- grant Glaüshouse Clearance;
- perform final Synthesis;
- grant Flynt recognition;
- treat Title as immunity from challenge;
- treat labor as a resource without witness or consequence;
- treat craft appearance as structural adequacy;
- destroy pressure;
- destroy Current;
- use Hollowing as a synonym for Synthesis;
- remove an interior without accounting for the Hollow and Hollowed results;
- infer Positive or Negative Current from Stonebend membership;
- infer Positive or Negative Aura from Reflective Aura;
- infer legitimacy from Diamond, blue color, geography, rank, species, or presentation;
- turn the Stonebender into an automatic succession resolver;
- activate a House office through an undocumented contest result;
- bypass the Bond lifecycle.

## 6. Constitutional Questions

Every Stonebend act MUST answer the applicable questions:

- What is being named?
- Who makes the claim?
- Under which authority?
- What title is established?
- What purpose does the title serve?
- Who owns the subject?
- Who holds custody?
- What burden accompanies the claim?
- Who actually bears the burden?
- What structure carries the burden?
- Can life or ordinary use exist within the claim?
- Can the named form survive craft inspection?
- Where must pressure be reallocated?
- What evidence supports title, labor, craft, and reallocation?
- Is the claim standing, challenged, or superseded by a later lawful claim?
- Can replay reach the same result?

## 7. Domain Model

### 7.1 House identity

Stonebend House identity is immutable.

The canonical House value is the existing `House::Stonebend` identity.

A Stonebend institution, office, role, participant, site, or object MUST reference Stonebend through a stable typed identity. A display label is not sufficient authority.

### 7.2 Governing institution

The current canonical institution is:

- `institution.stonebend.constitution` — Stonebend constitutional body.

Its existing domains are:

- title;
- labor;
- craft;
- construction;
- Hollowing.

The institution is the jurisdictional body. It is not the House itself.

### 7.3 Constitutional powers

Stonebend contains three constitutional powers:

- Title;
- Labor;
- Craft.

These powers are represented by:

- Hypergiant — Title;
- Proletariat — Labor;
- Freemason — Craft.

No power may constitutionally absorb the other two.

### 7.4 Public office

Its existing authority vocabulary is:

- PublicTitle;
- ConstitutionalIdentity;

The office holder is the public bearer of Title. The office holder is not sovereignly exempt from Labor or Craft challenge.

The office and the person holding it are distinct identities.

The canonical role and group are:

### 7.6 Craft body

### 7.7 Geralds

Geralds are Stonebend's civic circulation people.

They carry Current, fuel, material, tools, labor, and recovery loads. They preserve physical continuity across the Grove.

Gerald circulation supplies Title, Labor, and Craft. It does not replace them.

Geralds are not generic couriers and do not acquire title merely by carrying a titled object.

### 7.8 Site

The canonical headquarters and public proving ground is:

- `site.stonebend.stonebender` — The Stonebender.

Its existing zones are:

- standing rings;
- ritual lanes;
- burden relay.

The Stonebender supplies public witness and pressure testing. It does not, by physical contest alone, mutate office ownership or settle every constitutional challenge.

### 7.9 Material domain

Stonebend's House rock is Diamond.

Diamond contributes Structure, color, and cultural design. It does not replace Current, Hollow, Aura, evidence, or authority.

The existing material expression is:

- Hollow Current plus Reflective Aura produces Mercury Mirror under an applicable lawful Recipe;
- Mercurite is the accepted hard structural branch.

These statements do not authorize automatic conversion and do not make Hollow Current a signed Negative Current.

### 7.10 Biological and civic domain

Stonebend's primary biological logic includes bone, marrow, blood production, vessels, durable support, structural integrity, pressure-bearing tissue, and repair scaffolding.

This is a primary constitutional analogy and world domain. It is not species exclusivity and does not give Stonebend exclusive control over every bone, tool, or building.

### 7.11 Ownership, lifetime, and persistence

House identity is perpetual and immutable.

Institutions and offices persist according to institutional law.

Office tenure, individual membership, title claims, custody, and burden assignments are historical relationships and MUST be separately recorded.

A title declaration becomes immutable history when committed. Later challenge or supersession creates later history; it does not rewrite the original declaration.

## 8. Title, Labor, and Craft Balance

### 8.1 Hypergiant

Hypergiant answers:

> What are we building or preserving?

Hypergiant SHALL carry public Title, public naming, purpose, and outward negotiation.

Hypergiant SHALL remain answerable to the Proletariat and Freemason.

Hypergiant MUST NOT convert public visibility into absolute authority.

### 8.2 Proletariat

Proletariat answers:

> Can life actually exist inside this claim?

Proletariat SHALL provide labor witness, burden evidence, productive consequence, and challenge from lived work.

Proletariat MUST NOT be reduced to an unsigned labor total or assumed consent.

### 8.3 Freemason

Freemason answers:

> How must it be built, and can the named structure bear what it claims?

Freemason SHALL inspect material form, load path, waste path, recovery path, tools, and maintainability.

Nothing is structurally complete merely because its title is legitimate.

### 8.4 Balance law

Hypergiant, Proletariat, and Freemason constitute a balance, not a rank ladder.

The Hypergiant is the public face of Title and MUST remain challengeable.

The Proletariat and Freemason may each challenge Title alone or together when legitimacy fails.

No single role may collapse Stonebend into solo rule.

Existing Stonebend law permits either the Proletariat or the Freemason to take the Crown and become Hypergiant through a lawful succession consequence. Role membership alone does not perform that accession.

The Hypergiant office is constitutionally required to have a holder. A vacancy is a constitutional continuity failure requiring lawful succession; it is not permission to fabricate a holder.

The exact deterministic succession method following a successful challenge is Reserved. Existing descriptive text permits Crown transfer but the repository has no constitutional resolver comparable to Flynt's accession model.

## 9. Claim and Title Lifecycle and State Machine

A Stonebend claim has the following constitutional statuses:

- Undeclared;
- Declared;
- Standing;
- Challenged;
- ReallocationRequired;
- Superseded;
- Historical.

Legal transitions are:

- Undeclared to Declared through an attributable naming act;
- Declared to Standing when required title, purpose, burden, ownership, labor, craft, authority, and evidence conditions are present;
- Standing to Challenged through a competent Proletariat, Freemason, institutional, participant, or Bond challenge;
- Challenged to Standing when the claim is upheld without rewriting evidence;
- Challenged to ReallocationRequired when burden, authority, ownership, or structure can no longer lawfully remain where declared;
- ReallocationRequired to Superseded when a later lawful claim and allocation replaces reliance on the earlier claim;
- Standing or Superseded to Historical when the governed term or subject ends.

Declared does not imply Standing.

Standing does not imply Sandmanor proof, Glaüshouse Clearance, Flynt recognition, Bond maturity, or Tombstone eligibility.

Illegal transitions include:

- Undeclared directly to Standing;
- Challenged directly to Historical to avoid judgment;
- ReallocationRequired directly to Standing without an explicit allocation record;
- Superseded back to the same Standing claim by deleting the superseding history;
- any transition produced only by display order, force, or office title.

## 10. Pressure Reallocation

Stonebend reallocates pressure. It does not destroy it.

Every reallocation MUST identify:

- the pressure, burden, load, title, authority, or responsibility being moved;
- its prior bearer;
- its successor bearer or lawful destination;
- its magnitude or defined scope;
- authority;
- cause;
- evidence;
- causal order;
- any remaining unresolved burden.

Reallocation MUST conserve signed Current when Current moves.

Reallocation of title does not automatically transfer ownership, custody, obligation, office, Current, or recognition. Each affected interest requires its own lawful record.

An implementation MUST NOT encode reallocation as deletion from one participant followed by unexplained appearance at another.

## 11. Hollowing

Hollowing extracts usable interior resources while leaving an outer form.

The constitutional result is:

> Whole becomes Hollow plus Hollowed.

Every Hollowing act MUST identify:

- the Whole;
- lawful owner and custodian;
- authority and permission to extract;
- extraction method or Recipe;
- extracted Hollow;
- remaining Hollowed form;
- waste, loss, contamination, and recovery paths;
- evidence;
- resulting ownership and custody.

Hollowing MUST conserve material and Current under the units defined by its governing Recipe.

Hollowing MUST NOT:

- mean an empty chamber;
- mean placing Current in a container;
- mean generic refinement;
- mean Synthesis;
- cross the Being/Object boundary without Glaüshouse Synthesis authority and Recipe legality;
- erase the Hollowed remainder;
- fabricate value not present in the Whole or introduced by a lawful external source.

## 12. Stonebender Proceedings

The Stonebender is a public pressure test of Title, Labor, and Craft.

A Stonebender proceeding MAY provide evidence of:

- public Title under pressure;
- labor capacity and crowd will;
- structural discipline;
- burden transfer;
- opposition;
- renewed legitimacy;
- succession pressure.

A proceeding MUST identify participants, roles, rules, term, witnesses, evidence, injuries or material consequences, and result.

A proceeding MUST NOT automatically:

- create or remove the Hypergiant office holder;
- settle unrelated ownership;
- prove a Bond;
- grant Clearance;
- grant recognition;
- create Current;

The succession consequence of a proceeding remains Reserved until a ratified Stonebend accession rule exists.

## 13. Relationship to Current and Aura

Stonebend House identity does not determine signed Current or signed Aura.

Hollow Current is a Current state, not Negative Current.

Reflective Aura is an Aura state, not Positive Aura.

A Stonebend Bond may lawfully occupy any of the four constitutional polarity states:

- Positive Current with Positive Aura;
- Positive Current with Negative Aura;
- Negative Current with Positive Aura;
- Negative Current with Negative Aura.

Title may conceal burden. Burden may be truthfully revealed. Productive craft may be denied recognition. Illegitimate structure may be publicly celebrated. The signed histories MUST preserve those distinctions.

## 14. Relationship to Bond

A Stonebend-governed Bond uses Stonebend law to name the relationship, participants, roles, title, purpose, ownership, custody, obligations, permissions, burden, and expected structure.

It MUST still complete every applicable common Bond stage.

Stonebend naming is not Bond validation.

Stonebend title is not maturity.

Stonebend craft inspection is evidence and MAY satisfy House-specific requirements. It is not automatically a Tombstone.

Glaüshouse retains exclusive final Synthesis authority.

Flynt recognition, where required, remains a later constitutional function and does not retroactively create the named claim.

## 15. Challenges and Appeals

A challenge MUST identify:

- challenged claim or title;
- challenger identity and standing;
- challenged scope;
- evidence;
- alleged failure of title, labor, craft, allocation, or authority;
- requested consequence.

Challenge suspends only the reliance authorized by governing law. It does not delete the claim.

An appeal MUST review the decision and evidence. It MUST NOT replace the original claim with a more convenient version.

Proletariat and Freemason challenge rights are constitutional checks. They are not proof that every challenge succeeds.

The reviewing authority, deadlines, quorum, and office-succession consequence are Reserved pending ratification of a specific Stonebend procedure.

## 18. Failure Modes and Recovery

### 18.1 False title

The title becomes Challenged. Existing history remains. Reliance may be suspended. Lawful reallocation or supersession follows only through recorded authority.

### 18.2 Unlivable claim

Labor witness records the actual consequence. A visible structure or public title does not defeat that evidence.

### 18.3 Craft failure

The named form remains historically named but cannot claim structural adequacy. Repair, redesign, reallocation, or termination proceeds through a later lawful act.

### 18.4 Burden imbalance

The affected act fails or becomes ReallocationRequired. Saturating arithmetic, silent write-off, and involuntary unexplained transfer are forbidden.

### 18.5 Hollowing interruption

Only committed extraction results are authoritative. Partial physical consequences remain evidence and must be accounted; software rollback does not restore consumed material.

### 18.6 Office vacancy or disputed Crown

The repository currently contains no ratified Stonebend succession resolver. The institution records vacancy or dispute and blocks authority requiring an office holder. It MUST NOT choose a holder by insertion order, combat score, or developer convenience.

### 18.7 Corruption

Recover from authoritative records and deterministic replay. Do not invent a replacement title, allocation, or Hollowing output.
