# Stonebend Constitution V1 Draft — Superseded

Status: historical redirect; not constitutional authority

This draft was superseded by [The Stonebend Constitution V2](STONEBEND_CONSTITUTION_V2.md). It must not be used to infer authority, succession, office, terminology, or Hollowing law.

In particular, the former peer-role model and its obsolete labor-role terminology are not canonical. The constitutional people are the **Geralds**; the **Hypergiant** is the singular highest Stonebend office; the **Proliteriate** is the civic chamber; and the singular **High Freemason** leads the wider **Freemason** execution institution.
