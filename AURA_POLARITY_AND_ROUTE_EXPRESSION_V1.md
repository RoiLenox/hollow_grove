# Aura Polarity And Route Expression V1

This document records the locked Aura polarity layer implemented over the existing Flow/Glow grammar, Being/Object ontology, manager-language addressing, and frozen V1.1 execution path.

It does not create a new Aura substance, a new Frame lineage, or a new execution topology.

## Purpose

Aura polarity answers how shared `Glow` is oriented:

- toward legibility, diagnosis, explanation, and preserved choice
- or toward pressure, manipulation, misdirection, and outcome control

Canonical route lock:

- `Glaüshouse -> Sandmanor = Light Aura`
- `Glaüshouse -> Flynt = Dark Aura`

Canonical distinction:

- `Light Aura increases legibility and agency.`
- `Dark Aura increases control and pressure.`

Neither polarity is morally automatic.

## Shared Foundation

Both Light and Dark Aura use the same underlying `Glow` domain.

They remain separate from:

- `Flow`
- `CurrentPrism`
- `Current Capacity`
- `Aura Capacity`
- `Proxy / Moxy / Foxy`
- route geometry
- `Gesture`
- `ActionMode`
- `Aim`

Every Aura Frame may express both Light and Dark Aura.

The lineage remains unified:

- `Pixy -> Sprite -> Faerie -> Nymph -> Siren -> Muse`

## Light Aura

Light Aura orients Glow toward:

- clarification
- diagnosis
- truthful revelation
- education
- measurement
- communication
- translation
- consent
- shared understanding
- legibility
- preserved or expanded agency

Mode emphasis:

- `Light Seam` clarifies and repairs truthful boundaries and relations.
- `Light Beam` directs attention toward relevant truth, diagnosis, evidence, or choice.
- `Light Gleam` reveals authentic condition, earned recognition, and honest presence.

## Dark Aura

Dark Aura orients Glow toward:

- manipulation
- seduction
- fear
- compulsion
- misdirection
- obsession
- concealment
- propaganda
- psychic pressure
- emotional weaponization
- controlled spectacle
- outcome control

Mode emphasis:

- `Dark Seam` manipulates or counter-manipulates bonds, dependency, loyalty, obsession, or hostile psychic ties.
- `Dark Beam` captures, narrows, distorts, redirects, conceals, or weaponizes attention.
- `Dark Gleam` creates seductive, intimidating, prestigious, fascinating, terrifying, or falsely inevitable presence.

Dark technique is not automatically evil.

Consensual performance, defensive misdirection, counter-binding, and threat deterrence remain representable.

## Route Tendencies

Canonical routes remain unchanged:

- `Glausbahn`
- `Current Seanad`
- `Boardwalk`
- `Riptide`

Directional tendencies:

- `Glausbahn = direct legibility and structured Light transmission`
- `Current Seanad = contextual clarification, negotiation, and deliberative Light interpretation`
- `Boardwalk = managed public performance and controlled Dark spectacle`
- `Riptide = immersive undertow, seduction, disorientation, and Dark pressure`

Route geometry remains distinct from polarity:

- straight/curved is not the same question as Light/Dark
- `Foxy != Dark`
- `Moxy != Light`

## Evaluation

The implemented evaluator inspects:

- requested polarity
- inferred polarity
- source and destination House
- route tendency
- `Gesture`
- `Mode`
- `Object`
- `AddressingMode`
- `Aim`
- truthfulness
- consent
- agency effect
- proportionality
- reversibility
- consequence

Requested polarity is not trusted blindly.

Examples:

- a request labeled `Light` that conceals alternatives and overrides agency is inferred as `Dark`
- a `Dark` deterrence action may remain proportional and defensible
- mixed clinical disclosure remains representable as `Mixed`

## Glitz And Glamour

Existing terminology remains in force:

- `Glitz` tends toward Light Gleam
- `Glamour` tends toward Dark Gleam

Neither is morally absolute.

Canonical distinction:

- `Glitz invites recognition.`
- `Glamour shapes what recognition means.`
