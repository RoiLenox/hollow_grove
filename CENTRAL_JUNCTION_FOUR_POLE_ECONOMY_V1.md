# Central Junction and the Four-Pole Economy V1

Status: canonical additive constitutional law

This document governs Aura Ridge's shared public economy, Central Junction,
the South Ridge Exchange, the four productive poles, public indexes, listings,
lawful event contracts, and settlement. It does not alter combat, movement,
transformation, party, rendering, House sovereignty, or the recursion kernel.

## I. The Four Productive Poles

Hollow Grove has four productive poles:

| House | Economic function | Four-Pole position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sandmanor | Interior and exterior design | Creation of Form |
| Flynt | Engineering | Creation of Function |
| Stonebend | Craft | Continuance of Form |
| Glaüshouse | Repair | Continuance of Function |

> **Sandmanor designs the Form.**

> **Flynt engineers the Function.**

> **Stonebend makes the Form endure.**

> **Glaüshouse keeps the Function alive.**

The productive cycle is:

```text
Sandmanor designs what should exist
→ Flynt engineers how it can function
→ Stonebend crafts it into durable material Form
→ Glaüshouse repairs and renews its Function
→ the restored object, body, structure, or system returns to use
```

The poles classify principal contribution; they are not absolute monopolies.
A project may use several poles while retaining one evidence-supported primary
classification.

## III. Work Classification

The surface decision matrix is:

| | Urgent | Not urgent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Important | Act | Cultivate |
| Not important | Reroute | Release |

- **Act** — important and urgent work requiring direct attention now.
- **Cultivate** — important but nonurgent work to develop, fund, schedule,
  teach, practice, or allow to mature.
- **Reroute** — urgent work belonging with another person, office, House,
  institution, or pole.
- **Release** — work neither sufficiently urgent nor important and which
  should cease consuming Current, Aura, labor, attention, or institutional
  resources.

> **Reroute what still has somewhere to go.**

> **Release what has reached its end.**

Work is also classified as Form or Function and Creation or Continuance:

| | Creation | Continuance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form | Sandmanor | Stonebend |
| Function | Flynt | Glaüshouse |

> **Urgency determines when work must move. Importance determines why it matters. Form and Function determine what is being changed. Creation and Continuance determine whether it must be brought into being or kept alive.**

## VII. Market Institutions

The practical authority chain is:

```text
House Sector Hall
→ Junction Board
→ Clearing House
→ Junction Wire
```

The **Junction Board** is the neutral public market authority. It governs
listing standards, index membership and methodology, disclosure, professional
evidence audits, conflicts of interest, investigations, disputed
classifications and House assessments, and shared public rules. Houses submit
domain evidence; the Board determines whether it meets the public standard.
The Board does not execute trades or payouts.

The **Clearing House** performs completed-trade, share-transfer, project,
event-contract, payout, void, authorized reversal, and final-balance
settlement. It compares recognized evidence with outcome conditions written
before trading. It does not invent the outcome.

The **Junction Wire** is the public financial and business-information
network. It publishes indexes, prices, listings, disclosures, project updates,
employment demand, warnings, recognized failures, settled event outcomes, and
public notices. It is the Junction's ticker and business newswire.

## VIII. Summit and Future-Vision Terms

**Current Haze is unresolved possibility.** It is the atmosphere in which
possible futures remain obscured or indistinct. It is not an office,
settlement state type, audit authority, trading institution, or network.
Someone may say “The contract is still in the Current Haze” to describe an
unresolved outcome; no authority is created.

**Equal Gaze is reconciled perspective.** It is the achieved condition in
which distinct perspectives stand level enough for a shared future to appear.
It is not a court, exchange, regulator, audit board, or settlement office. A
Junction Board decision may be described as reaching Equal Gaze; the Board is
the institution and Equal Gaze is the condition achieved. Someone may say
“The ruling has reached Equal Gaze” without creating an Equal Gaze office.

**Aura Beam reveals or transmits the visible shared future.** Aura Beam remains
part of the Summit, recognition, and future-vision structure and retains its
existing higher transmission role. It is not a stock ticker or financial
broadcast office. The Junction Wire publishes market information.

The market decision chain is:

```text
work or event
→ relevant House Sector Hall verifies domain evidence
→ Junction Board audits and reconciles it
→ the decision may be described as reaching Equal Gaze
→ Clearing House settles the financial contract
→ Junction Wire publishes the result
```

Before recognition, ordinary speech may describe the matter as remaining in
the Current Haze. Ordinary implementation states are Proposed, Open,
UnderReview, Recognized, Disputed, Settled, and Voided.

## XI. Four Public Indexes

The four official indexes are:

- **Sandmanor Design Index** — Creation of Form: architecture, interiors,
  exteriors, planning, fashion, environments, public spaces, and spatial
  usability. It reflects what Hollow Grove is preparing to become.
- **Flynt Engineering Index** — Creation of Function: machinery, energy,
  transportation, communication, computation, infrastructure, industrial
  systems, and technical operation. It reflects what Hollow Grove is learning
  to make work.
- **Stonebend Craft Index** — Continuance of Form: construction, fabrication,
  tools, materials, workshops, durable goods, property development, and
  workmanship. It reflects what Hollow Grove can make stand and endure.
- **Glaüshouse Repair Index** — Continuance of Function: hospitals, clinics,
  rehabilitation, maintenance, restoration, disaster recovery, Sympiote
  renewal, mechanical repair, and functional preservation. It reflects what
  Hollow Grove refuses to lose.

> **Design shows what the world wants to become. Engineering shows what it can make work. Craft shows what it can make endure. Repair shows what it refuses to lose.**

The Houses do not own the indexes. A House may own enterprises, hold shares,
sponsor projects, form funds, dominate a sector through success, and influence
demand through policy. It may not own or set an official index, dictate
methodology, suppress verified negative evidence, or publish a competing
official result.

The Junction Board governs methodology. The South Ridge Exchange calculates
the official index. The Junction Wire publishes it.

> **The Houses do not own the indexes. They fill them. Aura Ridge measures what the world currently values.**

Each Sector Hall has a public Index Board connected to the Junction Wire. It
shows the official value and movement alongside verified productive evidence,
employment demand, active projects, warnings, and disclosures. Headline value
may include expectation and speculation; supporting evidence must be
constitutionally verified.

## XII. Listings and Markets

Each listed enterprise or project has one stable identity, one primary
economic pole, optional secondary exposure, explicit weights where weights are
used, and evidence supporting classification. Identity never derives from
insertion, catalog, or index position. Classification asks whether the subject
principally affects Form or Function and principally creates or continues it.

Central Junction initially supports:

- **Enterprise Market** — companies, clinics, workshops, studios, engineering
  firms, repair services, transport routes, construction firms, salvage crews,
  and productive institutions;
- **Project Market** — bridges, settlements, hospitals, transit, workshops,
  restoration, reconstruction, utilities, and public environments;
- **Event Market** — predefined constitutional outcomes such as Goblin wars,
  workshop claims, territorial disputes, project completion, lawful Title,
  infrastructure survival, Recipe trials, contests, succession, and recognized
  surrender.

Every event outcome and complete condition set is recorded before trading
opens. Price expresses market expectation. Constitutional settlement derives
from recognized evidence; the two never collapse.

## XIII. Blackroot Workshop Event Proof

The deterministic fixture is the **Blackroot Workshop Conflict**.

Faction A wins only if, by closing, it controls the workshop, production
remains operational, Stonebend recognizes the claim, and no active lawful
challenge remains. Faction B wins under the same complete conditions. The
recognized outcome is Draw if neither faction satisfies the complete set.
Contradictory evidence that makes both appear complete remains Disputed rather
than manufacturing a winner.

The fixture records evidence, obtains domain attestations, receives Junction
Board audit, reaches a recognized outcome that may be described as Equal Gaze,
settles through the Clearing House, and publishes through the Junction Wire.
No bookmaker, House, combatant, rumor, or unverified announcement determines
the outcome. The fixture changes no combat behavior.

## XIV. Conflicts of Interest

Junction Board reviewers and Clearing House officials may not secretly hold
prohibited positions in matters they audit or settle. Commanders may not
secretly wager on conflicts they direct. Material conflicts require disclosure
and recusal. Direct participants may receive limits, disclosure requirements,
or freezes. Deliberate sabotage for profit or fraudulent evidence may void a
position or settlement and trigger lawful review. Settlement evidence remains
auditable.

This is a constitutional safeguard, not a complete securities-law simulator.
