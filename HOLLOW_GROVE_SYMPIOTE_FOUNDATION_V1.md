# Hollow Grove Sympiote Foundation V1

Date: 2026-10-08

Status: adopted additive lore; first clinical/practice case implemented in the
world and gameplay runtime. Clinic-map art, broader product inventories, and
later Form realization mechanisms remain separate development work.

Authority: beneath the Compromise, Synthesis Canon, Glaüshouse Constitution,
Power Recipe law, Classification Reconciliation, and existing House paths.

## 1. Name and living meaning

**Sympiote** is the canonical singular; **Sympiotes** is the plural. This uses
the established name at the user's explicit request. The earlier proposed
shortened name is retired from active lore, code, and navigation.

A Sympiote is a crafted living graft made from and for its host. **Sympiote
foundation form** names the host's early developmental embodiment after lawful
reciprocal integration. In Boardwalk speech, people living in that stage may
be called Sympiotes. The person remains Hueman or their continuing Being;
their Name, Frame, memory, prior Form history, and rights persist.

Sympian still names the existing continuity classification. A graft, a living
Form, a continuity category, a profession, and a cultural affiliation remain
separate records. The new foundation creates no fifth House or new species
ladder and does not replace the Nature catalog.

### Small building units and bodily Form

The user's adopted scale names distinguish **Synbiote blocks**, the small
living Glaüshouse units worked into small things, from **Sympiote Form**, the
larger bodily expression. The [Synthesis continuum](GLAUSHOUSE_SYNTHESIS_CONTINUUM_V1_DRAFT.md)
owns the block definition. Synbiote is intentional for the small units;
Sympiote remains the established bodily and host-specific graft spelling.
Existing Sympiote grafts retain their records and are not renamed or decomposed
into an invented block inventory. Grades I, II and III describe development
of the host's Sympiote Form. Making an item with blocks grants its maker no
Form or grade. Block production and assembly methods remain future work.

## 2. Glaüshouse is the beginning

Glaüshouse is now the adopted source House of all Synthesis, from an item to a
whole Form. This foundation is its living-development path within the wider
[Synthesis continuum](GLAUSHOUSE_SYNTHESIS_CONTINUUM_V1_DRAFT.md). Grades I,
II and III concern the Sympiote foundation; they do not grade external items
or replace target-specific Synthesis requirements.

A learner goes to Glaüshouse to receive a personal Sympiote foundation. The
clinic helps explain its present function and needs, establishes compatibility
and scope, obtains specific consent, crafts for the host, records the actual
integration result, and supports recovery before voluntary return.

The governing story is:

```text
continuing Hueman → Glaüshouse assessment → personal consent
→ clinical establishment of the Sympiote foundation → recovery and teaching
→ voluntary return → cultivation and experience → later supported Synthesis
```

The initial foundation is personalized and flexible. Several directions remain
open. It is not a preset final creature selected from a power package: the
existing host-specific graft rule remains controlling. The learner may have a
hope for a later Form while its compatibility and realization remain questions.

People can explore practices before entering the clinic. Receiving this Form
is not a rewrite of every Being's birth or existing body, and starting another
pursuit does not silently erase a prior maintained Form.

### Adopted Sympiote Grades I, II and III

Adopted Sympiote grade structure, 2026-10-08: Grade I → Grade II → Grade III → distinct supported Synthesis branches.

The user corrected the earlier immediate-young-Gargoyle proposal:

> No so I want like three grades of synpiote like synpiote grade I II III and then you can either be a werewolf and gargoyle or a merman/mariner and maybe more

The foundation has three ordered grades: **Sympiote Grade I**, **Sympiote
Grade II**, and **Sympiote Grade III**. The learner remains in the Sympiote
stage through these grades, then may pursue a supported later Synthesis Form.
The named branches are **Gargoyle**, **Werewolf**, and **Mariner/Merman**.
Merman and Mariner name the same maritime Form. Additional supported branches
remain possible; this list does not close the earlier House exploration paths.

```text
Sympiote Grade I → Sympiote Grade II → Sympiote Grade III
                                      ├─ Gargoyle
                                      ├─ Werewolf
                                      ├─ Mariner / Merman
                                      └─ further supported branches: open
```

The user also clarified:

> No gargoyle and werewolf never combine

Adopted Form separation, 2026-10-08: Gargoyle and Werewolf never combine.

Gargoyle and Werewolf are distinct branches and never combine into a shared
Form. Grade III does not produce a Gargoyle–Werewolf hybrid. Any earlier
body-combination requirement that uses both must be reconciled with this rule;
no replacement Manticorp body requirement is chosen by this clarification.

Grades are foundation-development designations, separate from Synthesis Levels
1 and 2, Stonebend's eight pairings, House office/rank and a later Form's own
biological milestones. A grade records neither automatic transformation nor
universal compatibility with all branches. Each later Form still needs its
own applicable Recipe, compatibility, consent, actual establishment and care.

advancement and generalized branch transitions are not yet implemented.

## 3. First appearance and capacity

The adopted design keeps the person recognizable, with functional changes in
posture, movement, or tissue response. Individual fit matters. Exact sprites,
anatomical features, palette treatment, and animations remain presentation
work under the existing art and color constitutions.

The initial capacity is **small controlled bodily adaptation through focused
practice**. The implemented exercise vocabulary is Balance, Tension, and
Coordination. Each exercise is bounded and includes release. It supplies an
observed use of the foundation, not unrestricted shapeshifting, flight,
petrification, telepathy, an equipped Aura move, or another Nature.

Current and Aura already belong to the practitioner. The foundation changes
how some cultivated practice can be embodied; it does not create those aspects
from nothing. Ordinary movement and inward discipline may both inform growth.

## 4. Needs, limits, and comfortable use

The foundation needs appropriate nourishment, rest between exercises, and
clinical follow-up when its behavior changes or strain is reported. The
current runtime explicitly records rest obligations, strain, recovery, and
review. It has no invented food inventory, metabolic meter, or universal
medical timer.

The first milestone is **comfortable repeatable use of one adaptation**.
For this bounded case, that requires two recorded uses of the same adaptation,
with rest between them and rest afterward. Switching the exercise begins its
own repeatability check. These observations are a case acceptance condition,
not a universal level number, XP threshold, or automatic later transformation.

A practice request while rest is needed is rejected without mutation. Reporting
strain retains the actual Form and history, suspends ordinary practice, and
requires rest plus clinical review. Review renews the recovery plan; rest and
clinical discharge follow before ordinary practice resumes.

## 5. Clinical authority and personal agency

The live Glaüshouse clearance authority owns assessment, establishment,
discharge, and strain review. The first backend case obtains an accepted Clear
decision from the active Prima Donna holder in the loaded institutional world.
It does not insert an office holder or substitute a popular Boardwalk boss.
If that authority is absent, the request fails closed.

The patient owns consent, withdrawal, practice, and their exploration choices.
Consent to assessment is distinct from consent to graft establishment. A
changed establishment operator requires fresh assessment and consent to the
new scope. Withdrawal before establishment preserves refusal with no new
Form, Bond, debt, or penalty. The person may request another assessment later.

Post-establishment care preserves the living Form. It does not pretend that a
withdrawal or strain report instantly reverses an existing body. General
revision, regression, discontinuation, and collapse remain under existing
clinical law and need their own implemented cases.

This is one authored foundation scenario with a disclosed, limited host-bound
profile. It is not a general patient simulator or a substitute for the full
Glaüshouse registry's diagnosis, privilege, viability, material, rescue, and
procedure records. Additional clinical scenarios must supply their applicable
evidence and lawful requirements rather than assuming this case proves them.

## 6. Reused living-graft lifecycle

The implementation calls the existing Sympiote reducer in this order:

```text
sample host → read host Current–Aura Recipe → cultivate living tissue
→ craft from and for the host → graft with consent → monitor compatibility
→ record actual reciprocal integration and the emergent foundation Form
```

The first case records a successful foundation after its consent and live
clinical-authority checks. The existing underlying reducer retains its distinct
host rejection, Sympiote rejection, partial integration, failed integration,
and reciprocal Synthesis outcomes. Broader foundation outcome scenes must use
those distinctions; success in this authored case guarantees none elsewhere.

The existing graft archive remains HGSYM schema V1. No graft spelling,
classification, outcome meaning, or historical wire alias is changed.

## 7. Clinical and practice states

| State | Meaning | Available next decisions |
|---|---|---|
| AwaitingAssessment | no foundation episode or Form established | request assessment |
| AwaitingConsent | disclosed personal profile and clinical actor recorded | consent or withdraw |
| Consented | explicit establishment consent recorded | ask clinician to establish, withdraw, or request new assessment |
| Recovering | foundation lives; recovery and teaching remain | rest, report strain, then request discharge |
| Discharged | voluntary return is clinically recorded | bounded practice and rest, report strain, explore directions, consider offers |
| CareRequired | the Form persists but strain has interrupted ordinary practice | rest, request review, then complete renewed recovery |
| Refused | refusal persists and no foundation was established | request a later assessment without erasing the refusal |

The profile exists before establishment as a disclosed proposal. The
`form_established` field becomes true only after the graft records reciprocal
integration. Assessment, payment, dialogue, consent alone, or a chosen interest
cannot make it true.

## 9. Boardwalk bosses, crews, and offerings

The [Boardwalk social constitution](FLYNT_BOARDWALK_SOCIAL_CONSTITUTION_V1_DRAFT.md)
owns the current rival identities: the Pimp and his adult trans sex-worker
Troons on the public Boardwalk above, and the rubber-clad Gimp with his Gooners
in the private Underway nightclub section below. Gooners are devoted customers
and fetish fans with a smaller working inner circle. Historical Trooners names
and camp identifiers remain compatible. People in the Sympiote stage may become
customers while pursuing development; that records no automatic crew identity.

Proposed product emphases are qualified teaching, presentation, demonstrations,
and introductions around the Pimp; equipment, provenanced materials, practical
work, and dependable support around the Gimp. Actual stock, prices, suppliers,
teacher qualifications, contracts, and after-sale promises remain to design.
These are supporting proposals, not the crews' adopted defining occupations.
The user has deferred club admission, staffing and prices while discussing
later Synthesis development.

The implemented `ConsiderBoardwalkOffer` records that the person considered
one camp's offering. Both can be considered. It does not perform a purchase,
create an inventory item, form a Bond, assign Trooner/Gooner membership, or
authorize treatment. Other communities and House traditions also support
cultivation; neither boss monopolizes access to any future Form.

The rivalry asks who helps a learner become capable and who keeps the promise
when practice becomes difficult. Spectacle may outrun delivery; dependable
work may become an excessive demand. Either crew can deliver honestly, accept
criticism, repair a promise, or cooperate with its rival.

## 10. First lived scene

A learner arrives at Glaüshouse with a desired future capacity. The clinic
explains the foundation's present use, boundaries, needs, and alternatives.
After establishment, a Nightingale can help the person perform an appropriate
ordinary task, notice a change in movement, release the effort, and rest.

> Learner: “What am I becoming?”
>
> Nightingale: “You're still you. Learn what this Form needs, then choose
> where you want it to go.”

After discharge, the Pimp offers a public practice session and an introduction;
the Gimp offers equipment and help with a practical task. A Gargoyle-oriented
learner may explore holding and Fortification, while a Minorian-oriented learner
explores work within a cultivated place. Their different needs test the shared
foundation without claiming that a demonstration already grants either Form.

The scene is authored lore. Its backend lifecycle exists; a dedicated clinic
map, new dialogue interactions, sprites, and bodily animation are still future
presentation work. The current clients can inspect records without owning them.

## 11. Twelve-field functional contract and handoffs

The typed definition is `sympiote_foundation_lore()` in the owning module.
It is additive to the historical twelve-route catalog rather than rewriting
that catalog or its archive.

1. **Identity:** `lore.glaushouse.sympiote-foundation`, bound to
   `case.glaushouse.sympiote-foundation.v1`.
2. **Authority:** Glaüshouse Clear, with live clinical actor and scoped receipt.
3. **Place:** Glauspitals establishment/recovery and voluntary Boardwalk return.
4. **Participants:** continuing Hueman, clinical actor/recorder, and later offers.
5. **Verb:** Clear; the patient separately chooses and practices.
6. **Trigger:** a request for the host-specific foundation assessment.
7. **Evidence:** disclosed profile, consent/withdrawal, clinical receipts,
   graft history, rest/discharge, and bounded practice records.
8. **Choices:** eleven typed request/choice families in the state table and protocol.
9. **Consequences:** exact phase, actual Form, rest need, scoped milestone,
   interests, and considered offers; no automatic later Form or affiliation.
10. **Persistence:** checksummed gameplay V5, deterministic replay, and legacy migration.
11. **Presentation:** read-only GameView and lore inspection; clinic art remains separate.
12. **Refusal/failure:** missing identity/authority, changed operator, withdrawal,
    missing rest, strain, causal overflow, and altered replay.

Ordered responsibilities are clinical authority → qualified practitioner →
clinical/practice recorder → continuing Hueman. Every handoff has a specific
question in the typed contract. Actual decisions and results are distinguished
from authored responsibility labels. Clinical recovery owns unfinished care.

## 12. Records, inspection, and migration

The stable foundation Form is `form.sympiote.foundation.v1`. This is a
host-bound developmental state, not a new Deck Nature or a general Recipe ID.
Its profile records the host, facility, appearance, capacity scope, maintenance,
and limits. Clinical receipts preserve actor, office, function, scope, causal
position, decision ID, and evidence references.

Gameplay events preserve action, subject, before/after phase, actual Form flag,
rest need, current comfortable-use result, practice count, optional clinical
receipt, and evidence ID. Replay reconstructs the graft and compares the whole
expected event; altered authority, host, scope, result, or order is rejected.
Graft substep positions are local to that history, derived from the parent
position with checked arithmetic; world causal ordering is unchanged.

Gameplay archive V5 adds the event variant. V1–V4 remain readable and migrate
without inventing consent, a graft, practice, affiliation, or another Form.
Protocol V1 gains `AdvanceSympioteFoundationIntent`; existing requests remain
compatible. GameView adds a defaultable read-only `sympiote_foundation`.
Clinical receipt projection is limited to the latest 32; the full action and
receipt history remains authoritative and replayable in the archive.

`hollow-grove lore --json` exposes the contract. Add `--save path/to/save.json`
to verify and replay a recorded case without writing it. Lore inspection V2
separates the foundation record from the unchanged historical route definitions.
The kernel audit includes host/Form continuity, clinical authority and consent,
rest/repeatability, and the boundary between exploration and later realization.

## 13. Completion and remaining work

The first successful episode is complete when the same host has the actual
reciprocal foundation, clinical discharge, and comfortable repeatable use.
Protected refusal is a preserved lawful closeout, not a failed transformation.
Strain leaves a recoverable care obligation with the Form retained.

General clinical patient models, additional integration outcomes, later Form
Recipes and realization cases, anatomical asset design, and shop inventories
remain explicitly scoped future work. This contract does not make every
Synthesis path implemented or the whole game complete.

The runtime implementation belongs above the neutral recursion and semantic
kernels. Its migration record is [Sympiote Foundation Migration V1](docs/SYMPIOTE_FOUNDATION_MIGRATION_V1.md).
The preserved `lore-sync-2026-10-08-v3` kit predates this work; the new milestone
requires its own matching checkpoint and release before transfer.
