# Minoan County Court System and Restitution Cycle V1

Status: canonical shared judicial foundation
Host institution: **MINOAN COUNTY COURTHOUSE**
Executable owner: `src/world/minoan_court.rs`

This instrument establishes Hollow Grove's one shared judiciary. It extends the
existing Minoan County Courthouse without replacing its coastal duties or
turning Sandmanor into the owner of every House's law.

> **The Minoans host the Court. They do not own the law.**

> **One Court hears the dispute. Each House proves its own domain.**

## 1. Host and constitutional boundary

The Minoan County Courthouse remains the last major Sandmanor institution
before Glaüshouse. Its original jurisdiction continues:

- Aura Beach and Southern Law;
- marine and Current Sea incidents;
- Current Break permits and violations;
- rescue obligations and public safety;
- booking and arraignment;
- temporary lawful detention;
- emergency protection;
- and lawful transfer toward Glaüshouse.

The shared Court System uses that existing institution, site, records, and
public approach. The Minoans supply the host, coastal competence, public access,
and judicial service. They do not acquire Flynt engineering authority,
Glaüshouse clinical authority, Stonebend Title authority, Sandmanor Design
authority, or Central Junction market authority.

The Court is neither a fifth House nor a sovereign office.

## 2. Judicial authority and limits

The Court may determine standing, preserve evidence, hear testimony, establish
facts, interpret law, resolve competing Claims, determine whether a boundary
was crossed, issue targeted judgments, order temporary protection, review
error, protect higher constitutional law, certify amendment procedure, monitor
Restitution, and return a case to the stage that failed.

The Court may not:

- bear Diamond or exercise Hypergiant authority;
- forge a Stonebend Claim or issue the High Freemason seal;
- embody, replace, or abolish the Proliteriate;
- remove a principal Stonebend power without two-power concurrence;
- command Manticorp;
- fabricate Proof of Persistence;
- practice medicine or decide clinical viability without Glaüshouse evidence;
- replace Sandmanor Design or Proof of Reciprocity;
- calculate Central Junction indexes, settle trades, or publish the Junction
  Wire;
- create House-domain evidence;
- ratify an amendment by judgment;
- or create a universal enforcement arm.

> **The Court declares what the law requires. The constitutionally responsible institution performs what the law authorizes.**

## 4. Stable case identity

Every case receives one stable caller-supplied identity. That identity remains
unchanged across:

- every jurisdiction;
- Conciliation;
- First Hearing;
- Appeal;
- Constitutional Review;
- one or more Restitution attempts;
- return loops;
- equilibrium;
- and historical closure.

Identity never derives from a docket index, party order, stage order, judgment
position, insertion order, or random value.

The case preserves parties, standing, Claims, affected Titles, evidence source,
testimony, stage history, orders, findings, judgments, appeals, constitutional
review, remedies, Restitution evidence, recurrence, and closure.

## 5. The five-stage cycle

```text
Conciliation
→ First Hearing
→ Appeal
→ Constitutional Review
→ Restitution
```

The stages have explicit semantic order. Storage order does not define their
meaning.

### Conciliation

Conciliation asks:

> **Can equilibrium be restored voluntarily and lawfully?**

It records parties, relationships, agreed and disputed facts, immediate risk,
lawful boundaries, affected Titles, affected Yield, and whether voluntary
repair is possible.

It may produce negotiation, facilitated settlement, acknowledgment, temporary
agreement, voluntary correction, return, repair, or an agreement to proceed.
It may not conceal coercion, erase evidence, force surrender of a lawful right,
or avoid required formal review.

> **Before the law divides responsibility, it asks whether responsibility can be shared.**

> **Conciliation hears the relationship. First Hearing establishes the record.**

> **Conciliation seeks balance.**

A settlement does not close a case. Its promised repair must reach
Restitution.

### First Hearing

First Hearing asks:

> **What happened?**

It reviews standing and jurisdiction, preserves evidence, receives testimony,
authenticates House records, issues bounded protection, distinguishes facts
from legal and constitutional conclusions, and produces an initial judgment,
dismissal, or referral.

Standing is explicit and extensible. It may arise from direct injury, lawful
custody, Title or scope interest, an affected Proliteriate node, public duty,
constitutional office, contract, or another lawful interest.

> **First Hearing establishes truth.**

### Appeal

Appeal asks:

> **Was the case handled correctly?**

It reviews identified legal, procedural, jurisdictional, evidentiary,
proportionality, authority, conflict, or participation error. It is not an
automatic complete retrial and does not automatically stay judgment.

Dispositions are Affirmed, Modified, Reversed, Remanded, Vacated, Narrowed, or
Additional Evidence Required. A stay requires its own bounded protective
order.

> **First Hearing asks what happened. Appeal asks whether the answer was reached lawfully.**

> **Appeal corrects judgment.**

### Constitutional Review

Constitutional Review asks:

> **Can this judgment, law, Title action, or proposed amendment stand within the whole?**

It protects House boundaries, Claim/Title/Yield, the Stonebend triangle,
Diamond and vacancy, Proliteriate continuity, Title lifecycle, lawful
Synthesis, lawful Hollowing, public participation, amendment procedure, and
higher constitutional law.

It does not ordinarily retry properly settled facts. It cannot amend, ratify,
bear Diamond, forge a Claim, replace the Proliteriate, or transfer one House's
authority to another.

> **Appeal corrects the case. Constitutional Review protects the whole around the case.**

> **A judgment may protect the Constitution. It may not amend it by itself.**

> **Constitutional Review protects the whole.**

### Restitution

Restitution asks:

> **What must be returned, repaired, restored, replaced, corrected, or carried back to those who bore the harm?**

> **Judgment names what is owed. Restitution makes it return.**

Restitution includes property or custody return, Form repair, Title or gate
scope restoration, public-record correction, material replacement, labor or
burden compensation, care, transfer reversal, access or opportunity,
infrastructure, Central Junction records, Current, and provenance. Money may be
one remedy, but Restitution is not reducible to money.

Restitution repairs lawful relation and displaced Yield. It is distinct from
punishment.

> **Restitution makes the injured whole as far as lawfully possible. It does not rewrite the past.**

> **Restitution returns the Yield.**

## 6. Equilibrium and recurrence

Equilibrium does not require happiness, physical reversibility, erased history,
restored intimacy, or zero burden. It requires:

- a restored or clarified lawful boundary;
- delivery to the intended life or institution;
- lawful assignment of continuing burden;
- an accurate historical record;
- no hidden constitutional violation;
- and no immediate unresolved harm requiring the same case to continue.

Restitution dispositions are:

- Equilibrium Confirmed;
- Partially Satisfied;
- Remedy Failed;
- New Harm Created;
- Responsible Institution Defaulted;
- Additional Restitution Required;
- Returned to Conciliation;
- Returned to First Hearing;
- Returned to Appeal;
- Returned to Constitutional Review.

A return preserves the same case identity and failed attempt. Each recurrence
states its lawful reason, evidence, failed Restitution record, return stage, and
new cycle.

> **The Court does not end when it speaks. It ends when the remedy reaches the life it was meant to repair.**

## 7. Targeted remedies and protection

Judgments identify exact targets: person, Title core, gate scope, license,
bearer tenure, mandate, custody, contract, public record, material batch,
Synthesis record, Hollowing authorization, institution, or action.

A gate-scope remedy does not destroy a core Title. A mandate remedy does not
abolish the Proliteriate. A Hypergiant finding becomes evidence for the
existing challenge process; the Court cannot perform removal alone.

Protective orders identify authority, evidence, subject, action, exact scope,
reason, start, termination, review, and affected Titles. Delay cannot make
temporary protection permanent.

## 8. House evidence and enforcement

House evidence retains its authenticated source:

- Stonebend: Titles, Claims, seals, provenance, boundaries, Hollowing, and
  lifecycle;
- Flynt: engineering, persistence, deployment, failure, and infrastructure;
- Glaüshouse: care, consent, compatibility, Continuance, and bodily harm;
- Sandmanor: Design, reciprocity, formation, guardianship, education, and
  coastal civic evidence;
- Central Junction: measures, transactions, settlement, publication,
  circulation, and methodology.

The Court compares evidence and explains conflicts. It cannot fabricate the
source evidence.

The responsible institution performs the remedy. Stonebend updates Titles and
seals; Flynt performs technical correction; Glaüshouse delivers care;
Sandmanor corrects Design and coastal administration; Central Junction
corrects circulation and publication; the Proliteriate witnesses public
return; existing executive authorities enforce only within their lawful
boundaries.

## 9. Amendment process

An amendment begins as a Claim, not law:

```text
Proposal
→ Public Notice
→ Affected-Party Testimony
→ Conciliation where appropriate
→ First Procedural Hearing
→ Appeal where challenged
→ Constitutional Review
→ Proper Ratification
→ Stonebend Seal and Public Record
→ Implementation and Restitution Review
→ Constitutional Equilibrium
```

The proposal states exact text, superseded text, purpose, affected Houses,
offices, Titles and communities, altered authority, expected Yield, and
ratification scope.

The Court reviews clarity, standing, notice, testimony, concealed evidence,
improper bundling, ratification scope, protected boundaries, premature
implementation, and hidden authority. It may continue, correct, separate,
stay, certify eligibility, or declare process defective.

> **The Court determines whether the path was lawful. Ratification determines whether the change becomes law.**

### Ratification scopes

**House-local:** the affected House uses its lawful process.

**Cross-House:** every affected House assents separately. One House cannot
ratify away another's authority.

**Foundational Hollow Grove:** all four Houses assent, with public notice,
affected-Yield evidence, Court certification, and Stonebend sealing.

Central Junction participates when affected but is never counted as a fifth
House.

Court certification, House ratification, and Stonebend sealing are three
distinct records. None substitutes for another.

## 10. Amendment Restitution

Ratification and sealing do not complete implementation. Restitution verifies
that:

- the ratified text is the implemented text;
- superseded provisions remain historically preserved;
- affected Titles and scopes change lawfully;
- each House implements only what it assented to;
- notice and transition occur;
- unexpected harm is recorded;
- actual Yield matches the represented purpose;
- and constitutional equilibrium can hold.

Restitution may return implementation to the factual, appellate,
constitutional, or House-ratification stage. It may not silently rewrite the
ratified text.

## 11. Deferred systems

This milestone does not lock judge selection, elections, numerical thresholds,
complete evidence rules, discovery, juries, sentencing, incarceration,
policing, criminal prosecution, permanent detention, comprehensive property or
custody law, inheritance, taxation, public finance, valuation formulas, full
Illegal Hollowing or Illegal Synthesis procedure, prosecutors, defenders,
court staffing, or every physical courtroom.

It creates no Judicial House, Court sovereign, fourth Stonebend power, fifth
House, permanent Minoan ruler, universal police force, currency, market index,
or duplicate constitutional lifecycle.
