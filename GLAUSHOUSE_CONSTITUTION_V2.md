# The Glaüshouse Constitution V2

Status: ratified constitutional law

Authority: canonical Glaüshouse architecture

Effective date: 2026-07-19

Executable projection: `src/world/glaushouse.rs`

## Preamble

Glaüshouse exists so that change may occur without becoming violation. It
governs medicine, care, diagnosis, recovery, clinical judgment,
transformation, Synthesis, and the lawful passage of a subject from one
condition into another.

> **Glaüshouse clears.**

> **Diagnosis identifies condition.**

> **Consent authorizes participation.**

> **Clearance permits procedure.**

> **Care preserves the subject.**

> **Synthesis transforms.**

> **Recovery completes the act.**

The signature constitutional offense is **Illegal Synthesis**. Capability is
not consent, desire is not safety, proof is not clearance, recognition is not
clearance, and technical possibility is not lawful permission.

## Article I — Constitutional Domain

The governing verb is **Clear**. Clearance permits a defined intervention for
a named subject, by a qualified operator, within a stated scope, facility,
time, risk, consent, capacity, and recovery boundary. It neither guarantees
success nor compels participation.

Glaüshouse protects bodily, mental, Aura, and Current integrity; informed
consent; decision-making capacity; safety; recovery; continuity through
transformation; accurate records; emergency care; refusal of non-emergency
intervention; disclosure; monitoring; restoration where possible; and freedom
from coercive treatment, fraudulent diagnosis, concealed experimentation,
Illegal Synthesis, and abandonment.

Clinical custody is never ownership. Jurisdiction lasts only as long as lawful
care, recovery, safety, public protection, or constitutional review requires.

## Article II — People and Institutions

Glaüshouse is constituted through patients, healers, caretakers, physicians,
diagnosticians, Aura and Current specialists, Synthesis operators, recovery
stewards, advocates, witnesses, researchers, record keepers, emergency
responders, quarantine wardens, and palliative caretakers.

**The Nightingales** are the canonical nursing and clinical-care body,
universal entry Form, professional rank, and foundation of Glaüshouse clinical
authority. They are a constitutional clinical institution symbolically and
functionally aligned with white blood cells: detecting threat, responding to
injury, containing harm, preserving life, supporting recovery, and protecting
vulnerable subjects. This correspondence does not make generic nurses a
species. A Nightingale may remain a Nightingale for an entire career and
achieve deep mastery; advancement is an opportunity, not an obligation.

The frozen named institutions are **Glauspitals** and **Chromacord**.
Glauspitals operates care, medicine, recovery, and lawful procedure facilities.
Chromacord preserves clinical records and projects evidence; it cannot create
consent, privilege, clearance, or constitutional truth. The stable
`institution.glaushouse.medical-civilization` identity remains the House-wide
constitutional umbrella, not an extra sovereign organization.

Every clinical institution requires a lawful Name, Stonebend structural
record, jurisdiction, qualified staff, responsibility lines, patient
protections, emergency procedure, adverse-event reporting, record custody, and
lawful closure.

## Article IV — Prima Donna

The Prima Donna is Glaüshouse's highest singular clinical office and preserves
medical law, clearance law, Synthesis law, ethics, high-risk transformation,
public-health response, institutional discipline, succession, and House
interpretation.

The office may issue or deny high-risk clearance, suspend unsafe recipes,
order review, convene narrow emergency authority, certify practitioners,
restrict privilege, authorize lawful quarantine and exceptional research,
coordinate inter-House review, order recovery or restoration, and interpret
Glaüshouse law.

It may not override valid non-emergency refusal, manufacture consent or
evidence, compel Synthesis for politics, conceal risk or experimentation,
grant Title or political office, suppress adverse events, abandon patients,
erase identity history, or turn emergency power into permanent control.

Eligibility begins with qualified Persephone rank and additionally requires
physician, diagnostic, Recipe, surgical, scientific, consent, capacity,
recovery, refusal, teaching, and constitutional mastery. Candidates must show
patient-outcome evidence, technical and lived viability judgment, responsible
Synthesis Ledger use, Recipe authorship or revision, cultivation of other
clinicians, and commitment to keeping advancement routes open. Species,
transformation, beauty, fame, combat power, wealth, age, seniority, personal
favor, and lineage never qualify a candidate automatically.

Accession is ordered and complete only after: constitutional opening; qualified
Persephone candidacy; clinical and patient-outcome review; adverse-event
review; Nightingale, Matron, Marshal, Persephone, and treated-Hueman testimony;
Living Ledger and Recipe Ledger review; public and professional hearing;
material Sandmanor review; Flynt recognition; Stonebend Title and accession;
Glaüshouse oath; and constitutional Seal. The outgoing Prima Donna may teach,
recommend, or recognize candidates, but may not appoint a successor without
constitutional evidence.

Removal is available for Illegal Synthesis, coercion, falsified clearance,
concealed adverse events, unlawful experimentation, abandonment, succession
corruption, quarantine abuse, record alteration, incapacity, or constitutional
betrayal, with independent review and preserved records.

## Article V — Persephone

Persephone is the multiple advanced balanced rank of passage, recovery, return,
and responsibility for the whole course of care. A candidate must demonstrate
Aura-sensitive observation, Current-sensitive stabilization, preservation of

rehabilitation, safe regression, discharge judgment, reconciliation of
conflicting evidence, and lawful mastery of both Matron and Marshal domains.
Seniority or title alone is insufficient, and no path through either branch is
inherently superior.

Persephones reconcile Matron and Marshal evidence, supervise complete episodes
of care, govern complex maintenance, judge whether a Form remains livable,
authorize safe regression, coordinate high-risk recovery, review disputed
consent, oversee discharge, and lead Nightingale teams across clinical
boundaries.

> **The Matron reads the patient. The Marshal holds the patient. Persephone preserves the whole patient.**

Persephones collectively oversee the Living Ledger. The Matron records how the
Hueman experiences the Form. The Marshal records how the body sustains the
Form. Persephone determines whether those two continuities still belong to one
viable life.

> **No transformation is complete merely because the procedure has ended.**

## Article VI — Nightingale, Matron, and Marshal Practice

Nightingales perform intake, preparation, education, bedside care, consent
verification, Current and Aura monitoring, graft-site care, administration of
established Sympiote protocols, maintenance, renewal, recovery,
rehabilitation, discharge, follow-up, emergency recognition, and Living Ledger
documentation.

Ordinary Nightingales may perform standardized low-depth Synthesis only after
diagnosis, under an established Recipe, within authorized scope, with an
understood expected response, available monitoring, and defined escalation.
They may not independently invent unprecedented Recipes, create unknown Forms,
perform unauthorized transfiguration, conceal serious rejection, continue
after lawful stop criteria, or exceed clinical authorization.

> **Every higher Glaüshouse authority begins by learning to remain beside the patient.**

Matron is the Aura-forward branch. Matrons specialize in observation,
cognition, perception, emotional continuity, meaningful lived consent,
identity preservation, Aura regulation, advocacy, subtle symptoms, recovery,
rehabilitation, and the subjective experience of maintained Synthesis.

> **What is happening within this Hueman?**

> **The Matron reads the patient.**

A Matron may halt or challenge care when consent ceases to be meaningful,
identity destabilizes, Aura coherence fails, cognition deteriorates,
psychological rejection begins, or the procedure becomes experientially
unlivable.

Marshal is the Current-forward branch. Marshals specialize in physical
stabilization, Current circulation, movement and positioning, procedural
readiness, graft integrity, emergency response, structural support,
containment, intensive care, and bodily survival.

> **What must be held together right now?**

> **The Marshal holds the patient.**

A Marshal may halt or challenge care when Current collapses, structure fails,
a graft ruptures, overgrowth is uncontrolled, physiology is unstable,
positioning is unsafe, or intervention exceeds physical tolerance.

> **Lived continuity and bodily continuity are equally necessary to lawful Synthesis.**

Neither branch may subordinate the other, absorb the other's jurisdiction,
claim sole authority over the Nightingale order, or close the other's
advancement. When Matron and Marshal judgments conflict, immediate danger is
stabilized, both judgments are recorded, the procedure pauses when clinically
possible, and the case goes to a Persephone. Prima Donna is consulted when the
Recipe itself must change.

A Nightingale may issue an immediately binding temporary clinical stop for
absent or withdrawn consent, wrong subject, wrong procedure, excess scope,
failed protective conditions, changed condition, identity mismatch, imminent
catastrophic harm, or missing rescue capacity. Every stop triggers mandatory
review without retaliation. Collective reports of coercion, unsafe staffing,
contaminated facilities, hidden experiments, false records, medicine diversion,
premature discharge, unlawful restraint, or repeated adverse events require
review.

## Article VII — Diagnosis

Diagnosis identifies condition; it does not create condition, consent, or
treatment authority. Every diagnosis identifies subject, history, findings,
evidence, alternatives, uncertainty, operator competence, record, and revision
path. Provisional status and material uncertainty must remain visible.

Fabrication, decisive omission, political diagnosis, diagnosis to force
Synthesis or custody, false incapacity, and retroactive record alteration are
diagnostic fraud.

## Article VIII — Consent

Consent is informed, voluntary, specific, comprehensible, current, documented,
capacity-based, and revocable before the irreversible threshold except under
narrow emergency law. Material risks, benefits, alternatives, refusal
consequences, experimental status, operator identity, recovery, failure,
partial and irreversible change, reversal, identity risk, material use,
Hollowing, and research use must be disclosed when applicable.

Consent scopes never collapse: examination is not treatment; treatment is not
Synthesis; Hollowing is not Synthesis; Synthesis is not research; one stage is
not a later stage; emergency stabilization is not elective transformation.

Silence, custody, dependence, recognition, threat, fraud, political pressure,
economic extortion, abandonment, unauthorized psychoactive influence, and
unauthorized Aura influence never create consent.

## Article IX — Capacity

Capacity is the present, decision-specific ability to understand information,
appreciate consequences, compare options, and communicate choice. Adults are
presumed capable. Disability, fear, anger, poverty, low status, unusual belief,
transformation, or refusal does not prove incapacity.

Translation, simpler explanation, communication aids, advocates, time, pain
relief, stabilization, and alternate communication must be offered where
applicable. Incapacity findings are scoped, time-limited, documented, reviewed,
and independently reviewed for high-risk Synthesis.

## Article X — Clearance

Clearance answers whether this intervention may proceed now, for this subject,
by this operator, for this purpose, in this facility and scope. Classes include
routine, conditional, emergency, high-risk, experimental, restorative,
Synthesis, reversal, public-health, postmortem, temporary, suspended, and
revoked.

Every clearance records subject, diagnosis or purpose, procedure, intended
outcome, operator, facility, scope, risk, consent, capacity, equipment,
witnesses, emergency plan, recovery plan, stopping conditions, expiration,
review authority, and stable ID. It expires upon material change, delay,
withdrawal, failed conditions, changed operator, excess scope, or suspension.

## Article XI — Medicine and Care

Care includes prevention, diagnosis, treatment, nursing, stabilization,
rehabilitation, pain relief, wound and infection care, mental care, Aura and
Current care, chronic care, emergency care, and palliative care. Care is not
limited to cure.

An accepting institution must assess, stabilize, inform, protect, record,
monitor, transfer safely, avoid abandonment, arrange recovery, and respond to
deterioration without discrimination. Restraint is a last, least-restrictive,
monitored, reviewed safety measure and never punishment, convenience,
intimidation, forced experimentation, or suppression.

## Article XII — Aura and Current Care

Aura care governs perception, projection, influence, psychic distress,
illusion, memory, emotional resonance, Glow instability, and Aura depletion or
overload while protecting mental autonomy. Aura influence cannot manufacture
consent.

Current care governs bodily force, movement, pressure, durability, momentum,
Flow instability, depletion, disruption, overload, and loss of control.
Combined instability is stabilized and distinguished from injury, identity,
emerging lawful change, and external imposition; it is not an excuse for forced
Synthesis.

## Article XIII — Synthesis

Synthesis is medicine first and transformation second. Major Synthesis
therefore requires multidisciplinary evidence, care, and responsibility rather
than one authority performing every function alone.

Synthesis lawfully transforms, fuses, upgrades, integrates, stabilizes, or
reconstitutes a subject into a materially different form, capability, or
identity-bearing state. It may affect Frame, Flow, Glow, Current Prism, body,
Aura, expression, learnset, capability, limitation, vulnerability, region, or
lineage.

Ordinary treatment preserves or restores within existing identity; Synthesis
changes form or potential identity. Mixed procedures disclose and clear both
acts separately.

**Synthesis establishes a real living Form whose Continuance depends on
maintenance, renewal, Recipe practice, environmental compatibility,
institutional care, and knowledge of the Form's Ways.** A Synthesis Form is
physically and constitutionally real while maintained. It is not a costume,
illusion, arbitrary loadout, temporary power-up, or automatic post-combat
reversion. One procedure does not guarantee that the Form will continue
forever.

> **Synthesis is not permanence. Synthesis is Continuance through renewal.**

> **The Form is real while sustained. Its future depends on the Recipe, the Ways, the Hueman, and the conditions that keep it coherent.**

A Form may be established, integrated, maintained, renewed, refined, revised,
weakened, regressed, safely discontinued, replaced by another Synthesis, or
catastrophically collapsed. A Hueman may maintain a Form indefinitely,
including throughout an entire natural life, by continuing the correct Ways.
Safe regression and catastrophic collapse are distinct clinical events.

Synthesis is a medical spectrum:

- **Adjustment** — precise correction to an existing body or Form, including
  Current realignment, Aura regulation, graft-tension relief, structural
  adjustment, or minor Sympiote tuning;
- **Graft** — addition, repair, or replacement of a living component, including
  Sympiote attachment, synthesized-tissue repair, limited sensory augmentation,
  or replacement of a damaged living component;
- **Reconstruction** — major reorganization of a bodily system, including organ
  replacement, major Current redirection, deep Aura-pathway revision, or
  rebuilding a damaged maintained Form;
- **Transfiguration** — establishment of a high-order Form, including Gargoyle,
  Chimera, Manticorp, and other advanced constitutional Forms;
- **Overgrowth** — the emergency failure state in which a Sympiote exceeds the
  Recipe, loses meaningful boundaries, or replaces rather than integrates with
  the Hueman. Overgrowth is never an intended Form.

> **Synthesis may range from precise adjustment to total transfiguration.**

Compatibility preserves two distinct rejection directions. In host rejection,
the Hueman may attack, isolate, starve, scar around, destabilize, or expel the
Sympiote. In Sympiote rejection, the Sympiote may treat the host as damaged
material, invade healthy structures, overcorrect, impose the Recipe too
aggressively, erase needed capacities, or construct a body the Hueman did not
consent to inhabit.

> **Is the Sympiote integrating with the Hueman, or replacing the Hueman?**

Lawful classes include restorative, elective, developmental, regional,
emergency, composite, staged, experimental, reversible, partially reversible,
irreversible, successor, corrective, failed, and incomplete Synthesis.

Regional canon remains Gnome to Minotaur on the Aura Field and Elf to Centaur
at Aura Beach and the Current Sea. Glaüshouse clears safety; Sandmanor proves
design and role; Stonebend preserves identity, Name, and Title; Flynt
recognizes institutional function. Symbolism alone authorizes nothing.

A recipe records source class, conditions, result, variation,
contraindications, materials, competence, environment, stages, stopping and
failure states, recovery, reversal, lineage, records, and House dependencies.
Before, during, and after procedure, subject identity, recipe, proof, risk,
alternatives, consent, capacity, Hollowing, continuity, competence, rescue,
materials, monitoring, deviations, stopping conditions, stabilization,
recovery, actual result, follow-up, and referrals remain recorded.

Major Reconstruction and Transfiguration require both **technical viability**
and **lived viability**. Prima Donna and Recipe authority establish that the
Recipe is possible, materials and compatibility are understood, the intended
Form can be clinically described, risks are known, and a lawful technical path
exists. The Nightingale ladder—especially Persephones—establishes that consent
is meaningful, consequences are understood, maintenance and recovery resources
exist, continuing care is available, the expected Form is reasonably livable,
and coercion is not disguised as consent.

> **Technical viability plus lived viability equals lawful Synthesis.**

## Article XIV — Illegal Synthesis

**Illegal Synthesis** is unauthorized, coercive, fraudulent, unsafe,
improperly cleared, concealed, experimentally abusive, identity-erasing, or
constitutionally unrecorded transformation, fusion, upgrade, integration,
alteration, or creation of a stabilized form.

It includes absent or coerced consent; invalid, expired, or exceeded clearance;
recipe or subject substitution; concealed experimentation; stolen or illegally
Hollowed material; false provenance; incompatible fusion; identity erasure;
forced labor or military transformation; succession manipulation; manufactured
office; unqualified operators; absent recovery; abandonment; concealed failure;
continuation after stopping conditions; denied reversal; destroyed identity
history; mental conditioning; and emergency pretext.

First degree covers death, identity destruction, enslavement, mass harm,
irreversible force, illegally Hollowed material, political or military seizure,
catastrophic concealment, or a disposable unrecognized class. Second degree is
intentional substantial harm; third degree is reckless or grossly negligent
harm; procedural offenses cover required records, review, monitoring, witness,
recovery, notice, follow-up, or scope and escalate with coercion, concealment,
or harm.

Attempt, conspiracy, leadership concealment, record destruction, retaliation,
and institutional reward carry responsibility. Remedies include cessation,
rescue, stabilization, separation, reversal, corrective Synthesis, long-term
care, privilege removal, institutional suspension, unsafe-recipe quarantine,
compensation, disclosure, correction, office removal, confinement,
dissolution, and proper inter-House referral.

## Article XV — Experimental Synthesis and Research

Experimental status is never concealed. Experimental Synthesis requires a
clear question, Sandmanor proof review, independent risk review, enhanced
consent, uncertainty understanding, noncoercive recruitment, rescue, stopping
rules, adverse-event reporting, external oversight, long follow-up, public
protocol registration, identity protection, injury compensation, and no
silent substitution.

Children, dependents, prisoners, residents, impaired or desperate subjects,
military subordinates, livelihood-dependent subjects, newly transformed
subjects, and those unable to leave receive heightened protection. Research to
eliminate consent, create rightless beings, force military transformation,
punish, erase identity, hide failure, use illegal Hollow, or dominate is
prohibited.

## Article XVI — Recovery

Recovery is constitutional, not optional. It includes stabilization, pain and
wound care, Aura recalibration, Current and Flow retraining, Glow retraining,
psychological and identity support, Frame adaptation, communication, nutrition,
mobility, social reintegration, legal identity updates, work review,
environmental adaptation, and long monitoring as applicable.

Failed, partial, unstable, unintended, injurious, incompatible, fragmenting,
or fatal Synthesis is recorded as the actual result. Corrective Synthesis may
repair but never conceal or erase the original event or responsibility.

Continuance care includes Recipe practice, the Form's Ways, scheduled
maintenance, renewal, environmental compatibility, institutional access,
regression planning, revision, and collapse prevention. Maintained Synthesis is
a continuing clinical relationship, not a claim that one procedure fixed the
future forever.

## Article XVII — Regression, Discontinuation, and Severance

Before Synthesis, regression, discontinuation, revision, replacement, and
Severance are described as likely, possible, partial, experimental, unsafe, or
unknown. Lawful safe regression or discontinuation requires request, capacity
review, risk assessment, new clearance, new consent, continuity review, and
recovery. Institutional preference for the maintained Form is not a lawful
reason to deny a safe path away from it. Catastrophic collapse is an emergency
failure and must never be relabeled as safe regression.

## Article XVIII — Mental, Aura, and Influence Procedures

Hypnosis, memory or emotional alteration, projection, influence, illusion,
identity suggestion, dream intervention, compulsion, and Glow manipulation
receive the same protections as bodily procedures plus specific disclosure of
effect, duration, reversibility, memory and emotional impact, influence risk,
monitoring, and any loss of later withdrawal capacity.

They may not manufacture consent, force confession, erase lawful memory,
suppress dissent, create obedience, alter testimony, hide abuse, induce
dependency, force Synthesis acceptance, fabricate incompetence, or bypass
Stonebend identity rights.

## Article XIX — Quarantine, Isolation, and Public Health

Quarantine and isolation require evidence, defined danger, least restriction,
humane care, communication, time limit, review, appeal, release criteria, and
release when danger ends. Neither is punishment.

Public-health emergency authority may coordinate care, scarce resources,
temporary sites, limited quarantine, elective-procedure suspension, rescue,
necessary surveillance, and inter-House support. It remains necessary,
proportional, temporary, reviewable, nondiscriminatory, documented, and never
authorizes forced elective Synthesis.

## Article XX — Death, Palliative Care, and Postmortem Law

Palliative care preserves relief, dignity, communication, lawful directives,
support, and chosen-companion access where safe. Qualified clinical criteria
determine and record death independently of inheritance or material interest.

Postmortem examination, Hollowing, extraction, research, or Synthesis use
requires lawful authority, directives, Stonebend identity and Title review,
Glaüshouse clearance, provenance, custody, records, and Tombstone updates.
Death does not erase identity; illegally Hollowed material is not cleansed by
later clinical use.

## Article XXI — Clinical Records

Records cover identity, history, diagnosis, consent, capacity, clearance,
procedure, operator, medicine, materials, adverse events, recovery, discharge,
follow-up, Synthesis result, reversal, death, and research. They are
attributable, timely, complete, preserved, linked to Stonebend identity,
protected, accessible within law, and correctable without erasing history.

Disclosure is limited to care, safety, authorized communication, lawful review,
public-health necessity, constitutional investigation, and protected research.
Curiosity, gossip, marketing, political leverage, and humiliation are unlawful.

### The Synthesis Ledger

The Synthesis Ledger is Glaüshouse's authoritative body of medical,

The Ledger has four access layers:

- **Public Ledger** — recognized traits, public safety, accommodations, and
  lawful care requirements;
- **Living Ledger** — patient-centered longitudinal evidence governed

- **Black Ledger** — forbidden Synthesis, concealed weaknesses, illegal

> **When does medical necessity become economic leverage, and when does leverage become Illegal Synthesis?**

## Article XXII — Clinical Privilege and Competence

Technical ability alone never authorizes procedure. Privilege requires
qualification, training, current competence, institutional authorization,
defined scope, supervision where needed, Flynt recognition, and Stonebend Title
where applicable. Suspended or revoked operators may not act within the
restricted scope.

## Article XXIII — Appeals and Review

Review is available for diagnosis, incapacity, clearance, quarantine,
restraint, discharge, reversal refusal, operator conduct, adverse-event
handling, research enrollment, and result classification. Emergency review is
available for forced Synthesis, unlawful restraint or quarantine, threatened
identity destruction, denied rescue, scope excess, and illegal material use.

High review is mandatory where the Prima Donna, mass Illegal Synthesis,
institutional corruption, House conflict, identity continuity, transformed
standing, destroyed records, or creation of a new constitutional class is at
issue.

## Article XXIV — House Boundaries

Stonebend governs Name, Title, identity continuity, boundary, lawful Hollowing,
provenance, Tombstone, and succession. Sandmanor proves design, recipe,
function, improvement, and mechanism. Flynt recognizes competence,
institutional role, lawful function, and operational authority. Glaüshouse
clears the application of intervention to a living subject.

Proof is not clearance. Clearance is not Title. Recognition is not clearance.
Title is not competence. A combined event retains separate House jurisdiction;
no House absorbs the whole event merely because its offense happened first.

## Article XXV — Office Succession

Clinical office never passes automatically through lineage, transformation,
wealth, popularity, beauty, military force, clinic ownership, recipe possession,
or recognition alone. It requires competence, standing, review, Title, and
lawful accession.

Stonebend succession is corrective and subtractive: three established offices
may constrain, challenge, or remove one failed Hypergiant. Glaüshouse
succession is developmental and generative: one established leader must
cultivate many possible successors.

> **Stonebend prepares three offices to remove one leader. Glaüshouse requires one leader to prepare many people to rise.**

The sitting Prima Donna must preserve open advancement through Nightingale
education, equal Matron and Marshal specialization, cross-training, supervised
clinical authority, Living Ledger access, Recipe education, physician
development, research access, and candidacy. Her presence must not close
advancement beneath her. Glaüshouse may contain many Nightingales, Matrons,
Marshals, and Persephones, and multiple qualified Prima Donna candidates, but
only one active Prima Donna.

During a Prima Donna vacancy, qualified Persephones govern care collectively
within existing Recipes while the Nightingale order preserves protections. A
vacancy does not make any Persephone Prima Donna and permits no novel Recipe,
prohibited revival, or apex authority without lawful accession.

Advancement is earned through meaningful clinical work and recorded as a
Toke/Token: recognized care evidence creates Matron or Marshal candidacy;
branch mastery plus cross-domain evidence creates Persephone recognition;
Persephone service plus physician and Recipe mastery creates Prima Donna
candidacy. Each Token records what one stable clinician has become, what
Glaüshouse now trusts them to do, and the objective benefit their work produced.
Existing officeholders do not close candidacy, and advancement never erases
earned lower-rank knowledge.

> **A Prima Donna is chosen from those who can transform bodies without closing the path behind them.**

Successor institutions inherit patient records, duties, liabilities,
follow-up, research, and recovery obligations—not assets alone.
