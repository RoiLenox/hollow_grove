Status: canonical migration conformance record

## Frozen Boundary

The Sandmanor migration is implemented above Constitutional Runtime V2. It
does not modify the universal recursion kernel. Current Runtime V2 remains
authoritative for Bonds, signed Current and Aura, shared House decisions,
common regional Synthesis causality, persistence, replay, and provenance.
Sandmanor proves; it never substitutes proof for Stonebend Title, Glaüshouse
clearance or consent, Flynt recognition, Synthesis execution, or office.

| Subject | Stable placement | One superior or governing source |
|---|---|---|
| Minorians / Gnomes | `role.sandmanor.minorian`; `group.sandmanor.minorians` | equal interior civic tradition within the House-wide proof body |

Exactly one active Sandman is valid. An active accession references a completed
reciprocal Contest, its winner, resolved challenges, Stonebend Title, Flynt
recognition, a public statement of learning, and a Seal. Heredity, combat,
popularity, transformation, recognition alone, and legacy progression are
rejected authority origins.

## Determinism and Ambiguity Audit

- Every institution and office has one stable placement.
- Minorians and Minoans have equal standing and distinct interior/exterior
  domains; neither is subordinate.
- The Sandmen compatibility ID is a neutral proof-body adapter, not a third
  civic people, rival authority, or separate government.
- The Sandman has one lawful authority origin and one active holder invariant.
- Every proof identifies claim, scope, evidence, exact authority, and the
  applicable version; high-reliance claims require independent reproduction.
- Actual failures remain evidence. Prototype, simulation, revision, emergency
  status, and Tombstone status cannot be laundered into current proof.
- Intellectual lineage and successor correction obligations remain preserved.
- `Gnome → Minotaur` and `Elf → Centaur` each have one exact regional meaning.
- No proof, office, mastery, authorship, credential, consent, clearance, or
  recognition is inferred from legacy progression.
- No Sandmanor-specific type or authority enters the universal recursion kernel.
- Prefog possibility and Prefig prototype are never proof. Only the existing
  evidence-bearing proof lifecycle may advance a Prefig source.
- Failed Prefig evidence may return to Prefog for revision without erasure.
- Minotaur is mature cultivated Prefog at Aura Field; Centaur is mature
  embodied Prefig at Aura Beach and Current Sea.
- `institution.sandmanor.sandmen` remains only the compatibility proof-body
  adapter.
