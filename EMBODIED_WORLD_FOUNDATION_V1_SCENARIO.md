# The Hidden Wound and the Riptide Misdirection

## Purpose

Exercise the completed foundation through one coherent scenario without adding new semantic laws.

## Outline

1. A Being arrives with a hidden wound or concealed internal condition.

   Exercises:
   - Being/Object ontology
   - clinical Glow target selection

2. A Nightingale uses:

   - `Glow + Show + Beam + Hidden Wound + Proxy + Diagnose`

   to identify the condition.

   Exercises:
   - `Glow`
   - `Show -> Beam`
   - `Proxy`
   - diagnostic Light Aura candidate resolution

3. The Nightingale routes the finding toward Sandmanor using Light Aura.

   Possible route:

   - `Glausbahn` for direct clinical explanation
   - `Current Seanad` for negotiated contextual interpretation

   Exercises:
   - Light direction
   - route-aware polarity
   - requested versus inferred polarity

4. A Minorian measures and maps the finding.

   Exercises:
   - civic body
   - Sandmanor Interior
   - Light Beam legibility

5. A Minoan presents the condition legibly.

   Exercises:
   - civic body
   - Sandmanor Exterior
   - Light Gleam responsible presentation

6. A hostile or ambiguous Siren uses Dark Aura through the Glaüshouse/Flynt direction.

   Possible route:

   - `Riptide` for immersive misdirection and attention undertow
   - `Boardwalk` for controlled public spectacle

7. The Siren attempts either:

   - `Glow + Show + Beam + Response Network + Moxy + Misdirect`
   - or `Glow + Grit + Gleam + Crowd + Proxy + Project Dread`

   Exercises:
   - Dark Beam or Dark Gleam
   - `Moxy` or `Proxy`
   - coercive pressure versus proportional defense

8. The system evaluates:

   - truthfulness
   - consent
   - agency
   - pressure
   - proportionality
   - consequence
   - route alignment
   - requested versus inferred polarity

9. A Warden closes the immediate breach.

   Exercises:
   - civic body closure response
   - physical containment distinct from Aura pressure

10. Geralds carry the physical recovery load.

    Exercises:
    - civic circulation
    - material recovery burden

11. The scenario may include one Current Grip action:

    - `Troll + BridgeGrip` to hold a failing crossing
    - or `Troglodyte + retained TinkerGrip` to operate a precise mechanism despite apex Form

    Exercises:
    - inherited SkillRoot::Grip
    - lower-expression retention
    - Object-family and scale-aware resolution

12. Every action still resolves through:

    - `candidate Move -> Recipe -> V2 -> frozen V1.1`

## Integration Gap

No new semantic gap is declared by this scenario document itself.

If integration testing later exposes a real gap, record it from observed use rather than redesigning the foundation in advance.
