## Canonical decision

The Gimp does not become a Pimp by being cured, purified, made attractive, or
made superior. The Gimp remains the same continuous Being and may earn a new,
bounded cultural function: a Pimp who brokers visibility, shelter, and
opportunity without ownership or coercion.

“Gimp” is usable only as this character's self-chosen, reclaimed Boardwalk
title. It is never a diagnosis, a disability category, a slur for another
Being, or a status imposed on an unwilling person. “Pimp” is likewise a
patronage and visibility function, not a license for sexual access, control,
or debt.

The hidden-operator quality can use the **Keyser Söze archetype**—a feared or
rumored broker whose reach is larger than their public appearance—without
copying that character or treating secrecy as proof. The legend must remain
testable: witnesses may contradict it, affiliates may leave, and records must
show what the broker actually did.

The visual memory of the *Pulp Fiction* Gimp may inform the silhouette—silent,
masked, unsettling, and underestimated—but not the captivity, sexual violence,
or ownership surrounding that character. In Hollow Grove, the Gimp's silence
is chosen discipline and sovereign refusal. The underworld is a hidden civic
layer of routes, information, sanctuary, repair, and contested debts; it is not
a trafficking network.

The resulting title is **Gimp of the Underway**, also called the **Silent King
of the Underway** (publicly shortened to **the Gimp**, and later **Pimp of the
Boardwalk** when the patronage function is recognized). “King” describes cultural reach and witness-deferred authority,
not a constitutional office. The King may withhold speech, delegate through
signs and trusted Goons, and know every passage while remaining accountable to
evidence, refusal, and exit.

## The trial's real question

This is not a purity test. It is a **Sympiote integrity trial**:

> Can a living graft and its host produce a new social function while preserving
> identity, consent, refusal, bodily autonomy, and the truth of the prior life?

“Purity” may appear only as an in-world character's misleading rhetoric. The
authoritative record uses `integrity`, `reciprocity`, and `continuity` instead.

| Clear | Glaüshouse | May this Sympiote intervention proceed for this host now? | scope-limited consent and recovery clearance | withdrawn consent, replacement behavior, bodily risk, or no rescue path |
| Recognize | Flynt | What bounded Pimp function was actually demonstrated? | limited recognition with jurisdiction, duration, and revocation | competence not shown, ownership claim, or authority inflation |

The gates are conditional rather than a compulsory conveyor belt. A failed or
partial result is preserved evidence, not a moral failure and not a reason to
force another attempt.

## What the transformed role can do

The former Gimp may become the **Pimp of the Boardwalk**, or another explicitly
named local title, if the record supports it. The role may:

- broker disclosed opportunities and introductions;
- convert private knowledge of exclusion into safer routes back into public
  life;
- sponsor adult affiliates who remain independent Hoes or Goons by their own
  separate choice;
- use a mask, rumor, aliases, and controlled visibility as strategy;
- accept challenge, publish terms, and release an affiliate without retaliation.

As Silent King of the Underway, the role may also maintain a protected route
network and speak through appointed witnesses. Silence cannot be used to hide
terms, revoke consent, evade a challenge, or manufacture an uncheckable myth.

The role may not own a person, infer sexual consent from a title, convert
patronage into bodily entitlement, inherit another's debt, grant Name/Title/
office/Synthesis by declaration, or use the Sympiote to erase pain, history,
or refusal.

## Sympiote outcomes

The trial has five lawful outcomes:

1. **Reciprocal Pimp Synthesis** — the host and graft remain mutually legible;
   the new broker function is recognized within scope.
2. **Partial Integration** — a useful capacity exists, but the Pimp function
   is deferred or narrowed.
3. **Host Rejection** — the host refuses or expels the graft; continuity and
   refusal are preserved.
4. **Sympiote Rejection** — the graft attempts replacement, coercive
   overcorrection, or an unconsented body; intervention stops.
5. **Failed Trial / No Transformation** — evidence is insufficient or unsafe;
   the Gimp remains the Gimp without penalty.

No outcome changes a Being's worth. A new title is a revocable cultural role,
never a biological caste or constitutional promotion.
