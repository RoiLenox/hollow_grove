## Common Synthesis origin, separate targets

External item Synthesis, living grafts, partial changes and whole Forms share
the Glaüshouse root. The affected Object or Being keeps its own identity,
method, evidence, result and maintenance history. Ordinary repair does not
become Synthesis retroactively, and item work does not promote its maker's
Sympiote grade. The living three-grade foundation is one path inside the wider
practice, not a numbering of item-to-body scales. Material rules, item methods
and a unified executable model remain open.

## Independent records

Practitioner is the whole person. Unqualified Current is relational participation;
Aura is inward cultivation; Level 1 is **CURRENT + AURA → SYNTHESIS**. Faculty,
Current Form, learned Aura Set, Doctrine, profession, social role, clinical
privilege, office, cultural affiliation and Sympian continuity classification
remain distinct. Recipe possession records access to a method, not execution or
eligibility. Doctrine is strongly established but mechanically open.

Every contemplated change keeps the continuing Being/Frame, present Form and
its Continuance, relevant Current/Aura practices, known source/Recipe evidence,
requested result, applicable proof/clearance, consent, risks, alternatives,
maintenance environment, support/recovery and actual result distinguishable.
Record what is unknown. No uniform XP threshold, timer, multiplier, food meter
or universal biological mechanism is defined by this crosswalk.

Fangs → Claws → Horns → Wing Buds → Winglets → Wings

## Failure and continuity requirements

Preserve host rejection, Sympiote rejection, partial integration, failed
integration, reciprocal integration and Overgrowth as distinct meanings.
Overgrowth is an emergency failure state, not a desired endpoint. A missed
condition leaves the requested change unestablished; it does not erase a prior
maintained Form or retroactively alter identity. New proof is scoped to the
actual version, environment and subject; changed conditions require review.

Record stopping conditions, rescue, actual intervention, deviations,
stabilization, follow-up, referrals and remaining responsibilities. Withdrawal
can prevent future intervention without pretending that an established body
vanishes. Refusal cannot be relabeled incapacity. Clinical safety, proof,
identity and recognition retain separate authority owners.
