# CURRENT SYNTHESIS / HOLLOW GROVE

# Rock-Bottom World, Material, Biological, Social, Medical, and Gameplay Context

Use this as authoritative design context for the TUI.

Do not simplify these concepts into generic fantasy terms. Do not reinterpret the biological substances metaphorically unless explicitly stated. Preserve the exact distinctions between Current, Hollow, Aura, the House rocks, living Frames, social Structures, and operational Systems.

This is intended to establish the world from the lowest material level upward so that future implementation, generation, and reasoning remain consistent.

---

# 1. Bedrock Cosmology

The fundamental laws remain:

> **Current repeats.**
> **Aura reveals.**
> **Relativity bends.**
> **Synthesis transforms.**

These are not merely poetic themes. They describe the basic operations of the world.

The canonical additive Hueman faculty mapping is:

* Stonebend → Body → Presynce
* Flynt → Spirit → Resynce
* Glaüshouse → Mind → Precog
* Sandmanor → Soul Interior → Minorian/Gnome → Prefog
* Sandmanor → Soul Exterior → Minoan/Elf → Prefig

Presynce feels emergence. Resynce joins relation. Precog foresees consequence.
Prefog opens possibility. Prefig forms becoming. Faculties describe how a
Recipe perceives, relates, anticipates, generates, or provisionally embodies
action; they do not choose, create proof, bypass legality, or execute
Synthesis. The equal Sandmanor Soul cycle is `Prefog → Prefig → Proof →
Evidence or Failure → Revision → Prefog`.

* Current is the material of bodily continuity, circulation, repetition, force, and physical life.
* Aura is the atmospheric and perceptual medium of air, pressure, light, recognition, communication, and revelation.
* Relativity is the bending relationship between Current and Aura.
* Synthesis transforms existing materials, traits, residues, and meanings into new arrangements.

There is also a third materially distinct substance:

* Hollow is the extracted, pale, pus-like interior matter produced or recovered through Hollowing.

Current, Hollow, and Aura must not be collapsed into one another.

# 2. The Three Primary Substances

The canonical material mapping is:

> **Current = blood**
> **Hollow = pus**
> **Aura = air, pressure, and light**

This mapping must remain direct and simple.

Do not explain Hollow as blood plasma.

Do not describe Hollow as clear blood.

Do not describe Hollow as merely blood without red cells.

Do not describe Hollow as Current mixed with waste.

Do not describe Hollow as one possible kind of pus.

The formal rule is:

> **Pus is Hollow.**

Pus is the literal biological and visual basis of Hollow in this setting.

Likewise:

> **Blood is Current.**

And:

> **Air, pressure, and light are Aura.**

The substances can interact, but they are separate.

---

## 2.1 Current

Current is red, circulating, internal, physical, and repetitive.

Current corresponds to:

* blood
* bodily circulation
* momentum
* repeated motion
* force
* physical energy
* metabolic continuity
* fuel
* pressure moving through vessels
* bodily survival
* physical memory expressed through repeated action

Current moves.

Current returns.

Current circulates through living Frames and operational Systems.

Canonical operation:

> **Current circulates.**

Current is not Hollow.

Current is not Aura.

---

## 2.2 Hollow

Hollow is pale, thick, nutrient-rich, extracted, residual, and pus-like.

The most important literal statement is:

> **Hollow is pus.**

Hollow may visually resemble:

* pus
* white oil
* cream
* pale gel
* rendered fat
* liquid pearl
* nutrient slurry
* thick medicinal discharge

However, the direct biological reference is pus.

Hollow represents the useful interior substance extracted from something.

It may contain:

* raw nutrients
* oils
* fats
* minerals
* marrow-like richness
* useful tissue
* dissolved resources
* organic compounds
* concentrated interior value
* processed biological material
* magical potential held in material form

Hollow is not automatically useless waste.

It is extracted value.

It may be unpleasant, impure, fertile, medicinal, industrial, magical, or dangerous depending on what it came from and how it is treated.

Canonical operation:

> **Hollow accumulates and transforms.**

Its exact final magical abilities are intentionally not fully decided yet.

Do not prematurely lock Hollow into only one function such as fuel, medicine, mutation, crafting, or healing.

Hollow may later support several of these depending on source, preparation, refinement, and Recipe.

---

## 2.3 Aura

Aura is clear, atmospheric, radiant, invisible, pressing, and revealing.

Aura corresponds to:

* air
* breath
* atmospheric pressure
* light
* color
* perception
* recognition
* signaling
* influence
* projection
* illusion
* visibility
* reputation
* shared atmosphere

Aura surrounds things rather than circulating through them in the same way as Current.

Aura can press inward, radiate outward, reveal form, distort perception, or carry signals.

Canonical operation:

> **Aura surrounds, presses, and reveals.**

Aura is not blood.

Aura is not pus.

# 3. Hollow, Hollowing, and Hollowed

Use only the following three canonical terms:

Hollow is what is extracted.

It is the raw interior value, nutrients, useful substance, oils, minerals, tissue, and concentrated material taken from inside something.

Its primary visual and biological form is pus.

* seeds
* roots

* ores
* machines
* magical creatures

The exact composition depends on the source.

The universal category remains Hollow.

---

## 3.2 Hollowing

Hollowing is controlled action upon the interior of an existing subject or
material through opening, exposure, extraction, draining, separation,
inspection, preservation, or removal.

The simplest image of **extraction Hollowing**, the form represented by the
legacy Hollow fixture, is:

> **Extract bounded usable resources while preserving the lawful remainder.**

Hollowing can resemble:

* dressing an animal
* skinning and processing a carcass
* removing organs
* rendering fat
* extracting marrow
* draining pus
* pressing oil from seeds
* removing pulp from fruit
* drawing sap from a tree
* extracting resin
* mining ore from rock
* stripping minerals from stone
* draining useful fluid from a gland
* removing internal machine components
* salvaging a ruin while leaving its shell
* extracting useful material from compost
* harvesting the interior of a monster while preserving its hide

Extraction remains a major form of Hollowing, but not its whole constitutional
meaning. Material Hollowing may instead refine Current by separating and
removing authorized inert, unstable, contaminating, or obstructive burden while
preserving essential fractions and the continuing identity of the whole.

Hollowing is not merely creating an empty chamber. It is not simply placing
Current inside a container. It is not an arbitrary magical conversion:
lawful refinement retains the physical logic of opening, separation,
measurement, preservation, provenance, and removal.

Canonical definitions:

> **Extraction Hollowing removes a bounded interior resource while preserving
> the lawful remainder.**

> **Refinement Hollowing removes authorized burden while preserving essential
> support, provenance, and the identity of the whole.**

Stonebend is especially associated with deliberate Hollowing because Stonebend understands:

* boundaries
* shells
* pressure
* tools
* cutting
* extraction
* mining
* anatomical structure
* protected interiors
* preservation of durable form

Stonebend may develop professional, industrial, anatomical, or laboratory Hollowing techniques.

The parent Stonebend Constitution governs the shared authority, consent,
evidence, custody, continuity, restoration, and Seal requirements. The first
Stonebend foundation governs the narrow Current-to-Aether batch proof.

---

## 3.3 Hollowed

Hollowed describes a subject or material after a Hollowing operation. In
extraction Hollowing, the bounded interior resource has been removed. In
refinement Hollowing, the continuing whole remains with essential fractions
intact and authorized burden removed.

What remains may be:

* hide
* skin
* shell
* husk
* casing
* skeleton
* bone
* armor
* walls
* vessel
* emptied rock
* preserved exterior
* machine chassis
* abandoned body-form
* architectural shell

Hollowed does not necessarily mean useless.

A Hollowed remainder may become:

* armor
* clothing
* containers
* tools
* buildings
* trophies
* ceremonial objects
* machine housings
* weapon casings
* artificial body shells
* Frame components
* preserved Structures

Canonical operation:

```text
Whole
→ Hollowing
→ Hollow + Hollowed
```

Where:

* Hollow is the extracted interior value.
* Hollowed is the remaining outer form.

Strong compressed law:

> **Hollow is taken.
> Hollowing takes it.
> Hollowed is what remains.**

# 5. Formal World Ontology

Hollow Grove uses four formal top-level categories:

* **Frame**
* **Scene**
* **Structure**
* **System**

These categories exist to prevent the word Frame from becoming generic.

---

## 5.1 Frame

Frame is exclusive to named living-mech forms.

A Frame is:

> **The stabilized living-mech body that a Being inhabits or becomes.**

Examples:

* Hueman
* Gremlin
* Goblin
* Pixy
* Sprite
* Werewolf
* Gargoyle
* Merman
* Troglodyte
* Muse

Do not use Frame for:

* cities
* buildings
* hospitals
* roads
* governments
* institutions
* environments
* scenes
* societies
* transportation networks

Frame is sacred to living-mech identity.

---

## 5.2 Scene

A Scene is an active situation or context in which Frames interact.

Examples:

* a surgery
* a street market
* a full-moon battle
* a race
* a medical emergency
* an Aura harvest
* a courtroom hearing
* a transformation
* a rescue operation
* a hunt in Aura Basin

A Scene answers:

> **What is happening here?**

Scenes change over time.

The same Structure can host many Scenes.

---

## 5.3 Structure

A Structure is a built, grown, preserved, or arranged form that shapes what can occur.

Examples:

* hospital
* road
* city
* bridge
* mine
* workshop
* farm
* house
* fortress
* compost bed
* raceway
* ship
* care center
* institution
* market
* body organ when discussed structurally

A Structure answers:

> **What has been built, grown, or arranged here?**

Structures may be Hollowed without becoming Frames.

---

## 5.4 System

A System is the operational relationship connecting Frames, Scenes, and Structures through time.

Examples:

* medical-care network
* transportation network
* immune system
* trade system
* political system
* hospital administration
* Aura Field production system
* emergency-response system
* Hollowing industry
* crafting economy
* decision kernel

A System answers:

> **How do all these parts continue operating together?**

---

## 5.5 Scale relationship

Canonical scale grammar:

> **Frames act within Scenes.**
> **Scenes occur inside and around Structures.**
> **Systems connect Frames, Scenes, and Structures across time.**

Example:

* Gargoyle surgeon = Frame
* operation = Scene
* Glaüshouse hospital = Structure
* medical network = System

# 12. Stonebend Constitutional Body

Stonebend names. It protects identity, Title, structure, boundary, continuity, record, lineage, custody, inheritance, lawful Hollowing, and durable historical existence.

Its constitutional people are the Geralds. Membership among the Geralds provides civic standing; it never implies office.

* Proliteriate — the permanent distributed public network that witnesses public Yield through bounded temporary mandates;

They do not form three equal sovereigns. The Freemason forges the Claim, the
Hypergiant bears the Title, and the Proliteriate shields the Yield.

# 14. Sandmanor Peoples

* Minorians are Gnomes.
* Minoans are Elves.

They are equal civic traditions. Do not reverse, collapse, or rank them.

Break may host Manticorp maritime training, but Manticorp remains a Flynt

mantles:

```text
Gnome → Minotaur / Guardian of the Fields
→ Hecaton / Guardian of the Whole Farm

Elf → Centaur / Guardian of the Beach
→ Pegasus / Guardian of the Horizon
```

for the same Form-and-mantle tradition, not another creature, office, or
