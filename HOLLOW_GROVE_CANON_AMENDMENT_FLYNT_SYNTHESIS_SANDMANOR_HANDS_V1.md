## I. Flynt synthesis order

The locked order is:

```text
Gargoyle / Bolo
+ Werewolf / Crossbow
+ Mariner / Harpoon
→ Manticorp / combined synthesis
→ Chimera / perfected synthesis
```

**Manticorp is combined synthesis.** It is the first successful integration of
the previously isolated Flynt disciplines across any relevant synthesis track.

**Chimera is perfected synthesis.** It is the refined, coherent, disciplined
perfection of an already combined Manticorp synthesis.

Manticorp and Chimera are synthesis states, not fourth and fifth Mystery Men
schools. Hunter/Gargoyle, Chief/Werewolf, and Mariner/Mariner remain the three
isolated specialist disciplines.

The three tracks remain distinct and independently refinable:

1. Current Synthesis;
2. Aura Synthesis;
3. Weapon Synthesis.

A practitioner or system may therefore reach Manticorp or Chimera refinement
on one track without automatically reaching the same state on the others.
Exact mixed-state eligibility, maintenance, failure, recovery, and interface
rules remain open.

The combined Manticorp weapon must preserve restraint/binding, ranged
projection, and tethering/anchoring in one coherent system. Its exact
mechanism remains open. The exact perfected Chimera weapon form also remains
open.

### Manticorp institution and Manticore compatibility

The existing `flynt.institution.manticorp` identifier remains valid.
Manticorp's military body is now understood as the institutional expression,
training body, and custodian of combined-synthesis discipline; it is not an
unrelated use of the word. Membership alone does not grant a Manticorp
synthesis state.

The Tross's established Manticore mythological identity is preserved. It does
not create a third general synthesis state after Chimera. Existing runtime and
archive identifiers containing `manticore` or `divided-manticorp` remain stable
compatibility projections for Tross identity, Continuance, and Recipe
provenance. They must not be used to reverse the amended Manticorp → Chimera
order.

The unique constitutional Chimera remains First Companion and the foremost
constitutional embodiment of perfected synthesis. The identity/office record
and the generally named perfected synthesis state are different axes; neither
creates additional companions or offices.

## II. Sandmanor Hands and sovereign culmination

The complete locked branches are:

```text
Minorian / Prefog
Gnome → Faun → Minotaur → Hecaton
Grip → Traction → Leverage → Multiplication

Minoan / Prefig
Elf → Satyr → Centaur → Pegasus
Balance → Agility → Momentum → Flight
```

The fourth identity in each line is a perfected sovereign Hand culmination,
not an ordinary fourth biological Form transition:

- **Hecaton is perfected force and the Right Hand of The Sandman.**
- **Pegasus is perfected motion and the Left Hand of The Sandman.**
- **The Sandman is command of both and the final sovereign culmination above
  both Hands.**

The Sandman is not a fifth biological Form after either branch. The Sandman is
the singular sovereign whole that reconciles and commands both perfected Hands
without erasing their Minorian and Minoan distinctions.

Existing constitutional and runtime separation among three biological Forms,
Hand investiture, guardian mantle, social role, Aura mastery, and sovereign
office remains correct. A `SandmanorForm` transition therefore still ends at
Minotaur or Centaur; the complete cultural/progression branch continues through
a separate lawful Hand investiture. Deck Nature-path identifiers for Hecaton
and Pegasus are canonical projections of those Hand culminations.

The exact appointment, removal, and succession mechanics for each Hand remain
open. The Contest of Improvement and the accession of the singular Sandman
remain separate from merely possessing either Hand.

## III. Superseded readings

The following readings are superseded as active canon but retained wherever
needed as identified historical or machine compatibility material:

- `Chimera → Manticore` as Flynt's general synthesis mastery order;
- Chimera as only a lower or selective synthesis before a later general apex;
- Manticorp as only an unrelated military institution with no synthesis-state
  meaning;
- `Chimera → Manticorp` as the Deck's mastery order;
- the claim that Hecaton and Pegasus are outside the complete Sandmanor branch
  lines;
- any failure to identify Pegasus as Left Hand, Hecaton as Right Hand, and The
  Sandman as the sovereign culmination above both.

Historical names and stable IDs do not regain superseded semantics merely
because they remain readable in an archive.
