# Hueman v0.1.0

Date: 2026-07-09

## Status

Hueman is the standalone game expression of Hollow Grove.
It is not the Hollow Grove kernel.
It is not the Hollow Grove operating system.
It is not a fake file manager built out of OS objects.

## Relationship To Hollow Grove

Hueman and Hollow Grove are independent but compatible.

- Hollow Grove is the broader constitutional and operating environment.
- Hueman is the contained playable civilization sandbox.
- They share constitutional language without requiring one another to function.

Current Synthesis remains a read-only intermediate layer in this repository, but Hueman's long-term identity is not limited to consuming artifact outputs.

## Core Vision

Hueman should feel like:

- a civilization-building game
- a persistent adventure world
- a profession-driven RPG
- a sandbox
- a social and collaborative world
- a modern engineering playground
- a cozy creative environment

The operating phrase is:

> A civilization simulator disguised as an adventure game.

The game should be fun before the player fully understands its educational depth.

## The Player

The player is always Hueman.

- the Hueman avatar is permanent
- ranks, forms, and professions do not erase Hueman identity
- Gremlin, Goblin, Sprite, Pixy, Ghoul, Troll, and related forms are not hereditary biological races
- transformation comes through learning, competence, Synthesis, and refinement

## Player Grammar

### Current Flow

Movement through the world:

- walking
- running
- roads
- rivers
- vehicles
- climbing
- flying
- infrastructure and traversal

### Aura View

How the player understands what is present:

- lenses
- visors
- scopes
- overlays
- professional sight

### Current Form

Extraordinary embodiment used when ordinary Hueman capability is not enough:

- giant or work-beast forms
- emergency capability
- pressure-bearing transformations

### Aura Glow

Directional resonance:

- hot and cold guidance
- resource proximity
- nearby need
- hidden opportunities
- synthesis sites

Aura Glow should guide, not dominate.

## Development

Hueman develops through two paired progressions:

- Aura See
- Current Be

And two paired systems:

- Skill Tree for ordinary Hueman competence
- Transformation Tree for extraordinary form capacity

Core rule:

> Skill proves competence.
> Transformation expands capacity.

Equipment remains separate from transformation.

## Confirmed Form Origins

Current-side confirmed origins:

- Gremlin originates in Flynt
- Goblin originates in Stonebend
- their paths may intersect at Ghoul

Aura-side confirmed origins:

- Sprite originates in Glaüshouse
- Pixy originates in Sandmanor
- their paths may intersect at Faerie

These are braided origins, not a forced linear evolution ladder.
Later ownership of Troll, Ork, Ogre, Troglodyte, Spirit, Nymph, Siren, and Muse remains open.

## Starting Position

The player begins as Hueman in or near Aura Ridge.
The opening should expose the player to multiple constitutional possibilities without hard-locking them into a kingdom or form.

Current confirmed principles:

- Hueman begins before major kingdom form commitment
- Aura Ridge matters
- Flynt remains constitutionally present even if its physical city is not the literal opening tile
- exact opening geometry remains open

## Kingdom Roles In Hueman

- Stonebend names, titles, crafts, and reallocates pressure
- Sandmanor proves, measures, configures, and improves
- Glaüshouse clears, repairs, restores, and performs Synthesis
- Flynt engineers, deploys, and recognizes real-world use

These are constitutional functions first, not mere biome themes.

## Materials

The current signature materials are:

- Stonebend: Mercury Mirror
- Sandmanor: Prism Sand
- Glaüshouse: Glaüs Gel
- Flynt: Opal Oil

Accepted alloys:

- Mercurite
- Prismiron
- Glaüsteel
- Opaltanium

Open composite naming remains intentionally modular.

## Routes

Confirmed route pairs:

- Stonebend <-> Sandmanor: Aura Way / Mnt. Aura
- Stonebend <-> Flynt: Basin Motorspeedway / Stairway to Heaven

Straight routes are process.
Curved routes are transformation.

Not every unresolved idea belongs on Aura Ridge.
Bedrock is for stable constitutional placement.
Routes, borders, ridges, basins, and fields are where ideas travel and mature.

## Quest And Craft Law

Small craft should stay light.
Major civic work should use the full loop:

> Name it -> Prove it -> Clear it -> Recognize it

The game should prefer civilization problems over chore lists.
Combat remains valid, but it is not the only meaningful contribution.

## Play Scales

Hueman should support:

- everyday local play
- deeper professional play
- the long Sovereign Hueman arc

Sovereign Hueman is the largest long-term path, not the only point of the game.

## Vertical Slice Target

The current recommended first slice is still small and practical:

1. begin as Hueman in a compact Aura Field or nearby region
2. expose one local need
3. use Aura Glow to locate resources
4. use one Aura View lens
5. gather one resource chain
6. name a simple tool or project
7. prove it with a short test
8. clear it through a small repair or integration step
9. deploy it and earn local recognition
10. unlock the first meaningful form-development node

## Tooling

- Godot 4 for Hueman
- Aseprite for pixel art
- crisp readable pixel presentation over faux-retro imitation

Do not overbuild graphics before the core loop is playable.

## Open Questions Preserved

The active canon intentionally leaves these open:

- later branch ownership after Ghoul and Faerie
- final composite names for Stonebend, Sandmanor, and Glaüshouse
- final opening map geometry
- full Hueman/Hollow Grove live integration
- full multiplayer governance
- exact Synthesis requirements for every form

## Conclusion

Hueman is a family-friendly civilization sandbox where the player becomes competent by contributing to a living society.
It speaks Hollow Grove's constitutional language without replacing Hollow Grove itself.
