# Stonebend Title Lifecycle and Constitutional Continuity V1

Status: canonical Stonebend Third Pass
Authority: additive to `STONEBEND_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`,
`STONEBEND_AURA_WAY_AETHER_HOLLOWING_FOUNDATION_V1.md`, and
`STONEBEND_THREE_GATES_OFFICES_AND_TITLE_SCOPE_V1.md`

This pass completes Stonebend's shared constitutional grammar for the life of
a Title. It does not replace Bond history, the First Pass material continuum,
the Second Pass three-gate topology, or the complete Lazerhorn succession
path. It adapts their stable identities, evidence discipline, challenges,
scope boundaries, office tenures, and Tombstones.

## 1. Governing law

> A Title is not praise. It is a bounded public Claim backed by evidence.

> Recognition begins a Title. Continuance sustains it.

A Title remains lawful through the support its policy actually requires. That
support may include surviving identity, preserved Form, genuine evidence,
competence, custody, seals, maintenance, renewal, gate scope, bounded conduct,
and accountability for Yield. No universal calendar interval or administrative
schedule applies to every Title.

Stonebend Title recognition and Flynt recognition remain different acts.
Stonebend recognition accepts that a Name and bounded Claim may stand in the
public record. Flynt recognition concerns demonstrated Function in lived use.
The older rule that Flynt recognition is not itself a Stonebend Title remains
unchanged.

## 2. Shared lifecycle grammar

The complete semantic grammar is:

```text
Name
→ Claim
→ Application
→ Evidence Assembly
→ Eligibility Review
→ Gate Review where applicable
→ Challenge Period where required
→ Recognition
→ Investiture or Activation
→ Active Title
→ Maintenance
→ Renewal
→ Continuation, Limitation, Supervision, Suspension, or Remediation
→ Restoration, Succession, Surrender, Expiration, Removal, or End of Form
→ Tombstone
```

The implementation records these meanings through `TitleLifecycleStage` and
orders them by their explicit `semantic_order`. Caller insertion order and
record position have no constitutional meaning. Each stage, term, action,
review, and Tombstone uses a stable caller-supplied identity.

Not every Title uses every stage. Policy may omit a formal application,
challenge period, gate review, investiture, recurring renewal, succession, or
another inapplicable step. A constitutional office may require nearly all of
them.

The existing Bond aggregate remains the general constitutional event and
replay authority. The Third Pass is a Stonebend policy adapter for Title scope,
activation, maintenance, renewal, intervention, and asymmetric office
continuity; it is not a second universal Bond engine.

## 3. Recognition and activation

Recognition means Stonebend accepts that the Claim is sufficiently evidenced,
bounded, and lawfully structured. It establishes the Title in the public
record.

Activation means the recognized Title begins exercising an authorized
function. Policy may require investiture, a gate scope, an active license,
delivery of a seal, acceptance of responsibility, or a named final condition.

> Recognition establishes the Title. Activation permits its exercise.

A recognized inactive Title is lawful. It cannot exercise active authority
until its activation requirements are satisfied. Activation does not create a
new Title identity.

> A name may be lawful without being universally authorized.

Recognition at one Stonebend gate still does not manufacture recognition at
another.

## 4. Policy

`TitleLifecyclePolicy` may specify:

- required pre-recognition stages;
- required gate scopes;
- explicit or requirements-driven activation;
- activation requirements;
- maintenance obligations;
- semantic renewal policy;
- optional challenge-period policy;
- succession support;
- direct-restoration eligibility;
- and permitted terminal dispositions.

No annual, five-year, majority, quorum, or percentage rule is introduced.
Challenge periods use an explicit semantic closing condition and identify
eligible challengers, affected Proliteriate nodes, evidence, activation effect,
and resolving authority. Routine Titles do not require all three principal
Stonebend powers for every dispute.

## 5. Maintenance and renewal

Maintenance is continuing support during an active term. It may establish
competence, physical upkeep, custody, current proof, lawful operation,
preserved essential Form, supervision, provenance, public accountability, or
ordinary repair.

Renewal is a formal decision that the same stable Title may enter another
recognized term or interval.

Maintenance is not renewal. Renewal is not original recognition. A Title may
require maintenance without renewal, both, or neither. A renewed term receives
a stable term identity while the core Title identity remains unchanged.

Renewal preserves:

- stable Title and subject;
- current term;
- policy;
- reviewed evidence and scopes;
- maintenance records;
- known failures and remediation;
- Yield evidence;
- authority;
- disposition;
- boundaries;
- effective sequence;
- and any next-term identity.

The supported dispositions are:

- `Renewed`;
- `RenewedWithLimitations`;
- `RemediationRequired`;
- `Deferred`;
- `Rejected`;
- and `Expired`.

A rejection or expiration does not delete the earlier term. If an active term
ends, its ending must receive a Tombstone.

## 6. Failure and proportionate consequence

The Second Pass classifications remain authoritative:

- `HonestFailure`;
- `Negligence`;
- `Fraud`;
- `Illegality`;
- `ConstitutionalHollowness`.

Honest failure may support repair, maintenance, review, retraining, limitation,
or a voluntary pause. It cannot automatically remove a Title.

Negligence may support warning, supervision, limitation, remediation,
suspension, renewal restriction, or proportionate removal when severe or
repeated.

Fraud may support immediate challenge, suspension, scope or core removal,
public-record correction, and referral to future restitution, court, or
criminal procedure.

Illegality may support emergency limitation, challenge, suspension, removal,
evidence preservation, and an explicit referral. This pass does not implement
the referred court, criminal, restitution, or complete Illegal Hollowing
procedure.

Constitutional hollowness means the supporting structure has failed while the
outer appearance remains. A core intervention requires an explicit core
challenge; appearance alone cannot silently destroy the Title.

> Do not uphold what is hollow. Do not hollow what is whole.

## 7. Bounded interventions

Every intervention identifies its precise target:

- core Title;
- one gate scope;
- activation;
- license;
- bearer tenure;
- or mandate.

`Limitation` narrows an authority while leaving it active. `Supervision`
conditions active exercise on a named authority, evidence, scope, and review
condition. `Suspension` stops present exercise without automatically
destroying the Title, another scope, history, or restoration eligibility.
`Remediation` records the corrective work required. `Removal` ends exactly the
layer it names.

Scope removal cannot silently become core removal. Operational Deployment may
be suspended while Formation Recognition and Public Circulation remain.
Qualification may remain when a high-risk license is suspended. A Hypergiant
tenure may end while Diamond persists. A witness mandate may end while the
Proliteriate persists.

## 8. Restoration

> Restoration repairs the Title. It does not erase the break.

A restoration retains the original stable Title, interruption, cause,
remediation evidence, reviewing authority, restored scope, continuing
limitations, effective boundary, and sequence. The interruption remains
historically visible.

Direct restoration is unavailable when a different constitutional path is
required:

- a removed Hypergiant must complete the full Lazerhorn succession path;
- a recalled witness requires a new network mandate;
- a removed Freemason requires independent Forge replacement review.

## 9. Terminal dispositions and Tombstones

The shared terminal dispositions are:

- `HonorableCompletion`;
- `Surrender`;
- `Expiration`;
- `Death`;
- `EndOfForm`;
- `Succession`;
- `RemovalForFailure`;
- `RemovalForFraud`;
- `RemovalForIllegality`;
- `ConstitutionalDissolution`;
- and `Supersession`.

Every ended active term links to a Tombstone. A Tombstone is continuity, not
punishment by default. Honorable completion remains constitutionally distinct
from removal for failure, fraud, or illegality.

## 10. Diamond vacancy

> During vacancy, Stonebend continues. Diamond does not secretly change hands.

Diamond remains the stable sovereign Title while vacant. No person exercises
the complete Hypergiant authority, and no remaining office absorbs Diamond.
Existing administrators and gate institutions may continue only routine work
already supported by law. Extraordinary sovereign acts remain unavailable.

`DiamondContinuityMandate` is a bounded authority record, not a bearer,
investiture, regency, Acting Hypergiant, Interim Diamond, or fourth office. It
identifies:

- stable mandate and vacancy Tombstone;
- existing legal authority;
- enumerated permitted action;
- responsible administrator;
- affected gate or domain;
- start and termination conditions;
- evidence;
- Claim, boundary, and Yield traces;
- and mandatory later review.

It may preserve records, existing obligations, infrastructure, routine gate
work, immediate boundary defense, catastrophic-failure prevention, and
succession evidence.

It may not invest Diamond, appoint a Hypergiant, create sovereign law,
permanently expand a gate scope, erase a challenge, or remove a principal power.

> Necessity may preserve the boundary. It may not inherit the crown.

Emergency actions identify the emergency, constitutional basis, responsible
authority, evidence, bounded action, termination condition, and later review.
Use cannot make emergency authority permanent.

## 11. Freemason continuity and replacement

The active High Freemason remains the singular bearer of the Freemason seal.
Candidate review may be distributed, but no collective permanent Freemason
office is created.

A candidate presents stable identity, qualification, craft and constitutional
evidence, seal-bearing competence where applicable, conflict disclosures, and
a Claim. The outgoing bearer may train, nominate, recommend, and preserve
evidence. Recommendation is evidence only.

An `IndependentForgeReview` uses stable independent examiner identities and
rejects a candidate who examines themself. A qualified replacement additionally
requires Proliteriate Yield review and, when Diamond is occupied, bounded
recognition from the active Hypergiant. Neither a Hypergiant nor a Proliteriate
witness may assume the Forge through their other authority.

One ended tenure receives a Tombstone before a replacement becomes active.
`FreemasonOfficeState` enforces no more than one active bearer.

During vacancy, prior Claims, valid seals, evidence, Tombstones, and Title
history remain preserved. Extraordinary sovereign Claims that require the
Forge may pause; routine delegated verification remains bounded by existing
authority.

> The Forge may fall silent. Its records must not disappear.

## 12. Proliteriate continuity

The Proliteriate has no total office vacancy. Nodes, mandates, and witnesses
may end or change while the network identity persists. A dissolved node remains
in history and does not erase member identities. Dissolved nodes cannot create
new mandates unless lawfully reconstituted.

Temporary witness completion or recall returns authority to the network. A new
mandate and witness may be raised through stable identities. No permanent
speaker or permanent numerical selection threshold is introduced.

> The network is permanent. Its voice is temporary.

> The voice may be recalled. The people may not be abolished.

## 13. Claim, Title, and Yield through time

Every temporal review retains all three dimensions:

- Claim asks whether evidence, provenance, Name, Form, prerequisites, and
  supporting structure remain genuine.
- Title asks whether exercise, bearer, boundary, scopes, renewal, and conduct
  remain lawful.
- Yield asks who benefits, who bears the burden, what risk and inheritance
  accumulated, and whether actual consequence still justifies continuation.

> The Claim proves that power can stand. The Title permits power to act. The Yield determines whether power deserves to continue.

Routine delegated reviewers may perform the work. Each record remains
traceable to Freemason Claim authority, Diamond-derived boundary authority,
and Proliteriate Yield accountability. Deterioration produces a targeted scope,
Claim, maintenance, or Yield review rather than automatic destruction of the
whole history.

## 14. Office continuity matrix

| Ended authority | What ends | What persists | Return path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hypergiant | bearer tenure | Diamond, ordinary administration, records, gates, succession evidence | complete Lazerhorn succession |
| High Freemason | bearer and seal authority | prior Claims, seals, evidence, Title history, Diamond | independent Forge replacement |
| Proliteriate witness or delegation | bounded mandate authority | network, lawful nodes, people, testimony, replacement capacity | new mandate |

This asymmetry is deliberate. No Regent, Acting Hypergiant, Interim Diamond,
permanent Proliteriate Speaker, or fourth sovereign office exists.
