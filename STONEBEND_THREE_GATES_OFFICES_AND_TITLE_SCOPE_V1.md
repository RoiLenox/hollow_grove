## 2. Three Constitutional Gates

Stonebend has three distinct principal constitutional gates:

1. a Flynt-facing gate;
2. a Central Junction-facing gate;
3. a Sandmanor-facing gate.

> Stonebend has three constitutional gates: one facing Flynt, one facing Central
> Junction, and one facing Sandmanor.

“Stonebend gate” remains a generic category. Earlier references to one universal
physical Stonebend Gate are superseded by these three facings. Mt. Aura is an
ideal and route landmark, not itself a gate. Central Junction is a district, not
a House.

Each gate supports both `IntoStonebend` and `OutOfStonebend`. A gate is a
constitutional interface for evidence, claims, people, objects, Titles,
warnings, failures, and bounded authority. It is not merely a checkpoint.

### 2.1 Flynt-facing gate

This gate maps to the existing Stonebend–Flynt route system:

- Stairway to Heaven;
- Basin Motor Speedway.

Its domain is the transition from recognized Form into active Function. It
handles machinery, infrastructure, industrial deployment, transportation,
defense, technical command, operational licenses, high-risk systems, and Proof
of Persistence.

Its question is:

> Can this named Form function and persist under real pressure?

Stonebend supplies lawful identity, boundary, material recognition, and Title.
Flynt supplies engineering, operation, persistence, and functional-viability
evidence. Stonebend may not fabricate Flynt's Proof of Persistence.

Inbound operational evidence, machine failures, and deployment records return
to Stonebend for boundary and Title review. Outbound recognized Forms and
bounded operational permissions may proceed to Flynt testing or deployment.

> Stonebend proves the Form can bear its name. Flynt proves the named Form can
> do its work.

### 2.2 Central Junction-facing gate

This gate maps to Central Junction through the north Craft Corridor. The
Corridor is a district approach, not a House boundary.

Its domain is the transition from lawful identity into public circulation. It
handles certified goods, property identities, professional Titles, material
batches, Current and Aether provenance, lawful measures, seals, contracts,
commercial organizations, licensed instruments, and public Claims.

Its question is:

> Can this named thing circulate without losing its identity or deceiving the
> public?

Stonebend supplies identity, boundary, provenance, measure, seal, custody
evidence, and recognized scope. Central Junction supplies exchange,
methodology, calculation, clearing, settlement, and publication.

Inbound disputes, provenance failures, and measure challenges return to
Stonebend verification. Outbound certified identity, seal, measure, custody, and
circulation scope become eligible for Central Junction processes.

Stonebend does not own or set an index. The Junction Board, South Ridge
Exchange, Clearing House, and Junction Wire retain their ratified authority.

### 2.3 Sandmanor-facing gate

This gate maps to the established Stonebend–Sandmanor route:

- Aura Way;
- Mt. Aura.

Its domain is the transition from designed possibility and professional
formation into recognized Form. It handles designed works, education,
apprenticeship, supervised practice, examination evidence, qualified
candidates, and Claims seeking lawful naming.

Its question is:

> Has this possibility become stable enough to bear a lawful name?

Sandmanor supplies Design and formation evidence. Aura Way supplies the legible
advancement record. Mt. Aura preserves the ideal. Stonebend reviews the
identity, evidence, boundary, and lawful Claim. Stonebend may not replace
Sandmanor's Proof of Reciprocity or Design authority.

Inbound formation evidence may establish recognition eligibility. Outbound
recognized Form or Title returns to practice, design, education, or service.

## 3. One Title, Independently Bounded Gate Scopes

A recognized entity retains one stable core Title identity. Gate recognitions
attach independent, bounded scopes to that Title:

- `FormationRecognition` at the Sandmanor-facing gate;
- `PublicCirculation` at the Central Junction-facing gate;
- `OperationalDeployment` at the Flynt-facing gate.

> A name may be lawful without being universally authorized.

Recognition at one gate implies nothing at another. A lawful professional may
lack industrial-command permission. A lawful machine may fail persistence. An
operational product may be barred from circulation because its provenance is
defective. A proposal may circulate without being titled as completed
construction.

Limitation, suspension, rejection, or removal of one scope does not silently
erase the core Title, unrelated scopes, or history. Destruction of core identity
requires an explicit core Title challenge.

Gate failures return typed evidence for correction, remediation, limitation,
suspension, removal, renewal, or explicit core review. Honest failure,
negligence, fraud, illegality, and constitutional hollowness remain distinct.

## 4. Constitutional Terms

### Name

A stable identifier by which a person, object, work, institution, or office is
addressed. A Name alone grants no authority.

### Claim

A proposed public assertion supported by evidence. A Claim asks Stonebend to
recognize that something may bear a Name, boundary, capacity, office, or Title.

### Title

A bounded public identity or authority recognized by Stonebend.

> A Title is not praise. It is a bounded public claim backed by evidence.

### Gate scope

A domain-specific recognition attached to one stable Title. It determines where
and how that Title may operate.

### Office

A constitutional role carrying defined duties and powers. It is distinct from
its current bearer.

### Mantle

A transferable constitutional trust attached to a role, Form, or service. A
Mantle may end while the bearer or physical Form remains.

### Form

The actual living, crafted, or material body. A Form is not automatically an
Office or Title.

### License

A bounded permission to perform an activity under defined conditions. A License
need not create a new core identity.

## 5. The Constitutional Triangle

Stonebend separates three constitutional powers:

> The Freemason forges the Claim.

> The Hypergiant bears the Title.

> The Proliteriate shields the Yield.

Claim, Title, and Yield are different domains, not three rulers.

The Claim asks whether an authority, identity, or structure was sufficiently
built and proven to stand. The Title is the bounded authority Stonebend
recognizes and permits to act. The Yield is what the authority actually
produces: public benefit and burden, infrastructure, abundance, scarcity,
safety, risk, inheritance, opportunity, damage, labor, and long consequence.

> The Claim proves that power can stand. The Title permits power to act. The
> Yield determines whether power deserves to continue.

No one constitutional power governs all three.

## 6. Diamond and the Hypergiant

Diamond is Stonebend's singular continuing sovereign Title. The Hypergiant is
the active office bearer temporarily exercising Diamond.

> Diamond belongs to Stonebend. The Hypergiant bears its weight.

> The Hypergiant holds the Title. The Title does not become the Hypergiant's
> property.

Diamond is distinct from diamond as a natural stone, material, or old House-rock
rhetoric. It is not inventory, private property, a family possession, or a
second person identity.

Diamond retains stable constitutional identity across accession, vacancy,
removal, death, surrender, succession, and historical recording. A bearer ends;
Diamond does not. When no Hypergiant is active, Diamond is vacant or unborne.
No authority transfers automatically.

The Hypergiant executes valid Stonebend law, defends boundaries, enforces lawful
Titles and judgments, maintains bounded emergency continuity, represents
Stonebend externally, and carries Diamond's obligations at the three gates.

The Hypergiant may not forge their own sovereign Claim, declare metaphysical
perfection, appoint a successor by command, erase evidence, falsify continuity,
abolish the Proliteriate, counterfeit Freemason recognition, privately own the
public Yield, own Mt. Aura, or permanently close all three gates without review.

## 7. The Freemason

The Freemason is Stonebend's singular seal-bearing constitutional craft and
legitimacy power. The existing stable High Freemason office is the active bearer
of this power; the wider Freemason institution remains the craft, record,
survey, sealing, and execution institution.

To forge is to shape, test, heat, strike, examine, measure, refine, record, and
prove under pressure. It does not mean falsify.

The Freemason governs Claim structure, constitutional evidence, succession
Claims, Title prerequisites, lawful seals, public records, material proof,
provenance, scope boundaries, and eligibility. It may reject unsupported
Claims, certify eligibility, issue seals, challenge unsupported Titles,
preserve ended records, and certify gate boundaries.

The Freemason does not exercise Diamond or rule Stonebend merely because it
validates Claims.

> The Freemason may prove that a crown can stand. The Freemason may not place it
> upon their own head by proof alone.

A Freemason who seeks Diamond requires independent examination. Their own seal
or office authority cannot satisfy that examination.

## 8. The Proliteriate

The Proliteriate is Stonebend's permanent distributed public constitutional
network. It is not one person, one permanent chamber, an ordinary parliament, a
single union, a fourth monarch, or a population category that another office
may abolish.

The minimum generic node kinds are:

1. district nodes—where people live;
2. guild or workshop nodes—what people craft, maintain, or practice;
3. labor crew or worksite nodes—who bears immediate burden and risk;
4. inherited or commonwealth community nodes—what must continue across
   generations.

People and communities may participate in several nodes without acquiring
duplicate identity.

> Every burden must have a witness. Every Yield must return to those who bore
> it.

The network is enduring; its active voice is issue-specific.

> The network is permanent. Its voice is temporary.

A mandate records stable identity, participating nodes, issue or Claim,
affected Yield, testimony, authority granted, boundaries, witness, recall,
completion, and historical disposition. Node, participant, mandate, and witness
identities are caller-supplied and never derived from position or vote order.

## 9. Temporary Witness and the Spartacus Principle

The Proliteriate may raise a temporary, recallable witness when distributed
burden must confront concentrated power.

> The Proliteriate does not elect a ruler. It raises a witness.

> Spartacus is not king of the people. He is the person through whom the people
> stand.

Spartacus is constitutional rhetoric and an archetype, not a permanent fourth
office.

A witness carries a verified bounded mandate, presents the Yield, makes burden
visible, and speaks across participating nodes for that issue. The witness may
not rule Stonebend, exercise Diamond, forge a Claim, replace the Freemason,
become Hypergiant through popularity alone, expand the mandate, or retain power
after recall or completion.

Authority returns to the network when the mandate ends. A corrupted voice may
be invalidated, but lawful nodes may raise replacement representation.

## 10. Separation of Active Authority

The active Hypergiant and active Freemason must be distinct bearers. One active
authority cannot count as two powers. Neither may manufacture independent
Proliteriate concurrence for review of their own conduct. A temporary witness
may not simultaneously exercise Diamond or the Freemason seal.

This rule concerns simultaneous authority and self-review. It does not prohibit
lawful historical transitions between offices.

## 11. One Challenge, Two Remove

> One may challenge. Two may remove. None may rule alone.

Any one constitutional power may open a formal challenge against another. The
record identifies the challenger, target, grounds, evidence, answer, review
stage, and disposition. One challenge preserves and opens review; it does not
remove the target.

The other two distinct powers must concur to remove the active bearer or active
representation of the third. One power cannot count twice. Duplicate
endorsements do not produce two-of-three concurrence.

- Freemason plus Proliteriate may remove the Hypergiant. Diamond becomes vacant;
  Diamond itself survives.
- Hypergiant plus Proliteriate may remove the active Freemason. Prior valid
  records survive unless separately reviewed.
- Hypergiant plus Freemason may invalidate a corrupted Proliteriate delegation,
  panel, mandate, or witness. They may not abolish the network or the people.

> The voice may be recalled. The people may not be abolished.

The minimum lifecycle is:

```text
challenge opened
→ evidence preserved
→ challenged authority answers
→ Claim, Title, or Yield reviewed
→ rejection, remediation, limitation, or second concurrence
→ restoration or removal
→ historical record preserved
```

Removal records the subject, evidence, concurring powers, disposition, effect,
vacancy or replacement state, and Tombstone linkage.

## 12. Tombstone Continuity

A Tombstone preserves ended tenure or representation as durable history. It is
not punishment by default.

It records the office, bearer or representation, beginning and ending
authority, supporting Claim, major gate scopes where relevant, ending reason,
challenge or succession evidence, Yield where available, and successor or
vacancy relation.

Honorable completion, removal for failure, death, surrender, and succession are
different dispositions. None erases the stable subject or historical tenure.

## 13. Hypergiant Succession and The Lazerhorn

No one inherits Diamond automatically.

> No Hypergiant claims Diamond without climbing The Lazerhorn.

The semantic path is:

```text
vacancy or lawful opening
→ stable Claim
→ independent Freemason examination
→ Proliteriate Yield hearing
→ relinquishment of protected elevation
→ descent through consequence
→ Flynt or Basin Proof of Persistence
→ climb of The Lazerhorn
→ accession eligibility
→ lawful recognition
→ Diamond investiture
→ active Hypergiant tenure
```

These stages are ordered by constitutional meaning, never record insertion.
This pass records the route and evidence; it does not implement every physical
journey encounter.

An outgoing Hypergiant may identify, teach, prepare, endorse, or recommend a
claimant. Recommendation is only evidence.

> A Hypergiant may prepare an heir. Diamond recognizes no automatic
> inheritance.

Lineage, appointment, wealth, force, prior office, or popularity cannot bypass
the path. A Freemason claimant cannot self-certify. A former Hypergiant removed
from Diamond returns only as an ordinary claimant through every stage.

> Diamond may be lost. The Way back may not be shortened.

No permanent regent office is created by this pass. During vacancy, Diamond
remains real and unborne. The bounded continuity and emergency-action grammar
is supplied by
`STONEBEND_TITLE_LIFECYCLE_AND_CONSTITUTIONAL_CONTINUITY_V1.md`; it cannot
invest Diamond or appoint a Hypergiant.

## 14. Claim, Title, and Yield at Every Gate

Routine crossing work is delegable. The three principal powers do not
personally process every record. Each crossing must nevertheless trace to:

- Freemason Claim authority and evidence;
- Hypergiant Title or boundary authority;
- Proliteriate Yield accountability and testimony.

At the Sandmanor-facing gate, Claim review verifies formation and Aura Way
evidence; Title authority recognizes and enforces the boundary; Yield witnesses
who benefits and who bears the result.

At the Central Junction-facing gate, Claim review verifies identity, provenance,
seal, measure, and custody; Title authority enforces admission or restriction;
Yield witnesses access, labor, fraud consequences, and return of value.

At the Flynt-facing gate, Claim review verifies Form, material identity, and
deployment boundary; Title authority permits or restricts deployment; Yield
witnesses operational risk, reward, damage, and continuing justification.

Delegation does not collapse constitutional responsibility.
