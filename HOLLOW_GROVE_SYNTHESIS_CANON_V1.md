# Hollow Grove Current / Aura / Synthesis Canon V1

**Status:** additive canon lock for the art and gameplay phase
**Boundary:** semantic and operational law above the neutral recursion kernel.

## Core distinctions

**Current** is the heavy, circulating magical medium of bodily continuity,
force, repetition, pressure, and physical life. It supplies usable force but
has load, fatigue, interruption, and recovery limits.

**Aura** is Current revealed through relationship, perception, field pressure,
light, attention, and environmental coupling. It shapes where force applies and
how living beings respond. Presynce governs embodied presence and contact;
Resynce governs synchronization across separation and is the canonical faculty
for remote Telekinesis. Neither makes thought itself a free force.

**Synthesis** is bioalchemy: a Recipe-backed procedure that rearranges existing
materials, traits, residues, meanings, and capacities into a maintained living
Form. It is not automatic evolution, creation from nothing, or a permanent
upgrade without renewal.

**Current Synthesis** is the transformation discipline that uses Current as the
medium of Synthesis. The Current ladder is Gremlin → Goblin → Ghoul → Spectre
→ Troll → Ork → Ogre → Troglodyte. These are available Hueman Forms, not
automatically separate companions or species.

**Aura progression** is Pixy → Sprite → Faerie → Nymph → Siren → Muse. Aura
faculties change perception, pressure, relation, and projection; they do not
replace the Current body or erase Synthesis obligations.

## House practice

Stonebend develops structural Current Synthesis: material load, repair,
reinforcement, graft, and endurance.

Sandmanor develops measurement and Recipe theory: lineage, models, records,
calibration, pattern, and repeatability.

Glaüshouse develops embodied Aura and lawful clinical Synthesis: diagnosis,
compatibility, consent, clearance, recovery, and maintenance.

Flynt develops operational Current/Aura application: force, capacity, field
use, witness, recognition, challenge, and consequence. Flynt recognition never
replaces clinical clearance or creates Synthesis authority.

These are four traditions of one medium, not four separate magic systems.

## House kinesis pairings

Each House has one scoped Current alias and one scoped Aura alias. Every alias
resolves to an existing faculty and power root; the names do not replace
Presynce, Resynce, Precog, Prefog, Prefig, `CurrentPowerRoot`, or
`AuraPowerRoot`.

| House | Current alias → faculty/root | Aura alias → faculty/root | Domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stonebend | **Barokinesis** → Presynce/Somatokinesis | **Haptokinesis** → Presynce/Psychometry | load, pressure, structure, touch, tension, repair |
| Sandmanor | **Technokinesis** → Prefig/Shapeshifting | **Ideokinesis** → Prefog/Dreamwalking | construction, mechanisms, models, concepts, and possibility |
| Glaüshouse | **Biokinesis** → Precog/Biokinesis | **Precognition** → Precog/Clairvoyance | living tissue, medicine, adaptation, diagnosis, and consequence |
| Flynt | **Telekinesis** → Resynce/Telekinesis | **Noökinesis** → Resynce/Telepathy | external force, attention, relation, and group intention |

Stonebend kinesis remains body- and contact-bound through Presynce; it does not
remotely move objects or control minds. Sandmanor kinesis gives possibility
model and provisional form, not proof or permanence. Glaüshouse kinesis remains
consent-bearing clinical practice. Flynt Telekinesis and Noökinesis remain
scoped by Resynce, resistance, witness, consequence, and return.

The short form is: Stonebend moves and reads weight; Sandmanor moves and reads
design; Glaüshouse moves and reads life; Flynt moves and reads people and force
at distance.

## Locked paired progressions

Stonebend's Current progression is:

```text
Hueman → Gremlin → Goblin → Ghoul → Spectre → Troll → Ork → Ogre → Troglodyte
```

Its five capability grades group these Forms as Gremlin/Goblin (1), Ghoul/
Spectre (2), Troll/Ork (3), Ogre (4), and Troglodyte (5). Their Presynce
expressions remain body- and contact-bound.

Gargoyle is a one-off Flynt Synthesis variant, not a Stonebend ladder stage.
Its five Telekinesis grades grow from a Gremlin-compatible foundation toward
precision, load, defense, and aerial control under `Resynce / Telekinesis`.
Werewolf Somatokinesis uses `Presynce / Somatokinesis`. Merman Hydrokinesis is
a compound regional Recipe method whose canonical contributions must remain
explicit; it is not an additional power root.

Glaüshouse Precog has the Aura progression Pixy → Sprite → Faerie → Nymph →
Siren → Muse. Its manipulation vocabulary is Suggestokinesis, Affectokinesis,
Cognikinesis, Mnemonokinesis, Volitikinesis, and Prognokinesis. This line
nudges attention, emotion, interpretation, memory emphasis, decision pressure,
and probable outcomes; it does not directly seize a mind.

Flynt Noökinesis remains the distinct direct relational influence faculty under
Resynce. Noökinesis may move attention, intention, or group thought directly,
but remains bounded by resistance, consent, witness, consequence, and return.

## Relativity and return

Every Current, Aura, or Synthesis act names an anchor, frame, pressure,
distortion, and return. The anchor preserves identity; the frame determines
available capability; pressure provides the demand; distortion records cost or
danger; return restores, adapts, retires, or transforms the state.

No transformation may silently erase provenance, consent, failure, recovery,
recognition, or the continuing Hueman.

## Authority boundary

House traditions, Recipes, forms, and abilities are interpreted above the pure
recursion kernel. The kernel may carry a bounded state transition and replay
record, but contains no Current, Aura, House, Form, Recipe, or lore authority.
