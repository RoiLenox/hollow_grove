# Hollow Grove Practitioner / Current / Aura / Synthesis Canon V1

**Status:** LOCKED universal ontology; House-specific OPEN and WORKING fields are
identified explicitly below.
**Boundary:** semantic and operational law above the neutral recursion kernel.

This document is the shared authority for the words Practitioner, Current,
Aura, faculty, and Synthesis. House constitutions remain authoritative for
their different institutional geometries. Runtime fields that predate this
ontology are governed by the compatibility boundary below.

## Universal practitioner grammar

```text
                    PRACTITIONER
                         │
                 ┌───────┴───────┐
                 │               │
              CURRENT           AURA
                 │               │
                 └───────┬───────┘
                         │
                         ▼
                     SYNTHESIS
```

The canonical Level 1 equation is:

```text
CURRENT + AURA → SYNTHESIS
```

**Practitioner** means the whole person. Current and Aura are related aspects
of that practitioner; neither word names a separate kind of practitioner.

**Current** describes how the practitioner participates relationally with
other people, households, institutions, communities, environments, society,
and larger collective systems. It is the practitioner's outward part within a
larger whole. A Current title may resemble a medieval occupation while naming
an archetypal social function rather than a literal job.

**Aura** describes what the practitioner cultivates internally: knowledge,
ability, magic, perception, cognition, discipline, or internal capacity. Aura
is individual and inwardly cultivated rather than primarily relational.

**Synthesis** is the manifested form resulting from integration of the
practitioner's corresponding Current and Aura.

Current, Aura, and Synthesis are related but non-interchangeable concepts.

### Qualified gameplay axes and Doctrine

The Hollow Deck's qualified gameplay terms remain compatible with this
universal grammar:

- **Current Form** records what the practitioner's body has become;
- **Aura Set** records supernatural techniques the practitioner has learned
  and may equip;
- **Doctrine** describes how the practitioner understands, selects, restrains,
  and integrates learned practices into a coherent Way.

`Current Form` must remain qualified: it does not redefine unqualified
Practitioner **Current**, which remains relational participation in a larger
whole. Current Form and Aura Set are independent axes. A change to one does not
automatically advance the other.

Doctrine is **STATUS: STRONGLY ESTABLISHED / MECHANICALLY OPEN**. It is not a
third stat tree, an Aura synonym, an automatic level bonus, or an excuse to
collapse the two independent axes. Doctrine should become visible through
judgment, timing, posture, restraint, conduct, maintenance, and execution.

Synthesis Recipes remain collectible, inventory-mediated bridges among
otherwise distinct requirements. A Recipe can describe the relationship that
may produce a Synthesis; possession alone grants no Form, Aura technique,
Doctrine, proof, consent, Clearance, Title, office, or recognition.

## Two scales of Synthesis

### Level 1 — Practitioner Synthesis

Level 1 integrates a practitioner's corresponding Current and Aura:

```text
CURRENT + AURA → SYNTHESIS
```

House-specific Level 1 equations require independent canon. Sandmanor's new
ontology does not infer a Form from a social role plus an attendant class.

### Level 2 — Higher or Composite Synthesis

Already-manifested Syntheses and/or apex traditions may combine or reconcile
into a higher state. Level 2 is deliberately distinct from Level 1 and may use
House- or context-specific mechanics.

Locked Level 2 relationships include:

```text

→ Manticorp
→ Chimera
```

The detailed general mechanics of Level 2 Synthesis are **STATUS: OPEN**. No
Level 2 relationship may be forced into the Level 1 equation merely to satisfy
a common schema.

### Flynt — Capacity / Spirit / Resynce

Flynt's Steel Temper principle is: **“Capacity is revealed under load.”**

**Telekinesis** is Flynt's Current-side expression: outward action, movement,
or force upon what is external to the practitioner. **Nookinesis** is its
Aura-side expression: inward action or manipulation involving mind, cognition,
intention, internal state, or Aura. Nookinesis moves or acts on an internal
state; Glaüshouse Noomancy knows, reads, interprets, or regulates one. The two
terms are not interchangeable.

The inherited practitioner/Synthesis correspondences and the Flynt maritime
creature identity are:

```text
Warlock → Gargoyle
Lycan → Werewolf
Mariner = Merman
```

Warlock and Lycan belong to human practitioner traditions. Gargoyle and
Werewolf are their respective Synthesis forms. Mariner is the specifically
masculine Flynt mer-creature/form designation and Flynt's name for Merman, not
merely a human sailor or human practitioner tradition. The name of any distinct
source practitioner tradition for Mariner remains **STATUS: OPEN**. Their exact
Current/Aura equations are **STATUS: OPEN**.

Flynt's additive creature-sex grammar is:

```text
Gargoyle  ↔ Medusa
Werewolf  ↔ Vampire
Mariner   ↔ Mermaid
Manticore ↔ Chimera
```

The left side is masculine and the right side feminine. Medusa is a Flynt
creature/species designation; Mariner = Merman; Manticore is the masculine

pairs preserve distinct creature identities and do not themselves create Level
1 equations, transformation commands, offices, or additional apex beings.

The working functional concepts are Gargoyle = HOLD, Werewolf = DRIVE, and

```text
Gargoyle + Werewolf + Mariner/Merman → Manticorp → Chimera
```

**Manticorp is combined synthesis. Chimera is perfected synthesis.** The

Neither Manticorp nor Chimera is a fourth isolated specialty parallel to

## Legacy physical compatibility boundary

Earlier documents and schema V1 runtime types used `Current` for a bodily,
circulating, blood-associated quantity and `Aura` for an atmospheric,
pressure-, light-, or observation-associated quantity. Those are
**LEGACY PHYSICAL EXPRESSIONS / COMPATIBILITY MECHANICS**, not the universal
lore definitions of Current and Aura.

Existing archive fields, replay records, enum variants, `CurrentPowerRoot`,
`AuraPowerRoot`, and constitutional quantity types may retain their stable
names and historical interpretation. They must not be silently reinterpreted
as practitioner roles or internal practices, nor destructively renamed without
a versioned schema and aliases. New semantic ontology types live separately
from that compatibility machinery.

The same compatibility rule preserves legacy `Pixy`, `Faerie`, and old
platelet-correspondence `Wardens` wire identities where decoding historical
data requires them. Their stable identifiers do not displace active `Pixie`
and `Fairy` spelling or the canonical Flynt civic identity.

## Relativity, continuity, and authority

Every Current, Aura, or Synthesis act names an anchor, frame, pressure,
distortion, and return. The anchor preserves identity; the frame determines
available capability; pressure supplies demand; distortion records cost or
danger; return restores, adapts, retires, or transforms the state.

No transformation may silently erase provenance, consent, failure, recovery,
recognition, or the continuing Hueman. Sympian continuity categories remain
defined by `HOLLOW_GROVE_CLASSIFICATION_RECONCILIATION_V1.md`; they do not
replace Practitioner, Current, Aura, faculty, or Synthesis.

House traditions, recipes, forms, and abilities are interpreted above the pure
recursion kernel. The kernel may carry bounded transitions and replay records,
but contains no lore authority.
