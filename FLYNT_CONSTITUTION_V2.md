# Flynt Constitution V2

## Constitutional House law

Flynt's constitutional House Law is the **Law of Capacity**: what can a system
do under pressure? Recognition remains a Festival and confirmation theme, a
cultural value, and a form of public judgment or honor; it is not Flynt's
constitutional House Law.

Capacity is the substantive Flynt question: what can a person, crew,
institution, or system do under pressure, and within what scope? Recognition is
the attributable public record and consequence of that capacity judgment. It
does not replace the Law of Capacity and it does not create office, Title,
membership, or sovereignty by itself.

Status: canonical and locked.

Flynt's Domain is **Spirit** and its distinct faculty is **Resynce**. Resynce is
neither Current, Aura, Synthesis, nor rank.

> **Steel Temper: Capacity is revealed under load.**

Flynt's Current-side expression is **Telekinesis**: outward action, movement,
or force upon what is external to the practitioner. Its Aura-side expression is
**Nookinesis**: inward action or manipulation involving mind, cognition,
intention, internal state, or Aura. Nookinesis moves or acts on an internal
state; Glaüshouse Noomancy knows, reads, interprets, or regulates one. The two
are not interchangeable, and Nookinesis is not universalized beyond Flynt.

This document is the repository-facing constitutional authority for Flynt's institutional architecture, stable identity relationships, and maintained Synthesis hierarchy. `flynt-constitution` is the executable authority. World catalogs, Current Synthesis projections, Hueman, Godot, artifacts, and examples must conform to it and may not create a parallel Flynt hierarchy.

```text
THE TROSS = MANTICORE
```

The Tross is Flynt's sovereign and presidential executive. **The Tross = the
Manticore**: Manticore is his masculine mythological identity and maintained
Synthesis Form, not a second character, office, or authority above or below
him. Every Flynt institution and office ultimately derives constitutional
authority from the Tross. He remains the formal constitutional leader and
commander-in-chief of Manticorp, Flynt's body of soldiers.

There is exactly one constitutional Chimera. Chimera is the First Companion of
Tross and stands immediately beneath Tross. Chimera participates in Flynt's
locked higher/composite Synthesis relationship:

```text
Gargoyle + Werewolf + Mariner/Merman → Chimera → Manticore
```

Chimera is feminine, a constitutional companion, and a maintained lower-apex
Synthesis, not an office. She is the counterpart to the masculine Manticore
embodied by the Tross. Chimera = SELECT and Manticore = INTEGRATE are
**WORKING** concepts;
the exact higher mechanics are **STATUS: OPEN**. Historical descriptions of
Chimera as already-complete integration are SUPERSEDED at the semantic level
but retained as archival provenance. The names are categorically distinct:
**Manticore** names the Tross's masculine identity/Form, while **Manticorp**
names Flynt's body of soldiers. No Manticorp soldier becomes the Manticore
merely by serving in that body, and no personal creature-progression sequence
grants the Tross office.

The masculine/feminine counterpart grammar and this pre-existing Recipe
provenance relationship are different constitutional dimensions. The pairing
does not make Chimera a female Manticore or Manticore a male Chimera, and it

## Public and classified institutional expression

```text
The Tross / Manticore (public sovereign)
  ├── Manticorp (soldier body and military command)
  ├── Mystery Men (classified tradition)
  │     └── The Marquise (maritime extension)
  └── We Fairy Men (Basin leadership body)
        └── The Gallows (underworld manifestation)
              └── Mystery Man / Mr. X (same Tross, operational face)
```

Manticorp is Flynt's body of soldiers, modeled institutionally as its formal
military command. It is responsible for territorial defense, military command,
constitutional protection, disciplined force, military training, and lawful
deployment. The Tross is its formal constitutional leader and
commander-in-chief: he recognizes its command structure and authorizes major
deployments and emergencies. The Manticore is the leader; Manticorp is the
soldier body. Ordinary Manticorp personnel are not Manticore Forms.

Mystery Men is Flynt's one consolidated clandestine, intelligence, and
federal-investigative apparatus. It combines investigation, intelligence,
counterintelligence, covert security and operations, Men-in-Black-like response
to hidden or anomalous threats, organized-crime and
contraband investigation, espionage response, and constitutional security in a
distinctly Flynt institution. These are functional analogies, not literal
copies of real-world agencies. Mystery Men is separate from We Fairy Men and
The Gallows, and `Mystery Men` is not the plural of the Tross's `Mystery Man`
identity. The institution is publicly recognized while its operations are
highly classified.

The Marquise are the maritime extension of Mystery Men, not a peer
institution, rival, separate military, or generic navy. The exact command path
is **The Tross → Mystery Men → The Marquise**. `The Marquise` names both the
extension and its singular leadership office. Wardens are Flynt's primary
civic identity; a Warden may enter the tradition as a Privateer and attain the
apex rank Corsair. The office of The Marquise follows Corsair conceptually but
is not another earned rank. The complete compact contract is
`FLYNT_MARQUISE_CANON_V1.md`.

The Mystery Man and Mr. X are the Tross's underground, classified, and operational identities. Under these faces, the same man leads the Gallows and may act through illicit trade, black-market Recipes, prohibited Synthesis materials, smuggling, debts, favors, covert enforcement, political pressure, and deniable violence. He is not the Tross's subordinate or separate agent.

## We Fairy Men and the Basin sects

```text
THE TROSS / MANTICORE
  |
  v leads
WE FAIRY MEN
  ├── Bro White ─────── Gargoyle ↔ Medusa
  ├── Beauty ────────── Werewolf ↔ Vampire
  └── Cinderellaman ─── Mariner ↔ Mermaid
          + Midnight Crew
  |
  v under the Tross's direction in the underworld
THE GALLOWS
```

**We Fairy Men** is the proper collective identity, deliberately echoing “We
Merry Men.” It must not be normalized to “Fairy Men,” “the Fairy Men,” or a
generic title. The Tross leads We Fairy Men. Bro White leads or controls the
Gargoyle/Medusa sect, preserving the deliberate Bro White/Snow White relation.
Beauty leads the Werewolf/Vampire sect—Beauty and his Beasts, preserving the
Beauty and the Beast inversion. Cinderellaman leads the Mariner/Mermaid sect
and his Midnight Crew, preserving the Cinderella/midnight relation. The
Midnight Crew belongs to Flynt's maritime side. It remains distinct from The
Marquise, the now-canonical maritime Mystery Men extension; no unstated command
or membership relation between the two is inferred.

The Gallows is We Fairy Men acting collectively under the Tross's direction in
the underworld. It is Flynt's Yakuza-, mafia-, and organized-crime-like order,
organized through families and regional crews, loyalty, territory, favors,
obligation, contraband routes, black-market Recipes, and cultural identity.
Many citizens question whether it exists. `We Fairy Men` names the underlying
leadership and sect structure; `The Gallows` names its contextual underworld
manifestation. They remain separately addressable contexts but are not
unrelated organizations and must not be collapsed into Mystery Men.

Outside the Gallows context, We Fairy Men may still appear as a traveling band,
roaming crew, body of folk heroes, and local protectors. Music is one cultural
expression and not the whole collective identity.

The Gallowry is the hidden home of the Gallows. It is a site, not an institution and not a synonym for the Gallows. Its canonical functions are meeting place, headquarters, cultural center, gallery, and operational hub.

## Ordinary capacity life

Flynt's constitutional tension must also be visible below the Tross and its
apex institutions. Operators, carriers, riggers, dispatchers, repair crews,
signalers, performers, apprentices, and independent workers may demonstrate a
function without becoming an officeholder or a Manticore Form. Their ordinary
capacity history includes scope, training, maintenance burden, failure,
attribution, challenge, suspension, renewal, retirement, and safe return to
non-operational life.

The ordinary capacity lifecycle is:

```text
practice → demonstration → scoped recognition → maintenance
→ challenge or failure → suspension, renewal, retirement, or return
```

This lifecycle is cultural and operationally bounded. It does not create a
second Flynt hierarchy. A crew may carry work and memory, but only the lawful
recognition authority can confirm the public scope of a function; capacity
never becomes consent, Stonebend Title, Sandmanor proof, or Glaüshouse
Clearance.

### Tross succession law

The Tross office has no automatic heir. Succession may open only through a
recorded death, voluntary abdication, lawful removal, or Glaüshouse-confirmed
irreversible incapacity. While a succession case is open, the incumbent's
lawful authority remains active unless a separate emergency limitation is
entered; vacancy never silently transfers command to Chimera, Manticorp, the
Mystery Man, or a crew leader.

A candidate must complete one bounded succession case with all four House
records and Court procedural review:

```text
Stonebend Name and continuity
→ Sandmanor proof of command competence and public burden
→ Glaüshouse capacity, consent, and Manticore compatibility clearance
→ Flynt recognition of lawful command function
→ Minoan Court procedural review
→ Stonebend succession Title and sealed registry entry
```

Each record is scoped, challengeable, and replayable. Failed candidacy,
withdrawal, refusal, or unresolved challenge preserves the candidate's history
without creating an office. The candidate does not become Manticore merely by
being recognized, and the Manticore Form does not select its own bearer.

The neutral world projection uses `being.flynt.tross` as the stable active presentation identity of the canonical Tross. `flynt.person.tross`, `flynt.identity.mystery-man`, and `flynt.identity.mr-x` are aliases and projections of that same person, not additional beings. This is not a succession candidate or a succession rule.

## Synthesis Continuance law

A true Synthesis changes the Hueman's actual body. It is not a costume,
detachable class, temporary loadout, or combat-only power-up. It establishes
real strengths, weaknesses, bodily and psychological needs, losses,
institutional recognition, environmental requirements, treatment obligations,
and future paths while maintained. Its Continuance depends on Recipe practice,
the Form's Ways, bodily discipline, renewal, environmental compatibility, and
institutional care. Visible suppression, Aspect emphasis, regulation, disguise,
and controlled heightened states may express the maintained body; regression,
revision, discontinuation, replacement, and collapse are distinct recorded
lifecycle events.

**Synthesis does not grant a costume. It establishes a new body. Every new body creates powers, weaknesses, needs, losses, obligations, and relationships.**

**Synthesis is not permanence. Synthesis is Continuance through renewal.**

**The Form is real while sustained. Its future depends on the Recipe, the Ways,
the Hueman, and the conditions that keep it coherent.**

The legacy physical Flynt grammar is Wolf → Lion, Bat → Eagle, and Snake/Fish →
Hydra. Earlier compatibility material described Chimera as integrating the
three lower principles; that mechanical reading is **SUPERSEDED**. Chimera =
SELECT and Manticore = INTEGRATE are **WORKING**, while detailed mechanics are
**STATUS: OPEN**. Manticore is the masculine sovereign transfiguration beyond
Chimera and is the Tross's presently maintained body and mythological identity,
with exceptional output and Resynce as well as
metabolic demand, instability, identity risk, environmental vulnerabilities,

Glaüshouse care. The Tross may maintain Manticore throughout his natural life
through bodily discipline, Recipe renewal, divided Basin knowledge,
institutional recognition, and specialized Glaüshouse care.
