# Hollow Grove Visual Bible V1

Status: art-phase production guide  
Foundation: additive to the kernel and Foundation Freeze  
Runtime rule: art renders stable records; it does not invent authority

## 1. Core visual promise

Hollow Grove should feel handmade, constitutional, lived-in, and slightly
uncanny. Every image should communicate three things at once:

- a person or place has history;
- a lawful choice has consequences;
- a return route remains visible.

The visual language is compact, readable, and tactile: strong silhouettes,
limited colors, purposeful negative space, and details that reward inspection.

## 2. Shared visual grammar

### Platinum Path

Use a restrained platinum highlight—not constant white—to mark a lawful
handoff, replayable consequence, or protected return. The five tests have
recurring marks:

| Test | Visual mark | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Continuity | linked ring | history remains connected |
| Consent | open latch | choice can be accepted or refused |
| Witness | twin eye | evidence can be seen by others |
| Custody | four-point seal | the responsible authority is named |
| Return | open arc | a safe next route remains |

Never use the Platinum highlight to imply that an advisory is a decision.

### The Twofold Witness

Roi and Roselina should read as a visual pair without looking like officials:

- Roi: vertical rhythm, clocks, threads, echoes, repeated motifs;
- Roselina: horizontal rhythm, maps, thresholds, containers, spatial frames;
- together: a shared center with two distinct orientations;
- no crowns, badges of office, judicial benches, or command uniforms.

When they disagree, show a split composition, interrupted path, or doubled
frame—not a victor.

### Central Junction

Central Junction is a district, not a palace. Use overlapping public systems:
boards, wires, workshops, exchange counters, public seating, seasonal banners,
and routes visibly entering from multiple directions. Its Service Tournament
Function should feel civic and participatory, never imperial.

## 3. House visual poles

| House | Visual center | Material language | Platinum Path emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stonebend | form and record | stone, cut metal, etched seals | Continuity |
| Sandmanor | relation and design | cloth, gardens, water, woven color | Consent |
| Glaüshouse | care and repair | glass, ceramic, lamps, joined seams | Return |
| Flynt | function and deployment | cables, engines, scaffolds, measured light | Custody |

These are tendencies, not exclusive palettes. Central Junction may combine all
four without becoming a fifth House.

## 4. Route mood priorities

Start with one visual identity per route family:

- Aura Ridge: elevated public welcome and civic reintegration;
- Current Sea: depth, certification, and unresolved identity;
- Boardwalk: public visibility with exits and backstage shadows;
- Underway: hidden protection, endurance, and route memory;
- Central Junction: shared work, evidence, exchange, and Function;
- mines and rigs: pressure, risk, repair, and consequence.

## 5. Character sheet priorities

Create production sheets in this order:

1. Hueman;
2. Roi and Roselina as the Twofold Witness;
3. Cleopatra and Hal;
4. the Gimp of the Underway and Pimp of the Boardwalk;
5. the Returning Goon;
6. Clouseau, the PLEB-PLEB witness;
7. Central Junction workers and House witnesses.

Every sheet should include silhouette, palette, posture, movement loop, refusal
expression, accepted-handoff expression, and one object that shows history.

## 6. Asset constraints

Art may change appearance, animation, sound, layout, and accessibility copy.
Art must not silently change stable IDs, authority, consent, refusal, custody,
or the meaning of an archived consequence.

If an image depicts an advisory, label it as advisory through framing, staging,
or UI language. Do not use decision seals, Bond marks, or recognition motifs
until the authoritative record exists.
