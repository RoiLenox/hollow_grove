## One promise has several independent owners

| Responsibility | Proposed binding requirement | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Promisor | identifiable supplier responsible for the actual agreed scope | a camp label or boss's endorsement is not an actor binding |
| Performer | named worker/instructor/escort with applicable competence | performance and independent certification remain separate |
| Customer | continuing person and their accepted scope | interest in a Form does not imply consent to any service |
| Custodian | holder of the customer's object/material during service | custody does not transfer ownership |
| Recorder | attributed observations and delivery/dispute evidence | reporting cannot manufacture acceptance |
| Remedy owner | supplier obligation and any lawful independent review | a remedy proposal is not a Court order or settled refund |
| Domain authority | applicable live House or market authority | commercial popularity, payment and crew membership grant no authority |

Actual supplier identities, qualifications and remedy duties remain decision
fields. This draft assigns no new actor, office, inventory or Recipe ID. Keys in
`contracts/backend/lore_registry.v1.json` identify authored entries only.

## Quote and agreement requirements

A proposed quote must distinguish the supplied good/service, quantity or duration,
supplier/performer, customer need, conditions, material custody, delivery criterion,
price or exchange, due time, cancellation and correction terms, publicity scope,
and any third-party/domain conditions. An absent price is **unknown**, not zero;
a missing acceptance is not refusal or agreement. An unspecified remedy is open,
not an unlimited promise. A quote must not become executable while required
terms or actor bindings remain unresolved.

A changed quote requires a new version and fresh agreement to changed terms.
The old quote remains attributable. Agreed publicity is independent of receiving
service; declining publicity cannot be silently interpreted as declining the
product. Supplier substitutions disclose changed competence and responsibility.

## Proposed contract lifecycle

This is an authored model, not a new state machine installed in Core:

| Condition | Meaning and permitted next consideration |
|---|---|
| Candidate | catalog idea; no supplier obligation, order or payment inferred |
| Disclosed quote | bounded version, responsible parties and terms stated; customer may inspect, decline or seek revision |
| Scope agreed | actual customer agreement to that quote; domain prerequisites remain independent |
| Performance authorized | applicable conditions/evidence satisfied; agreed work may begin |
| Work stopped | refusal, changed need, defect, unsafe scope or unavailable condition recorded; preserve object/person and remaining duty |
| Delivery recorded | attributed result supplied within scope; customer acceptance is still separate |
| Accepted | customer accepted the inspected delivery; continuing care, return or warranty duties remain explicit |
| Disputed / non-delivered | defect, conflicting account or unmet promise retains evidence and obligations |
| Remedy recorded | actual correction/cancellation/authorized settlement linked to the original version and dispute |
| Closed within scope | the contractual disposition and remaining duties are explicit; no further purchase or affiliation follows automatically |

Performance permission, physical delivery, customer acceptance, financial
settlement, public account and dispute disposition must never share one
undifferentiated “success” flag. A canceled service may have a lawful closure;
a corrected defect preserves the original failure. A failed physical attempt
can be an accepted historical observation even when the claimed service fails.

## Failure, withdrawal and disputes

Keep non-delivery, defective delivery, changing needs, third-party refusal,
loss of access, reported strain, conflicting testimony and inability to meet a
condition distinct. Preserve the original terms, actual result, stop reason,
responsible actors, custody and subsequent remedy. Withdrawal stops future
participation under the withdrawn scope; it does not erase work already done,
instantaneously reverse a living Form or silently settle money.

A promise concerning an external party cannot waive that party's independent
choice. A merchant's claimed waiver cannot replace live House authority.
A rival's criticism is evidence to evaluate, not a verdict or automatic refund.
Judicial review, institutional performance and financial settlement each retain
their owning source. Remedy amounts and timelines await explicit agreed terms
or lawful decisions; this draft fabricates none.

## Persistence requirements for any later implementation

Retain one continuing contract reference; append separately attributable quote
versions, agreement, domain evidence, performance observations, deliveries,
customer decisions, stops, dispute/remedy records and settlement references.
Use stable caller-supplied references and causal ordering. Do not derive identity
from array position, price, current display name or a random UI event.

A retry cannot repeat physical work, payment or fulfillment. A conflicting reuse
of a request reference fails without mutation. Repair invalidates obsolete proof
for changed conditions while preserving earlier evidence. Monetary settlement
must use the applicable existing ledger/authority rather than a new local wallet.
New record formats require versioned migration; older histories must acquire no
invented orders, delivery, agreement or debt.
