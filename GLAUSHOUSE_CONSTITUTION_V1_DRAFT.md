# Glaüshouse Constitution V1 Draft

Status: Draft pending ratification

Authority: Normative where this document restates existing canonical law; proposed where it orders that law into a constitutional instrument

Date: 2026-07-18

## 1. Constitutional Status

This document is the proposed House constitution for Glaüshouse.

It consolidates existing Glaüshouse law concerning Clearance, repair, medicine, treatment, restoration, the Prima Donna, Persephone, Nightingales, the recovery floor, clinical access, and exclusive Synthesis authority.

It does not invent a medical decision engine, court resolver, succession algorithm, consent substitute, or autonomous care loop.

Upon ratification, this document SHALL govern Glaüshouse-specific authority. The Hollow Grove V2 Constitutional Specification SHALL continue to govern the common Bond lifecycle, signed Current, signed Aura, Tombstone, Toke, persistence, replay, and Synthesis Resolution.

Where this draft labels a matter Reserved, implementation MUST reject reliance upon an assumed answer.

The canonical display spelling is Glaüshouse. Existing Rust identifiers may remain ASCII `Glaushouse` where language conventions require it.

## 2. Constitutional Identity

Glaüshouse is the third immutable House position in the Four-House grammar.

Glaüshouse carries:

- Clear It;
- Repair;
- treatment;
- restoration;
- filtration;
- drainage;
- rehabilitation;
- integration;
- exclusive Synthesis.

Its material statement is:

> Jade clears.

Its inherited constitutional profile is:

- action: Clear It;
- logic: double negative;
- expression: Synbiote;
- operating-system domain: Repair;
- color: green;
- House rock: Jade;
- Aura leaning: Holographic.

Double-negative logic is a House grammar term. It is not Negative Current, Negative Aura, or arithmetic cancellation.

Its constitutional question is:

> What must be cleaned, drained, repaired, integrated, restored, or made livable before lawful release?

Glaüshouse is House 3. Its grammar recurs at Rule-of-Twelve positions 3, 7, and 11.

Position 7 is the primary Glaüshouse pole, the Abyss, and the opposite depth to Stonebend Position 1.

Position 6 remains the Sandmanor diagnostic threshold into Glaüshouse. Diagnosis at the threshold does not itself perform treatment or grant Clearance.

Geographic position, screen direction, color, mineral, expression, and operating-system analogy are projections. They do not independently grant clinical, Clearance, or Synthesis authority.

## 3. Purpose

Glaüshouse exists because proof alone does not establish that a thing is safe, restored, integrated, usable, or ready to return to living function.

Glaüshouse determines whether real damage, obstruction, contamination, disconnection, instability, or unfinished recovery remains.

Glaüshouse coordinates the work that makes functioning wholeness possible.

Glaüshouse alone performs Synthesis because constitutional integration must have one accountable House authority. Synthesis remains subject to Recipe legality, evidence, Bond gates, participant rights, Current conservation, and deterministic replay.

## 4. Responsibilities

Glaüshouse SHALL:

- diagnose conditions within its jurisdiction;
- distinguish diagnosis from treatment;
- identify what must be cleared;
- identify what must remain protected;
- clean, drain, filter, repair, reconstruct, rehabilitate, and restore under lawful authority;
- preserve patient, participant, Hueman, machine, Being, and Object identity through treatment unless an authorized transformation says otherwise;
- maintain continuity of care;
- maintain explicit clinical and repair access boundaries;
- preserve consent, agency, privacy, and evidence;
- expose premature or false Clearance to challenge;
- require bedside, floor, and material recovery evidence;
- assign public Clearance to an accountable authority;
- preserve Persephone's triage, descent, recovery, and relay role;
- preserve Nightingale recognition and clearing functions;
- preserve the lived recovery floor as a constitutional check;
- authorize and perform lawful Synthesis;
- distinguish bounded Recipe Synthesis from final Bond Synthesis Resolution;
- support deterministic replay;
- participate in Bonds under the common Bond constitution.

## 5. Non-responsibilities

Glaüshouse SHALL NOT:

- create the Stonebend claim it receives;
- replace Sandmanor proof with clinical opinion;
- grant Flynt recognition;
- declare Clearance because treatment was attempted;
- declare Clearance because a chart appears complete;
- treat charting software as constitutional truth;
- treat absence of visible symptoms as proof of restoration;
- conceal unresolved injury to protect institutional reputation;
- treat access as consent;
- treat consent as proof of successful treatment;
- force Synthesis merely because transformation is technically possible;
- bypass Recipe, V2 evaluation, or frozen V1.1 execution legality;
- create or destroy Current;
- rewrite Aura history;
- infer Positive or Negative Current from Hollow Current;
- infer Positive or Negative Aura from Holographic, Light, or Dark Aura;
- make medicine species-exclusive;
- turn Nightingales into generic nurses or Wardens;
- turn the Prima Donna into an unchallengeable sovereign;
- bypass the Bond lifecycle.

- Who holds custody?
- Who consented or authorized intervention?
- Which emergency or institutional authority applies?

Glaüshouse House identity is immutable.

The canonical House value is the existing `House::Glaushouse` identity.

All institutions, offices, roles, groups, sites, zones, clinical records, Clearance decisions, and Synthesis acts MUST use stable typed identities.

### 7.2 Governing institution

The current canonical institution is:

Its existing domains are:

- diagnosis;
- surgery;
- rehabilitation;
- Synthesis;
- medical education.

Glaüshouse owns professional medicine as a civilization-wide discipline. The whole Grove may practice medicine through lawful Glaüshouse training and institutions.

### 7.3 Public office

The current canonical singular House office is:

- `office.glaushouse.prima-donna` — Prima Donna.

Its existing authority vocabulary is:

- PublicClearance;
- ReleaseAuthority;
- FinalJudgmentAnswerability.

Prima Donna is the accountable public holder of final Clearance and release authority.

Prima Donna is an office. The office holder and office are distinct identities.

The Prima Donna office has a constitutional continuity requirement. A vacancy is a failure condition requiring lawful succession; it is not permission to fabricate a holder.

The office does not make every clinical observation, perform every repair, or personally execute every Synthesis.

### 7.4 Persephone

Persephone carries:

- triage;

### 7.5 Nightingales

Nightingales are Glaüshouse's civic recognition and clearing people.

They:

- recognize danger and injury;
- distinguish harmful from healthy conditions;
- clear damaged matter;
- coordinate healing;
- remember threats;
- preserve floor-level evidence against false appearance.

The canonical role is:

- `role.glaushouse.nightingale`.

Nightingales correspond functionally to white blood cells. They are not literally a species definition, generic healers, generic nurses, or Wardens.

Stonebend marrow may be a biological origin in the civic-body analogy while Glaüshouse remains their constitutional civic House. Origin and institutional role are distinct.

### 7.6 Recovery floor

The recovery floor represents bedside truth, ordinary repair work, rehabilitation, continuity, and the lived body against which public Clearance is tested.

Existing identities include:

- `role.glaushouse.recovery-staff`;

The recovery floor may challenge false Clearance under existing law. It does not automatically hold the Prima Donna office.

### 7.7 Practice ranks

Existing Hueman-facing Glaüshouse practice ranks run from Pixy through Sprite, Farie, Nymph, Siren, and Muse.

These are practice and form expressions. They are not automatic institutional offices.

Muse does not stand above Prima Donna as a sovereign office.

Form, species, House training, profession, and individual mastery remain separate.

### 7.8 Sites and zones

The canonical headquarters is:

Its existing zones are:

- diagnostic halls;
- surgical theaters;
- rehabilitation pools;

Local care centers across the Grove may remain affiliated with Glaüshouse while physically located in another House region.

Location does not change clinical authority or House identity.

### 7.9 Material domain

Glaüshouse's House rock is Jade.

Jade contributes Structure, filtration, drainage, cleanliness, smooth passage, moisture, soft-tissue care, cooling, restoration, and flexible living boundaries.

The existing material expression is:

Hollow Current is not Negative Current. Holographic Aura is not Positive Aura.

### 7.10 Biological domain

Glaüshouse's primary biological logic includes mucus, lymph, membranes, lubrication, drainage, filtration, soft-tissue care, wound cleaning, rehabilitation, healing fluids, comfort, restoration, and medicine.

Mucus clears the boundary. Lymph clears the interior.

This is primary domain law, not exclusive ownership of every fluid, membrane, or act of care.

### 7.11 Ownership, lifetime, and persistence

House identity is perpetual and immutable.

Clinical findings, treatment records, repair records, consent and authority evidence, Clearance proposals, challenges, decisions, Synthesis records, and release records become immutable history when committed.

Living care plans MAY change through later recorded decisions. Historical observations MUST NOT be rewritten.

Office tenure, professional membership, access grants, and custody are historical relations and MUST be separately persisted.

## 11. Clearance Semantics

Clearance means a competent Glaüshouse authority has determined that the defined condition or subject satisfies the defined release, safety, restoration, integration, or usability criteria at a specific causal boundary.

Clearance MUST identify:

- Clearance identity;
- subject;
- scope;
- diagnosed condition;
- applicable standard;
- completed intervention or observation;
- remaining risk;
- remaining obligations;
- patient or participant state;
- evidence;
- witnesses;
- proposing authority;
- deciding authority;
- challenge status;
- causal position;
- rule version.

Clearance is scoped. Clearance for one condition, function, zone, transformation, or release MUST NOT imply universal safety or complete restoration.

Clearance is not recognition. Flynt may later recognize field value or lived use without changing the historical Clearance decision.

## 12. Clearance Lifecycle and State Machine

Legal Clearance states are:

- NotProposed;
- AssessmentPending;
- TreatmentOrRepairActive;
- RecoveryObservation;
- ClearanceProposed;
- ClearanceChallenged;
- Cleared;
- NotCleared;
- Superseded;
- Historical.

Legal transitions are:

- NotProposed to AssessmentPending when a lawful assessment begins;
- AssessmentPending to TreatmentOrRepairActive when intervention is lawful and required;
- AssessmentPending to RecoveryObservation when no active intervention is required but observation is required;
- TreatmentOrRepairActive to RecoveryObservation after the committed intervention boundary;
- RecoveryObservation to ClearanceProposed when the proposing authority supplies complete evidence;
- ClearanceProposed to ClearanceChallenged through competent Persephone, recovery-floor, Nightingale, participant, institutional, or Bond challenge;
- ClearanceProposed to Cleared when applicable evidence and challenge requirements pass and the deciding authority commits;
- ClearanceProposed or ClearanceChallenged to NotCleared when criteria fail;
- ClearanceChallenged to Cleared only through review that preserves the challenge and proves criteria;
- Cleared or NotCleared to Superseded when a later condition or later lawful decision changes present reliance;
- decided or Superseded status to Historical when active reliance ends.

Cleared does not delete injury, treatment, risk, or challenge history.

NotCleared does not prove permanent failure.

Illegal transitions include:

- NotProposed directly to Cleared;
- TreatmentOrRepairActive directly to Cleared without an applicable recovery-evidence boundary;
- ClearanceChallenged directly to Historical to avoid judgment;
- NotCleared to Cleared by editing the original evidence;
- Cleared back to TreatmentOrRepairActive under the same historical boundary rather than a later care event;
- any transition caused solely by chart display, office name, or institutional reputation.

## 13. Challenge to Clearance

Prima Donna carries final public answerability for Clearance.

Persephone and the recovery floor may each challenge premature or false Clearance, alone or together, under existing law.

Nightingale evidence may support challenge where recognized danger, unresolved threat, damaged matter, or false appearance remains.

A challenge MUST identify:

- challenged Clearance proposal or decision;
- challenger identity and standing;
- scope;
- unresolved condition or risk;
- evidence;
- requested review.

Challenge does not itself prove NotCleared. It prevents unsupported reliance until the competent process resolves the challenged scope.

The exact reviewing panel, deadline, emergency override, quorum, and appeal path are Reserved.

No emergency rule may erase the challenge or evidence.

## 14. Repair and Restoration

Repair restores function without erasing real damage or risk.

Every repair MUST identify:

- subject or Object;
- damaged function;
- structural and living boundaries;
- proposed intervention;
- ownership and custody;
- permission or consent;
- materials and Current;
- evidence;
- expected result;
- actual result;
- remaining defect;
- recovery path.

Repair MUST NOT fabricate a pre-damage state.

Restoration MAY produce a new functioning state rather than an identical prior state. The difference MUST remain visible in evidence and history.

## 15. Synthesis Authority

Glaüshouse alone performs Synthesis.

Synthesis integrates separately developed systems, traits, materials, or constitutional consequences into one stable functioning whole.

There are two scopes:

- bounded Recipe Synthesis inside an active Bond or lawful execution context;
- final Bond Synthesis Resolution after maturity and the complete proof branch.

Both scopes remain Glaüshouse-governed, but they are not the same operation.

Every Synthesis MUST identify:

- Synthesis identity;
- scope;
- source systems, traits, materials, Bonds, or results;
- intended integrated result;
- Recipe or governing integration rule;
- participants;
- ownership and custody;
- consent and authority;
- Current inputs and dispositions;
- Aura observations;
- evidence;
- validator;
- Glaüshouse institution;
- executing or resolving authority;
- causal position;
- result and remaining obligations.

Bounded Synthesis MUST remain within the Recipe to V2 to frozen V1.1 legality boundary.

Final Bond Synthesis Resolution MUST remain after maturity, excess, eligibility, and the completed Tombstone/Toke or explicit no-proof path.

Synthesis MUST NOT:

- create Current;
- destroy Current;
- fabricate source traits;
- bypass ownership;
- bypass consent or applicable authority;
- hide failed integration;
- treat a Recipe name as execution;
- treat a successful Recipe execution as final Bond resolution;
- mutate Tombstone or Toke;
- grant Flynt recognition.

The Prima Donna office is the current final public Clearance authority. Existing law does not establish that the Prima Donna personally executes or validates every Synthesis. The exact separation of Synthesis proposer, validator, executor, and final resolver is Reserved except where the common Bond constitution already requires independent validation and authorized resolution.

## 16. Relationship to Current and Aura

Holographic Aura is an Aura state, not Positive Aura.

The Glaüshouse-to-Sandmanor Light Aura route and Glaüshouse-to-Flynt Dark Aura route are Aura orientations, not automatic Positive and Negative Aura.

Positive Current may exist under Negative Aura when functioning capacity is concealed, distorted, or institutionally denied.

Negative Current may exist under Positive Aura when burden, injury, or treatment cost is truthfully revealed and recognized.

Clearance and Synthesis MUST preserve both signed domains and MUST NOT collapse them into one wellness score.

## 17. Relationship to Bond

It MUST still complete every applicable common Bond stage.

For every House-governed Bond, only an authorized Glaüshouse institution may perform final Synthesis Resolution under the common constitution.

## 20. Failure Modes and Recovery

### 20.1 Diagnostic ambiguity

The condition remains unresolved or under observation. Treatment and Clearance authority MUST NOT guess certainty.

### 20.2 Treatment or repair failure

The failed intervention and actual consequence remain evidence. Recovery proceeds through a later lawful plan, not historical rewrite.

### 20.3 Premature Clearance

Challenge suspends the affected reliance. The original proposal or decision remains historical. Review may uphold, reject, or supersede it under a later record.

### 20.4 Loss of consent or authority

Prospective intervention stops unless an already-governing lawful emergency authority applies. Completed historical acts remain recorded.

### 20.5 Clinical access failure

Access is denied with explicit unmet requirements. The system MUST NOT leak protected content to explain denial beyond authorized detail.

### 20.6 Synthesis failure

No successful integrated result is claimed. Partial physical consequences and consumed inputs remain accountable. Software rollback does not fabricate restoration of material.

### 20.7 Office vacancy or dispute

The repository has no ratified Glaüshouse succession resolver. The institution records vacancy or dispute and blocks acts that specifically require Prima Donna authority. It MUST NOT choose a holder by rank, species, insertion order, or developer convenience.

### 20.8 Corruption

Recover from authoritative clinical, repair, access, Recipe, Bond, and evidence records. If certainty cannot be restored, Clearance and Synthesis remain blocked.
