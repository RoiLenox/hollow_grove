# Hueman Path Crossovers v0.1.0

Date: 2026-07-09

## Rule

Different kingdom-facing approaches may enter shared regions in different orders while still crossing through the same larger world body.

## Shared Regions

- `Aura Basin`
- `Aura Field`
- `Aura Beach`

All approaches can eventually pass through all three.
The important difference is order, mood, and constitutional pressure, not permanent exclusion.

## Route Law

- routes are constitutional verbs rather than mere connections
- straight routes remain process geometry
- curved routes remain transformation geometry
- geometry does not replace a route's dominant constitutional purpose
- `Boardwalk / Return` and `Riptide / Retrieve` form the Flynt <-> Glaüshouse process pair
- `Current Sea / Certify` and `Aura Ridge / Witness` remain separate Glaüshouse <-> Stonebend processes
- `Glausbahn / Refine` and `Current Seanad / Deliberate` form the Glaüshouse <-> Sandmanor process pair
- `Aura Way / Design` and `Mt. Aura / Aspire` remain the confirmed Stonebend <-> Sandmanor pair
- `Basin Motor Speedway / Produce` and `Stairway to Heaven / Ascend` remain the confirmed Stonebend <-> Flynt pair

## Aura Ridge

- Aura Ridge is an important straight trade and crossover body
- it is not the universal holding pen for every unresolved idea
- Glaushouse remains the visible hinge between major straight legs in the current map study
- Aura Ridge is civic witness and Equal Gaze, not Current Sea certification
- Central Junction is the civic, financial, business, and social district at
  the interior meeting point; the South Ridge Exchange is one market
  institution inside it
- the Craft, Repair, and Design Corridors lead respectively toward Stonebend,
  Glaüshouse, and Sandmanor, while Flynt powers the Junction through the
  Engineering Ring and its infrastructure network
- Current Haze remains unresolved possibility, Equal Gaze remains reconciled
  perspective, and Aura Beam remains future revelation or transmission; the
  Junction Board, Clearing House, and Junction Wire perform market governance
