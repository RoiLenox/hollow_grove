## Boundary

kernel, alongside the Semantic Foundation and the Compromise. It adds no House,
route, office, Frame, or executable authority.

## Five balanced material statements

```text
Current repeats; repetition carries memory and wear.
Aura reveals; revelation creates exposure and pressure.
Relativity bends; every bend retains an anchor and a frame.
Synthesis transforms; every transformation leaves remainder and obligation.
Hollow accumulates; refinement preserves provenance of what was separated.
```

These statements are descriptive constraints, not automatic meters. A future
playable case must show the invoked law through at least one body,
relationship, place, choice, or persistent record.

## Relativity grammar

Every lawful bend identifies:

1. **Anchor** — the identity, fact, or material relation that must remain true;
2. **Frame** — the observer, scale, position, or jurisdiction from which the
   relation is read;
3. **Pressure** — the Current, Aura, history, or circumstance causing the bend;
4. **Distortion** — what becomes harder to see, compare, or trust;
5. **Return** — the evidence, witness, or changed position that makes the bend
   answerable again.

A bend may change appearance, access, emphasis, or consequence. It may not
silently change the anchor into a different identity or authority.

## Balanced excesses

| Law | Healthy expression | Excess pressure | Return path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current | continuity, practice, endurance | rut, clot, exhaustion, repetition without learning | rest, circulation, repair, changed practice |
| Aura | legibility, communication, recognition | glare, exposure, pressure, coerced visibility | consent, shielding, recontextualization |
| Relativity | adaptation and mutual perspective | distortion, opportunism, incompatible frames | anchor evidence and reciprocal witness |
| Synthesis | lawful transformation and new capacity | overgrowth, identity strain, dependency, residue | recovery, maintenance, revision, or lawful severance |
| Hollow | extracted value, separation, refinement | infection, hoarding, provenance loss, destructive depletion | custody, treatment, restitution, preserved remainder |

These are not moral alignments. Any House may produce the healthy or harmful
expression, and every House should have a reason to need another House's
correction.

## House shadow grammar

- Stonebend protects continuity but can turn a Name into a cage.
- Sandmanor protects possibility and proof but can exclude lived reality through
  abstraction.
- Glaüshouse protects life and agency but can turn care into dependency or
  paternal control.
- Flynt protects capacity and function but can reduce people to instruments.

The shadows are recurring temptations, not additional constitutional offenses.

## Lifecycle of a consequence

The shared lived cycle is:

```text
emergence → naming → cultivation → use → strain → care or correction
→ continuation, transformation, or dissolution → memory and return
```

It does not imply immortality. Death, refusal, failure, and lawful dissolution
remain real outcomes. Continuity means that accepted history is not erased, not
that every form or relationship must continue forever.

## Flynt clarification

Flynt's Law of Capacity is the substantive question: **what can this system do
under pressure, and within what scope?** Recognition is the attributable public
record and consequence of that capacity judgment. Recognition does not replace
Flynt law, and capacity alone does not create office, Title, or sovereignty.
