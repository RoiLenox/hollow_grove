# Hueman Vertical Slice v0.1.0

Date: 2026-07-09

## Purpose

This document locks the first Hueman implementation target tightly enough to build against without pretending the whole civilization is already solved.

## Slice

The first slice is:

> Aura Ridge opener -> Opal Oil resource loop -> first Gremlin unlock

## Opening Position

The player begins as Hueman in a compact Aura Ridge settlement where:

- Stonebend, Sandmanor, and Glaüshouse pressures visibly meet
- Flynt is present through route hardware, field engineering, and recognition work
- the map stays small enough to test one full loop without requiring the whole world

## Local Need

Night pressure from wolves and failing fence lights is breaking the ridge hinge between:

- pasture
- trade path
- recovery shelter

The problem now supports two recognized deployment instincts using the same named and cleared tool:

- `route` branch: infrastructure-first route stabilization and fence relighting
- `defense` branch: direct flock defense anchored by the same pressure-safe field tool

The default implementation path remains engineering-first rather than combat-first.

## Branch Gates

`route` branch:

- proof gate: hinge-post stability, lantern burn duration, and safe current draw across a full route-night cycle
- clearance gate: hinge alignment, lantern sealing, and pressure reallocation that does not overload the pasture fence
- field output: `Hinge Seal Charge` x1
- credential: `Ridge Hinge Trust`
- unlocked next task: `Route Hinge Survey`
- start action: carry the seal charge down the trade leg and begin the survey at the first weak post
- completion result: identify the true bearing post and unlock the first named route maintenance plan
- next-task summary: carry the seal charge to two weaker posts, determine which post is truly bearing the redirected pressure, and prepare the first named route maintenance plan
- follow-up focus: later Flynt and Stonebend route work, hinge tuning, and pressure relocation
- failure condition: the hinge still buckles, the lantern gutters out, or the pressure shift just moves the problem farther down the line

`defense` branch:

- proof gate: anchor hold, glare spread, and operator recovery under a short high-pressure defense burst
- clearance gate: grip safety, heat shielding, and enough protected movement space to keep the flock separated from the wolf line
- field output: `Ward Flare Charge` x1
- credential: `Flockline Trust`
- unlocked next task: `Shelterline Night Watch`
- start action: carry the flare charge to the recovery shelter edge and begin the night watch
- completion result: map the fallback lane and unlock the first live-pressure rescue hold
- next-task summary: carry the flare charge to the recovery shelter edge, map the safest fallback lane for livestock and patients, and prepare the first live-pressure rescue hold
- follow-up focus: later Flynt and Glaüshouse emergency holding lines, rescue cover, and live-pressure response
- failure condition: the anchor slips, the work zone collapses, or the flock line breaks before dawn

## Resource Chain

The first chain uses:

- Regular Current
- Holographic Aura
- Opal Oil

The player should:

1. use a Flynt Aura View to identify safe drilling seams and unstable route hardware
2. follow Aura Glow to the best current seam and then to the fence node that most needs intervention
3. gather enough material for a very small Opal Oil refinement pass
4. name a small field tool: `Ridge Lantern Drill`
5. prove it in a short bench test
6. clear it through a small sealing and alignment step
7. deploy it at the actual ridge problem through either the `route` or `defense` branch
8. earn local Recognition
9. unlock the first Gremlin node: `Load-Bearing Grip`

## Transformation Gate

The player does not start transformed.

The first Current Form path is:

- `Gremlin`

The unlock condition is not abstract XP alone.
It should depend on successful field recognition of the `Ridge Lantern Drill`.
