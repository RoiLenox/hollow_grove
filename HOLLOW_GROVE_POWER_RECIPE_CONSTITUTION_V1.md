## Authority Boundary

This layer implements the expansion without changing the frozen recursion
kernel, the common Constitutional Runtime, House decision authority, or the
existing `SynthesisRecipe` compiler.

A power is a visible or experienced outcome. A Recipe is an addressable
Current–Aura method for producing that outcome. Outcome labels grant no House
ownership, office, proof, Clearance, recognition, Title, or permission to
mutate a Frame.

All definitions in the first catalog remain `Proposed`. They become taught,
provisional, recognized, disputed, or prohibited only through later
authority-bearing evidence.

retaining its own name or authority.

## Competing Shared Outcomes

The first catalog contains seventeen distinct methods:

- Flight: Resynce propulsion, Presynce density flight, Glaüshouse aerial
  adaptation, Prefog generated wings, and Prefig aerial form.
- Invisibility: Prefig glamour, Precog observation avoidance, Resynce
  recognition suppression, and Presynce visible-surface alteration.
- Healing: Presynce stable-form restoration, Precog living-system repair,
  Prefog replacement tissue, and a Resynce–Precog viable-pattern bridge.
- Super Speed: Resynce motion synchronization, Precog anticipated route,
  Presynce acceleration reinforcement, and a lawful integrated
  Resynce–Precog–Presynce Recipe.

Each method records capacity, Current cost, Aura cost, strain, range, duration,
precision, restrictions, strength, weakness, trace, and risk. Catalog
validation requires multiple competing authorities and a distinct trace for
every method reaching the same outcome.

## Power Wheel

The wheel has five faculty sectors. Each sector begins with an Instinct-ring
Current branch and Aura branch. Recipe nodes then occupy Technique or
Discipline rings; multi-Faculty methods occupy Synthesis bridges.

Every Recipe node requires:

1. Capacity;
2. Proof;
3. Recognition;
4. compatible Form and Frame;
5. discovery of or teaching in the Recipe.

The prepared field set holds at most four Recipes. Equipping a Recipe does not
execute it, establish legality, or alter persistent Frame state.

## Recipe Institutions

The draft projects one recipe function through each House:

- Stonebend Universal Proving Arena tests stable Form under resistance.
- Sandmanor Contest of Improvement compares and improves methods through proof.
- Glaüshouse Clinical Practicum diagnoses, grafts, clears, and directs recovery.
- Flynt's Manticorp Academy trains Resynce, Telekinesis, Telepathy, resistance,
  persistence, recognition, coordination, and lawful accession.

These institutions may teach, test, clear, or recognize a method. None may
claim ownership of the observable outcome.

## Sympiote Graft

`Sympiote` names a crafted living graft. It does not name or infer the Sympian
lineage. The typed lifecycle is:

```text
sample host
→ read host Current–Aura Recipe
→ cultivate living tissue
→ craft from and for the host
→ graft with consent
→ monitor compatibility
→ host rejection / Sympiote rejection / partial integration /
  failed integration / reciprocal Synthesis
```

A host rejection occurs when the Hueman attacks, isolates, starves, scars
around, destabilizes, or expels the Sympiote. A Sympiote rejection occurs when
the Sympiote treats the host as damaged material, invades healthy structures,
overcorrects, imposes the Recipe too aggressively, erases needed capacities,
or constructs a body the Hueman did not consent to inhabit. These are distinct
clinical directions.

> **Is the Sympiote integrating with the Hueman, or replacing the Hueman?**

A requested power package fails closed before craft. Emergent expression can
be recorded only from integration; successful reciprocal Synthesis requires a
named emergent Form. Events carry monotonic causal positions and evidence.
The history serializes into a checksummed archive and replays through the same
state machine.

The iconic `Gremlin → expected Goblin → emergent Gargoyle` diversion exists as
a proposed, non-executing witness. It does not automatically progress a Being
or ratify every route to Gargoyle.

## Stonebend Proving

Stonebend evaluates stability, restraint, repeatability, reciprocal control,
identity surviving pressure, Glaüshouse Clearance, and evidence. The derived
judgments are:

- Recognized;
- Provisional;
- Referred to Glaüshouse;
- Rejected;
- Severance, only for a coercive or actively destructive integration.

Power alone never grants Title or office. Glaüshouse creates and clears living
intervention; lived conditions integrate it; relevant institutions may train
it; Stonebend witnesses whether the result holds lawful Form.

## Persistence and Replay

The recipe catalog uses archive format `HGPRC`, schema V1. Sympiote histories
use `HGSYM`, schema V1. Both validate before encoding, carry deterministic
checksums, reject unsupported formats and schemas, and replay through the same
law used for live state.

The central invariant is:

> Powers are outcomes. Recipes are identities. The Houses must prove their methods.
