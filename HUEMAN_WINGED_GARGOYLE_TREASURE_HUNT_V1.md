# Hueman Winged Gargoyle Treasure Hunt V1

## Implementation status

The first playable vertical slice is implemented in the authoritative gameplay
runtime. `Y` advances the runtime-projected next bounded step; Rust owns
ordering, form changes, the capability candidate, replay, and safe return.
Gameplay archive schema V4 stores the canonical pilot events and migrates
V1–V3 histories without fabricating hunt progress. Godot renders a four-frame,
constitutional-palette Aseprite sheet through the fail-closed art manifest and
exposes equivalent screen semantics.

## Premise

The player hears that the first winged Gargoyle did not receive wings from a
single inventor. Four Houses each preserved one part of the Recipe, and every
account disagrees about what “flight” means.

The Recipe identity is `recipe.flynt.winged-gargoyle.v1`: Gargoyle is a Flynt
Synthesis branch. Stonebend supplies structure and material provenance,
Sandmanor supplies measurement and method, and Glaüshouse supplies clinical
compatibility, consent, Clearance, and recovery. Those contributions do not
transfer ownership of the resulting capability.

## Hunt sequence

1. **Stonebend scar-wall:** read impossible load marks and recover the structural
   fragment. The clue identifies a missing material and warns against brittle
   wings.
2. **Sandmanor sky archive:** compare damaged diagrams and reconstruct the lift
   model. The player learns that Current circulation alone cannot orient flight.
3. **Glaüshouse recovery ledger:** obtain consent-based compatibility evidence
   from an earlier failed attempt. The player learns the recovery and refusal
   conditions.
4. **Flynt storm witness:** reproduce a controlled aerial maneuver under witness
   and earn the operational fragment describing pressure, braking, and recoil.
5. **Material search:** recover the compatible structural substrate and an
   Aura-sensitive component without destroying their provenance, then preserve
   the Sandmanor method that makes their order reproducible.
6. **Trial synthesis:** assemble the incomplete Recipe only after the ordered
   structure, measurement, compatibility, witness, material, and method records
   meet their required confidence. A failed lawful trial records a variant,
   clue, refusal, remainder, or recovery debt rather than silently unlocking a
   capability.
7. **Final clearance:** return to Glaüshouse for compatibility review, perform
   the wing Synthesis, and test return to ordinary Hueman form.

## Rewards and consequences

After a stable trial and final Clearance, the Recipe may unlock only
`capability.flynt.telekinesis`, rooted in `Resynce / Telekinesis`. Presynce
remains structural body support; it is not a second remote-force unlock.
The candidate resolves through the existing executable power Recipe
`recipe.flight.resynce-propulsion`; the hunt does not create a parallel flight
method. Strain may impose Current fatigue, Aura disorientation, repair work, or
a temporary refusal state. The treasure hunt is complete only when the player
can fly, stop, recover, and return safely.
