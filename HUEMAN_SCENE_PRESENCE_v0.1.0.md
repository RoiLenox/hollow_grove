# Hueman Scene Presence v0.1.0

Date: 2026-07-09

## Rule

Scene presence describes what kinds of people, tools, traces, risks, and civic activity belong in a place before deeper systems are implemented.

## Presence Focus

- Stonebend presence should reveal title, burden, structure, and public craft pressure
- Sandmanor presence should reveal proof, count, records, comparison, and improvement pressure
- Glaushouse presence should reveal injury, restoration, floor truth, and synthesis need
- Flynt presence should reveal infrastructure, movement, hidden value, and deployable engineering

## Aura Ridge

Aura Ridge remains important because it can hold multiple constitutional pressures near the same opening region.

## Boundary

- scene presence is descriptive-only for now
- it does not create procedural actors or rewards yet
- it does not change Current Synthesis ownership
- it does not change Hollow Grove recursion
