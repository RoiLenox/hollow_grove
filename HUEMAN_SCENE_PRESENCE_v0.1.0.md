# Hueman Scene Presence v0.1.0

Date: 2026-07-07

## Rule

Each crossover scene carries a characteristic kind of presence before any encounter mechanics exist.

## Presence Sets

### Seam Market

- rumor carriers
- salvage brokers
- porters and exchangers
- temporary stalls, marks, and signal cloths

### Threshold Weather

- drifters and lookouts
- spray forms and kite traces
- warning markers at unstable edges
- bright debris that looks almost inhabited

### Pressure Shelter

- keepers, hoarders, and wardens
- bundled stores and shielded caches
- muffled listeners and inward-facing fires
- stacked signs of prior occupation

### Split Trace

- echoes, doubles, and uncertain witnesses
- abandoned signals that still feel recent
- partial camps, partial routes, partial stories
- clues that suggest more than one version of the scene

## Boundary

- This is descriptive Hueman occupancy only.
- It does not activate NPC systems.
- It does not activate dialogue, trade, or reward mechanics.
- It does not change crossover scenes or link physics.
- It does not change Current Synthesis ownership.
- It does not change Hollow Grove recursion.
