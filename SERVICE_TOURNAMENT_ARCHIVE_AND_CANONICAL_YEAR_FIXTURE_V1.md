# Service Tournament Archive and Canonical Year Fixture V1

- Status: frozen canonical milestone
- Archive format: `HGSTA`
- Archive version: `1`
- Golden year identity: `service-tournament.canonical-year.v1`
- Constitutional source: the existing Hollow Grove Compromise and the four House constitutions

## 1. Scope

This milestone freezes the persistence, replay, prize, Synthesis-semantic, and
audit boundary for the Service Tournament. It extends
`SERVICE_TOURNAMENT_CENTRAL_JUNCTION_CANON_V1.md`; it does not replace that
canon, create another Hollow Grove Constitution, or begin the playable
16-bit client.

The Service Tournament remains Central Junction's largest public Function: a
four-House war convention, civic festival, constitutional service trial, and
nonlethal simulation tournament. Central Junction becomes a temporary shared
operational arena without becoming a fifth House or a possession of the
champion.

The representatives remain exactly:

| House representative | External paired-service reference |
|---|---|
| Flynt, MI | ATF & Army |
| Stonebend | DEA & Air Force |
| Sandmanor | CIA & Navy |
| Glaüshouse | FBI & Marines |

Each row is one complete House identity. The references are cultural and
institutional models only. They create no real-world agency office, military
branch government, or eight-team competition inside Hollow Grove.

## 2. Constitutional Tournament lock

The War of a Thousand Hues remains nonlethal. Stonebend owns Blue, Sandmanor
owns Red, Glaüshouse owns Green, and Flynt owns Black. Flynt's Black is the
visible existing dark family—including Rich Black Blue, Gunmetal, Powder Blue,
Tropical Indigo, onyx, obsidian, and related values—not featureless pure black.

Every preserved paint layer records presence, action, sequence, and
provenance. Its cultural declaration remains:

> **This House was here.**

Intentional grammar remains handprint, straight line, circle, cross, burst,
arrow, and four overlapping House marks. The ordered layers are evidence:
black beneath blue records Flynt before Stonebend; green across black may
record Glaüshouse rescue within a Flynt route; four colors record uncontrolled
contest or complete cooperation.

A Service Mark must retain:

- its stable caller-supplied Service Mark ID;
- its stable Tournament year, scenario, War, and location identities;
- all Houses and participants involved;
- operation name and human account;
- ordered paint-layer IDs;
- source action-event IDs;
- constitutional significance;
- evidence references;
- preservation event and preservation authority.

An archive must reject a Service Mark whose ordered layers, source set,
scenario, location, Houses, participants, events, authority, or evidence do
not reconcile with the recorded paint.

## 3. Two coequal flagship Syntheses

### The Edge of Tomorrow

The Edge of Tomorrow is the apex Current prize. It is one already-completed,
singular, evolving Synthesis representing action, engineering, execution,
movement, force, rescue performed, and the physical realization of tomorrow.

Its lawful form may be sword, pistol, sword-pistol, tool-weapon, or another
lawful hybrid, but its identity is always:

`artifact.service-tournament.edge-of-tomorrow`

Refinement appends to that identity and continuous provenance lineage.
Refinement never replaces the artifact. The temporary Current recipient is
the **Bearer of the Edge** until the next Tournament.

### The Glass of a Thousand Hues

The Glass of a Thousand Hues is the apex Aura prize. It is one
already-completed, singular, evolving Synthesis representing perception,
revelation, diagnosis, intelligence, interpretation, color, and collective
recognition of tomorrow.

Its lawful form may be lens, glass, prism, mirror, visor, viewing instrument,
or another lawful perceptual configuration, but its identity is always:

`artifact.service-tournament.glass-of-a-thousand-hues`

Refinement appends facets and House contributions to the same continuous
lineage. Refinement never replaces the artifact. Its temporary custodian is
the **Keeper of the Glass**.

The artifacts are coequal and may have different recipients:

> **The Glass reveals the way.**
> **The Edge makes the way real.**

Custody is not ownership. Neither custody transfers sovereignty, controls
Central Junction, changes House succession, or subordinates one prize lineage
to the other.

## 4. Prize descent

Prize degree records realized Synthesis rather than ornamental trophy size:

1. **Flagship artifact** — the existing completed Edge or Glass.
2. **Completed Synthesis** — a finished usable major artifact, method,
   structure, technique, system, transformation, or tool.
3. **Synthesis Recipe / Pattern** — the complete lawful design, including
   materials, sequence, authorities, conditions, evidence, risks, and
   provenance, capable of producing a major Synthesis.
4. **Synthesis Core** — the rare central component needed by a greater
   Synthesis.
5. **Refined material** — a prepared component ready for use.
6. **Raw material** — an unfinished valuable ingredient.
7. **Access, authority, or mentorship** — bounded permission to use an
   archive, workshop, field, route, teacher, instrument, or constitutional
   process.

A Recipe is not a completed artifact. A Core is not a Recipe. The archive
stores degree and subject kind separately and rejects a mismatch.

House traditions remain open within their own law:

- Stonebend: stone, structure, naming, foundation, lawful form;
- Sandmanor: sand, separation, preparation, pattern, arrangement;
- Glaüshouse: glass, fusion, perception, treatment, revelation;
- Flynt: flint, edge, spark, activation, mechanism.

No new ornamental medal names are frozen here.

## 5. House Synthesis semantics

House meaning sits above the neutral recursion kernel:

`universal recursion kernel`
→ `constitutional runtime`
→ `House Synthesis semantics`
→ `Tournament runtime`
→ later `16-bit client`

The inward Aim operation and outward physical or social manifestation are
different typed values:

| Path | Inward operation during Aim | Outward manifestation after accepted Kiss |
|---|---|---|
| Flynt | Resynce | Recog |
| Stonebend | Presynce | Stonebend manifestation Precog |
| Sandmanor Minorian | Prefog | existing Minotaur |
| Sandmanor Minoan | Prefig | existing Centaur |
| Glaüshouse | Glaüshouse operation Precog | existing Sympiote |

`StonebendManifestation::Precog` and `GlaushouseOperation::Precog` are
type-distinct. They may share display language without sharing authority or
meaning.

Stonebend Precog concerns anticipated lawful form, naming, structure,
boundary, Title, and what something is constitutionally becoming.
Glaüshouse Precog concerns diagnosis, trajectory, treatment, viability, and
what a living system is becoming.

The Minorian path remains Prefog, Gnome source lineage, Minotaur
manifestation, and Aura Fields jurisdiction. The Minoan path remains Prefig,
Elf source lineage, Centaur manifestation, and Aura Beach and Current Sea
jurisdiction. No speculative replacement form is introduced.

**Sympiote is the sole canonical spelling.** It names the existing
Glaüshouse-compatible crafted living graft/state and is used by every
canonical record and display label.

Resynce restores or joins operational coherence. Recog is the outward,
physically or socially recognized manifestation of that coherence. The terms
remain typed and may not be collapsed into a sprite name or unscoped string.

## 6. Synthesis lifecycle lock

The existing grammar remains:

`Recipe`
→ `Compiler`
→ `Synthesis Script`
→ `Aim`
→ `Fire`
→ `Miss | Kiss`
→ `Point²`

- Aim records the inward House operation and the manifestation before the
  attempt.
- Fire executes the compiled attempt without itself manifesting the result.
- Miss leaves the canonical manifestation unchanged and produces no changed
  Point².
- Only an accepted Kiss applies exactly one lawful outward manifestation.
- Point² records relationship, evidence, authority, location, Recipe,
  provenance, manifestation, and result.

Client animation, sprite state, display labels, or timing never constitutes
canonical success.

## 7. `HGSTA` archive V1

The JSON archive envelope contains:

- `format: "HGSTA"`;
- `archive_version: 1`;
- a deterministic FNV-1a digest of the canonical payload;
- one or more stable Tournament-year records.

Version `0` is the supported legacy migration input for this milestone.
Migration validates and replays the old payload, then emits canonical version
`1`. Migrating an already-canonical version `1` archive is idempotent.

Every collection is canonicalized by stable identifier and explicit semantic
sequence before serialization. The archive persists:

- Tournament years and representatives;
- accepted Tournament events;
- scenarios, phases, objectives, participants, and temporary alliances;
- paint marks and ordered layers;
- Service Marks;
- real-emergency distinctions;
- scoring events and constitutional violations;
- War and Tournament results;
- prize awards;
- Edge and Glass refinement and custody;
- House Synthesis semantic events.

Loading verifies the digest and replays the production reducers. It rejects
unsupported versions, malformed stable IDs, wrong pairings, split or incomplete
rosters, lethal scenarios, broken references, impossible phase order, false
alliances, incomplete emergency distinctions, invalid scoring, contradictory
Service Mark layers, replacement masquerading as artifact refinement,
prize-degree inflation, custody-as-ownership, sovereignty claims, and
Synthesis lifecycle violations.

Identity never derives from insertion order, vector position, catalog
contents, display order, random generation, clock time, or client state.

## 9. Audit and tests

The command checks Tournament identity, archive version, Central Junction
placement, representative pairings, color ownership, nonlethality, scenario
count, emergency distinction, layered paint, Service Mark provenance, scoring,
restraint, Edge and Glass identity and custody, sovereignty prohibition,
deterministic replay, and checksum validity. It exits unsuccessfully if an
invariant fails.

Executable proof lives in:

- `tests/service_tournament_canon.rs`;
- `tests/service_tournament_archive_v1.rs`;
- the existing constitutional-runtime and recursion-kernel tests.
