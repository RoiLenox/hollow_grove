## Proposed first catalog

Each crew starts with three small, distinct offers. These are proposed display
names and service descriptions. Stable inventory IDs, prices, suppliers, payment
rules, and executable contracts await their owning design and user adoption.

| Crew | Proposed offer | What the customer receives | What makes delivery testable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pimp / Trooners | Return Mantle | a fitted garment or adornment adapted to disclosed movement and comfort needs | the customer can wear and remove it, move within their current limits, and request a fitting correction |
| Pimp / Trooners | Practice Salon | a booked session with a qualified instructor, a chosen small exercise, and private or public presentation by agreement | the instructor explains the exercise, respects stopping/rest, and reports the observed attempt without claiming a later Form |
| Pimp / Trooners | Introduction Booking | an introduction to a willing practitioner, workshop, or event with its terms stated beforehand | the introduced party actually meets the customer and can decline independently; an introduction promises no acceptance or office |
| Gimp / Gooners | Carry Kit | removable straps, grips, or carrying equipment fitted to an ordinary task | the customer can fit, release, and use it within their disclosed capacity; a defective component can be repaired |
| Gimp / Gooners | Fit and Repair | adjustment or repair of the customer's existing ordinary equipment | the agreed defect is addressed and the customer can inspect the result; body or graft treatment remains clinical work |
| Gimp / Gooners | Quiet Route Escort | a bounded escorted journey with destination, stops, and known access limits disclosed | the escort keeps the agreed route and rest stops or explains a change; access still depends on the place's actual rules |

The Mantle gives expression without prescribing an identity. The Salon makes
practice and presentation meet. The Booking gives opportunity an accountable
endpoint. The Kit, Repair, and Escort make practical support observable. Either
crew can produce excellent work or fail a promise; neither is predetermined
to be the honest or dishonest side.

### Terms every offer must answer

- What exactly is supplied, by whom, where, and when?
- What capacity or qualification supports the promise?
- What must the customer supply, and what is the agreed price or exchange?
- What can the customer inspect before accepting delivery?
- How can either party stop, decline publicity, cancel, or seek correction?
- What happens if the customer's needs change or strain appears?

Price is not a new universal currency or an invented runtime balance. Future
transactions must use the existing economic law and an explicit bounded
commercial contract. The offer must distinguish its own work from a clinical
clearance, Recipe fragment, sovereign decision, or independently granted access.

## First story proposal: First Return, First Promise

The protagonist is a continuing Hueman after actual foundation discharge.
This is a proposed new customer case, separate from the existing returning-Goon
case and its recorded choices. A new person's stable identity is not inferred
from a role label or substituted for an existing character.

The customer comes to the Neutral Promenade with a simple task: return to
ordinary work without exhausting the new foundation. They want to carry a
small useful object, meet a prospective teacher, and arrive with enough
capacity left to rest and practice later. The exact job and object remain
discussion choices rather than fixed inventory.

The Pimp offers a Salon session and an Introduction Booking, with a Mantle
fitting available if wanted. The Gimp offers a fitted Carry Kit and a Quiet
Route Escort, with Repair as the return service. Both want their contribution
recognized. The customer's stated need makes their actual delivery comparable.

### Scene sequence

1. **Discharge:** the clinic has already established the foundation, recorded
   recovery/teaching, and discharged the same person. Clinical state comes
   from the foundation runtime; market dialogue cannot manufacture it.
2. **Two offers:** each crew explains one bounded plan in the customer's own
   terms. The customer can inspect the offer and speak with its supplier.
3. **Choice:** the customer can choose either plan, agree to a small cooperation
   involving both, or return independently with another community's help.
4. **Ordinary use:** the chosen plan is tested in the disclosed small task.
   A comfortable result, an unsuitable fit, a missed introduction, and a route
   change are different observations rather than one universal success roll.
5. **Rest or care:** ordinary effort ends with rest. Reported strain keeps the
   Form and uses the existing clinical review/recovery route. A crew cannot
   turn a care need into a commercial or membership obligation.
6. **Account:** the customer describes what was actually delivered. Honest
   correction, a fulfilled promise, refusal, and unresolved work remain visible.
   Public praise belongs to the contributions the person chooses to recognize.

The four proposed paths are Pimp-led service, Gimp-led service, bounded shared
work, and independent return. They do not reuse the old returning-Goon event
or make its cultural affiliation choices into shop orders.

### Consequences and repair

| Observation | Proposed story consequence | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| Agreed service delivered | customer can acknowledge delivery and choose whether to return | future purchase and crew support remain voluntary |
| Poor garment/equipment fit | supplier owes the agreed fitting correction | clinical assessment owns any change in the body |
| Missed or misrepresented introduction | the booking promise is challenged and can be corrected or cancelled | the third party retains its own choice |
| Escort route changes | guide explains the reason and seeks agreement to a revised bounded plan | a route promise grants no access right |
| Customer reports strain | work stops; the Form persists; clinical care becomes the next practical need | the underlying commercial promise remains distinguishable from treatment |
| Customer declines both camps | independent return remains a valid closeout | neither crew may infer debt, failure, or lesser personhood |

Defining exact refunds, remedies, and payment requires the existing economic
and restitution authorities. This proposal does not add automatic money,
reputation points, membership, a Bond, or a replacement medical decision.
