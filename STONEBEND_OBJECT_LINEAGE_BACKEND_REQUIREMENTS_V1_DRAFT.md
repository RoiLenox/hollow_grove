## Continuing object, separate practitioner

The user's adopted Synthesis origin is Glaüshouse across item-to-whole-Form
scales. A future actual item Synthesis should therefore retain its Glaüshouse
method origin alongside Stonebend's identity/material history and applicable
Sandmanor proof. Exact item methods and materials remain open in the
[continuum contract](GLAUSHOUSE_SYNTHESIS_CONTINUUM_V1_DRAFT.md). This does not
reclassify the existing ordinary survey-frame repair as Synthesis or grant its
maker a living grade or Form.

One external object retains its identity, material origin, parts, condition,
functions, connections, modification history, custody and actual recipients.
The practitioner's Being, Nature/Form, skills, authority and clinical status
remain separate. A new demonstrated object function is not the maker's Synthesis.
Repairing a joint never grants Troll/Regenerate. Nature/verb language describes
proposed design principles here rather than an automatic crafting ladder.

The adopted doorway backend already has three actual revisions, live scoped
registration/proof, retained negative evidence, voluntary use and withdrawal
recovery. Its third revision is ordinary repair; it does not jump the person
into a later Nature. Those existing bounds and outcomes stay in their own
contract. Future scope needs a separate adoption and persistence decision.

## Material and revision requirements

For each proposed change, record the continuing object, predecessor revision,
changed/retained parts, source/custody, uncertainty, purpose, actual conditions,
applicable authority, observation, negative evidence and remaining duty.
Replaced material retains its history. Unknown provenance stays unknown.
Proof of one revision/condition does not transfer to a changed revision merely
because the name and shape remain familiar.

A material input, specimen, residue or detached part is independently addressable
under its actual custody. None automatically becomes a new Being, ownerless
property or unrestricted workshop stock. Record who supplied it, permission for
its use, change and return, actual recipient and unresolved ownership.

## Eight design questions

| Active Nature / working verb | Proposed object question | Required bounded evidence | Failure and return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gremlin / Scavenge | Can recovered members form a useful measuring instrument? | source and uncertain parts, fit, exact measuring trial and custody | reject unsuitable parts; no support claim inferred |
| Goblin / Rig | Can a removable arrangement supply a disclosed temporary function? | connection/release scope, exact version and separate support demonstration | slipping connection is negative history; isolate/unload and return or repair |
| Ghoul / Devour | Can a disclosed expendable input become useful bounded work? | input identity/quantity, conversion conditions, output, residue, observed heat and custody | unknown accounting/containment stops the claim; retain remainder and waste responsibility |
| Spectre / Phase | Can controlled clearance permit the required passage? | actual boundary/gauge, relative conditions, path and safe withdrawal | binding or an unknown boundary stops passage; retract without inventing universal phase-through |
| Troll / Regenerate | Can repair preserve identity while changing the defective part? | diagnosis, traceable replacement, retained old part, changed revision and fresh proof | resemblance does not restore rating; preserve old failure and remaining defect |
| Ork / Sunder | Can separation occur at the exact reviewed work boundary? | identified subject/material boundary, actual unloaded conditions, release and retained fracture evidence | uncontrolled break stops the scope; recipient and detached material keep separate custody |
| Ogre / Crush | Can compression serve a use within its independently reviewed conditions? | actual pressure/load scope, support, response, deformation and release/recovery | failure is retained; stop, unload/isolate and review changed conditions |
| Troglodyte / Hollow | Does the bounded opening serve its actual user while required support remains? | useful space, remaining structure, removed-material provenance and independent recipient choice | no useful outcome is inferred from subtraction alone; unresolved stability/custody/refusal stops work |

The active canonical spelling is **Ork**. Historical runtime/older draft wording
must not rename serialized variants in place. The design questions are neither
a numerical eight-step Recipe nor a requirement that every object undergo all
changes. A failed, refused or unnecessary change can remain an honest branch.

## Input/output model for the Ghoul proposal

The energy/material source remains open. Before selecting a mechanism, distinguish
supplied input, authorized consumption, actual conversion, observed useful work,
retained remainder, residue, heat/other effects, measurement uncertainty and safe
stopping/recovery. A quantity left unknown cannot be treated as zero, fully
consumed or fully recovered. A useful output cannot erase residue or its custody.
No numerical efficiency, containment rating or conservation exception is adopted.

Compare alternative mechanisms only after their evidence and cost fields are
complete. Failure to account for a result narrows or stops the claim; it cannot
be resolved by an impressive name, a later Nature label or an unexplained energy
source. The workshop, material provider, affected user and reviewer retain
separate responsibilities.

## Passage, force and Hollowing boundaries

The Spectre proposal concerns a bounded passage/clearance question; its exact
mechanism remains open. It grants no general permission to cross a sealed
boundary or invent an intangible Form. Record actual geometry/conditions,
permission, tested path, interference, stop and withdrawal outcome.

Separation/compression requires its own reviewed purpose, object version,
conditions, affected parties and recovery scope. Measuring proof is insufficient
for a force claim. A successful trial at one condition does not establish a
general rating, reciprocal consent, office or clinical intervention permission.

Hollowing retains its Stonebend identity/boundary law. Subtraction or useful
absence does not make removed material unowned, erase a subject's history or
permit irreversible disputed work. The final metaphysical interpretation stays
open. The recipient's actual need and refusal remain independently addressable.

## Proposed review and historical records

Keep requested change, work authorization, actual part/input change, exact
observation, proof disposition, user decision, delivered scope, custody and
recovery separate. Live Stonebend Name preserves applicable identity/custody;
Sandmanor Prove supplies the exact demonstrated claim. Flynt recognition and
Glaüshouse living-use/clinical review apply only within their actual jurisdictions.
Routine adjustments do not manufacture four sovereign decisions by default.

A legitimate failed trial must survive correction. Repair invalidates stale
proof for the changed scope while retaining its predecessor. A new offer cannot
erase an earlier refusal; withdrawal can stop future use and leave recovery
outstanding. A useful disposition may close the bounded contract while later
work remains explicit. No single “upgraded” flag proves this chain.

## Open requirements before implementation

Select actual input/residue rules, passage mechanism, separation/compression
scope, evidence producers, ordinary actor bindings, affected recipient,
maintenance/recovery duties and terminal Hollowing interpretation. Decide whether
a later case extends object history or references the completed first case,
without silently expanding the frozen existing event vocabulary or bounds.

Only after adoption should any new record/migration be implemented. Private
headless witnesses then need identity continuity, uncertain provenance, changed
conditions, actual failure, refusal, repair, recovery, retry/conflict and altered
history rejection. This draft is an auditable specification surface, not evidence
that those future mechanisms have already run.
