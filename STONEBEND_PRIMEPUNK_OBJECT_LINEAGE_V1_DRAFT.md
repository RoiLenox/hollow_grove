## The object and its continuing question

Follow one **repairable survey frame** from recovered material to a tool for
making useful, bounded space. This is a descriptive working name. It is not a
new named weapon, Recipe, species, or mandatory asset.

A workshop receives a damaged frame used to measure and support an opening.
Its former load rating and custody are uncertain. The first task is to recover
a useful measuring instrument without pretending it is already safe to carry
a load. The frame's references, replaced parts, measurements, failures, and
custody remain visible through each revision.

The recurring question is: **What can this same object now do, and what remains
unproven?** A revision changes the object's demonstrated function. It never
automatically changes its maker's Nature, Aura, Title, office, or Clearance.

## Proposed revisions

| Existing Nature / practical verb | Revision of the same object | Principle made visible | Cost, failure, and return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gremlin / Scavenge | Sort and fit recovered members into a square measuring frame; mark unknown provenance | recovery, fit, and reuse | reject unsuitable pieces; measuring use carries no support rating |
| Goblin / Rig | Add a removable tension line, adjustable connections, and a visible release | arrangement and load path | connections can slip; unload and return to measuring use |
| Ghoul / Devour | Trial a bounded drive module that converts a disclosed expendable input into work | conversion and expenditure | record input, residue, heat, and useful output; stop when containment or energy accounting is uncertain |
| Spectre / Phase | Add adjustable clearance gauges and a guided moving member | tolerance and controlled passage | passage can bind; retract rather than force an unknown boundary |
| Troll / Regenerate | Replace a damaged joint while retaining its old part and service history | repaired continuity | a repaired joint requires fresh testing; the former rating does not return by appearance |
| Orc / Sunder | Trial a controlled separation module at a disclosed work boundary | exact release and preserved fracture evidence | remove stored load before separation; an uncontrolled break stops the case |
| Ogre / Crush | Trial a compression module within an independently reviewed load envelope | bounded pressure and structural response | deformation and failed supports remain evidence; unload, isolate, and repair |
| Troglodyte / Hollow | Use the frame to bound and measure an opening whose cleared space serves an actual user | useful void and remaining support | removed material receives its own provenance; stop when stability, custody, or the user's refusal is unresolved |

These are design questions, not guaranteed mechanics or an eight-step crafting
recipe. A failed or unnecessary revision may remain a recorded branch. The
Ghoul drive's energy source, Spectre passage mechanism, later force modules,
and terminal Hollowing interpretation remain open until demonstrated.

## Inter-House work and decisions

Stonebend preserves the frame's identity, material history, custody, and the
distinction between the continuing object and recovered material. Sandmanor
reviews the exact version's claimed improvement, method, measurements, negative
results, and reproducibility. Flynt contributes engineered function and may
recognize only the deployed scope actually demonstrated. Glaüshouse reviews
living-use interventions and recovery when they fall within its jurisdiction.

This productive cooperation does not force every workshop adjustment through
four sovereign decisions. Record the required House question for each actual
use. A new demonstration cannot silently inherit an earlier version's proof,
and an institution's interest cannot supply a user's consent.

## Historical first-case proposal

The [adopted Survey Frame First Case](STONEBEND_SURVEY_FRAME_FIRST_CASE_V1.md)
now owns the bounded doorway episode, actual actor bindings, outcomes and
verified backend. The [prior first-case draft](STONEBEND_SURVEY_FRAME_FIRST_CASE_V1_DRAFT.md)
remains proposal history. The text below records the original design stage;
its unbound roles do not describe the completed adopted case.

Use only the Gremlin and Goblin revisions for the initial proposal. A resident
needs a damaged doorway measured before agreeing to a temporary rig. The player
can help inspect recovered parts, disclose the missing rating, offer a bounded
rig, or support delaying work. The resident can refuse the rig and retain a
safe route while the workshop seeks better evidence.

Before adoption, bind these responsibilities to existing lawful actors:

- affected resident: owns acceptance, refusal, and the access need;
- workshop executor: performs only the demonstrated work;
- material custodian: preserves identity and replaced-part provenance;
- independent reviewer: distinguishes measuring accuracy from load claims;
- witness: records the actual revision, stop, or delivered result;
- recipient: receives the usable object or the unresolved case and safe return;
- recovery owner: receives failed work and the associated evidence.

No new office or actor identity is assigned by this draft. A role becomes
implemented only after its lawful binding and event records exist.

## Acceptance for the working design

A review must be able to follow the same frame through one successful revision,
one failed load claim, one protected refusal, and one repair. Each record must
show what persisted, what changed, who decided, what was witnessed, and what
the user received. Presentation should make replaced parts, tension, clearance,
and release readable within the existing art limits.

If the frame only acquires a grander name or stronger-looking sprite, the
revision has not demonstrated the intended lore principle.
