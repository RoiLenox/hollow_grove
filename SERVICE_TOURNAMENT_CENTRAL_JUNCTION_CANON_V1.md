# Service Tournament — Central Junction Canon V1

- Status: canonical shared Function law
- Constitutional basis: the Hollow Grove Compromise and the four House
  constitutions
- Location: Central Junction
- Executable model: `src/world/service_tournament.rs`

## 1. Canonical Function

**The Service Tournament** is the largest public Function held at **Central
Junction**. It is at once a four-House war convention, civic festival,
constitutional exercise, and nonlethal simulation tournament. It combines
public spectacle, inter-House rivalry, diplomacy, engineering exhibitions,
emergency-response trials, intelligence scenarios, rescue operations,
territorial games, and controlled combat.

For the Tournament, Central Junction temporarily becomes the shared
operational arena of all four Houses. Opening procedures are organized and
ceremonial. As scenarios overlap, the district may become theatrical,
socially unruly, and operationally chaotic. The Function remains bounded by
the Hollow Grove Compromise, the four House constitutions, ordinary public
law, and the distinction between simulation and emergency. It never becomes
an actual war.

The Service Tournament creates no fifth House, common military government, or
new permanent office. It neither replaces Central Junction's existing public
institutions nor transfers a House power to a Junction institution.

## 2. The Four House Service Identities

Each House represents both paired services as **one complete
cultural-government identity**:

| House representative | Fixed paired-service reference | One Tournament identity |
|---|---|---|
| Flynt, MI | ATF & Army | Flynt |
| Stonebend | DEA & Air Force | Stonebend |
| Sandmanor | CIA & Navy | Sandmanor |
| Glaüshouse | FBI & Marines | Glaüshouse |

The real-world agencies and armed services are external cultural and
institutional reference models. They create no Hollow Grove agency, command,
office, faction, or jurisdiction. There are four House representatives—not
eight service teams. Each House retains its government, leadership,
institutions, internal factions, constitutional authority, and sovereignty.

### 2.1 Flynt, MI — ATF & Army

Flynt understands dangerous mechanisms and transforms the operational field.
Its Tournament identity emphasizes combat engineering, controlled mechanisms,
breaching, explosives knowledge, fortification, route creation, territorial
control, field improvisation, and lawful recognition of force.

This expression remains compatible with Tross, Manticorp, and the Mystery Men.
The Gallows remains the unlawful or unrecognized mirror. Neither external
reference creates a command parallel to Tross or an institution parallel to
Manticorp.

### 2.2 Stonebend — DEA & Air Force

Stonebend identifies, classifies, traces, contains, and regulates movement
while exercising superior structural overview. Its Tournament identity
emphasizes classification, chain of custody, tracking controlled substances,
Current, identities, or objects, aerial observation, precision, strategic
command, boundary control, lawful containment, and interdiction.

This expression remains subject to Stonebend authority over Title, naming,
structure, boundary, permanence, lawful identity, and Hollowing. Simulation
never authorizes Illegal Hollowing, creates a Title, or converts custody into
ownership.

### 2.3 Sandmanor — CIA & Navy

Sandmanor studies hidden relationships and moves through contested Currents.
Its Tournament identity emphasizes intelligence, secrecy, navigation,
maritime movement, foreign contact, source cultivation, indirect influence,
currents and routes, coastal operations, and interpretation of incomplete
information.

This expression remains compatible with Minorians and Minoans; Gnomes,
Minotaurs, Elves, and Centaurs; Aura Fields; Aura Beach; Current Sea; the
Sandman; and the Contest of Improvement. Intelligence success is not
Sandmanor proof unless the ordinary constitutional standard of proof is met.
Literal maritime exercises use Aura Beach, the coast, or designated canals.
The Navy reference does not reclassify Current Sea: that route remains the
civic sea-of-people concourse, where Sandmanor instead navigates crowds,
relationships, incomplete information, and contested public movement.

### 2.4 Glaüshouse — FBI & Marines

Glaüshouse identifies internal threats and deploys concentrated intervention
directly into the affected area. Its Tournament identity emphasizes
investigation, evidence, forensics, diagnosis, internal defense, rapid
deployment, rescue, casualty extraction, close intervention, and securing
dangerous scenes.

This expression remains compatible with Prima Donna / Doctor Ratchet,
Persephone / Nurse House, Nightingales, Chromacord, and the Recovery Ward.
Tournament treatment remains subject to capacity, consent, clearance,
recovery, and clinical stop. It never authorizes Illegal Synthesis.

## 3. The War of a Thousand Hues

The central paintball-style conflict of the Service Tournament is officially:

# **The War of a Thousand Hues**

**The Thousand Hues** is acceptable informal usage. The formal name is used
in event records, official results, Service Marks, and opening ceremony.

> **Let the Houses take their colors. Let Central Junction bear witness. The War of a Thousand Hues has begun.**

> **Four Houses enter with four colors. Central Junction leaves wearing a thousand hues.**

The four color families are:

| House | Color family | Established palette expression |
|---|---|---|
| Stonebend | Blue | Prussian Blue, Lapis Lazuli, Air Superiority Blue |
| Sandmanor | Red | Wine, Redwood, Blush |
| Glaüshouse | Green | Brunswick Green, Viridian, Eton Blue (the established pale green highlight) |
| Flynt | Black | onyx, obsidian, gunmetal, Rich Black Blue, and related weathered blue-black mixtures |

Tournament rendering derives these variations from
`src/constitutional/hollow_grove_visual_color_palette.json`. Flynt's Black is
its existing dark family, not featureless `#000000`. Onyx and obsidian are
cultural names for layered or mixed expressions derived from Flynt's
blue-black and gunmetal palette sources; they do not override the visual
constitution's reservation of pure black for Void, Absolute Absence, and
Erasure.

The inherited name **Eton Blue** remains unchanged in the visual palette even
though its green-valued swatch belongs to Glaüshouse's Tournament Green
family. Tournament family ownership follows the canonical House palette, not
the ordinary-language color in a swatch's name.

The title names the shades, mixtures, layers, splashes, fades, weathered
traces, and intersections created when those four families collide. The event
begins with four House colors. Central Junction ends wearing a thousand hues.

## 4. Paint as Operational and Historical Record

Paint is not merely an elimination indicator. Every mark records presence and
action:

- who reached a location;
- who held, breached, or defended it;
- who rescued someone there;
- who crossed another House's route;
- which alliances formed;
- which territory changed hands;
- which House arrived first;
- which House remained last.

Its cultural statement is:

> **This House was here.**

A Flynt mark says **Flynt was here.** A blue line across a bridge records
Stonebend securing it. Red through a market records Sandmanor's route. Green
around a recovery station records protected Glaüshouse medical ground. Black
across a gate records a Flynt breach. Several colors together may record
contest, succession, alliance, rescue, reversal, or cooperation.

### 4.1 Mark grammar

Intentionally placed symbols support a simple public grammar:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Handprint | We were here. |
| Straight line | Route secured. |
| Circle | Area defended or protected. |
| Cross | Rescue, treatment, or medical extraction. |
| Burst | Breach completed. |
| Arrow | Advance, pursuit, or directed movement. |
| Four overlapping House marks | Joint operation or shared constitutional action. |

Ordinary splashes remain ambiguous. Deliberate grammar and accidental traces
coexist.

### 4.2 Reading layers

Layer sequence is preserved where practical. Each recorded layer has a stable
mark identity, House, location, operational action, explicit layer sequence,
originating event, competitor, and evidence.

- Black beneath blue means Flynt reached or breached the location before
  Stonebend subsequently secured it.
- Green cutting through black may record Glaüshouse rescue inside a
  Flynt-controlled area.
- Red over blue may record Sandmanor overturning or infiltrating a Stonebend
  position.
- All four colors together identify either uncontrolled four-House contest or
  complete inter-House cooperation; the associated operation and evidence
  determine which.

Central Junction becomes a readable historical chart, not simple decoration.

## 5. Service Marks

Most paint may be cleaned after a War. Historically important marks may be
preserved as official **Service Marks**. A Service Mark may record:

- an impossible defense;
- a famous rescue;
- a decisive breach;
- the first mark of that year's War;
- a route that changed the competition;
- an unexpected civilian contribution;
- a joint operation involving several Houses;
- the location where all four Houses collided;
- the final action of that year's champion.

A Service Mark records its stable identity, year, War, location, House or
Houses, operation name, participants, category of significance,
constitutional significance, public account, preservation authorization,
source paint-mark identities, source action-event identities, and evidence.
It may not detach a preserved trace from the action that produced it.

Successive Tournaments therefore leave visible, evidence-backed historical
memory in Central Junction's architecture.

## 6. Nonlethal Simulation Warfare

The Service Tournament permits only nonlethal or simulated systems:

- paintball-style weapons and marking rounds;
- colored smoke and mock explosives;
- training vehicles;
- false documents and staged evidence;
- temporary identities and coded messages;
- drones and observation tools;
- movable barricades;
- scenario casualties;
- controlled environmental changes;
- rescue and extraction equipment.

Lethal violence, uncontrolled explosives, involuntary clinical procedure,
Illegal Hollowing, Illegal Synthesis, and actual warfare are outside the
Function. A real emergency suspends the fiction wherever necessary. Correctly
recognizing that change is part of the constitutional test.

## 7. Scenario Operations

Scenario types include territorial control, bridge defense, convoy escort,
evidence recovery, witness protection, staged contraband investigations, mock
kidnappings, sabotage discovery, rescue operations, casualty extraction,
route clearance, controlled breaches, aerial observation, naval or canal
navigation, infiltration, false emergencies, disaster response,
constitutional-crime investigations, and multi-House joint missions.

Every runtime scenario has a stable caller-supplied identity, Tournament
identity, operational-zone identity, nonlethal status, permitted simulation
systems, and one or more stable objectives. Scenarios may overlap until
Central Junction itself becomes the complete game board.

## 8. Tonal Progression

The canonical progression is:

1. ceremonial arrival;
2. formal opening;
3. structured competition;
4. rivalries and temporary alliances;
5. scenarios spreading beyond their intended zones;
6. civilians, merchants, medics, journalists, and spectators accepting
   informal roles;
7. temporary territories, rescue corridors, intelligence networks, black
   markets, propaganda, and improvised factions;
8. a final constitutional test distinguishing game, mission, public, and law.

The recurring cultural joke is:

> “This year, the scenarios will remain contained.”

They never remain completely contained.

## 9. Scoring and Victory

Victory is not an elimination count. Each House is scored for:

- mission completion;
- recognition of the true scenario;
- protection of civilians;
- rescue success;
- evidence integrity;
- engineering;
- mobility;
- territorial control;
- constitutional restraint;
- adaptability;
- cooperation;
- correct use of House authority;
- recognition that conditions have changed.

A combat-dominant House may lose for endangering civilians, falsifying
evidence, violating lawful jurisdiction, using excessive force, damaging
Central Junction unnecessarily, mistaking a real emergency for play, confusing
an objective with a distraction, or completing a mission through
unconstitutional means.

> **War performed safely enough to become culture, but seriously enough to expose character.**

The Tournament tests service, not simple supremacy.

## 10. Constitutional Purpose and Awards

The Service Tournament asks:

> **Which House can remain fully itself while still serving the whole of Hollow Grove?**

The champion may receive honor, a ceremonial title, stewardship of a future
Function, bounded temporary operational responsibility, custody of the
Tournament standard or cup, the right to place the first mark in the next War,
or public recognition.

No victory, score, award, market outcome, or Service Mark transfers permanent
sovereignty, makes Central Junction a House possession, permits one House to
rule another, or alters constitutional succession. Central Junction remains a
shared district and not a fifth House.

## 11. Runtime and Provenance Contract

The executable schema is `service-tournament/1.0.0` in
`src/world/service_tournament.rs`. It represents:

- the Service Tournament Function and its Central Junction location;
- one fixed paired-service identity for each House;
- exactly four House representatives;
- the War of a Thousand Hues and its four color owners;
- scenarios, simulation systems, and objectives;
- competitors, operational paint marks, mark grammar, and layer order;
- Service Marks and complete source provenance;
- multi-category scorecards, penalties, results, and bounded awards.

All Tournament records use explicit stable caller-supplied identifiers.
Tournament, War, event, scenario, mark, Service Mark, competitor, objective,
result, location, evidence, and authority identities never derive from
insertion order, and the runtime uses no random identity generation. Replay orders caller-supplied
events by explicit semantic sequence and stable event identity, rejects
duplicates and sequence conflicts, and validates all cross-record references.

The runtime rejects:

- an agency or branch registered as a separate House team;
- a fifth or duplicate House representative;
- a service identity attached to the wrong House;
- a lethal scenario;
- a paint family attached to the wrong House;
- a palette source outside that House family;
- a mark without originating-event, competitor, and evidence provenance;
- a Service Mark whose sources or source events do not match the preserved
  traces;
- a result without all four House scorecards and all constitutional scoring
  categories;
- any result or award that purports to transfer permanent sovereignty.

## 12. Constitutional Integration

This canon is an additive shared Function under the existing Hollow Grove
Compromise (`HOLLOW_GROVE_COMPROMISE_V1_DRAFT.md`):

- the Compromise supplies shared Function law and the nontransfer rule;
- each House constitution governs the House's own authority and restraint;
- Central Junction supplies the shared public district but gains no House
  sovereignty;
- the visual color constitution remains the sole executable palette;
- existing Stonebend Title and Hollowing, Sandmanor proof, Glaüshouse clinical
  and Synthesis, and Flynt recognition and command behavior remain intact.

This document creates no new Hollow Grove Constitution, no common armed
service, no secret-service government, and no eight-faction service layer.
