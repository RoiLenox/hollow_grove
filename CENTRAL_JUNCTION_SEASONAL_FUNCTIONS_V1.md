# Central Junction Seasonal Functions V1

- Status: frozen canonical calendar and shared-Function law
- Calendar schema: `canonical-calendar/1`
- Annual archive: `HGSEA` version `1`
- Golden annual cycle: `central-junction.canonical-year.2047.v1`
- Constitutional basis: the existing Hollow Grove Compromise and four House
  constitutions

## 1. Authority and architecture

The Hollow Grove canonical year and its four Great Functions are additive
shared law under the existing Hollow Grove Compromise. This canon creates no
new Constitution, House, sovereign calendar office, religion, or ownership of
Central Junction.

The implementation boundary is:

`universal recursion kernel`
→ `constitutional runtime`
→ `canonical calendar`
→ `Central Junction Functions`
→ later `16-bit presentation`

The neutral canonical calendar records caller-supplied astronomical
observations and chronology. The Central Junction layer attaches House
presidency and civic meaning. No seasonal or House lore enters the universal
recursion kernel. Godot, clients, sprites, and display order remain
non-authoritative.

Each Great Function is held at Central Junction and attended by the whole
Grove. Its presiding House defines constitutional emphasis without owning the
district, governing the other Houses, or receiving permanent sovereignty.

The shared kernel-facing Hollow Grove phase grammar is now:

> **The Way Back → The Initiation → The Gathering → The Festival → The Way
> Back**

`TheWayBack`, `TheInitiation`, `TheGathering`, and `TheFestival` are the only
top-level Grove phases. At seasonal scale, `TheFestival` maps to the complete
ceremonial event name **The Festival of Mirrors**. The Motion values below are
ritual meanings attached to Great Function records, not competing phase names.

## 2. The canonical year

The canonical year opens at the exact **Winter Solstice** and continues until
immediately before the next Winter Solstice. The next Winter Solstice closes
the prior archive and opens the next year without erasing continuity.

The recurring anchors are:

1. Winter Solstice
2. Spring Equinox
3. Summer Solstice
4. Autumn Equinox

An anchor is not a fixed Gregorian date. Each annual archive supplies:

- a stable observation ID;
- the recurring typed anchor;
- the authoritative astronomical timestamp used by that year;
- the locally observed civil date;
- the supplying authority;
- constitutional evidence.

The recurring anchor, that year's observed instant or date, and the multi-day
civic Function are separate records. Preparation may begin before the apex and
archival departure may follow it. The apex must lie strictly inside the civic
Function interval.

The Way Back may prepare before the new year opens. Its exact Winter Solstice
apex—not its preparation—opens the year.

## 5. Three Function dimensions

Every Great Function has three distinct typed dimensions.

**Celebration** expresses meaning publicly through joy, food, music,
decoration, family and community gathering, markets, parades, performance,
pageantry, cultural exchange, and hospitality.

**Ritual** establishes meaning constitutionally through remembrance, healing,
naming, lawful initiation, communion, recognition, commissioning,
preservation, recording, and transition through the year.

**Competition** tests embodied meaning through games, trials, exhibitions,
contests, demonstrations, House rivalry, service scenarios, and athletic,
intellectual, artistic, technical, and civic challenge.

No Great Function is only a holiday or only a competition. Intensity is the
typed `FunctionIntensity::{Light, Moderate, High, VeryHigh}`:

| Function | Celebration | Ritual | Competition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Way Back** | Very High | Very High | Light |
| **The Initiation** | High | Very High | Moderate |
| **The Gathering / Derrick** | Very High | High | Very High |
| **The Festival of Mirrors** | High | Very High | Moderate |

Each executable Function record must include at least one typed activity in
all three dimensions.

## 6. The Way Back

### Winter Solstice — Glaüshouse — Return

**The Way Back** opens the canonical year. It governs healing, remembrance,
reconciliation, restoration, homecoming, renewal, Bond review, safe closure of
the previous year, and determining what remains capable of returning.

Its cultural parallels may include New Year, homecoming, vigils, remembrance
festivals, lantern festivals, family reunions, cleansing, and renewal. These
are parallels only. The canonical name is The Way Back.

Glaüshouse presides through medicine, diagnosis, recovery, viability,
clearance, and the return of living possibility.

> **What can find its way back?**

Canonical activities may include memorial observances, public healing,
eligible Bond reconciliation, communal meals, lantern and glass-light
displays, restorative craft, rescue and caregiving commendations, gentle
diagnostic or recovery trials, and sealing the previous archive. Competition
remains Light and may not dominate.

## 7. The Initiation

### Spring Equinox — Stonebend — Incarnate

**The Initiation** governs lawful beginning, naming, embodiment, belonging, and
entrance into public form.

Its baptismal principle is the lawful threshold: receiving a Name or Title;
entering a House, apprenticeship, office, institution, project, or life stage;
receiving sponsors, witnesses, or mentors; and being recognized as belonging.
The analogy creates no automatic Title, office, membership, or consent.

Stonebend's **Eternal Christmas** is Christmas in the eternal, incarnational sense,
not winter weather or a fixed Gregorian date:

- possibility receives form;
- promise becomes present;
- something receives a body;
- a gift enters the world;
- a person receives shelter and place;
- an idea becomes materially real;
- a foundation is laid.

> **What is ready to enter the world and receive a place?**

Canonical activities may include public naming, Titles and apprenticeships,
dedications, foundation ceremonies, lawful project charters, buildings,
signs, boundaries, monuments, architecture and construction exhibitions,
structural trials, surveying, stoneworking, gifts of new beginning, and public
welcome.

Stonebend's Name, Title, structure, boundary, lawful identity, permanence, and
Hollowing law remain intact. The Initiation never authorizes Illegal Hollowing.

## 8. The Gathering, also known as Derrick

### Summer Solstice — Sandmanor — Commune

**The Gathering** is the formal name. **Derrick** is its accepted cultural
alias. It is the largest and most spectacular of the four seasonal Great
Functions.

It celebrates communion, shared tables, exchange, travel, design,
cultivation, public expression, diplomacy, performance, markets, games, House
exhibitions, and civic encounter. Cultural parallels may include communion,
the Olympics, a world's fair, midsummer festivals, pilgrimage, public feasts,
and interregional conventions. Hollow Grove's terminology remains
authoritative.

> **What can we become together?**

Minorians shape interior preparation, cultivation, arrangement, hospitality,
Aura Fields, Prefog, Gnome-to-Minotaur development, and maintenance of the
spaces in which the Gathering occurs.

Minoans shape outward travel, navigation, delegations, exchange, Aura Beach,
Current Sea, Prefig, Elf-to-Centaur development, and connection to the wider
world.

### The Service Tournament within The Gathering

The Service Tournament is nested inside The Gathering as one of Summer's
largest specialized events. It retains its stable Tournament identity,
`HGSTA` archive, four complete paired-service representatives, nonlethal War
of a Thousand Hues, Service Marks, Edge of Tomorrow, and Glass of a Thousand
Hues.

The Tournament does not replace the Gathering. Summer also contains food,
music, markets, shared communion, diplomacy, House exhibitions, public
Synthesis presentations, crafts, performances, athletic and civic events,
family participation, shared meals, and cultural exchange.

The Gathering is the largest seasonal Great Function. The Service Tournament
retains its existing status as Central Junction's largest specialized public
service trial and nested Function. The two scopes are not rival sovereign
claims.

## 9. The Festival of Mirrors

### Autumn Equinox — Flynt — Confirm

**The Festival of Mirrors** governs recognition, confirmation, commissioning,
reflection, harvest, implementation, and what the Grove carries forward.

Cultural parallels may include confirmation, commissioning, graduation,
harvest festivals, awards, invention and state fairs, public audits, and
preservation festivals.

> **What has proven worthy of being carried forward?**

Flynt presides through Resynce, Recog, engineering, recognition, operational
personhood, allegiance, implementation, and carrying a result into public use.

Activities may include constitutional year review, public recognition,
commissioning, Service Tournament audit, Service Mark preservation, invention
and engineering demonstrations, repair and refinement trials, Synthesis
Recipe ratification, lawful use of completed creations, Edge and Glass custody
recognition, reflection upon failure, and preparation for winter closure.

The mirrors signify the Grove seeing itself, participants confronting their
results, the year reflecting action back upon the Houses, achievement and
failure recognized together, Current and Aura facing one another, and Recog as
public manifestation.

Recognition never praises tactics automatically. Constitutional restraint,
civilian safety, evidence integrity, and lawful authority remain part of
confirmation. Festival recognition grants no ownership or permanent
sovereignty.

## 10. Function lifecycle

Every Great Function records exactly:

1. Preparation
2. Gathering
3. Eve
4. Astronomical Apex
5. Celebration
6. Return or Departure
7. Archive

Phase identities and timestamps are caller supplied. Phases are strictly
ordered, evidenced, and contained by the Function interval. The Astronomical
Apex phase equals the annual authoritative anchor observation.

Within one canonical year the anchors remain Winter, Spring, Summer, Autumn.
The next Winter boundary closes the archive and identifies the next year
without deriving identity from vector position.

## 16. Directional passage correction

The Winter Great Function and the permanent Way Back route share display
language but not identity:

- `function.central-junction.seasonal.way-back` is the recurring civic Function;
- `route.aura-way` is the permanent complete route;
- `passage.the-way-back` is the constitutional directional rule;
- each traveler receives a caller-supplied passage identity;
- `route-segment.stairway-to-heaven` is the ascent-only expression.

The controlling directional law is:

> **The Way Back descends from Stonebend and ascends from Flynt, MI.**

A descent is exactly Stonebend → Flynt through Aura Way. An ascent is exactly
Flynt → Stonebend through Aura Way and may use Stairway to Heaven. Stairway to
Heaven may never descend or originate in Stonebend.

Glaüshouse presides over the Winter Function, tends travelers, evaluates
viability, and supplies clearance without owning or originating the route.
Sandmanor arranges and provisions passage without owning or changing its
endpoints. The concise relation is:

> **The Way Back descends from Stonebend, ascends from Flynt, is tended by
> Glaüshouse, and is arranged through Sandmanor.**

The golden fixture records one descent, one Stairway ascent, Glaüshouse care,
Sandmanor arrangement, explicit authority, evidence, provenance, and no route
owner. Aura Way remains lawful outside the Winter Function.

The older `geography.route.aura-way` / `aura-way.design-corridor` catalog entry
is retained solely as an archive-compatible Design-screen projection. It is
not the permanent route identity, does not determine Way Back endpoints, and
must not be used to infer direction. The controlling permanent identity is
`route.aura-way`.

## 17. Current Sea, Riptide, Undertow, and Boardwalk

The hard physical relation is:

> **The Current Sea sets in Glaüshouse, rises by Riptide, and reaches Flynt,
> MI along the Boardwalk.**

The records remain type-distinct:

- body: `region.current-sea`;
- rising force: `force.riptide`;
- inverse hazard: `force.undertow`;
- lawful traveler route: `route.boardwalk`.

The Current Sea settles in Glaüshouse for diagnosis, treatment, clearing,
rest, reflection, viability review, and preparation for renewed movement.
Glaüshouse does not terminate Current permanently.

Riptide is a force inside the Current Sea. It may renew Current from
Glaüshouse toward Flynt without carrying a person. Undertow is the dangerous
downward or beneath-ward inverse and never satisfies successful emergence.
Neither force is a road.

The Boardwalk carries people, crews, patients, agents, goods, messages,
recognized identities, and constitutional processions between Glaüshouse and
Flynt. It may reference a Riptide event but never becomes that event:

> **The Current rises through Riptide.**
> **The person travels by the Boardwalk.**

Arrival creates no automatic Recog. The archived progression is separately
validated:

`Glaüshouse clearance`
→ `Riptide renewal`
→ `Boardwalk passage`
→ `Flynt Resynce`
→ `lawful Recog review`

Clearance does not grant Recog. Boardwalk arrival does not grant Recog.
Accepted Recog requires a separately identified accepted Resynce, manifestation
conditions, authority, evidence, and provenance.

The older `geography.route.current-sea` catalog token is retained only as an
archive-compatible civic-interface projection. It is not `region.current-sea`
and cannot redefine the body as a road. The older Riptide and Boardwalk map
projections likewise remain presentation/traversal witnesses beneath the new
typed force and route law.
