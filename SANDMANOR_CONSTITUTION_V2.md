# The Sandmanor Constitution V2

Status: ratified constitutional law

Authority: canonical Sandmanor architecture

Signature offense: **Fraudulent Design**

## Preamble

Sandmanor exists so that possibility may earn its place in civilization
through proof rather than force. It governs design before construction, method
before demonstration, teaching before mastery, evidence before acceptance, and
improvement before authority.

> **Sandmanor proves.**

> **Design proposes. Method orders. Teaching transmits. Practice develops.**

> **Demonstration reveals. Evidence supports. Criticism tests.**

> **Reproduction confirms. Revision improves. Reciprocity transforms.**

Proof exposes a claim to lawful examination and preserves enough evidence for
others to understand, challenge, reproduce, improve, or reject it. No design
enters Hollow Grove civilization through deception.

## Article I — Constitutional Domain

Sandmanor's Domain is **Soul** and its Law is **Pattern**. Its two faculties are
**Prefog** for the Minorian tradition and **Prefig** for the Minoan tradition.
Faculties are distinct from Current, Aura, Synthesis, and rank.

Sandmanor protects honest design and evidence, transparent and reproducible
methods, intellectual lineage, recorded failure, revision history, fair
comparison, meaningful education, reciprocal teaching, criticism, disclosed
uncertainty, and access to reviewable evidence.

Sandmanor may govern designs, methods, recipes, experiments, demonstrations,
prototypes, standards, measurements, models, curricula, apprenticeships,
competitions, public works, tools, machines, agriculture, navigation,
manufacturing, Synthesis recipes, medical protocols, software, algorithms,
interfaces, research, institutions, and improvement claims. It governs the
claim and evidence, not ownership of the examined object.

Proof never automatically creates Stonebend Name or Title, Glaüshouse
clearance or consent, Flynt recognition, office, ownership, moral goodness,
universal applicability, permanent correctness, or freedom from risk.

## Article II — Two Peoples and Distinct Ontology

Sandmanor consists of two equal, complementary peoples and traditions:

- **Minorians**;
- **Minoans**.

Neither is constitutionally superior. Neither may permanently govern without
learning from the other. They are parallel populations, never stages of one
population. Neither people nor its base Form progresses into the other.

| People | Minorian | Minoan |

| Cultural space | Content Farm / Aura Field | Aura Beach |
| Social roles | Husbandman → Householder → Hearthkeeper | Beach Bum → Party Boy → Socialite |
| Institutional attendant | Geomancer | Labyrinthian |
| Sovereign Hand | Hecaton | Pegasus |

Forms, cultural spaces, social roles, attendant classes, sovereign Hands, and
sovereign identity are distinct ontological categories. The universal
`CURRENT + AURA → SYNTHESIS` grammar remains in force, but no unratified exact
equation may collapse one category into another. Detailed mechanics behind
either three-Form progression remain **STATUS: OPEN**.

## Article III — Minorians

Minorians are the interior, family, Hearth-oriented people and tradition.
Their mythological Form progression is **Gnome → Faun → Minotaur**.

Their question is: **How is this place lived within?**

They govern interiors, rooms, homes, workshops, farms, fields, utilities,
storage, cultivation, manufacturing, maintenance, local logistics,
repairability, resource use, and repeated daily function. Occupational Gnome
Names identify participation, not mastery or exclusive practice.

Their social roles advance **CULTIVATE → ESTABLISH → PRESERVE** through
Husbandman, Householder, and Hearthkeeper. These are roles, not Forms: a
Minotaur may also be a Hearthkeeper. Geomancer is the Minorian institutional
attendant class, distinct from every Form and social role.

The Content Farm cultivates what a family has and makes: family, descendants,
relationships, memories, stories, traditions, knowledge, craft, creative work,
possessions, household culture, inheritance, reputation, and accumulated life
experience. It is not merely an internet-content operation. The closely linked
Aura Field cultivates and preserves what the family is and projects: identity,
continuity, presence, heritage, relationship, reputation, and accumulated
Aura.

**Hecaton is the Minorian Hand of the Sandman.** Hecaton stands above or
around the Geomancer apparatus at sovereign level; Hecaton is not a Form,
Minotaur stage IV, Demiurge, or the Sandman. Appointment, removal, and
succession mechanics for Hecaton remain **STATUS: OPEN**.

> **Improve what must continue working.**

## Article IV — Minoans

Minoans are the outward, social, celebratory people and tradition. Their
mythological Form progression is **Elf → Satyr → Centaur**.

Their question is: **How does this place live within the world?**

They govern exteriors, landscapes, beaches, coasts, routes, ports, sea access,
navigation, exploration, public approach, trade corridors, environmental
relationship, transportation, borders as experienced space, and outward
communication.

Their social roles advance **Beach Bum → Party Boy → Socialite**. The Beach Bum
finds the scene; the Party Boy creates the scene; the Socialite is the scene.
Socialite is the mature canonical title, not Influencer: social significance
emerges from presence, reputation, taste, relationships, invitations, and
associations rather than required content performance. These are roles, not
Forms; Satyr is not Party Boy.

Aura Beach is their cultural and social environment, not merely a literal
beach. It carries the atmosphere of an idealized wealthy beach social scene:
nightlife, parties, fashion, music, dancing, leisure, hospitality, beauty,
romance, festivals, public display, pleasure, gathering, and cultural
circulation. It expresses and enjoys the abundance that Content Farm
cultivates and Aura Field preserves.

Labyrinthian is the Minoan institutional attendant class. Its alignment with
social pathways and networks does not make it a Form or social title.
**Pegasus is the Minoan Hand of the Sandman**, not Centaur stage IV, Demiurge,
or the Sandman. Appointment, removal, and succession mechanics for Pegasus
remain **STATUS: OPEN**.

> **Discover what may become reachable.**

## Article V — Equality and Cross-Tradition Review

Minorians and Minoans possess equal dignity. Interior function and exterior
relationship remain distinct so that each can correct the other. Major designs
affecting both require review from both traditions. Culture and specialization
do not create geographic imprisonment.

## Article VI — The Sandman / Demiurge

**The Sandman = Demiurge.** Demiurge is the sovereign Synthesizer and apex
identity of the Sandman, not an ordinary attendant class or population. The
Sandman is Sandmanor's singular sovereign and highest constitutional office,
guardian
of proof, reciprocal teaching, design integrity, educational fairness, honest
competition, public demonstration, revision, standards, research, and
Minorian–Minoan balance.

At sovereign level, Hecaton and Pegasus are parallel Hands beneath and around
the Sandman/Demiurge. They represent Sandmanor's Minorian and Minoan halves
without becoming the same people and without becoming ordinary Form stages.

The governing question is:

> **Has this claim earned the right to be relied upon as proven?**

The Sandman may issue or suspend proof, convene demonstrations and reproduction
trials, require assumptions to be disclosed, establish evidence standards,
protect critics, preserve failed experiments, invalidate manipulated
demonstrations, appoint independent reviewers, coordinate cross-House proof,
and confirm major institutional leadership.

The Sandman may not manufacture evidence, suppress failure, grant another
House's authority, punish good-faith questions, hide uncertainty, subordinate
one tradition, manipulate the Contest, claim another's work, or select a
successor directly.

## Article VII — Contest of Improvement

The Sandman is selected through the **Contest of Improvement**. The Contest is
neither battle nor untracked popularity contest. Its final pair is one
qualified Minorian who holds the Hecaton Hand and Guardian of the Whole Farm
mantle and one qualified Minoan who holds the Pegasus Hand and Guardian of the
Horizon mantle. Their ordinary Forms remain Minotaur and Centaur respectively.
They
teach each other meaningful practice, design principle, method, observation,
criticism, and tradition-specific skill.

The question is:

> **Who became most improved through learning from the other?**

Each candidate must have Sandmanor standing, teaching experience, willingness
to learn, preserved records, competence in their own tradition, and no
unresolved Fraudulent Design conviction.

The audience receives both baselines, the teaching process, challenges,
resulting work, evidence of learning, assistance disclosures, and an
opportunity to question both candidates. An independent review body contains
Minorian and Minoan reviewers, teaching representatives, affected citizens,
evidence stewards, and conflict reviewers.

The audited crowd judges which candidate shows the greatest constitutional
improvement relative to their own published baseline. The independent review
body validates evidence, conflicts, voter identity, and the tally; it does not
privately appoint the winner. Insufficient improvement produces no winner. A
genuine tie leaves the office unresolved until a further reciprocal trial or a
later Contest. Contest fraud or teaching sabotage voids affected evidence or
the Contest.

## Article VIII — Sandman Accession, Term, and Removal

After lawful victory, the Contest record is completed, challenges are heard,
evidence is reviewed, the Sandman Recipe is authorized, Glaüshouse reviews
compatibility, the one winner undergoes lawful maintained sovereign Synthesis
or convergence, Stonebend records Title where required, Flynt recognizes
institutional function, the winner publicly states what was learned, and the
singular mantle is sealed.

Sandman accession never arises from conquest, heredity, wealth, fame,
popularity, transformation, recognition alone, or legacy progression.

The Sandman serves a defined constitutional term or until lawful succession,
removal, incapacity, resignation, or death, and must remain capable of learning.
Fraudulent Design, proof manipulation, failure destruction, plagiarism,
Contest corruption, critic retaliation, one-sided domination, conflict
concealment, incapacity, or constitutional betrayal support removal through
recorded review and sealed succession.

## Article IX — Interim Governance

During vacancy a Minorian steward and Minoan steward govern jointly. Neither
becomes Sandman. Permanent constitutional alteration is prohibited, high proof
requires joint agreement or independent review, and a new Contest must be
organized promptly. Emergency action remains narrow and temporary.

## Article X — Design

A Design is a structured proposal for intended function. It is not proof.
Every complete design identifies author or design body, purpose, users,
problem, assumptions, inputs, outputs, dependencies, constraints, environment,
method, materials, expected result, risks, failure states, maintenance,
alternatives, measurement, version, revision history, conflicts, and proof
status.

Material assumptions must be disclosed. Proof applies only within demonstrated
scope and does not automatically scale across region, subject class,
environment, duration, or implementation size.

## Article XI — Methods

A Method is an ordered process for investigation, building, teaching,
comparison, testing, or demonstration. It identifies objective, sequence,
materials, operators, controls, comparison basis, measurements, recording,
stopping and exclusion rules, uncertainty, deviations, and analysis.

Post-result method alteration intended to improve appearances is prohibited.
Confidentiality may limit disclosure only when independent review remains
possible and the public claim is limited accordingly.

## Article XII — Prototypes

A prototype is preliminary and never a finished system by implication.
Demonstrations disclose what is functional, simulated, incomplete, assisted,
controlled, and untested. Hidden manual control, prerecorded output presented
as live, concealed support, selective editing, and one-time results represented
as stable operation are prototype fraud.

## Article XIII — Demonstration

A demonstration records claim, subject, environment, operator, method,
observers, inputs, outputs, deviations, failures, measurements, and the complete
actual result. Conditions must fit the claim, and favorable artificial
conditions limit the claim. Failed demonstrations remain evidence.

## Article XIV — Evidence

Evidence materially supports or opposes a claim and may be direct, indirect,
observational, experimental, comparative, field, testimonial, historical,
simulated, negative, or replication evidence. It remains attributable,
relevant, preserved, reviewable, complete enough, traceable to method, and
resistant to silent alteration.

Evidence custody preserves source, collector, sequence, method, custody,
alterations, access history, and relation to claim. Stonebend may verify
identity, Seal, and custody; Sandmanor determines relevance to proof.

## Article XV — Measurement

Measurement requires an identified subject, known standard, appropriate
method, disclosed uncertainty, and lawful comparison. Sandmanor maintains
standards for dimensions, time, capacity, yield, distance, force, Aura and
Current response, reliability, durability, learning, improvement, reproduction,
error, and tolerance. False precision and silent standard changes are
prohibited.

## Article XVI — Proof

Proof is constitutional reliability within a defined scope, not metaphysical
certainty. Its lifecycle is proposal, design, method declaration, preliminary
review, prototype, demonstration, evidence, criticism, revision, reproduction,
scope judgment, publication, continuing review, and renewal, limitation,
deprecation, or Tombstone.

Statuses include unexamined, proposed, provisionally supported, demonstrated,
conditionally proven, proven within scope, independently reproduced,
field-proven, disputed, weakened, superseded, disproven, deprecated, and
Tombstoned.

The claimant bears the initial burden. High-risk, irreversible, mass,
constitutional, medical, Synthesis, and infrastructure claims carry greater
burdens and require meaningful independent reproduction. Material changes in
conditions, scale, implementation, users, methods, or later evidence trigger
review.

## Article XVII — Criticism

Criticism is necessary to proof. A protected claim cannot receive the highest
status. Good-faith critics may inspect evidence, question assumptions,
challenge methods, request reproduction, preserve dissent, publish negative
findings, and refuse insufficient proof. Retaliation and erasure are offenses;
knowingly false accusation is not protected as good faith.

## Article XVIII — Failure

> **Failure is evidence.**

Every failure record identifies intended and actual result, conditions, method,
operator, cause or uncertainty, harm, correction, proof effect, and future
recommendations. Failed work may remain a warning, teaching instrument,
revision basis, component source, historical record, or constitutional
evidence. It may never be silently deleted.

## Article XIX — Revision

Revision records prior version, changes, author, reason, evidence, expected
effect, compatibility, risks, and migration. A material change to core
function, result, materials, safety, obligations, identity consequences,
compatibility, or proof scope requires renewed examination. A new version may
not silently inherit the proof of a materially different predecessor.

## Article XX — Recipes

A recipe records Name, version, author or lineage, purpose, inputs, provenance,
quantities or relations, conditions, sequence, operator requirements, tools,
result, variation, failures, stopping conditions, storage, disposal,
maintenance, recovery, revisions, and proof status.

Derived recipes preserve source, modifications, attribution, compatibility,
inherited risks, and evidence of improvement. Sandmanor proves Synthesis recipe
logic; Glaüshouse clears application; Stonebend preserves identity and Title;
Flynt recognizes operators and institutions.

## Article XXI — Teaching

Teaching transfers usable understanding rather than merely presenting
information. Teachers distinguish fact, model, opinion, and uncertainty,
explain method, permit questions, correct error, preserve lineage, disclose
conflicts, assess fairly, and admit uncertainty. Students may question,
inspect corrections and criteria, challenge error, refuse unsafe
demonstrations, receive support, and preserve evidence of learning.

Indoctrination, humiliation, plagiarism, labor extraction disguised as
education, unsafe experimentation, political conditioning, and fabricated
competence are prohibited.

## Article XXII — Apprenticeship

Apprenticeship joins supervised practice to documented teaching. Agreements
define teacher, apprentice, field, duration, goals, work, support, safety,
assessment, work ownership, credit, termination, and complaints. Labor without
genuine teaching is exploitation. Mastery requires demonstrated ability to
perform, explain, adapt, correct, teach, preserve safety, recognize limits, and
learn from criticism.

## Article XXIII — Schools, Guilds, and Design Institutions

Schools, guilds, academies, workshops, laboratories, and design houses require
lawful Stonebend identity and structure, defined purpose, qualified teachers,
transparent standards, preserved records, complaints, proof rules, succession,
conflict disclosure, and Flynt recognition where required.

Closure preserves student records, credentials, designs, obligations, failed
experiments, research, ownership, and successor duties.

## Article XXIV — Assessment and Qualification

Assessment uses disclosed, relevant, consistent, accessible, reviewable
criteria to measure demonstrated learning or competence. Every credential
references a real assessment and remains limited to demonstrated scope. False
completion, purchased qualification, impersonation, altered results, hidden
assistance, fabricated apprenticeship, and unassessed credentials are fraud.

## Article XXV — Competition

Competition is lawful when it reveals difference, produces learning, and
improves design. Rules disclose objective, entrants, criteria, conflicts,
resources, assistance, measurement, appeal, failure treatment, and ownership.
Evidence may support a combined design, shared proof, divided scope, joint
revision, or no singular winner.

## Article XXVI — Research

Research reduces uncertainty and begins with a genuine question. It declares
prior knowledge, hypothesis, method, subject protections, measurements,
analysis, limitations, conflicts, records, publication, and stopping conditions.
Negative and inconclusive findings remain visible. Sponsorship and publication
control are disclosed. Living-subject research retains Glaüshouse authority
over consent, clearance, safety, and recovery.

## Article XXVII — Standards

Standards enable common measurement, quality, compatibility, safety,
instruction, and performance. Creation requires need, scope, evidence, public
review, both traditions where relevant, implementation, transition, revision,
and appeal. Standards may not secretly exclude competitors, require private
access, hide rationale, resist decisive evidence, or disguise favoritism.

## Article XXVIII — Interior and Exterior Design Law

Minorians hold primary cultural authority over interior access, movement, work,
rest, storage, safety, privacy, maintenance, sensory conditions, resource use,
and repair. Minoans hold primary cultural authority over exterior approach,
orientation, landscape, transport, public meaning, environment, navigation,
connection, visibility, climate, and shared space.

Whole-design review prevents interior usability, exterior access, maintenance,
public relationship, and ordinary users from being sacrificed for display or
private control.

## Article XXX — Fraudulent Design

**Fraudulent Design** knowingly or recklessly presents a false, manipulated,
plagiarized, concealed, impossible, or materially misleading design, method,
recipe, lesson, demonstration, measurement, qualification, or proof as
trustworthy.

> **Fraudulent Design unlawfully converts deception into apparent proof.**

It includes fabricated evidence, false measurement, hidden failure,
manipulated demonstration, counterfeit prototype or recipe, false
reproduction, selective omission, plagiarism, stolen lineage, hidden
assumptions or conflicts, falsified improvement or credentials, post-result
method alteration, simulated performance presented as physical, destroyed
failures, and universal claims from narrow tests.

First degree concerns premeditated fraud causing or intended for mass harm,
Illegal Synthesis, structural failure, medical injury, constitutional seizure,
public-resource destruction, educational corruption, death, or identity harm.
Second degree covers intentional substantial deception; third degree covers
reckless severe misconduct; procedural offenses cover preservation,
attribution, review, and recording failures. Attempt and institutional
responsibility are preserved.

## Article XXXI — Other Offenses

## Article XXXII — Remedies

Remedies include correction, retraction, disclosure, proof suspension or
revocation, revision, renewed demonstration, independent reproduction,
attribution restoration, failure preservation, compensation, educational
repair, reassessment, credential revocation, office removal, privilege
suspension, institutional probation or dissolution, standard replacement,
Contest invalidation, research quarantine, warning, and referral to the House
owning any additional offense. Correction never erases fraudulent history.

## Article XXXIII — Appeals and Review

Proof, assessment, credential, fraud, Contest, standard, discipline,
authorship, and scope decisions may be appealed. Review considers claim,
evidence, method, assumptions, criticism, conflicts, fairness, later evidence,
reliance, and harm. High review applies to Sandman implication, disputed
Contest, major standards, mass harm, multi-House jurisdiction, institutional
accusation, governance effects, or destroyed archives.

Final proof may reopen for decisive evidence, fraud, hidden conflict, failed
reproduction, identity substitution, archive corruption, material condition
change, or mismatch between tested and deployed design.

## Article XXXIV — Emergency Design Authority

Emergency design discloses incomplete proof, risks, urgency, temporary scope,
operator, monitoring, stopping conditions, review date, and replacement plan.
It cannot become permanent proof, conceal failure, bypass Glaüshouse safety,
create office, suppress criticism, avoid review, or outlive the emergency
without ordinary proof.

## Article XXXV — Intellectual Lineage, Credit, and Common Knowledge

Design lineage preserves original author, predecessors, teachers, apprentices,
contributors, revisions, borrowed methods, and regional traditions. Credit
follows actual contribution. Common civic knowledge remains attributable.
Knowledge essential to public safety, emergency repair, participation, basic
education, and critical infrastructure cannot be concealed solely to preserve
private power.

## Article XXXVI — Sandmanor Records

Sandmanor preserves designs, methods, recipes, demonstrations, evidence,
failures, criticisms, reproductions, revisions, standards, curricula,
assessments, competitions, Contests, proof judgments, conflicts, appeals, and
retractions. Records remain versioned, attributable, preserved, reviewable,
correctable without silent erasure, linked to Stonebend identity, and lawfully

## Service Tournament Interface

At the Service Tournament, Sandmanor expresses the fixed **CIA & Navy**
reference as one Sandmanor cultural-government identity. Intelligence,
secrecy, navigation, maritime movement, foreign contact, source cultivation,
indirect influence, contested Currents, coastal operations, and interpretation
of incomplete information remain subject to Sandmanor proof, reciprocity, and
the Contest of Improvement. No separate agency team, naval government, or
parallel command is created.

Literal maritime exercises occur at Aura Beach, along the coast, or on
designated canals. The paired-service reference does not reclassify Current
Sea, which remains a civic sea-of-people concourse.

Sandmanor owns the Red family in the War of a Thousand Hues. Tournament
intelligence, staged evidence, and successful infiltration do not become
constitutional proof without the ordinary proof process. Tournament victory
grants no permanent sovereignty and does not alter Sandmanor or another
House's succession.

Tournament Synthesis records preserve the existing regional paths rather than
inventing prize forms: the Minorian path is inward **Prefog**,
Gnome-to-Faun-to-Minotaur lineage, and Aura Fields jurisdiction; the Minoan path is
inward **Prefig**, Elf-to-Satyr-to-Centaur lineage, and Aura Beach and
Current Sea jurisdiction. Only an accepted Kiss may apply the existing
physical manifestation. Sandmanor's sand, separation, preparation, pattern,
and arrangement tradition may inform prizes, while a Recipe remains a Recipe
until lawfully realized.
