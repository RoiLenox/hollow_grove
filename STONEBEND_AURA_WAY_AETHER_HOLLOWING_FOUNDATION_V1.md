# Stonebend Aura Way, Aether, and Hollowing Foundation V1

Status: canonical first Stonebend pass
Scope: vertical law, Aura Way, the Aether–Current continuum, lawful material
Hollowing, proof, provenance, stone refraction, and preliminary Stonebend
recognition
Executable authority:
`src/world/stonebend/foundation.rs`
Parent constitutional authority: `STONEBEND_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`

This foundation supplements the Stonebend Constitution. It does not finish
Stonebend government, Hypergiant succession, the Title lifecycle, Gerald
institutions, Freemason structure, or the full Illegal Hollowing code. Existing
Stonebend authority, subject-Hollowing, and succession law remains in force.

## 1. The vertical law

Hollow Grove has one central vertical relationship:

> Mt. Aura is Aether.

> Riptide is Bathos.

> Aura is Aether revealed through Form.

> Current is Bathos embodied through weight.

Aether and Bathos are metaphysical poles. Aura and Current are their physical
manifestations. They describe one continuous relationship among ideal and
consequence, weightlessness and weight, ascent and descent, possibility and
embodiment, revelation and repetition.

Neither pole is a moral absolute. Aether without Bathos can become empty
perfection, unreachable elitism, or elevation without consequence. Bathos
without Aether can become cynicism, mockery, or burden without direction.

> Aether gives life something worth becoming. Bathos ensures becoming remains
> answerable to reality.

## 2. The Aether–Current continuum

> Aether is weightless Current.

> Current is the heaviest Aether.

Aether and Current are opposite burden states of one underlying medium, not
unrelated resources. Aether is the least-burdened limit: universal,
weightless, clear, highly potential, and not yet given a regional, mineral,
bodily, or cultural property.

Current is that same medium bearing material consequence. It is condensed,
mixed, resistant, embodied, storable, transportable, and repeatable. It may
flow, pool, circulate, settle into channels, carry residue, become
contaminated, be pumped, be stored, be separated into fractions, or be
exhausted.

> Aether is Current without burden. Current is Aether bearing the world.

Current is not waste, corruption, failed Aether, or evil. Its weight makes
embodiment, persistence, storage, circulation, memory, and consequence
possible.

> Current repeats because weight teaches it where to return.

This foundation preserves the bedrock law:

> Current repeats. Aura reveals. Relativity bends. Synthesis transforms.

## 3. Mt. Aura

Mt. Aura is the geographic and metaphysical symbol of Aether: the ideal,
pinnacle, perfected possibility, mastery aligned with responsibility, and the
highest truthful expression of a Way.

Mt. Aura is not a fifth House, a market institution, a licensing board, an
ordinary school, merely an examination site, or Stonebend property. Every
House may approach the ideal through its own discipline.

> Every House has a path up the mountain. No House owns the summit.

No finite Title, license, office, examination, or professional completion
possesses the ideal forever.

> No one finishes the ideal. They climb until their work can bear its name.

Mt. Aura remains the established Stonebend–Sandmanor `Aspire` route and retains
its High Mine branch. The ideal meaning adds no new route verb, authority gate,
or mandatory physical climb.

## 4. Riptide

Riptide is the geographic and metaphysical symbol of Bathos: descent, material
weight, bodily reality, consequence, exposure, collapse, and unsupported
elevation meeting what is real.

> Riptide reveals what only pretended it could stand.

Honest failure is not automatically Bathos. A person who attempts difficult
work, stays within lawful scope, admits uncertainty, learns, or fails without
deception has not committed a constitutional wrong. Bathos is especially
revealed where claimed mastery outruns competence or hidden appetite defeats a
performed ideal.

Riptide remains the Flynt–Glaüshouse `Retrieve` route and emergency Current
recovery geography. Stonebend does not own Riptide or Bathos. This symbolic
meaning does not alter rescue, intake, Current-well, or route behavior.

## 5. Aura Way

Aura Way is Hollow Grove's standard ordinary recognized institutional route
into a profession, office, lawful Title, advanced responsibility, or other
recognized completion.

Its first-pass ordered stages are:

```text
prerequisite
→ education
→ supervised practice
→ examination
→ demonstrated responsibility
→ recognition eligibility
```

An institution may express foundational education through school,
apprenticeship, or another lawful teaching form. The relevant House teaches
the profession. Aura Way makes the sequence, prerequisites, supervision,
evidence, examination, remediation, and possible recognition legible.

Aura Way is not a mystical shortcut, secret path, exceptional waiver, or
guarantee.

> Aura Way does not promise success. It promises that the steps are known.

> The Houses teach the work. Aura Way organizes the path. Stonebend names the
> completion.

Stage identity is semantic. It is never inferred from vector position,
catalog order, insertion order, or random values. A missing required stage
prevents recognition eligibility.

The stable Stonebend–Sandmanor `Design` entry is now an archive-compatible
institutional projection. The controlling permanent `route.aura-way` joins
Stonebend and Flynt: The Way Back descends from Stonebend and ascends from
Flynt, with Stairway to Heaven ascent-only. The Design projection preserves
its institutional sequence without controlling the permanent route's
endpoints or direction.

## 6. The legacy Design projection from Sandmanor to Stonebend

The archive-compatible Design projection is:

```text
Sandmanor
→ Aura Way
→ Mt. Aura
→ Sandmanor-facing Stonebend gate
→ Stonebend
```

Its philosophical sequence is:

```text
possibility
→ disciplined advancement
→ measurement against the ideal
→ evidence review
→ lawful identity
```

Sandmanor designs what might become. Aura Way develops that possibility
through known stages. Mt. Aura preserves the ideal. The Sandmanor-facing
Stonebend gate reviews the evidence that survived ascent. Stonebend names,
bounds, records, and seals what was actually proven. “Stonebend gate” is a
generic category; the Second Stonebend Pass establishes the three principal
facings without changing this route.

This is both geography and institutional metaphor. Not every profession
requires a literal climb.

## 7. Stonebend's limited first-pass authority

In this foundation Stonebend has preliminary authority over:

- lawful material Hollowing;
- containment and custody boundaries;
- material measures and proof records;
- stable source, process, and batch identity;
- Seals and provenance;
- stone cuts, orientation, and preserved lattice;
- recognized regional mineral profiles;
- storage and transport boundaries;
- lawful claims and unsupported claims.

Stonebend's central question is:

> Can this bear the name it claims?

Stonebend does not create the ideal, Current, Aether, geographic properties, or
metaphysical perfection. It makes transformation, measurement, provenance,
boundaries, and claims publicly legible.

> Stonebend names what has been proven. It does not pretend proof is
> perfection.

## 8. Current batches

A raw Current batch is a heavy, flowing, mixed material state. Its lawful
record preserves stable batch identity, the identity of its shared
Aether–Current lineage, source, quantity, burden state, constituent fractions,
custodian, and extraction evidence.

Fractions distinguish essential support from inert, unstable, contaminating,
or otherwise removable burden. These fields are constitutional records, not a
requirement that every value become a public game statistic.

Current batch identity is caller supplied and stable. It is never derived from
output position, collection order, or randomness.

## 9. Lawful material Hollowing

Hollowing is Stonebend's lawful refinement of Current. It is consistent with
the parent Constitution's wider law of controlled opening, exposure,
separation, extraction, preservation, and removal.

```text
Raw Current
→ lawful opening
→ fraction separation
→ removal of inert or unstable burden
→ concentration
→ proof measurement
→ refined Aether
→ lawful sealing and provenance
```

> Hollowing raises Current by removing what weighs it down.

Lawful material Hollowing records source and result identities, shared medium
lineage, responsible operator, decision authority, permitted removals,
essential preserved fractions, process identity, measured and evidentiary
proof, provenance, and Seal.

The resulting Aether batch has its own stable identity while retaining source
Current and common-lineage provenance. Reordering input records cannot change
the lawful result.

An authorized process may fail without becoming fraud. Failure produces no
secret substitute output and remains distinct from negligence, falsification,
coercion, over-Hollowing, or unauthorized extraction.

## 10. Proof

Proof has two connected meanings:

1. evidence that measures concentration, refinement, or potency;
2. evidence that the process was genuine, authorized, and correctly
   performed.

The first-pass lifecycle is `Unmeasured`, `Measured`, `Recognized`, or
`Rejected`. It does not establish a public numeric scale, lore-facing
percentage, or named proof grade.

Recognized proof is not metaphysical perfection.

## 11. Illegal Hollowing boundary

Lawful Hollowing may remove what is inert, obstructive, dead, false, unstable,
contaminated, or unnecessary to the continuing identity of the whole.

Illegal Hollowing includes unauthorized extraction, material destruction
concealed as refinement, removal of an essential supporting fraction,
falsified provenance, scope excess, and over-Hollowing that destroys the
supporting whole. The full criminal code remains governed by the parent
Constitution and is outside this pass.

> Do not preserve what is merely waste. Do not remove what makes the whole.

> Do not uphold what is hollow. Do not hollow what is whole.

An invalid attempt never silently replaces its source batch.

## 12. Refined Aether

> Aether is Current relieved of weight.

Refined Aether is lighter, less burdened, more volatile, more universal, and
less materially specific than Current. It remains linked to its source through
batch provenance.

Aether is not automatically Aura, a House fuel, a House color, an ordinary
Title, or an unrelated inventory resource. Lawful storage and transport may
require containment, sealed vessels, stability limits, proof, and custody
records. This pass does not lock particular industrial machinery.

## 13. Aura and the prism model

> Aura is Aether revealed through Form.

Aura is Aether made visible, particular, usable, shaped, colored, or
behaviorally specific. It is not an unrelated third fuel and is not ordinarily
a liquid stored inside every gemstone.

The Newtonian prism is the governing analogy:

```text
Aether passes through Form
→ Form refracts, resolves, filters, or resonates with it
→ a characteristic spectral property appears
→ the manifested property is usable Aura
```

> Current flows. Hollowing refines. Aether rises. Stone refracts. Aura reveals.

> Current supplies the force. Crystal supplies the pattern. Aura is the
> pattern revealed as light.

Aura may appear as visible light, field, resonance, heat, pattern, biological
response, signal, or another Form-dependent property. Light and spectrum
remain the primary explanation.

## 14. Geography and stone

Geography forms the stone through composition, lattice, regional pressure,
heat, impurities, environmental history, and formation conditions.

```text
geography
→ stone formation
→ internal pattern
→ Aether refraction
→ regional Aura
→ regional technology and culture
```

> Aether is universal. Aura is particular.

The same Aether batch may manifest different Aura through different regional
stone profiles. Geography affects the profile; it does not manufacture a
separate cosmic substance.

This pass creates no final House-to-stone assignment.

## 16. Stone preservation

Ordinary Aura refraction preserves the stone's internal lattice. A stone may
be discovered, recorded, examined, cut, oriented, polished, mounted, tuned,
sealed, and supplied with proofed Aether.

Melting is not the ordinary Aura process because it may destroy or transform
the lattice that reveals the stone's characteristic behavior. Synthetic recast
materials remain outside this foundation.
