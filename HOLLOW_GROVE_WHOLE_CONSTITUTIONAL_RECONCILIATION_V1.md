## 3. Current Synthesis boundary

```text
META / Hal
    ↔
PLEB / Clouseau (`PLEB ↔ PLEB` witness)
    ↔
BLEP / Underway reflection / Cleopatra
```

Hal, Clouseau, and Cleopatra are named cross-layer clients, not Houses or
kernel primitives. Their records may be received by Hollow Grove only as
scoped evidence with source, uncertainty, refusal, and causal position. They
cannot execute PLEB/META movement, create a Bond, grant a Title, clear a
Sympiote, recognize a function, or choose for a capable Being.

## 5. Federation acceptance contract

The Federation may bind a record only when all of the following are present:

- stable subject, actor, case, role, and event identities;
- originating authority and jurisdiction;
- causal position and parent references;
- evidence and provenance references;
- accepted, rejected, inconclusive, refused, or challenged result;
- consent and stop state when a Being or living intervention is involved;
- next handoff and completion condition;
- version, migration, checksum, and replay data.

Persona advisories and presentation output remain evidence until the responsible
Being, House, Court, or institution commits the lawful result. Rejected and
inconclusive records may enter Federation history as true failed attempts but
cannot produce their intended state.

## 6. Congruence checks

The repository is constitutionally congruent when these statements remain true:

1. No lower layer broadens a higher layer's authority.
2. No House performs another House's governing verb.
3. No Current Synthesis client rewrites PLEB/META grammar.
4. No AI/client output substitutes for consent, proof, clearance, recognition,
   Title, Bond, or Resolve.
5. No gameplay choice silently decides for a capable Being.
6. No presentation path mutates authoritative state.
7. Every accepted cross-layer result has a replayable Federation reference.
8. Every refusal, failure, contradiction, and inconclusive result remains
   historical evidence.
