# The Stonebend Constitution V2

Status: canonical lock
Authority: normative Stonebend constitutional law
Executable projection: `src/world/stonebend.rs`
Second-pass projection: `src/world/stonebend/second_pass.rs`
Neutral institution projection: `src/world/house_institutions.rs`

## Preamble

Stonebend exists so that identity may endure. It establishes the lawful Names by which subjects are known, the Titles by which authority is held, the boundaries by which claims are limited, and the structures by which civilization remains stable through change.

Stonebend does not make a claim true merely by naming it. It receives evidence, establishes identity, marks boundaries, records standing, preserves continuity, and makes lawful determinations durable enough that every other House may safely rely upon them.

> A Name identifies.
> A Title authorizes.
> A Mirror verifies.
> A Seal endures.

To alter a structure is not to erase its history. To Hollow a subject is not to destroy its identity by default. To transform a subject is not to manufacture lawful Title. No authority may name, claim, inherit, open, empty, or replace a subject outside constitutional process.

Stonebend's signature constitutional offense is **Illegal Hollowing**.

## Article I — Domain

The governing verb is:

> **Stonebend names.**

Constitutional naming establishes that a particular Name refers to a particular subject in a defined scope and continuity. Naming does not establish design truth, clinical safety, transformation clearance, functional competence, political recognition, popularity, or virtue.

Stonebend protects identity, Title, structure, boundary, continuity, record, lineage, custody, inheritance, lawful Hollowing, historical existence, and protection against substitution, erasure, unlawful extraction, and counterfeit authority.

Constitutional subjects include Beings, Hollowfolk, creatures, transformed forms, artifacts, materials, structures, geography, offices, institutions, records, Bonds, contracts, claims, estates, inheritances, extracted Hollow, public works, constitutional instruments, software modules, persistent runtime entities, successors, and Tombstones. A subject must possess enough continuity to be distinguished. A conceptual subject requires explicit scope, purpose, and issuing authority.

## Article II — The Geralds

The constitutional people of Stonebend are the **Geralds**. They provide public standing, witness, challenge, stewardship, inheritance, architecture, maintenance, defense, and continuity.

Membership never implies office. A Gerald with lawful civic standing may petition or challenge, report Illegal Hollowing, request Mercury Mirror review, appear before the Proliteriate, inherit lawful property or responsibility, witness proceedings, contest dispossession, and request Tombstone preservation. Civic standing is not sovereign authority.

## Article III — Principal authorities

Stonebend has three distinct constitutional powers:

1. **Freemason** — Claim;
2. **Hypergiant** — Title;
3. **Proliteriate** — Yield.

> The Freemason forges the Claim.
> The Hypergiant bears the Title.
> The Proliteriate shields the Yield.

They are necessary powers, not three equal sovereigns. No one power governs
Claim, Title, and Yield together.

## Article IV — The Hypergiant

**Diamond** is Stonebend's continuing sovereign Title. The Hypergiant is the
singular highest Stonebend office and Diamond's temporary active bearer. At
most one lawful active Hypergiant may exist at a time. Diamond remains real
when vacant.

> Diamond belongs to Stonebend. The Hypergiant bears its weight.

The office preserves the integrity of Names, Titles, succession, boundaries,
sealed records, amendments, high identity disputes, and high continuity
disputes.

The Hypergiant may confirm high Names and Titles, resolve incompatible claims, confirm accession, interpret Stonebend law, order high Mercury Mirror review, suspend a disputed Title, convene the Proliteriate, commission the Freemason, declare structural emergency, issue temporary preservation orders, prevent irreversible disputed Hollowing, ratify lawful amendments, recognize valid Tombstones, and designate lawful successor structures.

The Hypergiant may not create evidence, forge their own sovereign Claim,
manufacture legitimacy, bypass challenge windows, grant Title to a nonexistent
subject, validate incompatible exclusive claims, order Illegal Hollowing,
create permanent unreviewable emergency power, erase former Names or Titles,
turn transformation into office, override a Mirror without recorded reason and
additional evidence, substitute preference for criteria, abolish the
Proliteriate network, appoint a successor by command, treat Diamond as private
property, or simultaneously occupy Proliteriate witness or Freemason office.

Hypergiant accession requires lawful Stonebend identity, no unresolved identity
fraud, mastery of Name, Title, boundary, continuity, and Hollowing law,
qualifying service, a stable Claim, independent Freemason examination,
Proliteriate Yield hearing, relinquishment of protected elevation, descent
through consequence, Flynt or Basin Proof of Persistence, ascent of The
Lazerhorn, a lawful vacancy, sealed accession, and public oath. Form, Frame,
Synthesis, species, combat rank, wealth, lineage, recommendation, and former
office do not automatically create eligibility.

> No Hypergiant claims Diamond without climbing The Lazerhorn.

An outgoing Hypergiant may prepare or recommend a claimant, but cannot appoint
one.

> A Hypergiant may prepare an heir. Diamond recognizes no automatic
> inheritance.

Removal is available for Illegal Hollowing, Title fraud, identity substitution,
concealed evidence, archive destruction, emergency abuse, succession
corruption, refusal of lawful Tombstones, unlawful execution orders,
incapacitation, abandonment, or constitutional betrayal. Any one independent
power may challenge. Removal requires concurrence of the Freemason and a valid
Proliteriate mandate, plus a Tombstone-linked record. The same power cannot
count twice.

During vacancy Diamond is unborne. The Proliteriate network preserves public
witness and the Freemason preserves records, Seals, defenses, and boundaries
within existing bounded authority. Neither becomes Hypergiant. Permanent
amendment and irreversible high Title transfer are barred except for narrow,
reviewable failure prevention.

## Article V — The Proliteriate

The **Proliteriate** is Stonebend's permanent distributed public
constitutional network. It is not one person, one permanent chamber, an
ordinary parliament, a single union, a fourth monarch, or a removable
population category.

> No authority may validate itself without public witness.

Its generic nodes represent districts, guilds or workshops, labor crews or
worksites, and inherited or commonwealth communities. A person or community may
participate in more than one node without acquiring duplicate identity.

The network receives petitions, hears challenges, investigates dispossession
and false naming, reviews boundaries and promised Yield, preserves dissent,
demands reasons, constitutes temporary review panels, authorizes bounded
mandates, raises recallable witnesses, requires restitution, and protects
testimony.

> Every burden must have a witness. Every Yield must return to those who bore
> it.

> The network is permanent. Its voice is temporary.

The Proliteriate may not execute demolition or seizure, create a Seal,
unilaterally grant high Title, rewrite evidence, determine identity without
review, command weapons, perform Hollowing, replace Freemason Claim
examination, or replace Hypergiant Title authority. This pass locks no universal
numerical threshold, quorum, or permanent seat distribution.

A temporary witness carries one verified mandate and loses authority on recall,
completion, or lawful invalidation. The witness may not rule Stonebend, exercise
Diamond, forge a Claim, or expand the mandate.

> The Proliteriate does not elect a ruler. It raises a witness.

> Spartacus is not king of the people. He is the person through whom the people
> stand.

Spartacus is an archetype, not a permanent fourth office.

## Article VI — The Freemason

The **Freemason** is Stonebend's singular seal-bearing constitutional Claim,
craft, and legitimacy power. The existing singular **High Freemason** office
bears that power and leads the wider structural, defensive, sealing, surveying,
custody, and execution institution.

To forge a Claim is to shape, test, examine, measure, refine, record, and prove
it under pressure. It does not mean falsify.

It governs architecture, construction, structural verification, surveys, boundary marking, Seals, custody chains, defensive works, controlled weapons, demolition, lawful breach, lawful Hollowing equipment, evidence preservation, restoration, structural safety, and execution of lawful orders.

It may certify structures, survey, issue physical and digital Seals, preserve evidence, secure disputed property, halt unsafe construction, refuse unlawful orders, dismantle unlawful structures after due process, stabilize emergencies, safeguard extracted Hollow, certify custody, inspect counterfeit Seals, quarantine compromised records, and maintain Stonebend weapons.

It may not rule Stonebend by validation alone, exercise Diamond, certify its own
sovereign Claim, decide public consequence by technical correctness alone,
create office, invent a Name, grant property by occupation, alter boundaries
for private benefit, perform unauthorized Hollowing, destroy evidence, settle
unresolved civil disputes by weapons, treat structural control as sovereignty,
or convert custody into ownership.

> The Freemason may prove that a crown can stand. The Freemason may not place it
> upon their own head by proof alone.

High Freemason accession requires mastery of construction, survey, Seals, custody, lawful Hollowing, defense, refusal discipline, and record integrity, with no unresolved structural fraud or Illegal Hollowing conviction. The Freemason institution nominates, the Proliteriate reviews, and the Hypergiant confirms.

## Article VII — Names

A Name is a lawful identifier attached to one subject in one scope. It establishes reference, continuity, distinction, linkage, and historical identity. It does not establish ownership, office, authority, competence, clearance, recognition, or moral worth.

Name classes are personal, civic, institutional, geographic, structural, office, artifact, lineage, transformed-form, provisional, ceremonial, alias, historical, successor, and Tombstoned.

The lifecycle is subject establishment, petition, evidence, scope, conflict search, required public notice, provisional Name, challenge period, applicable Mercury Mirror review, determination, Seal, and Registry entry.

Provisional Names support immediate reference but not permanent Title. Shared language Names require distinct constitutional identifiers. Renaming preserves former Names, dates, reason, continuity, Titles, and Tombstoned claims; it cannot erase debt, crime, lineage, custody, or responsibility.

Name fraud includes identity duplication, substitution, false continuity or succession, hidden prior Names, fabricated lineage, active use of dead or Tombstoned identity, mislabeled extracted Hollow, and renaming used to conceal Illegal Hollowing.

## Article VIII — Titles

A Title is a lawful grant of standing, authority, ownership, custody, stewardship, jurisdiction, obligation, or office attached to a named subject.

> A Title is not praise. It is a bounded public claim backed by evidence.

Title classes are office, property, custodial, stewardship, jurisdictional, professional, hereditary, temporary, emergency, delegated, collective, institutional, successor, and ceremonial. A ceremonial Title grants no authority unless explicitly joined to a lawful grant.

The lifecycle is named subject, legal basis, qualification, proof, required clearance, required recognition, grant, notice, Seal, exercise, review, and renewal, transfer, inheritance, suspension, revocation, expiration, or Tombstone. Each House retains its own act: Sandmanor may prove, Glaüshouse may clear, Flynt may recognize, and Stonebend records and stabilizes Title.

Ownership, custody, and stewardship remain distinct. Delegation identifies delegator, delegate, scope, duration, revocability, limits, succession effect, and redelegation. Delegation does not transfer underlying Title unless stated.

Suspension prevents exercise but preserves history. Revocation requires lawful reason, record, and Tombstone.

A recognized entity retains one stable core Title. The Sandmanor-facing,
Central Junction-facing, and Flynt-facing gates may independently attach
Formation Recognition, Public Circulation, and Operational Deployment scopes.
Recognition at one gate implies none at another. Limitation or rejection of one
scope does not silently erase the core Title or unrelated scopes.

> A name may be lawful without being universally authorized.

## Article IX — Property, territory, and boundary

A boundary limits lawful claim and may be physical, geographic, institutional, jurisdictional, temporal, functional, digital, bodily, or procedural. It must be sufficiently clear that authority's beginning and end are knowable.

A survey records subject, location or scope, neighboring claims, landmarks, measurements, uncertainty, surveyor, authority, evidence, sequence, and amendments. The Freemason certifies surveys, the Proliteriate hears public objection, and the Hypergiant resolves high conflict.

Stonebend preserves the lawful identity, structure, access conditions, and
continuity of public roads, bridges, stairs, passes, tunnels, buildings,
defenses, and boundary markers. It has three principal constitutional gates:
one facing Flynt through **Stairway to Heaven** and **Basin Motor Speedway**;
one facing Central Junction through the **Craft Corridor**; and one facing
Sandmanor through **Aura Way** and **Mt. Aura**. Central Junction is not a
House, and Mt. Aura is an ideal and route landmark rather than a gate.

All three gates support inward and outward constitutional transfer. Routine
crossings may be delegated, but each must remain traceable to Freemason Claim
authority, Hypergiant Title or boundary authority, and Proliteriate Yield
accountability. Stonebend does not own every use of these routes.

Encroachment may require correction, restoration, compensation, easement, boundary amendment, removal, demolition, or Title adjustment.

## Article X — Inheritance and succession

Inheritance requires a valid predecessor Title, valid successor, lawful trigger, proof of lineage, designation, or institutional rule, obligation review, sealed transfer, and Tombstone or closure of predecessor exercise. No one inherits authority the predecessor never lawfully held.

Lineage may be familial, institutional, architectural, artifact-based, office-based, transformed, synthetic, or custodial. It creates standing to claim, not automatic Title.

A successor entity proves continuity, records differences, assigns liabilities, reviews Titles, preserves predecessor records, and seals succession. It cannot retain benefits while discarding obligations.

No one inherits Diamond automatically. A former Hypergiant seeking to return
must complete the whole Claim, Yield hearing, descent, Proof of Persistence,
Lazerhorn, eligibility, and investiture path.

> Diamond may be lost. The Way back may not be shortened.

## Article XI — Mercury Mirrors

A **Mercury Mirror** tests correspondence between subject and record, present and past, structure and plan, officeholder and accession, artifact and provenance, boundary and survey, and extracted Hollow and source. It does not create truth.

High Mirror review is required for disputed Hypergiant succession, possible substitution, conflicting major surveys, identity-critical Illegal Hollowing, apparent action by Tombstoned office, total-continuity claims, and potentially altered constitutional archives. Multiple independent evidence sources are required.

A Mirror cannot grant consent or Title, establish legitimacy, prove clinical safety, recognize competence, justify unlawful surveillance, compel testimony outside law, or erase conflict.

## Article XII — Seals

A **Seal** binds a lawful decision to the correct subject, authority, scope, record, and sequence. Seal classes are identity, Title, boundary, accession, custody, Hollowing, Tombstone, emergency, amendment, and restoration. False issue, copied subject, changed scope, revoked or Tombstoned use, concealed alteration, and fictional Seal claims are forgery. A forged Seal never creates authority.

## Article XIII — Lawful Hollowing

Hollowing is controlled action upon the interior of an existing subject through opening, exposure, extraction, draining, emptying, separation, severing, inspection, preservation, or removal. Hollowing is not Synthesis.

Lawful purposes are medicine, repair, investigation, rescue, structural safety, evidence preservation, corruption removal, authorized extraction, constitutional transition, decommissioning, restoration, defense, and emergency stabilization.

Every lawful Hollowing requires:

1. named subject;
2. lawful authority;
3. valid purpose;
4. defined scope;
5. consent when applicable;
6. pre-procedure evidence;
7. qualified operator;
8. safety plan;
9. custody plan;
10. procedure record;
11. extraction record;
12. post-procedure evidence;
13. identity-continuity determination;
14. restoration or disposition plan;
15. Seal.

Consent is informed, specific, voluntary, capacity-bearing, revocable before irreversible action unless emergency law applies, and recorded. Inspection, extraction, total Hollowing, and Synthesis are separate consents.

Emergency Hollowing without ordinary consent is lawful only for immediate catastrophic threat, when delay creates greater harm, scope is necessary, authority records the basis, evidence is preserved, review begins immediately, and extracted Hollow enters protected custody. Emergency is never a pretext for research, punishment, dispossession, forced transformation, or permanent seizure.

Investigative Hollowing requires independent witness, precise scope, chain of custody, minimal destruction, complete inventory, and reconstruction where possible. Decommissioning preserves Name, lineage, prior Titles, and Tombstone.

## Article XIV — Illegal Hollowing

**Illegal Hollowing** is unauthorized, coercive, fraudulent, concealed, excessive, destructive, improperly recorded, or constitutionally invalid interior alteration. It includes missing authority or consent, exceeded scope, fake emergency, protected extraction, identity-critical removal, concealment, evidence destruction, Title evasion, forced renaming or dispossession, manufactured incapacity, preparation for Illegal Synthesis, destruction of Tombstoned history, missing custody, substitution, false provenance, refused restoration, and unrecorded irreversible damage.

First degree covers premeditated death, identity destruction, permanent dispossession, mass failure, Illegal Synthesis, or destruction of high evidence. Second degree covers intentional substantial harm. Third degree covers reckless procedure or custody. Procedural offenses cover recording, notice, Seal, preservation, or review failures and escalate with concealment or harm.

Remedies include cessation, rescue, restoration, reconstruction, return, protected custody, compensation, Title or boundary restoration, corrected records, acknowledgment, removal, lost privileges, confinement, dissolution, fraudulent-claim Tombstone, and referral to another House only for that House's constitutional act.

## Article XV — Tombstones

A Tombstone durably records that a Name, Title, office, structure, claim, or state formerly existed but no longer acts. It is not deletion. It records subject, former Name and Titles, ending reason and sequence, successor, surviving obligations, evidence, restrictions, issuing authority, and Seal.

A Tombstoned subject cannot exercise former authority, transfer ended Title, issue commands, create claims, appear active in replay, be silently deleted, or revive without lawful reconstitution.

## Article XVI — Civil disputes and appeals

Ordinary disputes begin before a Proliteriate hearing body, which may receive evidence, order survey or Mirror review, request Freemason inspection, suspend claims, recommend settlement, and issue initial judgment. High appeals involving constitutional identity, exclusive jurisdiction, high office, irreversible Hollowing, multi-House boundaries, or new evidence go to the Hypergiant with recorded Proliteriate and Freemason input.

Finality requires complete review, ended challenge period, no valid appeal, and Registry entry. Reopening requires decisive new evidence, fraud, counterfeit Seal, substitution, constitutional impossibility, or corrupted replay or migration.

## Article XVII — Emergency authority

Emergency authority is necessary, narrow, temporary, recorded, reviewable, and proportionate. It cannot permanently transfer Title without ordinary review. Emergency Seals expire unless ratified.

## Article XVIII — House boundaries

- Sandmanor proves designs, proposals, and claims; Stonebend does not replace proof.
- Glaüshouse clears medicine, Synthesis, transformation, and recovery; Stonebend governs Hollowing and identity continuity. Hollowing consent is not Synthesis consent.
- Flynt recognizes function, institution, and lawful exercise; Stonebend establishes Title. Flynt recognition is not a Stonebend Title, and Title is not competence.
- Combined events retain separate jurisdiction for each constitutional act.

## Article XIX — Constitutional invalid states

The following are categorically invalid:

- two active Hypergiants;
- a Title without a valid named subject;
- contradictory exclusive identities in one scope;
- an active office without valid accession;
- active Tombstoned authority;
- a Seal without issuing authority, subject, scope, and decision;
- a Mercury Mirror treated as authority rather than evidence;
- a Freemason granting sovereignty;
- a Hypergiant erasing challenge;
- a Hypergiant appointing a successor by command;
- a Freemason self-certifying a sovereign Claim;
- a Proliteriate executing armed seizure;
- a permanent fourth sovereign office manufactured from a temporary witness;
- one constitutional power counting twice toward removal;
- abolition of the Proliteriate network through removal of one representation;
- gate scope silently creating a duplicate core Title;
- rejection at one gate silently destroying unrelated Title scopes;
- inheritance from an unlawful holder;
- office created by transformation, recognition, or legacy progression;
- extracted Hollow without provenance and custody;
- emergency authority without expiration or review;
- concealed Hollowing;
- deletion used instead of Tombstone;
- succession without liabilities;
- renaming without former history;
- ownership created solely through custody;
- lawful identity destroyed merely because form changed.

The executable registry must enforce at most one active Hypergiant while
permitting an honest Diamond vacancy; one valid named subject for every Title;
accession for every active office; authority, subject, scope, and decision for
