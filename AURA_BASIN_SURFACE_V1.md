## Geographic and Regional Lock

Aura Basin is one singular left-side interior surface. Hunt grounds, den seams,
race spurs, ranges, shelters, recovery yards, and camps are constituents of
Aura Basin, not separate Basins.

Aura Basin **serves Flynt first** as Hollow Grove's collision, hunt, rescue,
competition, material-recovery, and return ground. It exposes what remains
functional when Frames, territories, routes, creatures, tools, and ambitions
meet pressure.

Flynt attribution does not make Aura Basin the property of Manticorp, Mystery
Men, the Gallows, We Fairy Men, a Founding Leader, or any crew. Urban and rural
Flynt remain complementary expressions of one constitutional authority.

## Boundary and Route Access

Aura Basin is the left-side triangle:

| Boundary point | Normalized per-mille coordinate |
|---|---:|
| Stonebend | `(500, 140)` |
| Flynt | `(265, 500)` |
| Glaüshouse | `(500, 860)` |

Its lawful gameplay approaches are:

- Aura Ridge and Current Sea along the Stonebend–Glaüshouse side;
- Boardwalk and Riptide along the Flynt–Glaüshouse side;
- Basin Motor Speedway and Stairway to Heaven along the Flynt–Stonebend side.

These approaches connect witness, certification, return, retrieval, production,
and ascension to the Basin. Route entry alone grants no hunting right, force
authority, salvage claim, Hollowing authority, care role, recognition, office,
or Title.

## Flynt Attribution

Flynt recognizes functional performance, completed rescue, declared
competition, resolved claims, protected return, and lawful public consequence.
Recognition makes an achievement or obligation legible; it does not prove a
weapon, clear an injury, name ownership, or create an office.

Manticorp may train or deploy only through its existing authority. Mystery Men
may investigate within lawful scope without turning all Basin activity into
classified evidence. The Gallows and We Fairy Men may protect, guide, trade,
perform, or intervene without becoming public sovereigns.

The rural associations of Gargoyle guardianship, Werewolf wilderness and pack
movement, and Merman water return may appear here without manufacturing the
unique Chimera or bypassing Tross.

## Glaüshouse Complementary Loop

Aura Basin and Glaüshouse are two sides of one living gameplay loop:

```text
prepare, equip, and receive care in Glaüshouse
→ enter Aura Basin
→ hunt, race, rescue, test, compete, or recover material
→ observe real pressure, failure, injury, and consequence
→ identify and stabilize
→ transfer persons, Frames, and hazardous material under custody
→ diagnose, treat, repair, rehabilitate, or refuse
→ preserve the actual outcome
→ return only within capacity and consent
```

Glaüshouse teaches how Frames survive. Aura Basin reveals what survives when
Frames collide. Care is not subordinate cleanup, and injury is not a required
price of recognition.

## Four-House Responsibilities

Flynt recognizes rescue, performance, competition, resolved claims, lawful
return, and completed function. Recognition does not make a crew sovereign or
convert victory into office.

Glaüshouse clears triage, injury, exposure, contamination, recovery, and
transport to care. Custody, survival, and treatment do not prove combat or
grant territorial standing.

Stonebend names territory, quarry, recovered bodies, material lots, claims,
continuities, and lawful Hollowing. Hollowing requires exact identity,
authority, provenance, extraction records, and preservation of the exterior.
It never establishes ownership of a Being.

Sandmanor proves weapons, methods, routes, transformations, repair, and repeated
performance. A successful field trial does not create clinical Clearance,
Flynt recognition, office, or Title.

## Hunt, Territory, and Creature Continuity

A lawful hunt records purpose, quarry identity, location, participants,
equipment, consent where applicable, protected categories, witnesses, result,
injury, recovery, and material disposition. A rare encounter may be observed,
avoided, aided, challenged, or protected rather than automatically harvested.

Den seams preserve retreat and nesting space. Territory markings disclose
pressure and passage; they do not settle a constitutional boundary without
Stonebend process. Rescue corridors and disengagement remain available during
conflict.

Defeat does not erase the identity or history of a creature. Recovered remains
and extracted Hollow retain provenance.

## Hollow and Material Cycle

```text
identified body, resource, or machine remain
→ custody and hazard segregation
→ Stonebend authorization where Hollowing is proposed
→ measured extraction preserving exterior identity
→ Hollow and exterior recorded as distinct Objects
→ Glaüshouse contamination Clearance where required
→ Sandmanor material or repair proof
→ Flynt recognition of lawful function or resolved claim
→ use, reserve, return, or disposal
```

The Hollow Recovery Yard does not itself authorize Hollowing. The Hide and
Material Works cannot launder unknown origin into a marketable good. Failed
materials and damaged Frames remain recorded.

## Racing, Trials, and Competition

The Race Spur is a field-test connection to Basin Motor Speedway; it is not a
replacement route. Times preserve conditions, load, vehicle or Frame identity,
course state, assistance, failure, and stoppage.

Weapons and transformation trials require declared scope, barriers, observers,
stop authority, failure recording, recovery plans, and consent. A
transformation trial cannot execute or infer Synthesis.

The Common Competition Ring records rules, entry, witnesses, challenge,
stoppage, actual outcome, and any later recognition request. Winning does not
grant Tross, Chimera, command, Gallows standing, or Title.

## Hazards, Refusal, and Rescue

Hazards include territorial conflict, weather and Aura pressure, creature
attack, weapon failure, unstable transformation, vehicle collision, fire,
contamination, Hollow exposure, structural collapse, overwork, coercion, and
lost communication.

Any participant may stop or refuse a trial outside consent or capacity.
Observers may invoke a cease-fire or rescue response within their lawful role.
Emergency custody remains narrow, recorded, temporary, and reviewable.

## Gameplay Contract

`aura-basin.collision-grounds` is a fixed `20 × 18` map. Every one of its
sixteen working places has a walkable approach and interaction witness.

The immutable surface view exposes:

- singular identity and primary Flynt attribution;
- the exact three-point boundary;
- six lawful route approaches;
- every facility's stable identity, kind, and function.

Wrong-route entry fails without revision or event. Accepted entry persists and
replays through gameplay archive schema V3 with an explicit Runtime Federation
binding. Godot renders ground, trails,
rocks, dens, thickets, salvage, and witnesses without owning combat, Hollowing,
care, proof, recognition, or Flynt authority.

Inspect all three surface contracts with:

```bash
cargo run --quiet --bin aura_surfaces_audit
```

## Completion Boundary

This implementation establishes the place, regional responsibilities, material
and care loops, facility witnesses, traversal, persistence, and presentation.

The living-surface pass now adds wildlife health, territorial pressure,
injured-Being and damaged-Frame counts, contamination, salvage backlog, rescue
readiness, scheduled workers, and the evidence-gated injured-Being case. The
runtime refuses to turn a living subject into salvage. Highway deep iron can
support Frame recovery, while an unresolved Riptide blowout raises Basin
contamination.

It does not yet claim combat AI, individual creature simulation, vehicle
physics, a complete hunting economy, procedural salvage generation, clinical
simulation, or a generic Hollowing reducer. See
`HOLLOW_GROVE_LIVING_SURFACES_AND_EXTRACTION_V1.md`.
