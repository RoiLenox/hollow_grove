## Choices and endings

Offers distinguish measuring-only use from a temporary rig. Each offer refers
to a revision and its known proof. Suggestions are advisory; Mercy's separate
decision records the actual offer and exact proof used for acceptance.
An offer without support proof cannot authorize deployment.

Refusing a rig can preserve a previously accepted measuring-only scope.
Refusal cannot be reversed for the same offer. A changed revision allows a
new offer and a new decision; repair never cancels the former refusal or its
history. The resident can also ask work to wait before recovery or proof.

1. **Measurements delivered.** Current measuring proof and measuring-only
   acceptance deliver a useful service. Support remains undelivered.
2. **Temporary rig delivered.** Exact repaired-revision support proof and
   Mercy's matching acceptance permit the disclosed temporary use, release
   and return. Custody/recovery stay with Tess.
3. **Protected delay delivered.** Mercy's refusal or pause receives a safe
   disposition. The access need, evidence, custody and unfinished duties
   remain addressable.

Each delivery records Tess's execution/custody/recovery duty, Ivo's recording,
Mercy as recipient, safe access and remaining work. A delivered disposition
completes this bounded episode, not every doorway repair or every lineage
stage. No outcome creates debt or a Boardwalk purchase.

After rig delivery, Mercy may withdraw. Future deployment stops immediately;
Tess must recover the stopped rig and deliver a protected return. Completion
is withheld while recovery or that return remains outstanding. The original
delivery, consent, proof, object identity and damaged-part history remain
recorded. Withdrawal grants no future permission automatically.

## Kernel and presentation contract

`src/world/survey_frame.rs` owns typed state, actions and exact outcomes above
the neutral kernels. The gameplay aggregate stages this state transactionally
and appends one authoritative parent event only after all fallible work
succeeds. Replay recomputes the same action against recorded world authority
and rejects any differing actor, revision, proof, choice, result or delivery.
The child state contains bounded revision/proof/offer/decision/delivery
records; repeated advisory changes remain in the full parent journal.

Gameplay archive V6 migrates V1–V5 with empty object-case state and preserves
prior identity, Forms, clinic history and relationships. The migration is in
`docs/SURVEY_FRAME_MIGRATION_V1.md`. The historical twelve-route lore roster
remains exact; this is a separate adopted contract with its own verified binding.
Lore inspection V4 and architecture inspection V2 expose this additional case.

Protocol V1 adds `AdvanceSurveyFrameIntent` and a read-only `survey_frame` view.
Godot presents the backend's available actions in a workshop panel (**O**;
arrows choose, Enter requests, O/Escape closes). It displays current measuring
and support proof separately and never supplies a trial result, authority,
custody record or resident consent. Existing art palette, map authority,
identity and Twofold Witness remain intact. A dedicated workshop map and
character assets are later presentation work.

The installed launcher accepts an explicit `HOLLOW_GROVE_WORLD_ROOT` so an
existing, chosen live institutional catalog can be used. It does not populate
vacant offices. Ordinary defaults retain their fail-closed authority behavior.
