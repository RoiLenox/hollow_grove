## Four-Pole Economy

Hollow Grove's four Houses are frozen as productive poles:

| House | Economic function | Four-Pole position |
|---|---|---|
| Sandmanor | Design | Creation of Form |
| Flynt | Engineering | Creation of Function |
| Stonebend | Craft | Continuance of Form |
| Glaüshouse | Repair | Continuance of Function |

The poles classify principal contribution rather than establish absolute
monopolies. Cross-House enterprises and projects retain one evidence-supported
primary pole and may declare weighted secondary sector exposure.

The productive cycle is:

```text
Sandmanor designs Form
→ Flynt engineers Function
→ Stonebend makes Form endure
→ Glaüshouse keeps Function alive
→ the restored object, body, structure, or system returns to use
```

## Currency, Earned Evidence, and Work Disposition

Hollow Grove uses one unnamed standard ordinary currency across all Houses.
The neutral executable representation is `StandardCurrencyAmount`; no public
lore name has been ratified.

The following remain distinct:

- ordinary currency measures exchange;
- Toke/Tokens record earned work, experience, recognition, eligibility,
  access, authority, responsibility, or objective result;
- shares record enterprise participation or ownership;
- event contracts record positions on predefined outcomes;
- the four public indexes measure sector value and are not currencies;
- Gremlincoin remains nonmonetary Gremlin Way and progression evidence.

The surface work matrix is frozen as:

| | Urgent | Not urgent |
|---|---|---|
| Important | Act | Cultivate |
| Not important | Reroute | Release |

`Act` moves important urgent work now. `Cultivate` develops important
nonurgent work. `Reroute` sends urgent work to the person, office, House,
institution, or pole able to receive it. `Release` ends work that no longer
justifies Current, Aura, labor, attention, or institutional resources.

## Central Junction District

`CENTRAL JUNCTION`, ordinarily shortened to `The Junction`, is frozen as the
principal civic, financial, business, and social district where Hollow Grove
meets through capital, evidence, work, and public life.

Central Junction is a district, not a single building and not merely an
exchange. The South Ridge Exchange is the primary market institution located
inside the district.

The practical public authority boundaries are:

- **South Ridge Exchange:** enterprise listings, share trading, project
  financing, lawful event trading, official index calculation, and transaction
  processing;
- **Junction Board:** listing standards, index membership and methodology,
  disclosure requirements, professional-evidence audits, conflicts of
  interest, investigations, disputed classifications and House assessments,
  and public market rules;
- **Clearing House:** completed-trade settlement, share transfers,
  project-contract and event-contract settlement, payouts, voids, authorized
  reversals, and recognized final balances;
- **Junction Wire:** public prices, indexes, listings, disclosures, project
  updates, employment demand, warnings, recognized failures, settled outcomes,
  and notices.

The Junction Board does not execute trades or payouts. The Clearing House does
not invent outcomes. The Junction Wire publishes recognized information but
does not create the state it reports.

The approaches to Central Junction are frozen as:

- the Craft Corridor toward Stonebend;

- the Repair Corridor toward Glaüshouse;

Each House has one canonical public Sector Hall:

- Stonebend Craft Hall;
- Sandmanor Design Hall;

- Glaüshouse Repair Hall.

Each Sector Hall verifies professional facts within its domain and contains one
public Index Board connected to the Junction Wire. A Sector Hall does not set
market prices.

## Four Public Indexes

The official indexes are:

- Sandmanor Design Index;
- Flynt Engineering Index;
- Stonebend Craft Index;
- Glaüshouse Repair Index.

The Houses do not own the indexes. They fill them through productive evidence
and may affect or dominate them only through lawful productive success.

Index authority remains divided:

```text
Junction Board governs methodology and membership
→ House Sector Halls submit verified productive evidence
→ South Ridge Exchange calculates the official value
→ Junction Wire publishes the value and supporting public evidence
```

Every public Sector Hall Index Board displays the official sector value, price
movement, verified productive evidence, employment demand, major active
projects, warnings, and public disclosures.

Each listed enterprise and project has:

## Blackroot Workshop Deterministic Event Proof

The Blackroot Workshop Conflict is the frozen deterministic event-contract
witness.

Before trading opens, the contract records three possible outcomes: Faction A,
Faction B, and Draw. A faction wins only when all four predefined conditions
are satisfied by the closing date:

1. it controls the workshop;
2. production remains operational;
3. Stonebend recognizes the claim;
4. no active lawful challenge remains.

The fixture records eight domain-correct attestations across both factions.
Stonebend Craft Hall attests control, claim recognition, and lawful-challenge
state. Flynt Engineering Hall attests operational production.

The verified fixture resolves as:

```text
Blackroot event occurs
→ House Sector Halls record and attest evidence
→ Junction Board audits and recognizes Faction A
→ the decision may be described as reaching Equal Gaze
→ Clearing House settles the positions
→ Junction Wire publishes Faction A as the recognized outcome
```

Quoted market prices do not determine the constitutional outcome. Incomplete,
duplicated, mismatched, or wrong-domain evidence cannot produce recognition.

## Conflict-of-Interest Enforcement

The frozen model rejects direct prohibited interests:

- a Junction Board reviewer cannot secretly audit a contract position they
  hold;
- a Clearing House official cannot secretly settle a contract position they
  hold;
- a commander cannot secretly wager on a conflict they direct;
- disclosure and recusal remove a conflicted official from acting rather than
  legalizing continued participation.

Stable actor and position identities make these checks deterministic and
auditable before recognition or settlement.

## Summit Concepts Preserved

- **Current Haze is unresolved possibility.** A market matter may

  market office exists.
- **Equal Gaze is reconciled perspective.** A recognized ruling may be
  described as reaching Equal Gaze, but Equal Gaze is the condition achieved,

  retains its Summit, recognition, and future-vision role. It is not a
  financial ticker or publication institution; the Junction Wire publishes
  market information.

Their prior nonmarket meanings remain intact.
