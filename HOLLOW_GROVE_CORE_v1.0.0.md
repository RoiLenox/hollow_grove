## Core Identity

Hollow Grove is a constitutional operating language for:

- naming what a thing claims to be
- proving whether that claim survives inspection
- clearing whether the thing is safe, restored, integrated, and usable
- recognizing whether it has entered lived reality and demonstrated value

Hollow Grove must not be reduced to:

- game lore only
- a desktop skin
- a fantasy file browser
- a Godot-only world shell
- Hueman's native world

Hollow Grove can be expressed through:

- the Hollow Grove operating system
- Hueman
- creative work
- project organization
- civic design
- manufacturing systems
- personal and collaborative workflows

## Layer Relationship

The correct relationship is pool and ocean.

### Hollow Grove Is The Ocean

Hollow Grove covers the larger lived environment:

- the real Arch Linux system
- niri and river
- files, windows, applications, and services
- scripts, devices, and workflows
- creative output and real responsibilities

### Hueman Is The Pool

Hueman is a contained standalone civilization sandbox with:

- native characters and objects
- native materials and forms
- professions, politics, creatures, and routes
- its own economy, quests, and social play

### Independence Rule

- Hollow Grove must function without Hueman.
- Hueman must function without Hollow Grove.
- They may share constitutional language without collapsing into one product.
- Hollow Grove itself is not to be built inside Godot.
- Godot 4 and Aseprite are appropriate for Hueman.
- Arch Linux, niri, river, scripts, services, and native tools remain the Hollow Grove OS foundation.

### Stonebend

- action: Name it
- logic: positive
- expression: Natural
- OS domain: Craft

Stonebend establishes lawful Name, Title, structure, boundary, continuity, record, lineage, custody, inheritance, and lawful Hollowing. Naming does not manufacture proof, clearance, competence, recognition, or virtue.

Its constitutional people are the Geralds. Its three principal authorities are:

- Hypergiant — the singular highest office and custodian of constitutional integrity;
- Proliteriate — the permanent distributed public network that witnesses Yield through temporary bounded mandates;
- Freemason — the structural execution institution led by the singular High Freemason.

These authorities separate Claim, Title, and Yield; they are not three equal
sovereigns. Transformation never grants office, recognition never substitutes
for Title, and custody never becomes ownership by itself.

Stonebend's signature constitutional offense is Illegal Hollowing. Lawful Hollowing requires a named subject, authority, purpose, defined scope, consent when applicable, evidence, qualified operation, custody, continuity determination, restoration or disposition, and Seal.

The first Stonebend foundation is canonically defined in
`STONEBEND_AURA_WAY_AETHER_HOLLOWING_FOUNDATION_V1.md`. It adds the vertical
law without changing those authorities. Mt. Aura is Aether and Riptide is
Bathos. Aether and Current are opposite burden states of one underlying
medium: Aether is weightless Current; Current is the heaviest Aether. Aura is
Aether revealed through Form, while Current is Bathos embodied through weight.
Neither Aether nor Bathos is a moral absolute, and neither landmark is
Stonebend property.

Aura Way is the standard, legible route through prerequisites, education,
supervised practice, examination, demonstrated responsibility, and recognition
eligibility. Houses teach the work, Aura Way organizes the path, and Stonebend
names completion without declaring metaphysical perfection.

The second Stonebend pass is canonically defined in
`STONEBEND_THREE_GATES_OFFICES_AND_TITLE_SCOPE_V1.md`. Stonebend has three
bidirectional constitutional gates: Flynt-facing through Stairway to Heaven and
Basin Motor Speedway; Central Junction-facing through the Craft Corridor; and
Sandmanor-facing through Aura Way and Mt. Aura. Central Junction remains a
district, not a House, and Mt. Aura remains the ideal rather than a gate.

One stable core Title may carry independent Formation Recognition, Public
Circulation, and Operational Deployment scopes. Diamond is Stonebend's
continuing sovereign Title; the Hypergiant is its temporary bearer. The
Freemason forges the Claim, the Hypergiant bears the Title, and the
Proliteriate shields the Yield.

The Proliteriate is a permanent distributed network whose active voice is a
temporary, recallable, issue-specific witness. One power may challenge; the
other two distinct powers must concur to remove. Removal preserves a
Tombstone. Diamond may be vacant, never passes by automatic inheritance, and
cannot be invested without the complete Lazerhorn succession path.

The third Stonebend pass is canonically defined in
`STONEBEND_TITLE_LIFECYCLE_AND_CONSTITUTIONAL_CONTINUITY_V1.md`. A Claim is not
automatically a Title. Stonebend recognition establishes a bounded Title in the
public record, while activation separately permits its exercise under
policy-defined conditions. Maintenance, renewal, limitation, supervision,
suspension, remediation, restoration, and terminal disposition preserve one
stable Title identity and durable interruption history.

Diamond remains unborne during vacancy. Narrow recorded continuity mandates
may preserve existing duties or immediate boundaries but cannot become
Diamond, appoint a Hypergiant, or grow permanent through use. The Forge may be
vacant without losing its prior records; replacement requires independent
review and produces only one active High Freemason. The Proliteriate network
has no total office vacancy and gains no permanent speaker or numerical
threshold.

Lawful material Hollowing raises Current by authorized removal of removable
burden while preserving essential fractions, stable batch identity, common
lineage, provenance, evidence, proof, custody, and Seal. Geographic stone then
refracts universal Aether into particular Aura. Opal varies, Diamond
concentrates, and Quartz resonates; these examples are nonexclusive and create
no final House-to-stone mapping.

### Sandmanor

- action: Prove it
- logic: negative
- expression: Matural
- OS domain: Configuration

Sandmanor measures, compares, models, configures, tests, and improves.
Its proof must itself survive inspection.
Failure should expose which assumption broke and what must be redesigned.

Its two equal civic traditions are:

- Minorians — interior Gnomes whose Aura Farm contains Aura Fields and Content
  Farm and whose guardian line is Gnome → Minotaur → Hecaton;
- Minoans — exterior Elves whose coast runs Free Aura Beach → Southern Coast →
  Current Break → Minoan County Courthouse → Glaüshouse and whose guardian
  line is Elf → Centaur → Pegasus.

Guardian Forms require service proof, Recipe authorization, Glaüshouse
compatibility, maintained Synthesis, investiture, and renewal. Form and mantle
remain distinct. Current Break may host Manticorp training, but Flynt retains
Manticorp command; the Minoan County Courthouse remains Sandmanor law even
when it transfers someone into Glaüshouse care.

The Sandman is the singular highest constitutional office and maintained
sovereign Form-and-mantle tradition. The Contest of Improvement pairs one
lawful Hecaton and one lawful Pegasus. They teach and learn from each other,
preserve baselines, complete five reciprocal trial domains, and receive an
auditable crowd verdict based on improvement rather than starting rank.
Challenge review, Recipe authorization, Glaüshouse compatibility, lawful
convergence, Stonebend Title where required, Flynt recognition, a public
learning statement, and Seal precede investiture. A tie leaves the office
unresolved; the losing guardian keeps their Form and mantle unless separately
removed. Combat, heredity, popularity, transformation alone, recognition
alone, and legacy progression never create office. Aegon is the historical
name for the same sovereign now called the Sandman.

Design proposes; method orders; demonstration reveals; evidence supports;
criticism tests; reproduction confirms; failure teaches; revision improves;
reciprocity transforms. Proof is not Title, Glaüshouse clearance, Flynt
recognition, consent, ownership, or universal truth. Failed demonstrations and
prior versions remain constitutional evidence. Sandmanor's signature offense
is Fraudulent Design.

### Glaüshouse

- action: Clear it
- logic: double negative
- expression: Synbiote
- OS domain: Repair

Glaüshouse clears so that change may occur without becoming violation. It
governs medicine, diagnosis, consent, capacity, clearance, care, recovery,
Aura and Current stabilization, and Synthesis for Hueman and machine bodies.

Its principal constitutional authorities are:

- Prima Donna — the singular highest clinical office;
- Persephones — the multiple balanced whole-course rank;
- Matrons and Marshals — equal Aura-forward and Current-forward branches;
- The Nightingales — the universal clinical foundation, constitutional
  clinical-care institution, and bedside check.

The frozen current presentation is Doctor Ratchet (Prima Donna) and Nurse House
(one Persephone), while multiple Persephones may serve. Glauspitals operates
clinical facilities; Chromacord preserves and projects clinical evidence
without becoming authority.

Diagnosis identifies condition. Consent authorizes participation. Clearance
permits a scoped procedure. Care preserves the subject. Synthesis transforms.
Recovery completes the act. Hollowing consent never implies Synthesis consent;
proof and recognition never substitute for clearance; custody never becomes
ownership; transformation never creates Title or office.

Synthesis establishes a real living Form whose Continuance depends on
maintenance, renewal, Recipe practice, environmental compatibility,
institutional care, and the Form's Ways. A maintained Form may continue for
life, but no procedure guarantees it forever. Major Synthesis requires
technical and lived viability. Living Ledger authority belongs to Persephones
and the Nightingale order; Recipe Ledger authority belongs to Prima Donna.
Glaüshouse advancement preserves one stable clinician identity and is
generative, while Stonebend succession is corrective.

Glaüshouse alone performs lawful clinical and final constitutional Synthesis,
while the frozen common runtime remains authoritative over Bond Synthesis
Resolution. Its signature constitutional offense is Illegal Synthesis.

Gargoyle, Merman, and Werewolf are Flynt's three founding regional peoples. Their unique lower-apex constitutional synthesis is Chimera. Manticorp is the sovereign transfiguration beyond Chimera: Wolf becomes Lion, Bat becomes Eagle, and Snake/Fish becomes Hydra. The complete Manticorp Recipe is divided among the three enduring Basin leaders and their traditions: Bro White, Cinderellaman, and The Beauty. They remain distinct custodians, not one fused being.

## Current And Aura

### Current

Current carries, moves, and expresses pressure.
It is capability, embodiment, motion, bearing, and the ability to act.

Current asks:

> What can I do?

Current exists in two active states:

- Regular Current
- Hollow Current

At the material-foundation level, Current and Aether share one provenance
lineage. Current is the heavy, embodied state whose weight supports flow,
storage, memory, repetition, and consequence. Lawful Hollowing may refine a
recorded Current batch toward Aether without treating Current as evil, waste,
or an unrelated inventory resource.

### Aura

Aura is understanding, judgment, perception, context, and meaningful sight.

Aura asks:

> What do I understand, and how should I use it?

Aura exists in two active states:

- Reflective Aura
- Holographic Aura

At the material-foundation level, Aura is Aether revealed through Form. Aether
is not automatically Aura. Geography forms a stone's composition and lattice;
the preserved lattice refracts one universal Aether into a particular visible,
resonant, thermal, biological, patterned, or signal-bearing Aura.

Stonebend and Sandmanor lean Reflective Aura.
Glaüshouse and Flynt lean Holographic Aura.
