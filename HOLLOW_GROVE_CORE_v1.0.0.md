# Hollow Grove Core v1.0.0

Date: 2026-07-09

## Status

This document is the active constitutional reference for Hollow Grove.

Hollow Grove is the primary system.
Hueman is a standalone playable expression of Hollow Grove, not the foundation beneath it.
Current Synthesis remains an intermediate artifact and route layer in this repository, but it does not replace Hollow Grove's constitutional role.

## Core Identity

Hollow Grove is a constitutional operating language for:

- naming what a thing claims to be
- proving whether that claim survives inspection
- clearing whether the thing is safe, restored, integrated, and usable
- recognizing whether it has entered lived reality and demonstrated value

Hollow Grove must not be reduced to:

- game lore only
- a desktop skin
- a fantasy file browser
- a Godot-only world shell
- Hueman's native world

Hollow Grove can be expressed through:

- the Hollow Grove operating system
- Hueman
- creative work
- project organization
- civic design
- manufacturing systems
- personal and collaborative workflows

## Layer Relationship

The correct relationship is pool and ocean.

### Hollow Grove Is The Ocean

Hollow Grove covers the larger lived environment:

- the real Arch Linux system
- niri and river
- files, windows, applications, and services
- scripts, devices, and workflows
- creative output and real responsibilities

### Hueman Is The Pool

Hueman is a contained standalone civilization sandbox with:

- native characters and objects
- native materials and forms
- professions, politics, creatures, and routes
- its own economy, quests, and social play

### Independence Rule

- Hollow Grove must function without Hueman.
- Hueman must function without Hollow Grove.
- They may share constitutional language without collapsing into one product.
- Hollow Grove itself is not to be built inside Godot.
- Godot 4 and Aseprite are appropriate for Hueman.
- Arch Linux, niri, river, scripts, services, and native tools remain the Hollow Grove OS foundation.

## The Four Kingdoms

The kingdoms are immutable constitutional positions:

- Stonebend
- Sandmanor
- Glaüshouse
- Flynt

Their universal loop is:

> Name it.
> Prove it.
> Clear it.
> Recognize it.

### Stonebend

- action: Name it
- logic: positive
- expression: Natural
- OS domain: Craft

Stonebend establishes title, identity, purpose, and responsibility.
Stonebend's pressure function is reallocation.
Stonebend does not simply destroy pressure or merely bear it.
Stonebend determines where burden, load, or authority should go.

Its political balance is:

- Hypergiant
- Proletariat
- Freemason

That balance is Title, Labor, and Craft.
The Hypergiant may be challenged by the Proletariat and the Freemason when the Title is no longer legitimate.

Stonebend's signature industrial process is Hollowing.
Hollowing reallocates pressure out of a carried Current, leaving Hollow Current as a lightened but still meaningful state.

### Sandmanor

- action: Prove it
- logic: negative
- expression: Matural
- OS domain: Configuration

Sandmanor measures, compares, models, configures, tests, and improves.
Its proof must itself survive inspection.
Failure should expose which assumption broke and what must be redesigned.

Its stance is:

- Minorians
- Minoans
- Sandmen
- The Sandman

The Sandmen witness a contest between Minorians and Minoans and judge who has improved the most.
The winner becomes The Sandman.

### Glaüshouse

- action: Clear it
- logic: double negative
- expression: Synbiote
- OS domain: Repair

Glaüshouse restores, clears, integrates, and returns a thing to functioning wholeness.
It is the mechanic-hospital civilization for both Hueman and machine.

Its stance is:

- Prima Donna
- Persephone
- Nightingales

The Prima Donna grants Clearance.
Persephone and the Nightingales may challenge premature or false Clearance.

Glaüshouse alone performs Synthesis.
Synthesis is the constitutional act of integrating separate developed systems into one stable functioning whole.

### Flynt

- action: Recognize it
- logic: contrapositive / Contra
- expression: Synthetic
- OS domain: Engineering

Flynt recognizes what has entered lived use and proven field value.
Recognition is not the same as title.
Flynt can recognize hidden, denied, premature, or overlooked value through Contra.

Its stance is:

- Tross
- Delinquent
- Juvenile
- the White Dwarfs

The White Dwarfs are the Tross's personal guard.
Flynt is not merely an isolated fourth specialization.
It is the connective black-iris substrate of the broader Hueman civilization.

## Current And Aura

### Current

Current carries, moves, and expresses pressure.
It is capability, embodiment, motion, bearing, and the ability to act.

Current asks:

> What can I do?

Current exists in two active states:

- Regular Current
- Hollow Current

### Aura

Aura is understanding, judgment, perception, context, and meaningful sight.

Aura asks:

> What do I understand, and how should I use it?

Aura exists in two active states:

- Reflective Aura
- Holographic Aura

Stonebend and Sandmanor lean Reflective Aura.
Glaüshouse and Flynt lean Holographic Aura.

## Implementation Law

Major features should still be tested against the constitutional loop:

### Constitutional Fit

- What is being named?
- What is being proven?
- What is being cleared?
- What is being recognized?

### Kingdom Fit

- Is Stonebend handling naming, foundation, and pressure reallocation?
- Is Sandmanor handling proof, count, configuration, and improvement?
- Is Glaüshouse retaining Synthesis and true Clearance?
- Is Flynt retaining recognition, deployment, and field engineering?

### Player Or Workflow Fit

- Does it strengthen ordinary Hueman or ordinary operator capability?
- Does it preserve the Hueman identity?
- Does it create meaningful contribution rather than decorative complexity?
