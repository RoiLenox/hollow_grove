# Backend House Handoff Contracts V1

Status: source-backed documentation for the ten historical route contracts.
Their headless backend bindings are implemented in `src/world/lore_handoffs.rs`;
[Backend House Handoffs V1](docs/BACKEND_HOUSE_HANDOFFS_V1.md) defines their exact
results, authority, recovery, archive and witness coverage. Related House modules
remain separate owners, rather than proof of unlimited regional execution.
Scope: authority, evidence, receipt and continuity requirements, without game production.

Owners: `src/world/lived_lore.rs`, the four House constitutions, the Compromise
and `src/architecture_audit.rs`. No new actors, cases, offices or runtime IDs
are created. Labels for ordinary roles below still need actual bindings before
any actual world-case consequence can be claimed. Architecture V3 reports
four earlier gameplay bindings plus ten headless handoff bindings, with zero
unbound canonical backend routes. Ten game presentation integrations are deferred.

## Common backend handoff

Every applicable handoff needs the continuing subject/object, request scope,
current version, authenticated decision owner and function, evidence source,
executor, witness/recorder, actual recipient and recovery responsibility.
A request, an accepted sovereign decision, execution, independent observation,
personal consent and delivery are separate records. A negative result or a
protected refusal can be a lawful disposition; incomplete duties stay explicit.

Stonebend owns Name, Sandmanor Prove, Glaüshouse Clear and Flynt Recognize.
Delegation records a bounded mandate; it never creates a vacant principal
holder. A witness or executor cannot substitute for the decision authority.
Ordinary roles do not become offices because they appear in this index.

## Per-contract closure requirements

For each entry, locate applicable existing domain records before proposing a
new representation. Complete actual ordinary actor bindings, exact evidence and
receipt shape, decision scope, version/condition invalidation, independent
recipient/refusal records and recovery obligations. Record separately what
already exists and what still needs implementation or proof.

A private headless witness must cover lawful success, protected refusal,
unavailable/mismatched authority, changed evidence/scope, failure retained as
history, retry/conflict, archive migration, replay and altered-history rejection.
Passing source-link checks alone cannot change `binding_status` to verified.
A new authored handoff diagram cannot close a route gap. The implemented headless
service and private witness suite now establish the ten bounded backend bindings;
each actual case still needs its own bound actors, evidence and replayed receipts.

The high-risk living cases need meaningful capacity/consent and separate proof
and clinical clearance; uncertainty or silence grants no elective consent.
The Hollowing case preserves injured subject identity separately from extracted
sample custody. The public Name case retains former names and liabilities.
Flynt's urban/rural expressions retain one Tross and attributed custody.
Sandmanor's design cases preserve negative results and tested conditions.

## Evidence classification and scale

A declared contract, a related source module, an implemented domain rule and an
executed/replayed case have different evidence. Keep their status and provenance
separate. Routine reads may use indexes and bounded summaries, but the complete
archive retains original authority, criticism, refusal and recovery records.
Contract closure refers to the exact scope, not to every problem in the region.
