The Hollow Grove constitutional runtime binds the generic world-scale Point:

```text
positive world region = Light Aura
```

North or top is physically positive in this world binding. South or bottom is
physically negative. This orientation is not a camera convention. Scaling

Polarity is not morality. Positive does not automatically mean lawful, good,
or true. Negative does not automatically mean unlawful, evil, or corrupt.
Central Junction is the expanded zero region where polarity changes sign,
crossing and exchange occur, and constitutional events may be witnessed.

## Four-phase Hollow Grove cycle

The primary public, kernel-facing phase grammar is:

> **The Way Back → The Initiation → The Gathering → The Festival → The Way
> Back**

The exact serialized variants are:

1. `TheWayBack`
2. `TheInitiation`
3. `TheGathering`
4. `TheFestival`

The phase loop is scale-invariant across a constitutional kernel boundary,
Point or Point² consequence, player interaction, object, Bond, Synthesis,
route passage, House process, seasonal handoff, canonical year, and
physical–digital synchronization cycle. Each record carries its stable subject
and open `ScaleKey`; the universal recursion kernel itself remains free of the
Hollow Grove names.

> **The Way Back carries the prior state forward.**
> **The Initiation gives it form.**
> **The Gathering places that form into relationship.**
> **The Festival determines what became true.**
> **What became true enters The Way Back again.**

The Way Back receives history, evidence, consequences, injury, unresolved
conditions, Bonds, Titles, object identity, and the prior confirmed state. It
asks: **What has come back?**

The Initiation establishes an actionable intent, name, Title, role, boundary,
proposal, permission, physical embodiment, or candidate transformation. It
asks: **What form will it take?**

The Gathering places the initiated form into relationship with people,
objects, institutions, environments, Current, Aura, constitutional limits,
physical resistance, digital systems, and other active states. It asks:
**What happens when it meets the world?**

The Festival validates or rejects, collects evidence, compares, recognizes,
records, audits, confirms, checkpoints, and produces the next canonical state.
It asks: **What actually became true?**

The cycle has no absolute terminal state. A completed Festival supplies the
state consumed by the next Way Back:

> **Return is re-entry with consequence, not restoration to an untouched
> starting state.**

Rejection still completes the cycle. The Festival may retain the failed
attempt and its evidence while leaving the confirmed canonical state
unchanged. That unchanged confirmed state becomes the next Way Back input.
Rendering, animation, input, or an attempted manifestation cannot advance a
phase or alter canonical state.

Detailed lifecycle terms remain subordinate internal operations:

| Four-phase cycle | Internal substeps that may occur |
|---|---|
| The Way Back | load, enter, inherit, recover, reconcile |
| The Initiation | encounter, intend, aim, propose, fire |
| The Gathering | interact, resist, manifest, miss, kiss |
| The Festival | witness, validate, record, transform, Point² |

Not every cycle requires every internal substep. The older detailed sequence
is not a parallel top-level grammar.

At seasonal scale, the short phase `TheFestival` maps to the complete
ceremonial Great Function **The Festival of Mirrors**. The short phase does not
rename the event. `TheGathering` maps to the seasonal **The Gathering**, whose
alias Derrick remains an event alias rather than a separate phase.

## Canonical year and Function Junction

The canonical year begins at the authoritative Winter Solstice instant and
continues until the next Winter Solstice. Each archive stores the supplied
astronomical observation rather than inferring a fixed Gregorian date.

**Function Junction** is the formal constitutional seasonal transition.

It is not:

- a fifth Great Function;
- a geographic place;
- another name for Central Junction;
- the whole incoming House Season.

The fixed relation is:

> **Central Junction is the physical place and world center.**
> **Function Junction is the constitutional transition.**
> **The Great Function performs the transition.**
> **The incoming House Season is the resulting runtime phase.**

Each completed Function Junction carries a caller-supplied identity, year,
astronomical anchor, outgoing and incoming House Seasons, Great Function,
physical checkpoint, digital checkpoint, synchronization phase, authority,
evidence, and provenance. It must close the outgoing Season before opening the
incoming Season. It transfers no sovereignty.

## Physical–digital synchronization

The physical and digital worlds are not independent timelines. The canonical
year is their shared synchronization clock.

### The Way Back — physical to digital

The Way Back gathers lived experience, injury, memory, evidence, unfinished
states, challenged Bonds, material consequences, and unresolved discrepancies
into constitutional reconciliation.

### The Initiation — digital to physical

The Initiation gives approved names, identities, Titles, boundaries, designs,
permissions, apprenticeships, offices, structures, and Synthesis patterns
lawful physical expression. Stonebend decides whether a state may later become
permanent; incarnation alone is not a Permanence Seal.

### The Gathering / Derrick — bidirectional participation

The Gathering places physical and digital actors, objects, services, records,
and systems in one shared active state. They are used, exchanged, celebrated,
tested, competed through, and experienced communally.

### The Festival of Mirrors — comparison and confirmation

The Festival compares physical result against digital record. It performs
audit, recognition, confirmation, discrepancy exposure, commissioning,
implementation review, and the final checkpoint before the next Way Back.

The complete cycle is:

```text
Physical experience
    ↓
The Way Back
    ↓
Digital reconciliation
    ↓
The Initiation
    ↓
Physical incarnation
    ↓
The Gathering
    ↓
Shared physical–digital participation
    ↓
The Festival of Mirrors
    ↓
Comparison and confirmed checkpoint
    ↓
next Way Back
```

Physical and digital checkpoints are separate stable records. Neither may be
inferred from UI state, animation, map position, insertion order, or current
catalog contents.

## Permanence

The hard constitutional rule is:

> **Stonebend always enforces the Laws of Permanence.**

The generic capacity for stable, versioned consequence does not itself grant
constitutional authority. Nothing becomes permanent without Stonebend's final
seal.

Permanence means:

> **The world cannot pretend the thing never happened.**

Permanence is not immutability. A permanent subject may undergo lawful
amendment, repair, succession, inheritance, release, dissolution, reformation,
or transformation. Every change must be explicit, witnessed, versioned,
provenance-preserving, and constitutionally authorized. Dissolution creates a
tombstone; it never silently deletes history.

## Four Laws of Permanence

Each House owns one necessary proof. Stonebend alone owns the final
constitutional condition.

| House | Law | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Stonebend | **Law of Identity** | It is the same lawful thing across time. |
| Sandmanor | **Law of Pattern** | Its arrangement and relationships are explicit. |
| Glaüshouse | **Law of Integrity** | It can endure without hidden corruption or collapse. |
| Flynt | **Law of Recognition** | It actually operates and is recognized in the world. |

Stonebend asks:

> **What exactly is being made permanent?**

Sandmanor asks:

> **What arrangement is being preserved?**

Glaüshouse asks:

> **Can this endure without becoming harmful, dead, or fraudulent?**

Flynt asks:

> **Does the world actually recognize and operate this thing?**

All four accepted proofs must refer to the same stable subject. A missing,
rejected, duplicated, or conflicting proof blocks the seal without erasing the
petition or the underlying event.

## Illegal Hollowing

Illegal Hollowing remains Stonebend's signature constitutional crime.

Unauthorized alteration of identity, boundary, Title, canonical record,
permanent structure, lawful interior, provenance, or permanence state is
Illegal Hollowing.

Lawful amendment is not Illegal Hollowing. A lawful change retains the prior
version and adds a new version through explicit Stonebend authority and
evidence. Lawful dissolution creates a tombstone. Permanence protects memory,
not stasis.
