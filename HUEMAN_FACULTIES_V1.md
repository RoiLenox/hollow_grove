# Hueman Faculties V1

Status: canonical additive Hueman faculty law

Authority boundary: semantic world and Recipe domain above the frozen universal
recursion kernel and Constitutional Runtime V2

## Orthogonal faculty axis

A faculty is not Current, Aura, Synthesis, office, or rank. Practitioner names
the whole person; Current and Aura are the two Level 1 inputs; faculty remains
a distinct House-specific direction of recognition.

| House or tradition | Domain | Faculty | Semantic direction | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stonebend | Body | Presynce | recognition of present, material, embodied reality in relation to Identity | LOCKED name; WORKING expanded description |
| Flynt | Spirit | Resynce | recognition of Capacity through response, change, pressure, or load | LOCKED name; WORKING expanded description |
| Glaüshouse | Mind | Precog | recognition of trajectory or condition in relation to Integrity | LOCKED name; WORKING expanded description |
| Sandmanor / Minorian | Soul Interior | Prefog | recognition of Pattern that already exists but is obscured | LOCKED name; WORKING expanded description |
| Sandmanor / Minoan | Soul Exterior | Prefig | recognition or projection of Pattern before manifestation | LOCKED name; WORKING expanded description |

The shortest working formulations are:

> **Presynce recognizes present, material, embodied reality in relation to Identity.**

> **Resynce recognizes Capacity through response, change, pressure, or load.**

> **Precog recognizes trajectory or condition in relation to Integrity.**

> **Prefog recognizes Pattern that already exists but is obscured.**

> **Prefig recognizes or projects Pattern before manifestation.**

These ownerships are fixed. Faculty ownership is not an independent House
decision, office, credential, or grant of authority.

## Presynce — Body — Stonebend

Presynce recognizes the present embodied condition in service of Identity.
Stonebend's active Synthesis ladder is:

```text
Gremlin → Goblin → Ghoul → Spectre → Troll → Ork → Ogre → Troglodyte
```

The faculty creates no parallel form identity. Hollow Grove's exact
Current/Aura level crosswalk is:

```text
Gremlin ↔ Pixie
Goblin ↔ Sprite
Ghoul ↔ Fairy
Spectre ↔ Spirit
Troll ↔ Siren
Ork ↔ Sylph
Ogre ↔ Nymph
Troglodyte ↔ Muse
```

`Pixy` and `Faerie` remain stable compatibility aliases for active `Pixie` and
`Fairy`.

## Resynce — Spirit — Flynt

Resynce recognizes Capacity through response, change, pressure, or load. Flynt
expresses this amid movement, force, action, engineering, routes, encounters,
and response under pressure.

We Fairy Men and The Gallows remain distinct addressable contexts of one
underlying structure. We Fairy Men is the creature-sect leadership body beneath
the Tross; The Gallows is its organized underworld manifestation when acting
collectively under his direction. Faculty relation does not make the Gallows a
public civic authority, collapse either context into Mystery Men, or make any
of them a replacement for Flynt's OPEN general societal designation.

## Precog — Mind — Glaüshouse

Precog recognizes trajectory or condition in relation to Integrity. It may
support evidence-grounded perception and disclosed uncertainty, but is never
omniscient and never guarantees the future.

Glaüshouse's Current-side expression is Biokinesis and its Aura-side expression
is Noomancy. Faculty remains orthogonal to both.

## Prefog — Soul Interior — Sandmanor Minorian

Prefog recognizes Pattern already present but obscured. Its association with
Hearth, interior, family, land, continuity, and established Pattern does not
make it a rank or a Current/Aura component.

## Prefig — Soul Exterior — Sandmanor Minoan

Prefig recognizes or projects Pattern before manifestation. Its association
with horizon, anticipation, outward movement, possibility, and the Current Sea
does not make the Current Sea literal ocean geography. The Current Sea may
manifest across land.

## Sandmanor relationship

Prefog and Prefig are equal faculties belonging to distinct parallel peoples
and traditions. They do not define a universal decision pipeline and do not
replace Sandmanor's distinct Forms, social roles, cultural domains, attendant
classes, sovereign Hands, or Sandman/Demiurge identity. Their current ontology
is defined in `HOLLOW_GROVE_SYNTHESIS_CANON_V1.md` and
`SANDMANOR_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`.

Existing proof and revision systems may consume faculty observations, but a
faculty observation is not proof, credential, standard, recognition, office,
or successful Synthesis.

## Recipe integration and V1 compatibility

A `SynthesisRecipe` may contain zero or more `FacultyManifestation` records.
Legacy Recipes remain valid with an empty manifestation list.

The existing typed fields for trigger, decision posture, expression, Frame,
Flow, Glow, Current/Aura polarity, Bond phase, and regional jurisdiction are
**LEGACY PHYSICAL / OPERATIONAL COMPATIBILITY MECHANICS**. Their archive schema
remains V1. They may represent how a faculty was executed historically but do
not redefine the faculty's semantic direction or the Practitioner ontology.

Recipe compilation validates manifestations and emits only existing
`SynthesisScript` values derived from Recipe intents. Faculty declarations emit
no scripts and execute nothing independently.

## Frame / Flow / Glow boundary

- Frame remains a stabilized Being or transformation identity.
- Flow and Glow retain their historical V1 runtime meanings.
- Flow and Glow do not define Practitioner Current or Aura.
- `Pixy` remains a legacy decode alias for active `Pixie`.
- `Faerie` remains a legacy decode alias for active `Fairy`.
- Spirit is the active advanced Marshal-branch Glaüshouse Synthesis.
- Sylph is the active Aura counterpart at pairing level 6 and creates no
  clinical office by itself.

## Constitutional bounds

Every faculty remains bounded by evidence, uncertainty, House authority, Bond
state, regional jurisdiction, required institutional recognition, consent, and
the applicable Recipe and compatibility mechanics. It cannot choose for the
player, manufacture legality, create proof, grant office, or execute Synthesis
independently.
