# Hueman Faculties V1

Status: canonical additive Hueman faculty law

Authority boundary: world and Recipe domain above the frozen universal
recursion kernel and Constitutional Runtime V2

## Canonical Ownership

| House | Hueman domain | Faculty | Constitutional expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stonebend | Body | Presynce | embodied synchronization with emerging physical movement |
| Flynt | Spirit | Resynce | synchronization with Beings, groups, routes, and encounters |
| Glaüshouse | Mind | Precog | evidence-grounded perception of emerging patterns and probable consequences |
| Sandmanor / Minorian / Gnome | Soul Interior | Prefog | generation and preservation of multiple legal possibilities |
| Sandmanor / Minoan / Elf | Soul Exterior | Prefig | provisional embodiment of a selected legal possibility |

The shortest canonical formulation is:

> **Presynce feels emergence.**

> **Resynce joins relation.**

> **Precog foresees consequence.**

> **Prefog opens possibility.**

> **Prefig forms becoming.**

These ownerships are fixed. Faculty ownership is a constitutional
classification, not an independent House decision, office, credential, or
grant of authority.

## Presynce — Body — Stonebend

Presynce is the Body's synchronization with emerging physical movement. A
Being feels force, balance, tension, pressure, momentum, and bodily motion
before deliberate thought completes. It is embodied anticipation, not
supernatural prophecy. It does not guarantee a dodge, counter, success, or
safe result.

The complete Current Form ladder is the principal Presynce embodiment ladder:

```text
Gremlin
→ Goblin
→ Ghoul
→ Spectre
→ Troll
→ Ork
→ Ogre
→ Troglodyte
```

The whole ladder remains Presynce. It is not divided between Presynce and
Resynce. Each stage remains a `FrameId`; the faculty layer creates no parallel
form identity.

## Resynce — Spirit — Flynt

Resynce synchronizes Spirit with other Beings, groups, routes, and encounters.
It recognizes rhythm, allegiance, challenge, invitation, trust, rivalry,
commitment, and relational momentum. It operates through relational Flow,
Bond state, affiliation, routes, encounters, and recognition. It does not read
minds and never mutates a Being's Current Form.

### We Fairy Men

The We Fairy Men express mobile communal Resynce as the itinerant frontier
ridgefolk of Aura Ridge. They travel as caravaners, scouts, guides, traders,
performers, salvagers, escorts, and pathfinders. They keep routes open, carry
news and trade, recognize fellow travelers, and live through movement across
the wild Ridge.

This faculty jurisdiction does not make We Fairy Men a formal Flynt state
institution or give them Flynt civic authority. It also does not erase or
rewrite their placement in the frozen Flynt Constitution. Their stable group
identity remains distinct from the Gallows institution.

### The Gallows

The Gallows express consequential underground Resynce inside Flynt: reputation,
debt, favor, obligation, covert enforcement, illicit negotiation, contraband,
black-market Recipes, unofficial violence, and deniable political pressure.
The Mystery Man and Mr. X lead the Gallows as underground identities of the
Tross; they are not a second person or a subordinate agent. The Gallows and We
Fairy Men remain distinct addressable entities, with distinct faculty regions
and functions. Cultural relation is not institutional identity, and neither is
silently merged into the other.

## Precog — Mind — Glaüshouse

Precog perceives emerging patterns, intentions, meanings, and likely
consequences before they become explicit. It projects probable continuations
from present evidence through Glow. Every projection discloses uncertainty
and remains bounded by the current Frame, available Glow, Aura and Current
polarity, condition, evidence, and lawful context. Precog is never omniscient
and never guarantees the future.

## Prefog — Soul Interior — Sandmanor Minorian

Prefog is the inward Minorian/Gnome half of the Soul. It keeps multiple legal
futures, designs, responses, and transformations open without prematurely
selecting one. It governs imagination, ambiguity, conceptual exploration,
unrealized potential, inward experimentation, and possibility generation.

Prefog is not uncontrolled randomness. A Prefog declaration identifies legal
candidate count, evidence, uncertainty, current Frame, polarity, and any Bond,
regional, or institutional bounds. A Prefog possibility is not proof.

## Prefig — Soul Exterior — Sandmanor Minoan

Prefig is the outward Minoan/Elf half of the Soul. It selects a legal
possibility already chosen by the existing deterministic decision posture and
gives it an initial demonstrable form. It governs prototypes, demonstrations,
architecture, engineering, applied teaching, craftsmanship, regional
construction, and the first embodiment of a design.

Prefig may create only a provisional or demonstrable embodiment. It does not
create proof, permanence, credential, standard, recognition, office, or
successful transformation. The existing Sandmanor proof lifecycle alone may
advance evidence from a Prefig source into demonstrated, reproduced,
field-proven, credentialed, or standardized reliance.

## Equal Sandmanor Soul

Minorian and Minoan are equal constitutional traditions. Prefog is not
superior to Prefig; Prefig is not execution labor subordinate to Prefog.
Neither independently claims complete Sandmanor authority. A valid process
permits inward possibility and outward demonstration to correct one another.

The canonical relationship is:

> **Prefog opens possibility.**

> **Prefig gives possibility form.**

> **Proof tests the Prefig.**

> **Failure and evidence return the result to Prefog for revision.**

```text
Prefog → Prefig → Proof → Evidence or Failure → Revision → Prefog
```

Failed Prefig evidence remains part of the constitutional record. Returning to
Prefog never deletes, relabels, or launders the failure.

## Regional Soul Manifestations

- `Gnome → Minotaur` remains exclusive to Aura Field. Minotaur is the mature
  Minorian embodiment of cultivated Prefog through cultivation, tending,
  experimentation, fertility, stored possibility, and improvement of the
  Fields.
- `Elf → Centaur` remains exclusive to Aura Beach and its Current Sea
  jurisdiction. Centaur is the mature Minoan embodiment of Prefig through
  movement, demonstration, exterior construction, navigation, patrol, and
  public embodiment. Centaurs roam Aura Beach and safeguard Current Sea
  approaches, crowd movement, and public access.

Minotaur and Centaur are regional Synthesis manifestations. They are not new
constitutional peoples, offices, proof authorities, or replacements for the
underlying Minorian/Gnome and Minoan/Elf traditions.

## Recipe Integration

A `SynthesisRecipe` may contain zero or more `FacultyManifestation` records.
Legacy Recipes therefore remain valid with an empty manifestation list.

A manifestation declares:

- faculty, domain, and canonical ownership;
- the faculty-specific trigger and expression;
- disclosed uncertainty;
- existing constitutional `EvidenceRef` requirements;
- current `FrameId`;
- required `FlowId` or `GlowId` where applicable;
- allowed Current/Aura polarity;
- relevant Bond phase;
- regional jurisdiction;
- institutional recognition where required.

Recipe compilation validates manifestations and then emits only the existing
`SynthesisScript` values derived from Recipe intents. Faculty declarations emit
no scripts and execute nothing independently.

## Frame / Flow / Glow Boundary

- Frame remains the stabilized Being or transformation identity.
- Flow remains the Current-based physical power set.
- Glow remains the Aura-based perceptual or psychic power set.
- Faculty manifestation describes how the Being anticipates or forms action
  through the current Frame, Flow, and Glow.

Presynce normally manifests through physical Frame state and Flow. Resynce
manifests through relational Flow, Bonds, affiliation, routing, encounters, and
recognition. Precog requires Glow-backed evidence projection. Prefog produces
multiple legal candidate possibilities. Prefig gives the already-selected
candidate a provisional or demonstrable Recipe form.

## Existing Decision Posture

```text
Observe:  Presynce detects physical emergence; Resynce detects relational emergence.
Generate: Prefog produces multiple legal candidates.
Evaluate: Precog projects probable consequences from evidence and uncertainty.
Choose:   existing deterministic constitutional decision logic selects a legal candidate.
Execute or demonstrate: Prefig provisionally embodies the selected candidate through the Recipe.
```

There is no faculty `Choose` operation. Faculties do not form a second decision
engine.

## Constitutional Bounds

Every faculty remains bounded by current Frame, available Flow and Glow,
Current and Aura polarity, Recipe legality, evidence, uncertainty, House
authority, Bond state, regional jurisdiction, and required institutional
recognition.

Categorically invalid claims include:

- Presynce as automatic dodge or counter;
- Resynce as mind reading or Frame mutation;
- Precog as certainty or guaranteed future;
- Prefog as proof, mastery, or uncontrolled randomness;
- Prefig as proof, permanent reality, credential, standard, office, or
  independent Synthesis execution;
- faculty ownership reassigned to another House or Sandmanor tradition;
- legacy membership promoted into faculty, mastery, proof, credential, or
  office;
- Minotaur or Centaur treated as a new people or authority;
- `institution.sandmanor.sandmen` treated as anything beyond the existing
  compatibility proof-body adapter.

## Persistence and Migration

Faculty manifestations use the versioned `HGFAC` JSON archive implemented by
`src/world/hueman_faculties.rs`. Encoding, decoding, and replay validate the
same law and reproduce canonical bytes deterministically.

No legacy affiliation or Recipe state infers a faculty. Existing Recipes load
with zero manifestations; legacy memberships remain memberships only.

## Runtime Boundary

The universal recursion kernel and frozen common Constitutional Runtime V2 are
unchanged. Current Runtime remains authoritative for identity, Bonds, Current,
Aura, shared House decisions, common Synthesis causality, persistence, replay,
and provenance. Sandmanor V2 remains authoritative for proof. Faculty law is a
typed Recipe/world declaration layer and nothing more.
