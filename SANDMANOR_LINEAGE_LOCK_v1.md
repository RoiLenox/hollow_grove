Status: LOCKED ontology supplement beneath `SANDMANOR_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`

population and they do not evolve into one another.

The locked complete branch progressions are:

```text
Gnome → Faun → Minotaur → Hecaton
Elf → Satyr → Centaur → Pegasus
```

The biological Form portions end at Minotaur and Centaur. Hecaton and Pegasus
complete the branches through separate sovereign-Hand investiture rather than
ordinary biological Form transition. The paired tiers are Gnome ↔ Elf, Faun ↔
Satyr, Minotaur ↔ Centaur, and Hecaton ↔ Pegasus. These are structural
parallels, not identical mechanics. Their deeper biological or metaphysical
transition mechanics remain **STATUS: OPEN**.

The locked social-role progressions are:

```text
Hearthkeeper → Householder → Husbandman
Beach Bum → Party Boy → Socialite
```

The first line maintains, organizes, and stewards private household life.
The second finds, creates, and becomes a center of public social life. They are
complementary, not moral opposites. Socialite is the mature Minoan title;
Influencer is not a synonym or alternate canonical stage.

Geomancer and Labyrinthian are institutional attendant classes. They are not
Forms or social roles. **Hecaton is perfected force and the Right Hand of The
Sandman. Pegasus is perfected motion and the Left Hand of The Sandman.** They
complete their respective branches but are not ordinary fourth biological
Forms. Their appointment, succession, and removal mechanics remain
**STATUS: OPEN**.

**The Sandman = Demiurge.** Demiurge is the sovereign Synthesizer and apex
identity, not an ordinary third attendant class. The Sandman is command of both
Hands and the final sovereign culmination above them. The two peoples converge
in the Sandman's sovereignty without becoming the same population.

Content Farm cultivates what a family has and makes. Aura Field preserves and
projects what the household is. Aura Beach expresses, celebrates, and enjoys
what life has produced. This cultural movement is not species evolution and
not a literal economic pipeline.

Presence in this ontology contract alone grants no proof, clearance, Title,
recognition, social role, attendant office, sovereign Hand, or Sandman office.
