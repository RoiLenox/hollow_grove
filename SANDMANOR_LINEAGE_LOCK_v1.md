# Sandmanor Dual Lineage Lock

“Sandmanor contains two equal transformation lineages. The interior Minorian lineage is Gnome → Minotaur → Hecaton. The exterior Minoan lineage is Elf → Centaur → Pegasus. Hecaton expands capacity through four-armed material mastery. Pegasus expands reach through flight and horizon mastery.”

The interior and exterior associations describe culture and specialization, not permanent geographic class restrictions. Hecaton has four arms; its one-hundred-hands meaning is symbolic rather than a literal arm count.
