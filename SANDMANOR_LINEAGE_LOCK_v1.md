# Sandmanor Dual-People Ontology Lock

Status: LOCKED ontology supplement beneath `SANDMANOR_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`

population and they do not evolve into one another.

The locked mythological Form progressions are:

```text
Gnome → Faun → Minotaur
Elf → Satyr → Centaur
```

The paired tiers are Gnome ↔ Elf, Faun ↔ Satyr, and Minotaur ↔ Centaur.
These are structural parallels, not identical mechanics. Their deeper
biological or metaphysical transition mechanics remain **STATUS: OPEN**.

The locked social-role progressions are:

```text
Husbandman → Householder → Hearthkeeper
Beach Bum → Party Boy → Socialite
```

The first line cultivates, establishes, and preserves private household life.
The second finds, creates, and becomes a center of public social life. They are
complementary, not moral opposites. Socialite is the mature Minoan title;
Influencer is not a synonym or alternate canonical stage.

Geomancer and Labyrinthian are institutional attendant classes. They are not
Forms or social roles. Hecaton and Pegasus are the two sovereign Hands
associated with the reigning Sandman. They are not fourth-stage Forms, and
their appointment, succession, and removal mechanics remain **STATUS: OPEN**.

**The Sandman = Demiurge.** Demiurge is the sovereign Synthesizer and apex
identity, not an ordinary third attendant class. The two peoples converge in
the Sandman's sovereignty without becoming the same population.

Content Farm cultivates what a family has and makes. Aura Field preserves and
projects what the household is. Aura Beach expresses, celebrates, and enjoys
what life has produced. This cultural movement is not species evolution and
not a literal economic pipeline.

Presence in this ontology contract alone grants no proof, clearance, Title,
recognition, social role, attendant office, sovereign Hand, or Sandman office.
