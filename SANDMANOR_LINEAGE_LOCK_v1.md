# Sandmanor Dual Lineage Lock

Status: retained lineage supplement beneath `SANDMANOR_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`

“Sandmanor contains two equal transformation lineages. The interior Minorian lineage is Gnome → Minotaur → Hecaton. The exterior Minoan lineage is Elf → Centaur → Pegasus. Hecaton expands capacity through four-armed material mastery. Pegasus expands reach through flight and horizon mastery.”

The interior and exterior associations describe culture and specialization, not permanent geographic class restrictions. Hecaton has four arms; its one-hundred-hands meaning is symbolic rather than a literal arm count.

The V2 Constitution first ratified Gnome → Minotaur in the Aura Field and Elf →
Centaur at Aura Beach and the Current Sea as executable regional Synthesis
designs. `SANDMANOR_GUARDIAN_AND_SUCCESSION_V1.md` now ratifies the adjacent
Minotaur → Hecaton and Centaur → Pegasus guardian paths through explicit
service proof, Recipe authorization, Glaüshouse compatibility, maintained
Synthesis, and investiture. Presence in the lineage contract alone still grants
no proof, clearance, Title, recognition, mantle, or Sandman office.
