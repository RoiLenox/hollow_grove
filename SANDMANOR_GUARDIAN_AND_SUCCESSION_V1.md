# Sandmanor Guardian and Succession Constitution V1

Status: ratified constitutional supplement to `SANDMANOR_CONSTITUTION_V2.md`

Executable model: `src/world/sandmanor/milestone.rs`

This supplement completes Sandmanor's peoples, geography, guardian Syntheses,
mantles, reciprocal Contest, and sovereign succession. It does not alter
combat, movement, party, rendering, the PokéWilds reference workspace, Central
Junction market behavior, another House's authority, or the recursion kernel.

## 1. The Two Directions of Design

Sandmanor is the House of Design and reciprocal improvement. Its two peoples
are complementary and constitutionally equal:

- Minorians are the interior Gnome-origin people. They cultivate life from the
  inside outward.
- Minoans are the exterior and coastal Elf-origin people. They protect and
  design life from the outside inward.

Neither is a cosmetic faction, caste, or subordinate branch. The distinction
governs geography, work, culture, guardian Recipes, Synthesis, mantles,
leadership eligibility, and reciprocal teaching.

Minorian life emphasizes nourishment, patience, seasons, routine,
stewardship, interior organization, storage, attention, coordinated labor, and
responsibility for what enters a life. Minoan life joins relaxed New Zealand
coastal life, California surf and beach culture, lifeguarding, spring-break
freedom, maritime competence, public hospitality, environmental stewardship,
movement, and horizon-awareness. Most Minoans live ordinary recreational,
rescue, design, conservation, hospitality, harbor, teaching, and public lives;
they are not reduced to soldiers or police.

> **The Minorian asks how a place is lived within.**

> **The Minoan asks how a place lives within the world.**

> **The Minorians cultivate what enters a life.**

> **The Minoans guard where life meets the world.**

> **Interior gives the world intimacy. Exterior gives intimacy a world.**

## 2. Aura Farm

Aura Farm is one Minorian cultivation system with two constitutional halves:

1. **Aura Fields** cultivate physical nourishment and useful material life:
   crops, orchards, herbs, medicinal plants, flowers, fibers, livestock, soil,
   irrigation, storage, preservation, tools, and cultivated environments.
2. **Content Farm** cultivates mental, cultural, and Aura nourishment: stories,
   lessons, articles, memory, tutorials, public information, entertainment,
   performance, images, archives, advertising, trends, and digital
   environments.

They are not separate Houses, currencies, or economic indexes.

> **Food feeds the Body. Content feeds the Mind and Aura.**

A healthy Content Farm educates, preserves memory, entertains responsibly,
cultivates skill, nourishes attention, and maintains cultural continuity.
Content production is not inherently corrupt.

An exploitative Content Farm may prioritize volume over quality, exhaust its
audience, imitate without understanding, manufacture outrage, cultivate
addiction, automate empty repetition, harvest attention without nourishment,
or falsify popularity.

> **A field can be overworked. So can an audience.**

Minorian duty protects the field, worker, audience, and future harvest rather
than maximizing present yield at their expense.

## 3. Aura Beach and Southern Law

The north-to-south order is:

```text
Sandmanor
→ Free Aura Beach
→ Southern Coast
→ Current Break
→ Minoan County Courthouse
→ Glaüshouse
```

**Free Aura Beach** is the northern liberty-oriented coast of surfing,
swimming, festivals, music, dance, sport, hospitality, public art, fashion,
gathering, cafés, trails, conservation, and ordinary public coastal life.
Lifeguards protect life and liberty; they are not general police.

> **You are free until your freedom endangers someone else.**

Warnings, education, rescue, de-escalation, and public responsibility precede
punishment when circumstances allow.

Regulation increases gradually southward because Current danger, rescue,
restricted water, emergency traffic, Manticorp training, smuggling, evacuation,
disorder, and Glaüshouse intake require permits, patrols, safety inspections,
lawful searches, curfews, closures, or detention in proportion to risk.

This is **Southern Law**, a Minoan coastal jurisdiction. It is neither a fifth
House, universal Hollow Grove criminal law, Glaüshouse law, nor a replacement
for Stonebend law.

## 4. Current Break and Manticorp

Current Break is controlled dangerous southern coast used for rescue-swimmer
training, open-water endurance, storm drills, evacuation, amphibious entry,
emergency extraction, hazard navigation, team carries, restricted-current
trials, and authorized Manticorp maritime training.

Manticorp remains one Flynt institution:

- Flynt controls command, curriculum, military identity, equipment, and
  institutional advancement.
- Sandmanor governs the coast and authorizes access to Current Break.
- Minoan guardians teach rescue, weather, environmental awareness, coastal
  survival, and lawful maritime conduct.
- Manticorp candidates remain Flynt-bound guests or authorized trainees.

> **Flynt teaches candidates how to operate the machine. The Minoans teach them what happens when the world refuses to behave like one.**

Current Break never creates, owns, or commands a second Manticorp.

## 5. Minoan County Courthouse

The official name is **MINOAN COUNTY COURTHOUSE**. It stands immediately after
Current Break and immediately before the Glaüshouse border. It is the last
major Sandmanor institution on the southern coast.

The courthouse handles Minoan marine and coastal law, permits, restricted-water
and Current Break violations, Manticorp training-zone cases, public-order
matters, booking, arraignment, temporary holding, lawful detention,
rescue-liability and supported coastal-property disputes, and lawful transfers
into Glaüshouse.

The courthouse remains Sandmanor/Minoan authority. A transfer into Glaüshouse
gives Glaüshouse authority over emergency medicine, stabilization, Synthesis
compatibility, repair, recovery, rehabilitation, or already-lawful clinical
custody. It does not transfer ownership of the courthouse or make Glaüshouse
the law House.

> **The Minoan County Courthouse determines what must answer to the law. Glaüshouse determines what can be repaired afterward.**

Ordinary speech includes:

- “The courthouse is the last stop before Glaüshouse.”
- “North of the courthouse, the lifeguards warn you.”
- “At the courthouse, Southern Law takes your name.”
- “Do not carry that past the courthouse.”

The name County does not impose a world-wide county system.

## 6. Guardian Synthesis and Mantle

Guardian progress is never automatic species evolution or a numerical
level-up:

```text
ordinary service
→ demonstrated qualification
→ Recipe authorization
→ Glaüshouse compatibility and lawful Synthesis
→ investiture into a mantle
→ continued service, maintenance, and renewal
```

Qualification, Recipe, Synthesis Form, mantle, jurisdiction, and present
authority remain separate records. Stable person identity continues through
every stage.

The Synthesis Form is a real maintained body. The mantle is a public trust. A
guardian whose mantle is suspended, removed, or retired loses authority but
does not automatically lose or regress the physical Form.

> **The Form remains a body. The mantle remains a trust.**

The maintained-Synthesis doctrine applies: Continuance depends on Recipe
practice, Ways, maintenance, renewal, compatible environment, institutional
care, and the Hueman. Political defeat never becomes automatic bodily
punishment.

## 7. Minorian Guardian Line

```text
Gnome → Minotaur → Hecaton
```

A Gnome proves responsible tending of a physical or cultural field. Output
volume alone never qualifies a candidate.

Minotaur is a real Synthesis Form. Its mantle is **GUARDIAN OF THE FIELDS**,
assigned to a defined field, farm, orchard, medicinal system, storage and
distribution system, Content Farm, or other cultivated environment. Proof may
include surviving scarcity, restoring soil, protecting workers and livestock,
maintaining irrigation, preventing waste, correcting an exploitative Content
Farm, preserving public knowledge, coordinating emergency, and accepting
failure.

> **The Gnome tends the field. The Minotaur answers for it.**

Hecaton is the higher maintained Minorian Synthesis. Its mantle is **GUARDIAN OF THE WHOLE FARM**.
Its many hands signify coordination across fields,
workers, tools, irrigation, medicine, livestock, storage, distribution,
education, Content Farms, public information, and cultural nourishment.

Eligibility requires preventing separate cultivated systems from failing one
another through whole-system coordination, sustainable distribution,
nonexploitative labor, truthful crisis information, and responsibility for
cascading failure.

> **The Minotaur guards a field. Hecaton keeps the fields from failing one another.**

Hecaton is broader responsibility, not merely a stronger Minotaur.

## 8. Minoan Guardian Line

```text
Elf → Centaur → Pegasus
```

An Elf proves responsible care for a defined coast through ordinary coastal,
recreational, rescue, design, conservation, public-event, education,
hospitality, harbor, weather, surf, or swimming work.

Centaur is a real Synthesis Form. Its mantle is **GUARDIAN OF THE BEACH**.
Proof includes rescue, endurance, patrol, crowd protection, hazard
recognition, evacuation, hospitality, open-water movement, and responsibility
under pressure. The mantle may close a dangerous beach, order evacuation,
organize rescue, control immediate hazards, and coordinate an assigned shore.
It grants neither unlimited police nor military power.

> **The Elf watches the shore. The Centaur answers when the shore breaks.**

Pegasus is the higher maintained Minoan Synthesis. Its mantle is **GUARDIAN OF THE HORIZON**.
It governs regional awareness across coastwide weather, storms,
distant danger, settlement evacuation, Current Sea approaches, public
movement, land-sea-sky relationships, navigation, and multiple coastal
communities.

> **The Centaur guards the beach. Pegasus guards the horizon.**

Pegasus is regional overview and early recognition, not merely a flying
Centaur.

## 9. Suspension, Removal, and Renewal

A guardian mantle may be suspended or removed for abandonment, exploitation,
reckless endangerment, falsified evidence, unlawful Recipe use, corruption,
abuse, lost competence, refusal of lawful renewal, or existing constitutional
removal procedure.

Records support suspension, investigation, removal, lawful restoration, Recipe
renewal, maintained Form, voluntary retirement, and successor recognition.
Loss of mantle and physical regression remain distinct events.

## 10. One Design Pole

Sandmanor remains one Creation-of-Form pole and has one Sandmanor Design Index.
It contains two subdisciplines:

- **Interior and Cultivated Design**: interiors, rooms, workspaces, storage,
  atmosphere, lighting, furniture, cultivated environments, farms, Content
  Farms, routines, nourishment, and lived support.
- **Exterior and Coastal Design**: landscape, beach, coast, public space,
  street, route, façade, port, park, gathering, movement, and environmental
  relationship.

Projects may record interior/cultivated and exterior/coastal weights, but both
exposures remain in the single official index. No Minorian or Minoan currency
or index exists.

## 11. Reciprocal Improvement

Hecaton masters cultivation, nourishment, patience, seasonal thinking,
interior stewardship, coordinated labor, Aura Fields, Content Farm, and
long-term Continuance.

Pegasus masters liberty, movement, public hospitality, coastal stewardship,
rescue, readiness, weather, horizon-awareness, Aura Beach, and Current Sea
approaches.

> **The Minorian learns to open the gate. The Minoan learns to tend what lies behind it.**

The traditions remain distinct enough to teach one another. The sovereign
must receive another Way without losing their own.

## 12. Contest of Improvement

Each final Contest pair contains exactly:

- one qualified Minorian Hecaton holding the Guardian of the Whole Farm mantle;
- one qualified Minoan Pegasus holding the Guardian of the Horizon mantle.

No two Hecatons, two Pegasi, base Forms, mantleless candidates, or candidates
with unresolved disqualifying corruption form the final pair.

The Hecaton sincerely teaches cultivation, nourishment, patience, seasonal
thinking, interior stewardship, coordinated labor, Aura Fields, Content Farm,
and responsibility for what enters a life. The Pegasus sincerely teaches
liberty, public movement, hospitality, coastal stewardship, rescue, readiness,
horizon-awareness, Aura Beach, and Current Sea approaches.

> **The crowd measures change, not rank.**

> **The Sandman is not the one who proves he already knows the most. The Sandman is the one who proves he can become more through another Way.**

## 13. Baselines and Five Public Trials

Before teaching, the record preserves stable candidate identity, existing Form
and mantle, competencies, weaknesses, teacher, lessons, trials, evidence, and
baseline. Final evidence records new competence and integration relative to
that candidate's own baseline.

Absolute mastery, original power, age, candidate order, insertion order,
inheritance, random number, incumbency, or House favoritism never selects the
winner.

The five canonical trial domains are:

1. **Aura Field Trial** — the Pegasus performs cultivation and interior
   stewardship without the Hecaton completing the work.
2. **Content Farm Trial** — the Pegasus cultivates truthful, educational,
   memory-preserving, or attention-nourishing public content.
3. **Liberty and Hospitality Trial** — the Hecaton preserves free public life
   without enclosing or abandoning it.
4. **Rescue and Horizon Trial** — the Hecaton demonstrates evacuation, hazard
   recognition, weather response, rescue, and awareness beyond an enclosed
   domain.
5. **Reciprocal Integration Trial** — both Ways address one combined field,
   coast, information, shelter, movement, festival, storm, or settlement
   problem without erasing individual improvement.

## 14. Crowd Judgment and Review

The crowd is the constitutional judge of improvement. The existing independent
Contest review body audits eligibility, identity, baseline, trial evidence,
conflicts, and ballots; it does not replace the crowd with private
appointment. Thus the Contest remains evidence-based rather than a popularity
contest.

One recognized judgment may count per eligible stable identity. Duplicate,
ineligible, conflicted, coerced, or evidence-mismatched judgments do not count.
The public tally records excluded judgments and the reason they failed.

The crowd evaluates genuine learning, measurable baseline-relative change,
integration rather than imitation, respect for the other Way, improvement to
original mastery, public trust, and teaching conduct. It cannot reward
sabotage, humiliation, false evidence, imitation without understanding,
degrading the other candidate, coercion, or duplicate ballots.

A valid tie produces no arbitrary Sandman. The office remains unresolved until
a further reciprocal trial or later Contest yields a lawful verdict.

## 15. Teaching Integrity

Each candidate must teach sincerely. False, incomplete, or dangerous
instruction intended to prevent improvement is Contest sabotage. It may
invalidate the saboteur, void affected evidence, require replacement trials,
or trigger discipline.

The Contest measures the capacity both to learn and to share mastery:

> **I can receive another Way without losing my own.**

## 16. Sandman Convergence and Mantle

The most-improved candidate becomes eligible through:

```text
crowd verdict
→ constitutional recognition
→ Sandman Recipe authorization
→ Glaüshouse compatibility review
→ lawful maintained Sandman Synthesis or convergence
→ investiture into the singular Sandman mantle
```

The Sandman is not a physical fusion of two people. The winner integrates the
other Way while preserving one stable person identity. The losing candidate
retains their guardian Form and mantle unless independently removed, remains
honored, may continue serving, and may enter a later lawful Contest.

The Sandman Form is a real maintained Synthesis/convergence. The Sandman mantle
is the singular sovereign authority. Only one active mantle exists. Removal of
the mantle does not automatically erase the Form.

The mantle requires equal protection of both peoples; liberty with
responsibility; restraint of unnecessary Southern Law and reckless beach harm;
healthy Aura Fields and Content Farms; coastal and Current Sea protection;
guardian Recipe integrity; reciprocal teaching; qualified successors; and no
subordination of either people.

## 17. Historical Name

> **Long ago, he was called Aegon. Today, he is known as the Sandman.**

Aegon is a historical name for the same Sandman sovereign identity and
tradition. It is not another Form, office, ruler, mantle, or transformation
stage. **Aegaeon is not canonical terminology.**

## 18. House and Runtime Boundaries

- Sandmanor proves service, guardian qualification, Recipe viability,
  reciprocal learning, trials, and the Contest result.
- Glaüshouse governs compatibility, clinical Synthesis, maintained
  Continuance, regression, and medical care. It does not select the Sandman.
- Stonebend may preserve identity, formal recording, or Title where existing
  law requires. It does not select the winner.
- Flynt may recognize lawful function and retains sole Manticorp command. It
  does not control Minoan guardians.
- Central Junction receives Sandmanor Design evidence without owning Sandmanor
  institutions, the Contest, or either design discipline.

The event-backed guardian model uses caller-supplied stable IDs and replays
qualification, Recipe authorization, Synthesis, investiture, suspension,
restoration, removal, and retirement without deriving identity from array,
candidate, ballot, insertion, random, or catalog order.
