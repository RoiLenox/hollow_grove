## How a decision becomes adopted

Record the user's actual answer, the exact affected scope and the owning source.
Review existing constitutional constraints before changing the status or designing
records. A general instruction to continue does not retroactively settle every
explicitly open product, amount, biological equation or office procedure.
The source register and its validator must evolve together when a proposal is
actually adopted; the present checker intentionally rejects silent promotions.

After any required implementation, execute the relevant private headless
witnesses and record what they establish. Source/link checks alone grant neither
eligibility nor verified consequence. Original saves and earlier source meaning
remain attributable across migration and review.
